SENATE . . . . . . . . . . . . . . No. 1615
By Mr. Oliveira, a petition (accompanied by bill, Senate, No. 1615) of Jacob R. Oliveira for
legislation to improve patient health care coordination and provide a clear discharge plan. Public
Health.
The Commonwealth of Massachusetts
_______________
In the One Hundred and Ninety-Fourth General Court
(2025-2026)
_______________
An Act to improve health care coordination and safe discharge.
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority
of the same, as follows:
1 SECTION 2. In the case of a patient with a home care agency, a hospital shall notify a
2 patient's home care agency and primary care doctor on discharge steps and medication
3 instructions in a clear format. A hospital shall ensure notification on treatment steps and any
4 updated medication to the patient's primary care doctor and home health care agency.
5 SECTION 3. Section 236 of Chapter 111 of the General Laws, as so appearing, is hereby
6 amended by adding after the words “the urgency of the need for caregiver services”, in
7 subsection (c), the following words:- A hospital shall ensure clear discharge plan to a family
8 caregiver including clear instructions to caregivers with limited language proficiency.
9 SECTION 4. Section 51D of Chapter 111 of the General Laws, as so appearing, is hereby
10 amended by adding after the words “provided separately” the following words:- provided further
11 that medication instructions are clear.
