An Act regarding free expression. 
 
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority 
of the same, as follows: 
 
1  SECTION 1. Chapter 71 of the General Laws is hereby amended by inserting after 
2 section 82 the following 3 sections:-   
3  Section 82A. (a) A determination that materials are appropriate for inclusion in a school 
4 library by a school library teacher licensed by the department or, in the absence of such school 
5 library teacher, by the school employee responsible for the selection and display of library 
6 materials shall: (i) be age appropriate; (ii) serve an educational purpose; and (iii) be based on the 
7 teacher’s or employee’s professional training and not on personal, political or doctrinal views. 
8 For the purposes of this section a school library shall mean a library within a school department 
9 of a city or town, a regional school, an independent vocational school or a vocational school or 
10 agricultural school operated by a county.    
11  (b) No challenged material selected for inclusion in a school library collection pursuant to 
12 subsection (a) shall be removed except upon a determination and vote by the school committee 
13 following notice, a public hearing and a finding by a review committee of school personnel, 

2 of 6 14 appointed by the school committee and the superintendent, that the material is devoid of any 
15 educational, literary, artistic, personal or social value or is not age appropriate for any child who 
16 attends the school.    
17  (c) A decision of the school committee to remove materials from a school library may be 
18 challenged by a student, parent or guardian through a petition for a writ of mandamus in the 
19 supreme judicial court or superior court. 
20  (d) Nothing in this section shall preclude school employees responsible for school library 
21 content from deciding to remove materials that have become outdated or to accommodate new 
22 materials; provided, however, that the decision to remove materials shall not made based on 
23 personal, political or doctrinal views. 
24  Section 82B. Each school district, charter school and local education agency shall 
25 establish a written policy for its school library for: (i) the selection of school library materials, 
26 including textbooks, instructional materials, books or other library resources; (ii)  the use of 
27 library materials and library facilities; and (iii) responding to challenges requesting to remove or 
28 restrict library materials based on the objections of a person or group. The written policy shall: 
29 (i) be in accordance with standards adopted by the American Library Association; (ii) be posted 
30 on the school district, charter school or local education agency’s website; and (iii) provide that if 
31 material selected by the school library teacher or responsible school employee is the subject of a 
32 challenge requesting to remove or restrict its use, such material shall remain on the shelves of the 
33 school library pending a vote of the school committee made during the process for responding to 
34 such challenge in accordance with subsection (b) of section 82A. The board of library 
35 commissioners for the commonwealth through its affiliate, the Massachusetts Library System, 

3 of 6 36 shall: (i) make resources available to help school committees or other administrative authority of 
37 a school library create or modify written policies for the selection of library materials and the use 
38 of materials and facilities in accordance with the standards adopted by the American Library 
39 Association; and (ii) make sample policies available on its website.  
40  Annually, school districts, charter schools and local education agencies shall submit a 
41 report to the department, which shall include information on all challenges requesting to remove 
42 or restrict educational materials from its schools during the previous calendar year.   
43  Section 82C. No school employee shall lose the employee’s department-issued license or 
44 professional certification or be dismissed, disciplined, placed on probation, involuntarily 
45 transferred, be otherwise subjected to an adverse employment action, fined or imprisoned for the 
46 selection of library materials when the selection is made in good faith and in accordance with the 
47 policy adopted pursuant to section 82B.
