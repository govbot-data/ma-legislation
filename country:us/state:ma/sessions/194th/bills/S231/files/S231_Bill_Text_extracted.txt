 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority 
of the same, as follows: 
 
1  Section 87XX1/2 of chapter 112 of the General Laws, as appearing in the 2022 Official 
2 Edition, is hereby amended by inserting the following sentences:-  
3  The board may authorize any courses or programs for continuing education credits that 
4 satisfy this section. The board may adopt rules and regulations for the continuing professional 
5 education required for renewal of licenses.
