An Act honoring as veterans certain persons who served in the National Guard and Reserves. 
 
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority 
of the same, as follows: 
 
1  SECTION 1. Clause Forty-third of section 7 of chapter 4 of the General Laws, as 
2 appearing in the 2022 Official Edition, is hereby amended by adding the following paragraph:- 
3  “Reserve veteran”, shall mean a person (a) whose last discharge from the National Guard 
4 or Reserve was under honorable conditions; and (b) who performed at least 20 years of service as 
5 computed under 10 U.S.C. section 12732; provided, however, that a reserve veteran shall not be 
6 considered a veteran for the purposes of this clause solely by reason of satisfying this definition. 
7  SECTION 2.  Chapter 115 of the General Laws is hereby amended by adding the 
8 following section:- 

2 of 2 9  Section 16.  Reserve veterans shall be honored and recognized as veterans in the 
10 commonwealth; provided, however that a reserve veteran shall not be eligible for benefits 
11 administered under this chapter or any other general or special law solely by reason of being a 
12 reserve veteran.  
13  The commissioner shall develop a program for outreach to reserve veterans in order to 
14 effectively recognize their service and further their inclusion in all honorary and ceremonial 
15 events for veterans throughout the commonwealth.
