An Act relative to preventing discrimination against persons with disabilities in the provision of 
health care. 
 
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority 
of the same, as follows: 
 
1  SECTION 1. Section 1 of chapter 151B of the General Laws, as appearing in the 2020 
2 Official Edition, is hereby amended by striking out, in line 138, the word “handicap” and 
3 inserting in place thereof the following word:- disability. 
4  SECTION 2. Section 4 of said chapter 151B, as so appearing, is hereby amended by 
5 adding the following subsection:- 
6   (a) As used in this subsection, the following words shall have the following meaning 
7 unless the context clearly requires otherwise: 
8  “Disability”, shall have the same meaning as defined in section 1 of chapter 151B. 

2 of 5 9  “Short-term survival”, an individual’s assessed probability of surviving an acute illness 
10 from which they are presently suffering and being successfully discharged from a hospital or 
11 other inpatient medical facility. 
12  (b) It shall be an unlawful practice: 
13  (i) For any public or private entity or agency of the commonwealth, to approve or 
14 implement a plan for the distribution of scarce healthcare resources during a crisis, including, but 
15 not limited to, crisis standards of care implemented during a public health emergency, that deny 
16 an individual lifesaving treatment or place an individual at reduced priority for lifesaving 
17 treatment if such a determination is based on:  
18  (A) a presumption that an individual has a reduced quality of life due to a disability or 
19 chronic health condition;  
20  (B) a presumption that an individual’s life is less worth saving due to a disability or 
21 chronic health condition; or  
22  (C) any measure, metric, or third party analysis which has the effect of setting a value for 
23 the life of an individual or individuals with a specific disability or medical diagnosis that is less 
24 than the value given to the life of an individual or individuals without a disability; provided 
25 however that this subsection shall not prohibit such a plan from considering an individual’s 
26 prospects for short-term survival in determining whether they are prioritized for care. 
27  (ii) For any public or private entity or agency of the commonwealth, to withhold any 
28 medical treatment to an individual based on:  

3 of 5 29  (A) a presumption that an individual has a reduced quality of life due to a disability or 
30 chronic health condition;  
31  (B) a presumption that an individual’s life is less worth saving due to a disability or 
32 chronic health condition; or  
33  (C) any measure, metric, or third party analysis which has the effect of setting a value for 
34 the life of an individual or individuals with a specific disability or medical diagnosis that is less 
35 than the value given to the life of an individual or individuals without a disability. 
36  (iii) For any public or private entity or agency of the commonwealth, when determining 
37 whether a healthcare treatment should be available within a formulary, or determining the value 
38 of a healthcare treatment, to employ a measure or metric which assigns a reduced value to the 
39 life extension provided by a treatment based on a pre-existing disability or chronic health 
40 condition of the individuals whom the treatment would benefit. 
41  (iv) For a hospital or other entity engaged in the provision of healthcare to:  
42  (A) condition the provision of procedures or treatment on an individual having an order 
43 to not resuscitate, POLST (Physician Orders for Life-Sustaining Treatment), advance directive or 
44 any instruction relating to the administration, withholding or withdrawing of life-sustaining 
45 procedures or artificially administered nutrition and hydration;  
46  (B) communicate to any individual or person acting on behalf of the individual, before or 
47 after admission to the hospital, 

4 of 5 48  that treatment is conditioned on the individual having an order to not resuscitate, an 
49 advance directive or any instruction relating to the administration, withholding or withdrawing of 
50 life-sustaining procedures or artificially administered nutrition and hydration;  
51  (C) suggest to any individual, or person acting on behalf of the individual, who contacts 
52 the hospital regarding treatment for the individual that admission or treatment is conditioned on 
53 the individual having an order to not resuscitate, an advance directive or any instruction relating 
54 to the administration, withholding or withdrawing of life-sustaining procedures or artificially 
55 administered nutrition and hydration; or  
56  (D) discriminate in any other way against an individual based on whether the individual 
57 has an order to not resuscitate, an advance directive or any instruction relating to the 
58 administration, withholding or withdrawing of life-sustaining procedures or artificially 
59 administered nutrition and hydration. 
60  (c) This subsection shall not prohibit a hospital from providing written materials and 
61 information about advance directives to an individual or prohibit a licensed health care 
62 professional from engaging in a discussion with an individual about the written materials and 
63 information, so long as the professional does not disproportionately advise an individual to sign 
64 an advanced directive based on the race, ethnicity, gender, sexuality, or disability status of said 
65 individual. 
66  (d) Nothing in this subsection shall prevent healthcare practitioners, hospitals or other 
67 healthcare entities from providing a medically appropriate course of treatment to an individual 
68 that they believe will extend that individual’s life, improve their symptoms or alleviate pain and 
69  suffering. 

5 of 5 70  (e) The secretary of health and human services shall promulgate regulations to implement 
71 this subsection. 
72  SECTION 3. The secretary of health and human services shall promulgate regulations for 
73 the implementation of subsection 20 of section 4 of chapter 151B not later than 60 days after the 
74 effective date of this act.
