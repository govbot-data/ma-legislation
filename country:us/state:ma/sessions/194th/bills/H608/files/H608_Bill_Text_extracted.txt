An Act relative to independent child protection.
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority
of the same, as follows:
1 Notwithstanding any general or special law to the contrary, if a child with a disability for
2 whom a school committee currently provides or arranges for the provision of special education in
3 an approved private day or residential school placement, including placement in a pediatric
4 nursing home or his/her parent or guardian moves to a different school district, such school
5 committee of the former community of residence shall pay the approved budgeted costs,
6 including necessary transportation costs, safety expenses and inspections, of such day or
7 residential placement, including placement in a pediatric nursing home, of such child for the
8 balance of such fiscal year.
