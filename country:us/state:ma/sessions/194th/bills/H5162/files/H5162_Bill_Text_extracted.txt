An Act designating a segment of Route 20 as the Brodeur Curve. 
 
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority 
of the same, as follows: 
 
1  SECTION 1. The segment of Route 20 between mile markers 104.6 and 105.1 in the 
2 town of Oxford shall be designated and known as the Brodeur Curve, in memory of Michael and 
3 Kyle Brodeur of Charlton, who were struck head on by an impaired driver in November of 1997 
4 at the designated location of this act. Michael Brodeur was killed and Kyle Brodeur lived with 
5 severe brain damage until his untimely passing in 2025 due to his injuries from the accident. For 
6 the decades that followed the accident, Kyle and his mother, Lisa Brodeur, became advocates to 
7 improve the safer travel of Route 20; received recognitions for dedicating themselves to the 
8 education of high school students and driver's education students on the dangers of impaired 
9 driving, which also included helping individuals who lost their driver's licenses for doing so to 
10 make better choices; provided comfort to other victims of motor vehicle accidents and their 
11 families, and shared their story to save lives and help those with brain injuries. 

2 of 2 12  SECTION 2. The Massachusetts Department of Transportation shall erect and maintain 
13 suitable markers on the roadway bearing such designation in compliance with the standards of 
14 the department.
