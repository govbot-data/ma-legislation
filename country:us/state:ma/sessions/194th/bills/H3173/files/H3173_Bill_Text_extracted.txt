An Act relative to taxes on overtime wages.
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority
of the same, as follows:
1 SECTION 1. Section 1 of chapter 62 of the General Laws, as most recently amended by
2 section 100 of chapter 140 of the acts of 2024, is hereby further amended by adding the
3 following paragraph:-
4 (s) “Overtime wage”, wages paid to an hourly employee for working in excess of 40
5 hours per week; provided, that wages earned by salaried employees shall not constitute overtime
6 wage.
7 SECTION 2. Paragraph (b) of part B of section 3 of said chapter 62, as appearing in the
8 2022 Official Edition, is hereby amended by adding the following subparagraph:-
9 (6) An amount equal to the overtime wages earned by the taxpayer within the taxable
10 year.
