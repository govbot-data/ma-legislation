HOUSE . . . . . . . . No. 5179
The Commonwealth of Massachusetts
________________________________________
HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES, March 5, 2026.
The committee on Higher Education, to whom were referred the petition
(accompanied by resolve, House, No. 1422) of James C. Arena-DeRosa for
an investigation by a special commission relative to expanding the in-state
higher education tuition and fee waiver program; the petition (accompanied
by bill, House, No. 1442) of Bradley H. Jones, Jr., and others relative to the
expansion of the valedictorian tuition waiver program in the public system
of higher education; the petition (accompanied by bill, House, No. 1443) of
Bradley H. Jones, Jr., and others relative to higher education tuition and fee
waivers for seniors; the petition (accompanied by bill, House, No. 1450) of
Marc T. Lombardo relative to in-state tuition rates and fees at public
institutions of higher education; the petition (accompanied by bill, House,
No. 1459) of Danillo A. Sena and others for legislation to establish the
MassGrant program to provide financial assistance to eligible residents
enrolled at an eligible institution; the petition (accompanied by bill, House,
No. 1463) of Jeffrey Rosario Turco relative to a tuition and fee freeze at all
public colleges and universities; and the petition (accompanied by bill,
House, No. 1465) of Steven Ultrino that the Board of Higher Education be
directed to establish a program for certain school teachers and
paraprofessionals to attend courses at public institutions of higher education
at no tuition cost, reports recommending that the accompanying order
(House, No. 5179) ought to be adopted.
For the committee,
DAVID M. ROGERS.
