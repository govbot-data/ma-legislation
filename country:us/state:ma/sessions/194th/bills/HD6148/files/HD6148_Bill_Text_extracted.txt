(2025-2026)
_______________
An Act establishing a sick leave bank for Mark Giampa, an employee of the Massachusetts
Department of Correction.
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority
of the same, as follows:
1 Notwithstanding any general or special law to the contrary, the Massachusetts
2 Department of Correction shall establish a sick leave bank for Mark Giampa, an employee of the
3 Massachusetts Department of Correction. Any employee of the Massachusetts Department of
4 Correction may voluntarily contribute 1 or more sick, personal or vacation days to the sick leave
5 bank for use by Mark Giampa. If Mark Giampa terminates employment with the Massachusetts
6 Department of Correction or requests to dissolve the sick leave bank, any remaining time in the
7 sick leave bank shall be transferred to the extended illness leave bank. Sick leave bank days shall
8 not be used for absences unrelated to the illness or disability that necessitated the establishment
9 of the sick leave bank as determined by the Massachusetts Department of Correction.
