An Act relative to home care services. 
 
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority 
of the same, as follows: 
 
1  SECTION 1. Section 4 of chapter 19A of the General Laws, as appearing in the 2024 
2 Official Edition, is hereby amended by inserting before the second to last paragraph the 
3 following paragraph:- 
4  The department shall ensure that home care program services are made available to 
5 persons diagnosed with amyotrophic lateral sclerosis regardless of their age if they are otherwise 
6 eligible for such services.  
7  SECTION 2. The secretary of health and human services shall ensure that the relevant 
8 agencies under their purview, including the department of aging and independence, shall 
9 promulgate regulations as necessary to implement this act.
