An Act authorizing the town of Swansea to grant additional alcoholic beverage licenses.
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority
of the same, as follows:
1 SECTION 1. Notwithstanding the numerical limitations for such licenses that are
2 imposed upon the Town of Swansea pursuant to Section 17 of Chapter 138 of the General Laws,
3 or by any other general or special law to the contrary, the Swansea licensing authority may grant
4 up to six additional licenses for the sale of all alcoholic beverages to be drunk on the premises
5 under Section 12 of said Chapter 138.
6 SECTION 2. The additional Section 12 licenses that are allowed pursuant to this Act may
7 be granted by the Swansea licensing authority only for the types of establishments that are
8 otherwise eligible for Section 12 licenses, and only for such establishments that are located on
9 the property known as and comprising the “Swansea Mall Redevelopment Area”; this property
10 being identified as the area located on the northerly and southerly parcels along Route 6 from the
11 westerly boundary of Gardner Neck Road to the westerly boundary of Milton Reiser Road, as
12 contained in the Town of Swansea Zoning Map, Business B District, and from Route 6 and
1 of 3

13 Route 118 in a northerly direction 3,900 feet +/- to the northerly most point of the Swansea Mall,
14 so called, as contained in the Town of Swansea Zoning Map, Business B District.
15 SECTION 3. Any additional Section 12 license that is allowed pursuant to this Act and is
16 granted hereunder by the Swansea licensing authority shall not be transferable to any other
17 person, corporation, or organization for a period of five (5) years from the date of original
18 issuance. Nor may any such license be transferred in violation of the limitations of Section 2 of
19 this Act. Any transfer in violation of Sections 2 or 3 of this Act shall render said license null and
20 void.
21 SECTION 4. Notwithstanding Sections 12 and 77 of Chapter 138 of the General Laws,
22 the Swansea licensing authority may, in its sole discretion, restrict the grant of any additional
23 Section 12 license that is allowed pursuant to this Act to holders of common victualler licenses.
24 SECTION 5. Whenever an additional Section 12 license that is allowed pursuant to this
25 Act is granted hereunder by the Swansea licensing authority and subsequently revoked by said
26 licensing authority or determined by said licensing authority to no longer be in use at the location
27 of original issuance, then said license shall be returned physically, with all of the legal rights and
28 privileges pertaining thereto, to said licensing authority.
29 SECTION 6. No additional Section 12 license that is allowed pursuant to this Act may
30 be granted hereunder by the Swansea licensing authority unless the applicant files with said
31 licensing authority letters from both the Department of Revenue and a letter from the Division of
32 Unemployment Assistance indicating that the applicant is in good standing with those entities
33 and that all applicable taxes, fees, and contributions have been paid.
2 of 3

34 SECTION 7. Except as otherwise provided in this Act, the additional Section 12 licenses
35 that are allowed hereunder shall be subject to all provisions, restrictions and requirements of said
36 Chapter 138.
37 SECTION 8. This Act shall take effect upon its passage.
