An Act establishing Indigenous Peoples day. 
 
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority 
of the same, as follows: 
 
1  Chapter 6 of the General Laws is hereby amended by adding a new section 12VVV as 
2 follows:– 
3  Section 12VVV. The governor shall annually issue a proclamation setting apart the 9th 
4 day in August as Indigenous Peoples Day and recommending that it be observed by the people, 
5 with appropriate exercises in the schools and otherwise.
