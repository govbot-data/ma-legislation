HOUSE . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . No. 3706
By Representative Jones of North Reading, a petition (accompanied by bill, House, No. 3706) of
Bradley H. Jones, Jr., and others that the Registrar of Motor Vehicles establish a Medal of
Fidelity license plate. Transportation.
The Commonwealth of Massachusetts
_______________
In the One Hundred and Ninety-Fourth General Court
(2025-2026)
_______________
An Act establishing a Medal of Fidelity license plate.
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority
of the same, as follows:
1 SECTION 1. Section 67B of chapter 33 of the General Laws, as appearing in the 2022
2 Official Edition, is hereby amended by adding the following paragraph:-
3 Qualifying next of kin of a fallen service member may apply to the adjutant general for a
4 medal of fidelity, in a form prescribed by the adjutant general. There shall be within the
5 application for the medal of fidelity a notification that informs applicants of their eligibility to
6 receive the medal of fidelity license plate, pursuant to section 2 of chapter 90, free of charge
7 upon approval of the application. The notification shall read as follows: IF YOU RECEIVE THE
8 MEDAL OF FIDELITY, YOU ARE ENTITLED TO RECEIVE THE MEDAL OF FIDELITY
9 LICENSE PLATE FREE OF CHARGE and shall include a link to the online application
10 established by the registrar of motor vehicles.
1 of 2

11 SECTION 2. Section 2 of chapter 90 of the General Laws, as appearing in the 2022
12 Official Edition, is hereby amended by inserting after the twenty-third paragraph the following
13 paragraph:-
14 The registrar shall furnish without charge to owners of private passenger motor vehicles
15 who have been awarded the medal of fidelity pursuant to section 67B of chapter 33 upon
16 presentation of satisfactory evidence of such status, as determined by the registrar, distinctive
17 registration plates for 1 private passenger motor vehicle owned and principally used by such
18 recipient, bearing on the left side an image of the medal and ribbon and bearing the words
19 "MEDAL OF FIDELITY'' along the bottom. The registrar, in consultation with the adjutant
20 general of the Massachusetts national guard, shall notify all past recipients of the medal of
21 fidelity, established pursuant to said section 67B of said chapter 33, of their eligibility for the
22 medal of fidelity license plate through a letter, which shall include the application for the medal
23 of fidelity license plate. Notification of past recipients of the medal of fidelity concerning their
24 eligibility for the medal of fidelity license plate shall be completed no later than six months after
25 the license plate is designed and in circulation.
