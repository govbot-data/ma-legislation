An Act relative to Topical Steroid Withdrawal Syndrome. 
 
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority 
of the same, as follows: 
 
1  Chapter 111 of the General Laws is hereby amended by adding the following section:- 
2  Section 245. There is hereby established a commission to research topical steroid 
3 addiction and Topical Steroid Withdrawal Syndrome, a severe and debilitating condition that can 
4 result from intentional or unintentional exposure to topical steroids.  
5  The commissioner shall have 1 member who is a representative of the department and 7 
6 members appointed by the governor, 1 of whom shall be a dermatologist, 1 of whom shall be a 
7 licensed pediatrician, 1 of whom shall be a licensed pharmacist, 1 of whom shall be a licensed 
8 immunologist and 3 of whom shall be individuals or parents who have been impacted by Topical 
9 Steroid Withdrawal Syndrome.  
10  The special commission may conduct public hearings, forums, or meetings to gather 
11 information regarding medical protocols for prevention, early detection and treatment of Topical 

2 of 2 12 Steroid Withdrawal Syndrome and generate awareness of the condition among health care 
13 providers, patients and consumers when prescribing medications. 
14  Annually, on or before June 30, the commission shall submit a report to the governor and 
15 the clerks of the house of representatives and the senate who shall forward the same to the joint 
16 committee on public health, along with recommendations, if any, together with drafts of 
17 legislation necessary to carry out those recommendations into effect. The special commission 
18 may file such interim reports and recommendations as it considers appropriate.
