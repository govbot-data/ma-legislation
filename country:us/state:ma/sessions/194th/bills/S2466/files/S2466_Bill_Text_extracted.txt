An Act expanding the mission of the Office of the Veteran Advocate.
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority
of the same, as follows:
1 Chapter 115B of the General Laws, as appearing in the 2022 Official Edition, is hereby
2 amended by striking it in its entirety and inserting in place thereof the following:-
3 "Chapter 115B: OFFICE OF THE MILITARY AND VETERAN ADVOCATE
4 Section 1. As used in this chapter, the following words shall, unless the context clearly
5 requires otherwise, have the following meanings:
6 “Active Duty” shall include (1) full time service; and (2) any period of active or inactive
7 duty for training during which the individual was disabled or died.
8 “Armed Forces” shall include army, marine corps, navy, air force, space force, coast
9 guard and any additional military service created by the U.S. Department of Defense including
10 reserve components thereof.
1 of 5

11 "Executive agency'', a state agency within the office of the governor, including the
12 executive office of education, the executive office of public safety and security, executive office
13 of health and human services, and their constituent agencies, the Massachusetts interagency
14 council on housing and homelessness and the executive office of housing and livable
15 communities, and their constituent agencies.
16 "Nominating committee'', the nominating committee established in section 3.
17 "Office'', the office of the veteran advocate established in section 2.
18 "Veteran advocate'', the veteran advocate appointed under section 3.
19 “Uniformed Services” shall include armed forces, commissioned corps of the public
20 health service and commissioned corps of the national oceanic and atmospheric administration.
21 Section 2. There shall be an office of the military and veteran advocate, which shall be
22 independent of any supervision or control by any executive agency. The office shall provide
23 overarching support to the uniformed service and to all veterans and their families and serve as a
24 liaison and advocate on behalf of the servicemember and veteran population residing in the
25 Commonwealth to all relevant federal agencies. The office shall: (i) ensure that veterans and
26 servicemembers in the care of the commonwealth or receiving services under the supervision of
27 an executive agency in any public or private facility receive humane and dignified treatment at
28 all times, with full respect for the veterans' or servicemembers’ personal dignity and right to
29 privacy; (ii) ensure that veterans and servicemembers in the care of the commonwealth or
30 receiving services under the supervision of an executive agency in any public or private facility
31 receive timely, safe and effective services; (iii) aid and coordinate with local veterans service
32 agents to ensure veterans receive all available state and federal benefits; (iv) examine, on a
2 of 5

33 system-wide basis, the care and services that executive agencies provide veterans and
34 servicemembers; (v) advise the public and those at the highest levels of state government about
35 how the commonwealth may improve its services to and for veterans, servicemembers, and their
36 families; (vi) develop internal procedures appropriate for the effective performance of the office's
37 duties; (vii) assist in developing procedures for the executive agencies to best serve the veteran
38 and servicemember community;; and (viii) investigate incidents where a veteran suffered a
39 fatality, near fatality or serious bodily or emotional injury while receiving services from an
40 executive agency, or circumstances which result in a reasonable belief that an executive agency
41 or a constituent agency failed in its duty to care for a veteran and, as a result, the veteran was at
42 imminent risk of, or suffered serious bodily or emotional injury or death.
43 Section 3. The office of the military and veteran advocate shall be under the direction of
44 the military and veteran advocate. The military and veteran advocate shall be the administrative
45 head of the office and shall devote full time to the duties of the office. The military and veteran
46 advocate shall be appointed by a majority vote of the attorney general, the state auditor and the
47 governor from a list of 3 nominees submitted by a nominating committee to recommend a
48 military and veteran advocate. The nominating committee shall consist of: the secretary of health
49 and human services, or a designee, who shall serve as chair; the secretary of veterans' services, or
50 a designee; the secretary of public safety, or a designee; the commissioner of mental health, or a
51 designee; the house and senate chairs of the joint committee on veterans and federal affairs; the
52 adjutant general of the Massachusetts national guard; a representative of the veterans of foreign
53 wars department of Massachusetts; a representative of the American legion department of
54 Massachusetts; the chair of the board of trustees of the veterans' home in the city of Chelsea; and
55 the chair of the board of trustees of the veterans' home in the city of Holyoke. The nominating
3 of 5

56 committee shall submit salary recommendations for the candidates commensurate with the
57 candidates' experience and other similar state positions. The work of the nominating committee
58 shall be coordinated by the executive office of health and human services.
59 Any person appointed to the position of military and veteran advocate shall be selected
60 without regard to political affiliation and on the basis of integrity and demonstrated ability in
61 military culture and veteran welfare, justice, auditing, law, health care, management analysis,
62 public administration and investigation or criminal justice administration. The military and
63 veteran advocate may, subject to appropriation, appoint such other personnel as the military and
64 veteran advocate deems necessary for the efficient management of the office.
65 The military and veteran advocate shall serve for a term of 5 years. In the case of a
66 vacancy in the position of the military and veteran advocate, a successor shall be appointed in the
67 same manner for the remainder of the unexpired term. No person shall be appointed as military
68 and veteran advocate for more than 2 full terms.
69 The military and veteran advocate may be removed from office for cause by a majority
70 vote of the attorney general, the state auditor and the governor. Such cause may include
71 substantial neglect of duty, gross misconduct or conviction of a crime. The cause for removal of
72 the military and veteran advocate shall be stated in writing and shall be sent to the clerks of the
73 senate and house of representatives and to the governor at the time of removal and shall be a
74 public document.
75 Section 4. The military and veteran advocate shall report annually to the governor, the
76 senate president, the speaker of the house of representatives, the senate and the house committees
77 on ways and means, and the joint committee on veterans and federal affairs on the activities of
4 of 5

78 the office, including, but not limited to, an analysis of the delivery of services to veterans,
79 servicemember and their families and recommendations for changes in agency procedures which
80 would enable the commonwealth to better provide services to and for veterans, servicemembers
81 and their families and priorities for implementation of those changes to services. The report shall
82 be made public on the website of the veteran advocate."
