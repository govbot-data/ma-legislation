Although this report officially covers the period through March 31, I can add that the remaining 
capacity to spend was effectively depleted in the following weeks , and we ceased to pay  claims.  
 No claims can  be paid until a supplemental appropriation is made in sufficient amount both to 
cover the $13.3  million  deficit in the account and to provide funding for the final quarter of Fiscal 
Year 2026.  Based on the settlements already awaiting payment  from April and May, estimated 
additional payments  for the month of June, and the need to fund a few weeks of payments in July 
before the anticipated signing of the Fiscal Year 2026 GAA, our estimate is that $1 1.5 million in 
incremental funding is required.  Therefore, in order for the Commonwealth to meet its obligations,  
I respectfully request a total of $2 5.0 million in supplemental  appropriation as soon as poss ible.  
Any amount carried into Fiscal Year 2027 is likely to be used very quickly, which would slightly 
reduce the need for later appropriations.  
 
A detailed report is attached that provides information on payees, amounts, and the associated department or agency for settlements and judgments paid during Quarter 3 from appropriation 
account 1599 -3384.  This information is also posted quarterly on the Comptroller’s transparency 
website, CTHRU, which may be accessed via this link, https://www.macomptroller.org/cthru .
