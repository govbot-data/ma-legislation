An Act ensuring law enforcement identification for public safety.
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority
of the same, as follows:
1 SECTION 1. Section 10 of Chapter 6E of the General Laws is hereby amended in the
2 first paragraph by inserting after item “xvi” the following new item:- (xvii) the officer used a
3 mask or personal disguise in violation of section 34B of chapter 268
4 SECTION 2. Chapter 268 of the General Laws is hereby amended by inserting after
5 Section 34A the following new section:-
6 Section 34B. Use of a mask or personal disguise by a law enforcement officer.
7 a) As used in this section, the following words shall, unless the context clearly requires
8 otherwise, have the following meanings:
9 “Agency”, a law enforcement agency.
10 “Law enforcement agency”, (i) a federal, state, county, municipal or district law
11 enforcement agency, including, but not limited to: a city, town or district police department, the
1 of 3

12 office of environmental law enforcement, the University of Massachusetts police department, the
13 department of the state police, the Massachusetts Port Authority police department, also known
14 as the Port of Boston Authority police department, and the Massachusetts Bay Transportation
15 Authority police department; (ii) a sheriff's department in its performance of police duties and
16 functions; (iii) a public or private college, university or other educational institution or hospital
17 police department; or (iv) a humane society police department in section 57 of chapter 22C.
18 “Law enforcement officer'' or ''officer'', any officer of an agency, including the head of
19 the agency; a special state police officer appointed pursuant to section 57, section 58 or section
20 63 of chapter 22C; a special sheriff appointed pursuant to section 4 of chapter 37 performing
21 police duties and functions; a deputy sheriff appointed pursuant to section 3 of said chapter 37
22 performing police duties and functions; a constable executing an arrest for any reason; or any
23 other special, reserve or intermittent police officer.
24 “Mask” or “personal disguise”, any covering that obscures the face of an officer that
25 renders them unidentifiable, or any clothing that conceals an officer’s status as an officer; this
26 does not include a face shield if it does not conceal the wearer’s face.
27 b) Any officer found to be utilizing a mask or personal disguise while performing their
28 duties shall be required to pay a fine of not more than $1000.
29 c) An officer is exempt from this section for the purposes of: (i) active undercover
30 operations or assignments authorized by court order; (ii) tactical operations and states of
31 emergency declared by the governor where protective gear is required for personal safety; (iii)
32 compliance with applicable law governing reasonable accommodations for a documented
33 disability or occupational health and safety; or (iv) protection of identity during active
2 of 3

34 prosecution; provided, however, that a mask or personal disguise may be used only if no other
35 reasonable alternative exists and the necessity is documented.
