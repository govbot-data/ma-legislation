An Act facilitating cost efficient transportation. 
 
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority 
of the same, as follows: 
 
1  SECTION 1: Section 30A of chapter 85 of the General Laws is hereby amended by 
2 inserting after the words “provisions of chapter ninety” the following words:- ; provided, that a 
3 farmer or person involved in the harvesting of forest products as defined by the regulations of the 
4 registry of motor vehicles, who is operating multiple vehicles under a single farm plate 
5 registration under the provisions of MGL Chapter 90 Section 5, shall only be required to pay a 
6 single fee which shall cover permits for all vehicles operating under said registration; and 
7 provided further, that said fee shall be based on that vehicle operating under this registration, 
8 with the greatest difference between the weight for which the permit is issued and the weight for 
9 which the vehicle is registered. 
10  SECTION 2: Section 30A of chapter 85 of the General Laws is hereby amended by 
11 striking the words “or any motor vehicle, trailer, semi-trailer or semi-trailer unit having five or 
12 more axles which with its load weighs not more than ninety-nine thousand pounds” and inserting 
13 in place thereof the following:- or any motor vehicle, trailer, semi-trailer or semi-trailer unit 

2 of 3 14 having five or more axles or operating in combination with a detachable trailer which with its 
15 load weighs not more than ninety-nine thousand pounds. 
16  SECTION 3: Section 19A of chapter 90 of the General Laws is hereby amended by 
17 inserting after the words “travel without such a permit” the following words:-  
18  ; provided further, that a truck, motor vehicle, trailer, semitrailer, semitrailer unit or 
19 tandem unit in combination with a trailer weighing not more than ninety-nine thousand pounds 
20 may travel on a public way with such a permit. 
21  SECTION 4: Section 1 of Chapter 90 of the General Laws, as so appearing in the 2022 
22 Official Edition, is hereby amended by adding the following definitions:-  
23  “Truck-trailer unit”, a motor unit composed of a motor vehicle designed to carry a load 
24 and having a minimum of three axles, such as a dump truck, log truck, or other truck, paired with 
25 a trailer having a minimum of two axles. 
26  SECTION 5: Section 30A of Chapter 85 of the General Laws, as so appearing in the 
27 2022 official edition, is hereby amended by striking the words “motor vehicle, trailer, semi-
28 trailer or semi-trailer unit” each time it appears, and inserting in place thereof, in each instance, 
29 the words “motor vehicle, trailer, semi-trailer, semi-trailer unit, or truck-trailer unit”. 
30  SECTION 6:  Section 30A of Chapter 85 of the General Laws, as appearing in the 2022 
31 Official Edition, is hereby amended by adding the following paragraph:- 
32  Notwithstanding any general or special law to the contrary, the weight threshold for 
33 determination of a superload shall be those vehicles at or in excess of one hundred and ninety-
34 nine thousand pounds gross vehicle weight. The department shall also implement an application 

3 of 3 35 and fee schedule for expedited superload permits authorized under this section. A superload 
36 permit may include conditions and terms as determined by the Administrator; provided further, 
37 that allowances shall be made for escort flexibility, where practicable, and engineering studies 
38 conducted not older than 12 months prior. For purposes of this section, a “superload” is defined 
39 as any vehicle or combination of vehicles which exceed 14 feet in width; or 14 feet in height or 
40 greater; or greater than 135 feet in length; or one hundred and ninety-nine thousand pounds  or 
41 greater in gross vehicle weight; or a combination of the above.
