An Act to eliminate double utility poles and authorize municipal enforcement. 
 
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority 
of the same, as follows: 
 
1  Section 34B of chapter 164 of the General Laws, as so appearing, is hereby amended by 
2 adding the following sentence:- A city or town may enforce this section by the enactment of a 
3 local ordinance or bylaw prohibiting double poles beyond 12 months, authorized by this section, 
4 violation of which may be punishable by a fine to be imposed on the owner of such double poles 
5 not to exceed a maximum of $2,500 per occurrence.
