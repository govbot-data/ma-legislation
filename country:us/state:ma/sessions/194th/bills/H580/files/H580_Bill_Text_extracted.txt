An Act relative to wages for substitute teacher. 
 
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority 
of the same, as follows: 
 
1  (a) Teachers employed in a public elementary or secondary school as substitute teachers 
2 shall not be paid less per day than either 85 per cent of 1/185 th of the salary of a beginning 
3 teacher who holds a bachelor's degree or a wage less than the per hour wage set forth in section 1 
4 of chapter 151, whichever is greater. The salary of the substitute teacher shall be computed as 
5 required in this subsection based on the statewide average salary for beginning teachers who hold 
6 bachelor's degrees. The department of elementary and secondary education shall compute the 
7 statewide average salary to be used for purposes of this subsection, using the latest data available 
8 to the department, but not data from earlier than the preceding school year. 
9  (b) This section shall not apply to substitute teachers represented in a bargaining unit in 
10 the school district by which they are employed.
