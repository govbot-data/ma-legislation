An Act to ensure safe medication administration.
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority
of the same, as follows:
1 Section 7 of chapter 94C of the General Laws, as appearing in the 2022 Official Edition,
2 is hereby amended by inserting after subsection (h), the following new subsection:-
3 ( i ) Notwithstanding any general or special law to the contrary, only a dentist, nurse,
4 physician, podiatrist, perfusionist, optometrist or veterinarian licensed as a professional under
5 chapter 112 or any student enrolled in a course of study for said profession acting under the
6 supervision of said licensed person and in accordance with the law, may administer any
7 controlled substance in schedule II, III , IV, V or VI of Section 3 of chapter 94C. This subsection
8 shall not be construed to prohibit the following:
9 (1) self-administration;
10 (2) administration of epinephrine pens in an emergency;
1 of 2

11 (3) administration of controlled substances by emergency medical system personnel or
12 (4) administration of controlled substances by any ill, injured or infirm person's domestic
13 partner or a family member;
14 or
15 (5) administration of controlled substances in a long-term care facility by a medication
16 aide certified under section 72W1/2 of chapter 111.
17 For the purposes of this section, "self-administration" shall include the ability of any ill,
18 injured or infirm person who has sufficient understanding of their prescribed controlled
19 substances, the indications and contraindications for such controlled substance; a recollection of
20 the administration schedule for such controlled substance and an ability to communicate this
21 knowledge and the experienced effect of having taken a controlled substance, to supervise an
22 unlicensed person in the direct administration of such controlled substance to them.
