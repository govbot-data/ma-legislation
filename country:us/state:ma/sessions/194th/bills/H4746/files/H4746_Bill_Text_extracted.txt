376 Section 4. Consumer rights.
377 (a) A consumer shall have the right to:
378 (1) Confirm whether or not a controller is collecting or processing the consumer's
379 personal data and access such personal data, including, but not limited to, any inferences about
380 the consumer derived from such personal data;
381 (2) obtain from a controller a list of specific third parties, other than natural persons, to
382 which the controller has transferred either (i) the consumer’s personal data; or (ii) any personal
383 data;
384 (3) correct inaccuracies in the consumer's personal data, taking into account the nature of
385 the personal data and the purposes of the processing of the consumer's personal data, and instruct
386 a controller or processor to make reasonable efforts to notify all third parties or processors to
387 which the controller has transferred such personal data of such corrections;
388 (4) delete personal data provided by, or obtained about, the consumer, including personal
389 data the consumer provided to the controller, personal data the controller obtained from another
390 source, and derived data and instruct a controller or processor to make reasonable efforts to
391 notify all third parties or processors to which the controller has transferred such personal data of
392 such deletion request;
20 of 48

393 (5) obtain a copy of the consumer's personal data collected or processed by the controller,
394 in a portable and, to the extent technically feasible, readily usable format that allows the
395 consumer to transmit the data to another controller without hindrance, where the processing is
396 carried out by automated means; and
397 (6) opt out of the collection and processing of the consumer’s personal data for purposes
398 of:
399 (i) targeted advertising;
400 (ii) the sale of personal data; or
401 (iii) profiling in furtherance of solely automated decisions that produce legal or similarly
402 significant effects concerning the consumer.
403 (b) A consumer may exercise rights under this section by a secure and reliable means
404 established by the controller and described to the consumer in the controller's privacy notice. A
405 consumer may designate an authorized agent in accordance with section 5 of this act to exercise
406 the rights of such consumer specified in this section on behalf of the consumer. In the case of
407 personal data of a known child, the parent or legal guardian may exercise such consumer rights
408 on the child's behalf. In the case of personal data concerning a consumer subject to a
409 guardianship, conservatorship or other protective arrangement, the guardian or the conservator of
410 the consumer may exercise such rights on the consumer's behalf.
411 (c) Except as otherwise provided in this chapter, a controller shall comply with a request
412 by a consumer to exercise the consumer rights authorized pursuant to said sections as follows:
21 of 48

413 (1) A controller shall respond to the consumer without undue delay, but not later than 45
414 days after receipt of the request. The controller may extend the response period by 20 additional
415 days when reasonably necessary, considering the complexity and number of the consumer's
416 requests, provided the controller informs the consumer of any such extension within the initial
417 45-day response period and of the reason for the extension.
418 (2) If a controller declines to take action regarding the consumer's request, the controller
419 shall inform the consumer without undue delay, but not later than 45 days after receipt of the
420 request, of the justification for declining to take action and instructions for how to appeal the
421 decision.
422 (3) Information provided in response to a consumer request shall be provided by a
423 controller, free of charge, not less than twice per consumer during any twelve-month period. If
424 requests from a consumer are manifestly unfounded, excessive or repetitive, the controller may
425 charge the consumer a reasonable fee to cover the administrative costs of complying with the
