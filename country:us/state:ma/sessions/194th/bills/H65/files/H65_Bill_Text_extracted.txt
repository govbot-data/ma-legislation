Proposal for a legislative amendment to the Constitution for a Constitutional right to housing.
A majority of all the members elected to the Senate and House of Representatives, in
joint session, hereby declares it to be expedient to alter the Constitution by the adoption of the
following Article of Amendment, to the end that it may become a part of the Constitution [if
similarly agreed to in a joint session of the next General Court and approved by the people at the
state election next following]:
ARTICLE OF AMENDMENT.
1 ARTICLE OF AMENDMENT.
2 1.) The Constitution of The Commonwealth of Massachusetts, is hereby, and thus
3 amended, to provide that:
4 Each, and every inhabitant of The Commonwealth of Massachusetts, has a Right to
5 Housing.
1 of 2

6 2.) The provision of this Right, with reasonable, and appropriate equality for all
7 individuals, shall be subject to appropriate legislation by the Great and General Court.
8 3.) The Commonwealth of Massachusetts, shall through its appropriate legal authorities,
9 provide sufficient, and comprehensive planning, for affordable, well-constructed, and reasonably
10 varied housing for all residents. These housing policies, focused on restoration, rehabilitation,
11 and new construction of housing units, shall be across all identifiable population groups, without
12 discrimination. The creation of such housing, shall be consistently balanced, in such a manner, as
13 to appropriately encourage, and if needed, supplement market created housing; balanced across
14 the geographical range, and variety of the Commonwealth.
15 The goal of these housing policies, should be to encourage the reasonable safety, and
16 good health, of all residents of Massachusetts, to provide respectful living circumstances, assist
17 sound economic conditions, and provide fundamental dignity for all residents.
18 4.) Appropriate regulations, partnerships with various levels of government, and
19 transparent partnerships with non-public, non-profit, and profit making organizations, shall be
20 authorized.
21 5.) These goals shall be accomplished, while respecting appropriate, and reasonable local
22 self-government ordinances, and regulations.
