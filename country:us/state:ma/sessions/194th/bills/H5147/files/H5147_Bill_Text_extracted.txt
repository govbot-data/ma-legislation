HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES, February 25, 2026.
The committee on Public Service, to whom was referred the joint
petition (accompanied by bill, House, No. 4236) of Susannah M. Whipps
(by vote of the town) that the town of Northfield be authorized to continue
the employment of Floyd Dunnell, III as fire chief, notwithstanding the
maximum age requirement, reports recommending that the accompanying
order (House, No. 5147) ought to be adopted.
For the committee,
DANIEL J. RYAN.
