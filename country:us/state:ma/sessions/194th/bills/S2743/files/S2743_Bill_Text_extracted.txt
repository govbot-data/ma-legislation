 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority 
of the same, as follows: 
 
1  SECTION 1. Chapter 6E of the General Laws, as appearing in the 2024 Official Edition, 
2 is hereby amended by adding the following section:-  
3  Section 17. The commission and the municipal police training committee established in 
4 section 116 of chapter 6 shall jointly establish minimum certification standards for officers 
5 investigating the deaths of domestic violence survivors that shall include, but not be limited to, 
6 training and continuing education concerning: (1) identification and detection of staged crime 
7 scenes; (2) engaging a multidisciplinary team in deaths following a history of domestic violence; 
8 and (3) indicators of domestic homicide in suspicious death cases, as defined in section 12 of 
9 chapter 209A, including but not limited to: (i) premature death or death in an untimely manner; 
10 (ii) an appearance of death due to suicide or accident at the scene; (iii) death following evidence 
11 of one romantic partner wanting to end the relationship; (iv) death in the home or place of 
12 residence; (v) the body is found by a current or previous romantic partner; (vi) a history of 

2 of 4 13 domestic violence that includes coercive control, strangulation, suffocation or other life-
14 threatening behavior; (vii) the abuser, a child of the abuser or a child of the decedent was the last 
15 person to see the decedent alive; (viii) the abuser had control of the scene of death before law 
16 enforcement arrived; and (ix) evidence of tampering with the scene of death or other evidence, 
17 including but not limited to any indication that the body had been moved.   
18  SECTION 2. Section 3 of Chapter 38 of the General Laws, as appearing in the 2024

20 inserting in place thereof the following words:- (18) suspicious death cases, as defined in section

22  SECTION 3. Chapter 209A of the General Laws, as appearing in the 2024 Official 
23 Edition, is hereby amended by adding the following sections:-   
24  Section 12. (a) As used in this section, the following words shall have the following 
25 meanings unless the context clearly requires otherwise:  
26  “Close friend”, a person who has maintained regular contact with a domestic violence 
27 survivor sufficient to be highly familiar with the survivor’s activities, health and religious or 
28 moral beliefs, as attested to in a sworn affidavit to the law enforcement agency investigating the 
29 death of the domestic violence survivor. The affidavit must state facts and circumstances 
30 demonstrating that familiarity.   
31  “Domestic violence”, abuse or coercive control, as defined in section 1 of this chapter.  
32  “Suspicious death case”, the death of a domestic violence survivor that involves three or 
33 more of the following factors: (i) premature death or death in an untimely manner; (ii) an 

3 of 4 34 appearance of death due to suicide or accident at the scene; (iii) death following evidence of one 
35 romantic partner wanting to end the relationship; (iv) death in the home or place of residence; (v) 
36 the body is found by a current or previous romantic partner; (vi) a history of domestic violence 
37 that includes coercive control, strangulation, suffocation or other life-threatening behavior; (vii) 
38 the abuser, a child of the abuser or a child of the decedent was the last person to see the decedent 
39 alive; (viii) the abuser had control of the scene of death before law enforcement arrived; or (ix) 
40 evidence of tampering with the scene of death or other evidence, including but not limited to any 
41 indication that the body had been moved.  
42  (b) In a suspicious death case, law enforcement shall: (i) ensure that the personnel 
43 investigating the case have timely and successfully achieved the certification standards required 
44 under section 17 of chapter 6E; (ii) interview family or household members and close friends, if 
45 any, of the domestic violence survivor who can provide information regarding the relevant 
46 domestic violence history prior to making any findings as to the manner or cause of death; (iii) 
47 request a complete autopsy; and (iv) provide domestic violence survivor services, supports or 
48 resources to family or household members and close friends who request such services, supports 
49 or resources.   
50  (c) If a suspicious death case is determined not to be a homicide and the investigation is 
51 closed, a family or household member or close friend shall have the right to request: (i) copies of 
52 any records of the investigation not subject to exemption under chapter 66 or section 97D of 
53 chapter 41; and (ii) an autopsy and second opinion from a board-certified forensic pathologist 
54 certified by the American Board of Pathology; provided, however, that the autopsy shall not 
55 destroy the chain of custody.  

4 of 4 56  SECTION 4. This act shall take effect on January 1, 2027. Section 3 shall apply to 
57 suspicious death case investigations opening or arising on or after July 1, 2027.
