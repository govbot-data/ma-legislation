HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES, July 14, 2026.
The committee on Ways and Means, to whom was referred the Senate
Bill promoting pet equity, treatment and safety (Senate, No. 3028), reports
recommending that the same ought to pass with amendments striking out
all after the enacting clause and inserting in place thereof the text
contained in House document numbered 5581; and by inserting before the
enacting clause the following emergency preamble:
“Whereas, the deferred operation of this act would tend to defeat its
purpose, which is to promote pet equity, treatment and safety, therefore it
is hereby declared to be an emergency law, necessary for the immediate
preservation of the public convenience.”.
For the committee,
AARON MICHLEWITZ.
