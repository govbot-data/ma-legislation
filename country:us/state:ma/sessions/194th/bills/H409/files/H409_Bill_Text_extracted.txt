of the same, as follows:
1 Notwithstanding any general or special law to the contrary, the comptroller shall transfer

3 writing, from the Race Horse Development Fund established in section 60 of chapter 23K of the
4 General Laws to the Community Preservation Trust Fund established in section 9 of chapter 44B
