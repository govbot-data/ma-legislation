2  "SECTION 1. Chapter 71 of the General Laws is hereby amended by inserting after 
3 section 82 the following 3 sections:-   
4  Section 82A. (a) For the purposes of this section and sections 82B and 82C, inclusive, the 
5 following words shall have the following meanings unless the context clearly requires otherwise: 
6  “School library”, a library within a school department of a city or town, a regional school, 
7 an independent vocational school or a vocational school or agricultural school operated by a 
8 county.    
9  “School library materials”, textbooks, instructional materials, curricula, books or other 
10 library resources. 
11  “School library teacher”, the school library teacher in the district licensed by the 
12 department, or in the absence of such a school library teacher, the school official responsible for 
13 the selection of school library materials and the use of school library materials and facilities. 

2 of 7 14  (b) A determination that materials are appropriate for inclusion in a school library by a 
15 school library teacher shall require that the materials: (i) are age appropriate; (ii) serve an 
16 educational purpose; and (iii) be based on the teacher’s or employee’s professional training and 
17 not on personal, political or doctrinal views.  
18  (c) No challenged material selected for inclusion in a school library collection pursuant to 
19 subsection (b) shall be removed except upon a determination and vote by the school committee 
20 or board of trustees of a charter school following: (i) notice; (ii) a public hearing; and (iii) a 
21 finding by a review committee of school personnel appointed by: (A) the school superintendent, 
22 in consultation with the school committee; or (B) in the case of a charter school, the board of 
23 trustees, that the material, taken as a whole, based on clear and convincing evidence, is devoid of 
24 any educational, literary, artistic, personal or social value or is not age appropriate for any child 
25 who attends the school. A school committee shall only consider a challenge to material in a 
26 school library from an individual who is a staff member employed by a school overseen by the 
27 school committee, a parent or guardian of a student currently enrolled in such a school or a 
28 student currently enrolled in such a school. 
29  (d) A decision of the school committee or the board of trustees of a charter school to 
30 remove materials from a school library may be challenged by a student, parent or guardian 
31 pursuant to section 5 of chapter 249. 
32  (e) Nothing in this section shall preclude school employees responsible for school library 
33 content from deciding to remove materials that have become outdated or to accommodate new 
34 materials; provided, however, that the decision to remove materials shall not be made based on 
35 personal, political or doctrinal views. 

3 of 7 36  Section 82B. (a) Each school district, charter school and local education agency shall 
37 establish a written policy for its school library for: (i) the selection of school library materials, 
38 including textbooks, instructional materials, books or other library resources; (ii) the use of 
39 library materials and library facilities; and (iii) responding to challenges requesting to remove or 
40 restrict library materials. The written policy shall: (i) be in accordance with standards adopted by 
41 the American Library Association; (ii) be posted on the school district, charter school or local 
42 education agency’s website; and (iii) provide that if material selected by the school library 
43 teacher is the subject of a challenge requesting to remove or restrict its use, such material shall 
44 remain on the shelves of the school library pending a vote of the school committee or the board 
45 of trustees of a charter school made during the process for responding to such challenge in 
46 accordance with subsection (b) of section 82A. The board of library commissioners for the 
47 commonwealth through its affiliate, the Massachusetts Library System, shall: (i) make resources 
48 available to help school committees or other administrative authority of a school library create or 
49 modify written policies for the selection of library materials and the use of materials and 
50 facilities in accordance with the standards adopted by the American Library Association; and (ii) 
51 make sample policies available on its website.  
52  (b) Annually, school districts, charter schools and local education agencies shall submit a 
53 report to the department, which shall include information on all complaints and challenges 
54 requesting to remove or restrict educational materials from its schools during the previous school 
55 year. The report shall include, for each complaint, challenge or attempt to remove or restrict 
56 school library materials, the: (i) school name and location; (ii) title and author of the school 
57 library materials; and (iii) outcomes of such complaint, challenge or attempt to remove or restrict 
58 school library materials.  

4 of 7 59  Section 82C. No school employee shall lose the employee’s department-issued license or 
60 professional certification or be dismissed, disciplined, placed on probation, involuntarily 
61 transferred, be otherwise subjected to an adverse employment action, fined or imprisoned for the 
62 selection of library materials when the selection is made in good faith and in accordance with the 
63 policy established pursuant to section 82B.
