The Commonwealth of Massachusetts
________________________________________
HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES, May 7, 2026.
The committee on Revenue, to whom were referred the petition
(accompanied by bill, House, No. 3066) of Mark J. Cusack for legislation
to further regulate the taxation of certain common carriers; the petition
(accompanied by bill, House, No. 3100) of William C. Galvin for
legislation to exempt disabled veterans from sales tax when leasing a
motor vehicle; the petition (accompanied by bill, House, No. 3147) of
Bradley H. Jones, Jr., and others relative to the conservation land tax
credit; the petition (accompanied by bill, House, No. 3178) of Christopher
M. Markey relative to establishing an electric agricultural and landscape
equipment tax credit; and the petition (accompanied by bill, House, No.
3259) of Jeffrey Rosario Turco for legislation to establish a local
community newspaper subscription tax credit, reports (under the
provisions of House Rule 27) recommending that the accompanying order
(House, No. 5371) ought to be adopted.
For the committee,
ADRIAN C. MADARO.
