HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES, March 12, 2026. 
 The committee on Financial Services, to whom was referred the 
petition (accompanied by bill, House, No. 1138) of Mindy Domb for 
legislation to increase health insurer reporting transparency; the petition 
(accompanied by bill, House, No. 1192) of Kevin G. Honan relative to 
establishing alternative payment arrangements to promote health care non-
discrimination; the petition (accompanied by bill, House, No. 1241) of 
David Henry Argosky LeBoeuf relative to eligibility for reimbursement by 
certain health plans; and the petition (accompanied by bill, House, No. 
1300) of David M. Rogers relative to cannabis for eligible patients, reports 
recommending that the accompanying order (House, No. 5226) ought to 
be adopted. 
 
For the committee, 
 
JAMES M. MURPHY.
