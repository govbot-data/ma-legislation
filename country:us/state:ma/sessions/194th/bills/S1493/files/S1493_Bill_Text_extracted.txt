92 SECTION 5. Chapter 111: Section 70I. Reduction of Duplicate Diagnostic Services
93 Section 70I. (a) For the purposes of this section, the following term shall have the
94 following meaning:
95 “Health insurance payer”, an individual or entity that pays for or arranges for the
96 purchase of health care services provided by acute hospitals, the office of Medicaid and its
97 contracted managed care entities and the group insurance commission.
98 (b) Each hospital in the Commonwealth shall file with the department, within thirty (30)
99 days following the start of the hospital’s fiscal year, a written plan designed to eliminate the
5 of 7

100 unnecessary duplication of diagnostic services performed on a patient by the hospital or health
101 system or another hospital or diagnostic facility.
102 (c) The plan shall include the following:
103 1) Procedures for sending and receiving diagnostic, imaging and other test results from or
104 to another hospital or provider of care, including the adoption of interoperable electronic health
105 records; 2) A plan to improve the hospital's ability to send and receive such test results from or to
106 other providers of care; and 3) A defined and written procedure for determining whether any
107 such test results can be appropriately used in the patient's treatment.
108 (d) The department shall notify the hospital that the plan has been approved or
109 disapproved within thirty (30) days after filing, based on a determination as to whether the plan
110 adequately addresses the issues of patient safety and costs of duplicating diagnostic tests. If such
111 a hospital’s plan has not been acted upon by the department within forty-five (45) days, the plan
112 shall be deemed approved.
113 (e) The department shall approve any plan that it determines is reasonably likely to
114 eliminate unnecessary duplicate diagnostic services and has a reasonable expectation for
115 successful implementation.
116 (f) If the department determines that a hospital’s plan is unacceptable or incomplete, the
117 department shall disapprove of a hospital’s plan and require the hospital to submit a revised plan
118 within thirty (30) days and may recommend specific elements for approval. If the revised plan
119 continues to be disapproved, or if a hospital fails to submit a plan, the commissioner may issue
120 an order that such a plan be submitted immediately, or the hospital may be subject to penalties
121 pursuant to subsection (f).
6 of 7

122 (g) If the department determines that a hospital has: (i) willfully neglected to file a plan
123 with the department subsection (e); (ii) failed to file an acceptable plan in good faith with the
124 department; (iii) failed to implement the plan in good faith; or (iv) knowingly failed to provide
125 information required by this section to the department, the department may assess a civil penalty
126 to the hospital of not more than $500,000.
127 (h) Health insurance payers may deny payment for any duplicate services furnished
128 unless the hospital can establish that the duplicate service was medically necessary and
129 appropriate. In the event that a health insurance payer denies payment for duplicate services, the
130 hospital may not balance bill the insured for those services.
131 (i) The department shall promulgate regulations implementing this section, which shall
132 include standards for determining whether a diagnostic service shall be considered a duplication
133 of a previous diagnostic service.
