An Act relative to turning on red signals.
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority
of the same, as follows:
1 SECTION 1. Section 8 of Chapter 89 of the General Laws, as so appearing in the 2022
2 Official Edition, is hereby amended by striking the second paragraph and inserting in place the
3 following paragraph:-
4 At any intersection on ways, as defined in section 1 of chapter 90, in which vehicular
5 traffic is facing a steady red indication in a traffic control signal, the driver of a vehicle which is
6 stopped as close as practicable at the entrance to the crosswalk or the near side of the
7 intersections or, if none, then at the entrance to the intersection in obedience to such red or stop
8 signal shall not turn against a red or stop signal at any such intersection and a sign may be
9 erected at each intersection giving notice thereof. Any person who violates the provisions of this
10 paragraph shall be punished by a fine of not less than thirty-five dollars.
11 SECTION 2. The Massachusetts Department of Transportation shall issue guidance to the
12 division highways, cities and towns for the implementation and enforcement of this act.
