An Act relative to organ transplant vehicles. 
 
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority 
of the same, as follows: 
 
1  SECTION 1.  Section 7B of chapter 89 of the General Laws, as appearing in the 2022 
2 Official Edition, is hereby amended by inserting after the word “department”, in line 7, the 
3 following words:- , the driver of an organ transplant vehicle, as defined in section 245 of chapter 
4 111, that is transporting human organs or medical personnel for the purpose of organ recovery or 
5 transplantation. 
6  SECTION 2. Chapter 111 of the acts of 2022 is hereby amended by adding the following 
7 section:- 
8  Section 245.  (a) For the purposes of this section, the following terms shall have the 
9 following meanings: 
10  “Organ procurement organization”, an entity certified as an organ procurement 
11 organization by the federal centers for Medicare and Medicaid services. 

2 of 3 12  “Organ transplant vehicle”, any vehicle owned by an organ procurement organization that 
13 is used to transport human organs or medical personnel for the purpose of organ recovery or 
14 transplantation. 
15  (b) All organ transplant vehicles shall be registered with the department for operation as 
16 an organ transplant vehicle.  
17  (c) During the time that an organ transplant vehicle is transporting human organs or 
18 medical personnel for the purpose of organ recovery or transplantation, the organ transplant 
19 vehicle shall be considered an emergency vehicle. 
20  (d)  No organ transplant vehicle shall be operated as an emergency vehicle within the 
21 commonwealth unless:  
22  (i) the organ procurement organization that owns and operates the vehicle is in 
23 compliance with any regulations promulgated pursuant to this section for the operation of such 
24 vehicles;  
25  (ii) the vehicle is used solely for the purpose of transporting human organs or medical 
26 personnel for the purpose of organ recovery or transplantation while operating as an emergency 
27 vehicle;  
28  (iii) the vehicle is designated and clearly identified as an organ transplant vehicle, and, 
29 prior to its operation as an organ transplant vehicle, the organ procurement organization which 
30 owns and operates the vehicle has registered with the department pursuant to subsection (b); and 
31  (iv) the driver of the vehicle has undergone any training for the operation of an organ 
32 transplant vehicle as the department may require.  

3 of 3 33  (e)  The department shall promulgate regulations to carry out the purpose of this section. 
34 Such regulations shall include, but not be limited to: 
35  (i)  standards for the registration and operation of organ transplant vehicles; 
36  (ii) standards for organ transplant vehicle operations, vehicles and equipment including 
37 compliance with this chapter and the training of organ transplant vehicle drivers for the safe 
38 operation of such vehicles during an emergency operation; and 
39  (iii) a requirement for organ procurement organizations to establish and enforce written 
40 policies concerning minimum qualifications and physical requirements for organ transplant 
41 vehicle drivers and for the equipping and maintenance of such vehicles. 
42  (f) The commissioner may revoke or suspend an organ transplant vehicle’s registration on 
43 proof that an organ procurement organization has failed to comply with the provisions of this 
44 section or any regulations promulgated pursuant this section.
