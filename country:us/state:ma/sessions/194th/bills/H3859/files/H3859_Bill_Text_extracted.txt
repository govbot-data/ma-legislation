An Act to streamline veterans benefits. 
 
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority 
of the same, as follows: 
 
1  Section 1. Section 5 of Chapter 115 of the General Laws is hereby amended by striking 
2 the first sentence and inserting in place thereof the following: Veterans’ benefits shall be paid to 
3 a veteran or dependents by the Commonwealth; provided, that no benefits shall be paid to a 
4 veteran unless he has actually resided in the Commonwealth for 1 day preceding the date of his 
5 application for such benefits, nor shall any benefits be paid to any dependent of a veteran unless 
6 he has actually resided in the Commonwealth continuously for three years next preceding the 
7 date of his application for such benefits, nor unless the veteran of whom he is a dependent has 
8 actually resided within the commonwealth continuously for three years next preceding the date 
9 of such dependent’s application for such benefits.  
10  Section 2. Section 6 of Chapter 115 of the General Laws is hereby stricken in its entirety 
11 and replaced by inserting its place the following: The application for veterans benefits is a 
12 function of the Veterans Service Officer in the city or town or district where the veteran or his 
13 dependent resides. The Veterans Service Officer is required to certify to the Commonwealth for 

2 of 2 14 direct payment to the veteran or dependent receiving the benefit the following information: the 
15 names of recipients thereof, the amounts to be paid to or for each applicant, the reasons for 
16 granting him such benefits, dates of residence in the Commonwealth, the names of the veterans 
17 on account of whose services the benefits were granted, the names, if any, of the companies, 
18 regiments, stations, organizations or vessels in which they respectively served, or to which they 
19 were appointed, and in which they last served, and the relationship of each dependent to the 
20 veteran on the account.
