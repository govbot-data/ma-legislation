Layman’s Summary of Proposed 225 CMR 29.00.
In November 2024, Governor Maura Healey signed into law An Act promoting a clean energy
grid, advancing equity and protecting ratepayers (“2024 Climate Act”), which streamlined the
siting and permitting process for clean energy infrastructure. The current siting and permitting
process for small energy infrastructure is a key barrier to the development of clean energy and
the Commonwealth’s ability to achieve our mandated reduction in greenhouse gas emissions by
2050. The law calls for the Department of Energy Resources (the “Department”) to create an
optional streamlined local permitting process for small clean energy facilities that caps the
permitting timeline at 12 months, and establishes a single appeals process, while also enhancing
community input and supporting equitable project review.
By setting forth new regulations and guidance for small clean energy infrastructure projects, the
Department seeks to provide a more efficient and consistent permitting pathway that will
facilitate and expedite the development of necessary clean energy infrastructure, while respecting
the historic autonomy of cities and towns.
225 CMR 29.00 establishes the processes that local governments will utilize to issue one
consolidated permit for a small clean energy facility that encompasses all decisions from local
boards, commissions, and relevant decision makers within twelve 12 months of the application’s
submission. The regulation is intended to provide consistency and reduce administrative delay in
the siting and permitting of small clean energy facilities while protecting public health, safety,
and the environment. Key provisions of 225 CMR 29.00 include:
(1) The application of the site suitability methodology that is developed by the Executive
Office of Energy and Environmental Affairs to the local siting and permitting process.
That methodology encourages energy infrastructure development in desirable areas,
including in the existing built environment, on previously developed, impacted, or
otherwise lower conservation value lands. This additionally includes steps the Applicant
must take to minimize or mitigate environmental impacts;
(2) Pre-filing requirements that establish the outreach steps the Applicant needs to undertake
with stakeholders ahead of formally filing its project application. These pre-filing
requirements include meeting with local officials to ensure design and permitting
accuracy and hosting at least one collaborative meeting with stakeholders, as well as
other outreach efforts;
(3) An application process created by the Department that will be used by project proponents
to apply for a single local siting permit;
(4) The review process for an application by a Local Government, including how Local
Governments can determine if an application is complete;
(5) A framework and guidance for the common conditions and requirements for projects
eligible for constructive approval as a result of a Local Government’s failure to issue a
decision on the application within twelve 12 months;
3 of 24

(6) A framework and guidance for the baseline public health, safety, and environmental
standards to guide project design and operation;
(7) The technical assistance the Department may provide to Local Governments and
Applicants during the application review process;
(8) The process for filing a petition for De Novo Adjudication of a decision on the
application with the Energy Facilities Siting Board; and
(9) Guidance for a Clean Energy Infrastructure Permitting bylaw or ordinance Local
Governments may adopt.

27 The purpose of 225 CMR 29.00 is to establish standard conditions, criteria, requirements,
28 and procedures for the efficient and appropriate siting and permitting of Small Clean
29 Energy Infrastructure Facilities by Local Governments.

267 29.04: Applicability
268
269 (1) 225 CMR 29.00 applies to the local siting and permitting of Small Clean Energy
270 Infrastructure Facilities by Local Governments upon the election of an Applicant to
271 submit a Consolidated Local Permit Application. Nothing in 225 CMR 29.00 shall be
272 construed to disallow an Applicant from seeking local approvals on an individual basis
273 outside of the 225 CMR 29.00 consolidated permitting process, or, in the case of a Small
274 Clean Transmission and Distribution Infrastructure Facility, file an application with the
275 Board pursuant to M.G.L. c. 164 § 69U.
276
277 (2) The standards and requirements in 225 CMR 29.00, including the availability of De Novo
278 Adjudication of a Local Government’s decision on a Small Clean Energy Infrastructure
279 Facility before the Board, shall not apply to an Applicant who does not elect to submit a
280 Consolidated Local Permit Application.
281
282 (3) Adding Facility Type to the Definition of Small Clean Energy Generation Facility.
283 Following a rulemaking by the Department in consultation with the Board, the Department
284 may add additional facility types to the definition of a Small Clean Energy Generation
285 Facility, provided that the type of generation facility added produces no greenhouse gas
286 emissions or other pollutant emissions known to have negative health impacts.
287

409 29.08: Pre-Filing Requirements
410
411 (1) The Applicant shall complete all pre-filing requirements in 225 CMR 29.08 not more
412 than 60 days before submitting a Consolidated Local Permit Application to the Local
413 Government Representative. A Consolidated Local Permit Application submitted
414 before the completion of all pre-filing requirements, or more than 60 days after
415 completion of all pre-filing requirements, shall be automatically denied without
416 prejudice.
417
418 (2) Exemptions. All Small Clean Energy Infrastructure Facilities that qualify for an
419 exemption under 225 CMR 29.07(1) are exempt from the requirements of 225 CMR
420 29.08.
421
422 (3) Pre-filing Municipal Meeting. The Applicant shall schedule a meeting with members
423 of the Local Government which may include part-time or full-time staff, and the
424 chairpersons of relevant boards, or their designees. The Applicant and Local
425 Government shall mutually agree on the time and place of the meeting. The meeting
426 shall take place within 45 days of the Applicant requesting the meeting and before the
427 pre-filing information session described in 225 CMR 29.08(4). The purpose of the pre-
428 filing municipal meeting is for the Applicant and Local Government to:
429 (a) Verify basic project and site information, including zoning information, known
430 areas subject to state or local environmental laws, and any other areas,
431 structures, or buildings of cultural or historical significance that could be
432 impacted by the project;
433 (b) Identify to the best extent possible all needed decisions and permits from the
434 Local Government;
435 (c) Identify, to the best extent possible, any applicable local bylaws and processes;
436 (d) Identify, to the best extent possible, any other potential permitting, design, or engineering obstacles or
437 items of concern that might hinder the Applicant’s ability to secure all needed decisions within the 12-
438 month timeframe; and
439 (e) Discuss dates, times, and locations for a public meeting, if applicable.
440
441 (4) Pre-filing Information Session Requirements. The Applicant shall host not less than
442 one pre-filing information session open to the public which shall conform to
443 Department specifications in its Guideline on Pre-Filing Stakeholder Engagement. The
14 of 24

444 Applicant shall host the information session in a publicly accessible location, and it
445 shall be held in a hybrid format if feasible.
446 (a) Public Notice Requirements. The Applicant shall provide notice of the pre-
447 filing information session not less than 10 days in advance. The Applicant shall
448 provide notice in a manner consistent with the following requirements:
449 1. The Applicant shall provide notice to Abutters and Local Stakeholders of
450 the proposed Site Footprint via United States Postal Service First Class
451 Mail®. Determination of ownership and common boundary shall be made
452 with reference to the current local tax assessors' records and maps. Notice
453 shall include but not be limited to the following:
454 i. The proposed Small Clean Energy Infrastructure Facility’s site
455 address, a map of the Site Footprint, and the name of the project
456 developer;
457 ii. A description of the size of the proposed Small Clean Energy
458 Infrastructure Facility, which includes the Site Footprint and the
459 project’s generation and energy storage capacity, if applicable;
460 iii. Public meeting details, including dates, times, locations, and
461 accessibility information, if applicable; and

628 29.10 Consolidated Local Permit Application Review Process.
629
630 (1) Completeness Determination. The Local Government Representative shall determine
631 whether a Consolidated Local Permit Application is complete not later than 30 days after
632 receipt. The Local Government Representative shall share the Consolidated Local Permit
633 Application with the local Board of Health and other relevant local departments or boards
634 to assist in determining completeness.
635 (a) Completeness Standard. A Consolidated Local Permit Application shall be
636 deemed complete by the Local Government Representative if:
637 1. All permit fees have been paid to the Local Government pursuant to 225
638 CMR 29.09(1); and
639 2. The Applicant has provided all necessary documentation, materials, and
640 disclosures required under 225 CMR 29.09(2).
641 (b) If a Consolidated Local Permit Application is deemed incomplete, the Local
642 Government Representative shall notify the Applicant and specify deficiencies.
643 The Applicant shall have 30 days, and any additional time as determined by the
644 Local Government Representative, to cure any deficiencies before the
645 Consolidated Local Permit Application is rejected. The Local Government shall
646 determine whether the revised Consolidated Local Permit Application cures all
647 deficiencies not later than 30 days of receipt.
648 (c) If the Local Government Representative determines the Applicant has not cured
649 all deficiencies, it may allow the Applicant an additional 30 days to cure stated
650 deficiencies or deny the Consolidated Local Permit Application without prejudice.
651 An Applicant whose Consolidated Local Permit Application is denied shall be
652 required to repeat all applicable pre-filing requirements described in 225 CMR
653 29.08.
654 (d) If the Local Government Representative does not determine whether a
655 Consolidated Local Permit Application is completed within 30 days of receipt, the
656 Consolidated Local Permit Application shall be deemed to be complete and
657 eligible for review under 225 CMR 29.10.
658
659 (2) The Applicant shall provide a digital copy of the complete Consolidated Local Permit
660 Application to the Department within 10 days of the completeness determination
661 described 225 CMR 29.12.
662
663 (3) Review. The Local Government shall complete its review and issue a decision on a
664 Consolidated Local Permit Application not more than 12 months after its receipt of a
665 complete Consolidated Local Permit Application, or from the date it is deemed complete
666 pursuant to 225 CMR 29.09. All Local Government boards and departments reviewing
19 of 24

667 the Consolidated Local Permit Application may conduct their reviews concurrently
668 provided that those reviews are complete within 12 months. Local Government boards
669 and departments shall maintain intergovernmental collaboration throughout the
670 application review process.
671 (a) The Local Government’s 12-month review period shall begin upon its receipt of
672 the Applicant’s Consolidated Local Permit Application and terminate upon the
673 date that the Town Clerk stamps the Consolidated Local Permit.
674 (b) In the case that a Local Government lacks the resources, capacity or staffing to
675 review a Consolidated Local Permit Application within 12 months, it may, not
676 later than 60 days after receipt of such application or at any time thereafter with
677 the consent of the Applicant, request in writing a De Novo Adjudication of such
678 application by the EFSB Director pursuant to 980 CMR 14.02(1)(c) and
679 14.02(2)(b). If the EFSB Director accepts the Consolidated Local Permit
680 Application for said De Novo Adjudication, any permit fees received by Local
681 Government for its review of the Consolidated Local Permit Application shall be
682 refunded in full to the Applicant within 30 days of the Director’s acceptance of
683 the Consolidated Local Permit Application, or as soon as practicable.
684
685 (4) Significant Changes to a proposed Small Clean Energy Infrastructure Facility. If the
686 Applicant identifies a Significant Change to a Small Clean Energy Infrastructure Facility

689 Local Permit Application incomplete. The Applicant may then submit all necessary
