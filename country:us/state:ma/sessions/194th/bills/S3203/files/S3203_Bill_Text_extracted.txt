1  by adding the following sections:-  
2  "SECTION X. Section 47H of chapter 175, as appearing in the 2022 Official Edition, is 
3 hereby amended by striking out the final two sentences and inserting in place thereof the 
4 following:- 
5  For purposes of this section, “infertility” means a condition or status characterized by any 
6 of the following: 
7  (1) A licensed physician’s findings, based on: a patient’s medical, sexual, and 
8 reproductive history; age; physical findings; diagnostic testing; or any combination of those 
9 factors. This definition shall not prevent testing and diagnosis of infertility to establish infertility 
10 with or without appropriate exposure to gametes, per the patient’s provider. 
11  (2) The need for medical intervention, including, but not limited to, the use of donor 
12 gametes, donor embryos, gestational carrier to achieve a live birth either as an individual or with 
13 a partner. 

2 of 11 14  (3) The failure to establish a pregnancy or to carry a pregnancy to live birth after 
15 unprotected sexual intercourse. For purposes of this section, “unprotected sexual intercourse” 
16 means no more than 12 months of unprotected sexual intercourse for a person under 35 years of 
17 age or no more than 6 months of unprotected sexual intercourse for a person 35 years of age or 
18 older. Pregnancy that does not result in a live birth will not restart the 12-month or 6-month time 
19 period to qualify as having infertility. 
20  (4) An impairment of reproductive ability due to factors, including, but not limited to,  
21 medical condition, male factor, female factor, combined or unexplained reproductive challenges, 
22 as well as genetic disorders or iatrogenic infertility. 
23  Coverage for medically necessary expenses of diagnosis and treatment of infertility shall 
24 include, but shall not be limited to: (i) a minimum of six oocyte retrievals and unlimited fresh 
25 and frozen embryo transfers, using single embryo transfer when recommended by patient’s 
26 physician and medically appropriate; (ii) embryo transfer; (iii) artificial insemination; (iv) 
27 surgical sperm extraction procedures; (v) third-party reproduction including in vitro fertilization 
28 with donor egg, sperm, or embryo or gestational carrier; (vi) procedures necessary to screen or 
29 diagnose a fertilized egg before transfer, including, but not limited to, preimplantation genetic 
30 testing for aneuploidy, preimplantation genetic testing for chromosome structural 
31 rearrangements, and preimplantation genetic testing for monogenic or single gene disorders. 
32  In administering coverage for medically necessary expenses of diagnosis and treatment of 
33 infertility, a carrier or participating provider, as those terms are defined in section 1 of chapter 
34 176O, shall not: 
35  (1) impose conditions for eligibility beyond what is provided in the law; 

3 of 11 36  (2) exclude, limit, or otherwise restrict coverage or processing of benefits for fertility 
37 medications that are different from those imposed on other prescription medications; 
38  (3) exclude or deny coverage of any fertility services, including medication, based on an 
39 individual’s participation in fertility services provided by or to any third party. For purposes of 
40 this paragraph, “third party” includes: (i) any fresh or cryopreserved oocyte, sperm, or embryo, 
41 regardless of the initial coverage source of the donor or the genetic material; and (ii) a gestational 
42 carrier that enables an intended parent, member, and/or partner of a member to become a parent. 
43  (4) exclude services based on the quantity of the patient’s existing cryopreserved oocyte, 
44 sperm, or embryos; the provider’s discretion will determine if cryopreserved oocyte, sperm, or 
45 embryo provides a reasonable chance of success and whether additional fertility services are 
46 required; 
47  (5) implement any deductible, copayment, coinsurance, benefit maximum, waiting 
48 period, or other limitation on coverage that is different from those imposed upon benefits for 
49 services not related to infertility; 
50  (6) impose limitations on coverage based solely on arbitrary, non-medically based factors 
51 including, but not limited to, number of attempts, dollar amounts, or age; or 
52  (7) provide different benefits to, or impose different requirements for different groups, 
53 based on diagnosis. 
54  Limitations on coverage coverage for medically necessary expenses of diagnosis and 
55 treatment of infertility shall be based on clinical guidelines and the patient's medical history. 
56 Clinical guidelines shall be maintained in written form and available to any enrollee. Standards 

4 of 11 57 or guidelines developed by the American Society for Reproductive Medicine, the American 
58 College of Obstetrics and Gynecology, the Society for Assisted Reproductive Technology, or 
59 similar relevant medical societies may serve as a basis for such clinical guidelines. Making, 
60 issuing, circulating, or causing to be made, issued or circulated, any clinical guidelines that are 
61 based upon data that are not reasonably current or that do not cite with specificity any references 
62 relied upon shall constitute an unfair and deceptive act and practice pursuant to section 2 of 
63 chapter 93A. 
64  Consistent with Massachusetts anti-discrimination law, coverage for medically necessary 
65 expenses of diagnosis and treatment of infertility shall be provided without discrimination based 
66 on age, ancestry, color, disability, domestic partner status, gender, gender expression, gender 
67 identity, genetic information, marital status, national origin, race, religion, sex, or sexual 
68 orientation. 
69  This section shall not be construed to deny or restrict any existing right or benefit to 
70 coverage and treatment of infertility or fertility services under an existing law, plan, or policy. 
71 This section shall not be construed to interfere with a medical provider’s, physician’s, or 
72 surgeon’s clinical judgment.
