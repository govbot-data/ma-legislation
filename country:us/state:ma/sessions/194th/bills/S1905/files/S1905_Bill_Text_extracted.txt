An Act clarifying military buyback options.
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority
of the same, as follows:
1 SECTION 1. Section 3 of Chapter 71 of the Acts of 1996, as most recently amended by
2 chapter 468 of the acts of 2002, is hereby amended by striking the second paragraph.
3 SECTION 2. Paragraph (h) of subdivision (1) of section 4 of chapter 32 of the General
4 Laws is hereby amended by inserting the following sentence at the end of the fourth paragraph:-
5 Members in service of a retirement system eligible for such creditable service under this
6 paragraph shall make application for such creditable service and complete all makeup payments
7 while a member in service and before the date any retirement allowance becomes effective.
