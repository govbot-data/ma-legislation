HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES, July 31, 2026.                           
 
The committee on Ways and Means , to whom was referred the Senate 
Bill authorizing the division of capital asset management to enter into a 
lease with the town of Plymouth for purposes of maintaining Pilgrim 
Memorial park in the town of Plymouth (Senate, No. 3235 ), reports 
recommending that the same ought to pass with amendment s striking out 
all after the enacting clause and inserting in place thereof the text 
contained in House document numbered 5636; and by striking out the title 
and inserting in place thereof the following title: “An Act authorizing the 
division of capital asset management and maintenance to enter into a lease 
with the town of Plymouth for purposes of maintaining the Pilgrim 
Memorial state park in the town of Plymouth.”. 
 
 
For the committee, 
 
AARON MICHLEWITZ.

 By striking out all after the enacting clause and inserting in place thereof the following:– 
 
1  (a) Notwithstanding any general or special law to the contrary, the commissioner of 
2 capital asset management and maintenance, in consultation with the commissioner of 
3 conservation and recreation, may enter into a 25-year lease with the town of Plymouth for the 
4 fields and facilities, together with the land and appurtenances of a parcel of land known as the 
5 Pilgrim Memorial state park, which is located at 79 Water street in the town of Plymouth. The 
6 lease may provide for improvements to the field and facilities, together with the land and 
7 appurtenances. 
8  (b) Any lease executed pursuant to subsection (a) shall be on terms, conditions and 
9 consideration acceptable to the commissioner of capital asset management and maintenance, in 
10 consultation with the commissioner of conservation and recreation, and may be renewed 
11 decennially for 10-year terms at the discretion of the parties named above; provided, however, 
12 that any lease entered into pursuant to subsection (a) shall provide that: (i) the town of Plymouth 
13 shall provide general oversight for land, facilities, fields and appurtenances associated therewith 
14 during the term of the lease; (ii) the town of Plymouth shall carry comprehensive general liability 
15 insurance naming the commonwealth as a co-insured, to protect the commonwealth against all 
16 claims for personal injury or property damage arising from or on land and appurtenances 

2 of 2 17 associated therewith during the term of the lease; (iii) Pilgrim Memorial state park shall be 
18 accessible to the general public when not in use by permittees; (iv) the town of Plymouth shall 
19 not design or construct any facilities on the parcel of land without the written approval of the 
20 commissioner of capital asset management and maintenance and the commissioner of 
21 conservation and recreation; (v) the town of Plymouth shall be responsible for all utility costs, 
22 subject to the lease, and for the general day-to-day maintenance of Pilgrim Memorial state park; 
23 and (vi) the town of Plymouth shall have sole responsibility for the development of rules and the 
24 establishment of fees for the use of Pilgrim Memorial state park.; and 
25 by striking out the title and inserting in place thereof the following title: “An Act authorizing the 
26 division of capital asset management and maintenance to enter into a lease with the town of 
27 Plymouth for purposes of maintaining the Pilgrim Memorial state park in the town of 
28 Plymouth.”.
