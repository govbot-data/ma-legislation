13 One representative of the Gateway Municipality rider community population shall serve
14 on the advisory board as a voting member for a term of 3 years. Each Gateway Municipality in
15 the region, as defined by MGL Chap 23A Section 3A, shall appoint a representative that is an

17 however, that the representative of the disabled community, rider community and gateway
18 municipality rider community shall not be appointed by the same city or town in the region. For
19 authority regions that contain more than one Gateway Municipality, each municipality shall
20 appoint a representative on a rotating basis in an order as determined by the board. The mayor or
21 city manager and the chairman, town manager or town administrator shall appoint a resident of
22 the city or town for this purpose.
