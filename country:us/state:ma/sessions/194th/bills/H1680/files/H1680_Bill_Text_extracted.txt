In the One Hundred and Ninety-Fourth General Court
(2025-2026)
_______________
An Act relative to a ban on Kratom.
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority
of the same, as follows:
1 Subsection (d) of class A of section 31 of chapter 94C of the General Laws, as appearing
2 in the 2022 Official Edition, is hereby amended by adding the following paragraph:-
3 (9) Kratom, which for the purposes of this paragraph, shall mean any parts of the plant
4 Mitragyna speciosa, whether growing or not, and any compound, manufacture, salt, derivative,
5 mixture, product or preparation of the plant, including mitragynine and the metabolite 7-
6 hydroxymitragynine.
