An Act authorizing the town of Avon to appoint a town clerk. 
 
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority 
of the same, as follows: 
 
1  SECTION 1: As of the effective date of this act, the elected incumbent town clerk shall 
2 continue to serve in such elected position for the term for which she was elected. The town clerk 
3 shall, during this period, be subject to the call of the appointing authority for the position as forth 
4 in the Town of Avon Town Charter. Upon the expiration of such elected term, the elected office 
5 shall be abolished; provided, however, that the elected incumbent holding office as of the 
6 effective date of this act shall become the first appointed clerk under this Act. If the elected 
7 incumbent removes from office prior to the expiration of the elected term, the elected position 
8 shall thereupon be abolished and such office filled in accordance with appointing procedures as 
9 set forth in the Town of Avon Town Charter. 
10  SECTION 2: The Town of Avon Town Charter shall be amended by striking out the 
11 existing Section 3-4(a) “Term of Office” and replacing said section with the following language 

2 of 2 12 “Appointment, Term of Office - There shall be a town clerk, appointed by the Select Board, to 
13 serve for a term of three years.” 
14  SECTION 3. This act shall take effect upon its passage.
