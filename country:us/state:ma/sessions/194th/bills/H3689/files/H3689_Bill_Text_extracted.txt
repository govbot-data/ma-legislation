An Act relating to road building equipment classifications. 
 
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority 
of the same, as follows: 
 
1  Notwithstanding any general or special law to the contrary, the Massachusetts 
2 Department of Transportation shall amend the project guidelines relating to road building 
3 equipment classifications for chapter 90 work, by reducing the minimum weight requirement for 
4 trucks, from 27 thousand pounds to 16 thousand pounds.
