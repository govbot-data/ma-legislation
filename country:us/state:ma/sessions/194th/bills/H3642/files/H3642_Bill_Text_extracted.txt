An Act relative to retrofit headlights in vehicles.
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority
of the same, as follows:
1 SECTION 1. Section 7 of chapter 90 of the General Laws, as appearing in the 2022
2 Official Edition, is hereby amended by inserting, in line 80, after the words “such way.” the
3 following sentence:- No motor vehicle so operated shall display a headlamp that: (i) flickers or
4 cannot reliably sustain a consistent brightness level; (ii) is improperly installed; (iii) does not
5 utilize pulse width modulation, or similar technology to prevent flickering; or (iv) does not meet
6 certain other quality standards, as determined by the registrar.
7 SECTION 2. Said chapter 90 is hereby further amended by inserting after section 9D the
8 following section:-
9 Section 9E. No person shall manufacture, sell or offer for sale or trade any motor vehicle
10 headlamp that: (i) flickers or cannot reliably sustain a consistent brightness level; (ii) does not
11 utilize pulse width modulation, or similar technology to prevent flickering; or (iii) does not meet
12 certain other quality standards, as determined by the registrar
1 of 2

13 SECTION 3. Not later than 180 days following the passage of this act, the registrar of
14 motor vehicles shall, pursuant to chapter 30A, promulgate regulations for the implementation of
15 this act, which shall include quality standards for motor vehicle headlamps.
