An Act for community housing packages.
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority
of the same, as follows:
1 SECTION 1. The Massachusetts General Laws are hereby amended by inserting the
2 following new chapter:
3 An Act for Community Housing Packages.
4 PURPOSE OF THIS ACT:
5 Over a period of many recent years, it has been concluded by many communities,
6 especially in Eastern Massachusetts, that the supply of affordable, high-quality, and/or housing
7 of almost any kind, has fallen short of the actual needs, thereby creating severe economic
8 pressures, often for, both employees, and employers, in convenience to actual work places.
1 of 3

9 Only by, creating improved incentives, and distinct standards, might these problems be
10 eased, to the extent that it is possible, and these matters, are the business, and purpose of this act.
11 Mandatory requirements, have value; but cannot be understood, as the only way to accomplish
12 expanding needed housing supply.
13 SECTION 2.
14 1.) The Department of Communities and Development, shall be authorized to create, after
15 due consideration, Community Housing Packages (CHPs), for helping to create, and recruit,
16 improved economics, across the 351 municipalities of Massachusetts, to expand, and strengthen
17 local communities, and especially affordable, safe, and durable housing units. These CHPs, shall
18 be part of a comprehensive state planning, and coordination process, intersecting with Master
19 Planning processes, as needed, and appropriate.
20 2.) The Department of Communities and Development, shall be authorized to create
21 suitable Regulations, to provide for the provisions of this statute, taking into account, particular
22 local preferences for, and including strengthened, safe, and durable housing units; that reflect
23 state-of-the-art, fire-resistant, and energy-efficient standards. While pressures to create
24 significant increases in housing units, are understandable; these pressures should not prevent
25 opportunities, to create substantially, better, well-built, longer-term quality construction.
26 3.) Such Community Housing Packages shall be based on encouraging, where possible,
27 mixed-use development, and by consulting a recognized list of pre-screened, neighborhood, and
28 community-friendly developers, and contractors, with high-quality lasting construction
29 standards.
2 of 3

30 4.) The Department of Housing and Community Development, shall establish, keep, and
31 annually renew, a listing of high-quality, neighborhood, and community-friendly developers, by
32 welcoming, pre-screening, of such companies, interviewing them, and publicizing such
33 businesses, in various appropriate positive publicity, by public announcement, and general
34 media.
35 5.) Community Housing Packages (CHPs), shall take into account relevant modest, to
36 medium intensity regional transit options, in encouraging newer local economic development;
37 and intersect with state transportation planning.
38 6.) These Community Housing Packages (CHPs), shall also be distributed across the
39 width, and breadth, of The Commonwealth, taking into account opportunities, to strengthen local
40 businesses, local communities, and ranges of income levels.
