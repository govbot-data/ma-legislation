An Act prohibiting injurious operations or offering services or products that discriminate against
or injure protected classes.
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority
of the same, as follows:
1 SECTION 1. The first sentence of the second paragraph of section 92A of chapter 272 of
2 the General Laws as appearing in the 2022 Official Edition is hereby amended by inserting after
3 word “place”, the following words:- or business,
4 SECTION 2. The first sentence of the second paragraph of section 92A of chapter 272 is
5 hereby amended by inserting after words “patronage of”, the following words:- or offers
6 products or services to
7 SECTION 3. Chapter 272 Section 98 of the General Laws is hereby amended to read as
8 follows:
1 of 2

9 Whoever makes any distinction, discrimination or restriction on account of race, color,
10 religious creed, national origin, sex, gender identity, sexual orientation, which shall not include
11 persons whose sexual orientation involves minor children as the sex object, deafness, blindness
12 or any physical or mental disability or ancestry relative to the admission of or whose operations,
13 products or services result in a discriminatory or physically injurious effect on any protected
14 person to, or his treatment by or in any place of public accommodation, resort or amusement, as
15 defined in section ninety-two A, or whoever aids or incites such distinction, discrimination,
16 treatment, physically injurious effect or restriction, whether or not the aggrieved protected party
17 seeks access to the patronage, products or services offered by the place of accommodation, resort
18 or amusement, shall be punished by a fine of not more than twenty-five hundred dollars or by
19 imprisonment for not more than one year, or both, and shall be liable to any person aggrieved
20 thereby for such damages as are enumerated in section five of chapter one hundred and fifty-one
21 B; provided, however, that such civil forfeiture shall be of an amount not less than three hundred
22 dollars; but such person so aggrieved shall not recover against more than one person by reason of
23 any one act of distinction, discrimination or restriction. All persons shall have the right to the full
24 and equal accommodations, advantages, facilities and privileges and the right to not be
25 physically injured by the operations, products or services of any place of public accommodation,
26 resort or amusement whether or not the aggrieved protected party seeks access to the patronage,
27 products or services offered by the place of accommodation, resort or amusement, subject only to
28 the conditions and limitations established by law and applicable to all persons. This right is
29 recognized and declared to be a civil right.
