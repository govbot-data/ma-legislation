HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES, July 31, 2026.                           
 
The committee on Ways and Means , to whom was referred the Senate 
Bill authorizing the Massachusetts Department of Fish and Game to 
convey easements over certain parcels of land (Senate, No. 3236 ), reports 
recommending that the same ought to pass with amendment s striking out 
all after the enacting clause and inserting in place thereof the text 
contained in House document numbered 5637; and by striking out the title 
and inserting in place thereof the following title: “An Act authorizing the 
department of fish and game to convey easements over certain parcels of 
land in the town of Bourne.”. 
 
For the committee, 
 
AARON MICHLEWITZ.

4 of 5 62  SECTION 4. (a) The value of the easements described in sections 1 and 3 and the value 
63 of the property interest to be conveyed to the commonwealth pursuant to section 2 shall be 
64 determined by an independent appraisal prepared in accordance with the usual and customary 
65 professional appraisal practice by a qualified appraiser commissioned by the commissioner of 
66 capital asset management and maintenance. The commissioner of capital asset management and 
67 maintenance may, in consultation with the commissioner of fish and game, accept an appraisal of 
68 said easements and property interest conducted prior to the effective date of this act. The 
69 commissioner of capital asset management and maintenance shall submit the appraisal to the 
70 inspector general for review and comment. The inspector general shall review and approve the 
71 appraisal, and the review shall include an examination of the methodology used for the appraisal. 
72 The inspector general shall prepare a report of his review and file the report with the 
73 commissioner of capital asset management and maintenance for submission by the commissioner 
74 to the house and senate committees on ways and means and the joint committee on state 
75 administration and regulatory oversight. The commissioner of capital asset management and 
76 maintenance shall submit copies of the appraisals and the inspector general’s report to the house 
77 and senate committees on ways and means and the joint committee on state administration and 
78 regulatory oversight before the execution of the conveyances. 
79  (b) Algonquin Gas Transmission, LLC shall compensate the commonwealth in an amount
