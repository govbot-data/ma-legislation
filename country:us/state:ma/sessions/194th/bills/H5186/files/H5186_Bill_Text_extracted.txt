HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES, March 9, 2026. 
 The committee on Tourism, Arts and Cultural Development, to whom 
was referred the petition (accompanied by bill, House, No. 3590) of 
Mindy Domb and David T. Vieira relative to the position of poet laureate 
for the Commonwealth of Massachusetts, reports recommending that the 
accompanying order (House, No. 5186) ought to be adopted. 
 
For the committee, 
 
SEAN GARBALLEY.

1  Ordered, That the committee on Tourism, Arts and Cultural Development be authorized 
2 to sit during a recess of the General Court to make an investigation and study of House document 
3 numbered 3590, relative to the position of poet laureate. 
4  Said committee shall report to the General Court the results of its investigation and study 
5 and its recommendations, if any, together with drafts of legislation necessary to carry such 
6 recommendations into effect, by filing the same with the Clerk of the House of Representatives 
7 on or before December 31, 2026.
