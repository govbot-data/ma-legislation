An Act providing insurance coverage for the medically necessary treatment of port-wine stains.
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority
of the same, as follows:
1 SECTION 1. Chapter 175 of the General Laws is hereby amended by inserting after
2 section 47UU the following section:
3 Section 47VV. (a) For the purposes of this section, the following terms shall have the
4 following definitions unless context clearly indicates otherwise:
5 “Port-wine stains”, A capillary malformation skin condition diagnosed as nevus
6 flammeus, Sturge-Weber syndrome, or Klippel-Trénaunay syndrome, also known as a port-wine
7 birthmark. “medically necessary treatment”, medical treatment intended to prevent or address
8 functional impairments related to vision, oral function, or physical mobility, medical
9 complications such as inflammation, bleeding, infection, or hypertrophy of the lesion or
10 significant psychosocial distress, particularly in pediatric patients.
1 of 4

11 “early intervention treatment”, topical, intralesional, or systemic medical therapies, Food
12 and Drug Administration-approved laser treatments, surgery or other medical procedures aimed
13 at treating or preventing health complications associated with port-wine stains.
14 (b)Any individual or group policy of accident and sickness insurance issued under this
15 chapter; any subscription certificate under an individual or group medical service agreement
16 delivered within the Commonwealth under Chapter 176B; any group health maintenance
17 contract issued by a health maintenance organization under Chapter 176G; and any coverage
18 offered by the Group Insurance Commission under Chapter 32A shall provide coverage for
19 medically necessary early intervention treatments of port-wine stains in individuals 18 years of
20 age or younger.
21 (c) Coverage shall not include treatment solely for cosmetic purposes unless such
22 treatment also meets one or more of the criteria defined as medically necessary in subsection (a).
23 (d) No policy shall impose additional deductibles, coinsurance, or limits on the number of
24 covered treatments for port-wine stains that are more restrictive than those imposed on other
25 medically necessary services.
26 SECTION 2. This act shall apply to contracts that take effect or are renewed on or after
27 January 1, 2026.

40 (b) Notwithstanding any general or special law to the contrary, the Center for Health
41 Information and Analysis shall conduct a review of a mandated health benefit proposal to require
42 coverage for the medically necessary treatment and early intervention of port-wine stains in
43 individuals 18 years of age or younger. The review shall include consideration of treatment
44 intended to prevent or address: (i) functional impairments related to vision, oral function, or
45 physical mobility; (ii) medical complications such as inflammation, bleeding, infection, or lesion
46 hypertrophy; and (iii) significant psychosocial distress, particularly in pediatric patients;
47 provided however, that the review shall not include a review of treatment solely for cosmetic
48 purposes unless such treatment also meets one or more of the criteria defined as medically
49 necessary in subsection (a) of Section 3 .
50 The review shall be performed by the center consistent with section 38C of chapter 3 of
51 the General Laws. The center shall evaluate the impact of such a mandate as a requirement for all
