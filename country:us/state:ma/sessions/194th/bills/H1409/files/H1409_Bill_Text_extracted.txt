An Act relative to MassHealth. 
 
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority 
of the same, as follows: 
 
1  SECTION 1. Section 13C of chapter 118E of the General Laws, as appearing in the 2022 
2 Official Edition, is hereby amended by inserting after the first paragraph the following 
3 paragraph:- 
4  The executive office shall not subject a nursing facility, as defined in 101 CMR 206.02, 
5 to a downward adjustment of its health care payments based on the failure of the facility to meet 
6 a specified number of hours per patient per day; provided, however, that nursing homes that have 
7 a Medicaid utilization rate of 80% or higher, as determined by EOHHS, shall not be subject to 
8 the downward adjustment of its healthcare payments. 

2 of 2 9  SECTION 2. The executive office of health and human services shall promulgate or 
10 amend any regulations necessary to implement this act.
