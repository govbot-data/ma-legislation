In the One Hundred and Ninety-Fourth General Court
(2025-2026)
_______________
An Act establishing a sick leave bank for Jeffrey Yacinthe.
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority
of the same, as follows:
1 Notwithstanding any general or special law to the contrary, the Massachusetts
2 Department of Elementary and Secondary Education shall establish a sick leave bank for Jeffrey
3 Yacinthe, an employee of the Department.. Any employee of the Department of Secondary and
4 Elementary Education may voluntarily contribute one or more sick, personal, or vacation days to
5 the sick leave bank for use by Jeffrey Yacinthe. If Jeffrey Yacinthe terminates employment with
6 the Department or requests to dissolve the sick leave bank, any remaining time in the sick leave
7 bank shall be transferred to the Department of Elementary and Secondary Education-paid leave
8 bank. Sick leave bank days shall not be used for absences unrelated to the illness or disability
9 that necessitated the establishment of the sick leave bank as determined by the Department.
