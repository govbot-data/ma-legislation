SENATE DOCKET, NO. 2885 FILED ON: 5/2/2025
Senate, May 12, 2025 -- (Filed by Mr. Tarr) Order relative to the calling of a proposal for a
legislative amendment to the Constitution to require a supermajority vote for the utilization of
rainy day funds.
The Commonwealth of Massachusetts
Senate, May 12, 2025.
_______________
In the One Hundred and Ninety-Fourth General Court
(2025-2026)
_______________
1 Ordered, That, the Senate hereby calls for a joint session of the two Houses, conformably
2 to the provisions of Article XLVIII (as amended by Article LXXXI) of the Amendments to the
3 Constitution, to consider the following proposal:
4 Proposal for a legislative amendment to the Constitution to require a supermajority vote for the
5 utilization of rainy day funds (Senate, No. 13) with reference to which the committee on
6 Revenue has reported, in accordance with Joint Rule 23, that the amendment ought NOT to pass.
