An Act establishing the city of Cambridge employment and job training trust.
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority
of the same, as follows:
1 SECTION 1. There is hereby established a trust to be known as the City of Cambridge
2 Employment and Job Training Trust (the “Trust”). The purpose of the Trust is to assist in the
3 creation and maintenance of employment opportunities within the City of Cambridge for the
4 residents of the City of Cambridge through employment and job training services.
5 SECTION 2. There is hereby established a board of trustees hereinafter referred to as the
6 Board, which shall include no fewer than 5 and no more than 9 trustees, including ex officio the
7 Cambridge City Manager or designee. Those members of the Board who are not serving in an ex
8 officio capacity shall be appointed by the City Manager and are designated as public agents for
9 purposes of the Constitution of the Commonwealth.
10 SECTION 3. The powers of the Board, all of which shall be carried on in furtherance of
11 the purposes set forth in this act, shall include but not be limited to, the following:
1 of 5

12 (a) to receive and accept personal property, by gift, grant, devise or transfer from any
13 federal, state or local governmental or other public entity, or any person, trust, corporation or
14 other private entity, including without limitation funds or other personal property tendered to the
15 Trust in connection with the Cambridge Zoning Ordinance or any other city ordinance;
16 (b) to retain personal property, including without limitation personal property that is
17 uninvested and investments that yield no income or yield a high rate of income;
18 (c) to make grants for Trust purposes upon such terms as the Board deems advisable;
19 (d) to make loans for Trust purposes upon such terms of repayment or otherwise as the
20 Board deems advisable and to extend the time for payment of any obligation of the Trust;
21 (e) to sell, exchange, or transfer personal property of the Trust for such consideration and
22 upon such terms as the Board deems advisable;
23 (f) to execute, acknowledge and deliver assignments, transfers, pledges, contracts,
24 promissory notes, releases and other instruments, sealed or unsealed, necessary, proper or
25 incidental to any transaction involving Trust assets in which the Board engages for the
26 accomplishment of the purposes of the Trust;
27 (g) to employ advisors and agents, including without limitation accountants and
28 appraisers, and to delegate to such persons or firms such ministerial or discretionary powers as
29 the Board deems necessary;
30 (h) to pay reasonable compensation to all advisors and agents, and to apportion such
31 compensation between income and principal as the Board deems advisable;
2 of 5

32 (i) to apportion receipts and charges between income and principal as the Board deems
33 advisable;
34 (j) to exercise all powers and rights, including proxies and powers of attorney, necessary
35 and proper in connection with the ownership of any securities held as part of Trust personal
36 property; and
37 (k) to compromise, arbitrate, defend, enforce, release, settle or otherwise adjust claims in
38 favor of or against the Trust and to accept any personal property, either in total or partial
39 satisfaction of any indebtedness or other obligation, and subject to the provisions of this act, to
40 continue to hold the same for such period of time as the Board may deem advisable.
41 (l) to exercise all other powers authorized by common law, statute, the Constitution, or
42 other provisions of this act.
43 SECTION 4. Notwithstanding any general or special law to the contrary, all monies paid
44 to the Trust by public or private contribution or in accordance with the Cambridge Zoning
45 Ordinance or any other city ordinance shall be paid directly into the Trust, and such monies do
46 not have to be appropriated or accepted and approved into the Trust. General revenues
47 appropriated into the Trust become Trust property; and to be expended, these funds do not need
48 to be further appropriated. All monies remaining in the Trust at the end of any fiscal year,
49 whether or not expended by the Board within 1 year of the date they were appropriated into the
50 Trust, remain Trust property.
51 SECTION 5. The Trust is a public employer and the members of the Board are public
52 employees for purposes of Chapter 258 of the General Laws.
3 of 5

53 SECTION 6. The Trust shall be deemed a municipal agency and the trustees special
54 municipal employees, for purposes of Chapter 268A of the General Laws.
55 SECTION 7. The Trust is a governmental body for purposes of Sections 18 to 25 of
56 Chapter 30A of the General Laws.
57 SECTION 8. The Trust is exempt from Chapters 59 and 62 of the General Laws and from
58 any other provisions concerning the payment of taxes based upon or measured by personal
59 property or income imposed by the commonwealth or any political subdivision thereof.
60 SECTION 9. The books and records of the Trust shall be audited annually by an
61 independent auditor in accordance with accepted accounting practices.
62 SECTION 10. The declaration of trust, which establishes and controls the Trust, as
63 provided in this act, shall be filed in the office of the Cambridge City Clerk.
64 SECTION 11. The Trust is a board of the city for purposes of Chapter 30B and Section
65 15A of Chapter 40 of the General Laws; provided, however, that agreements and conveyances
66 between the Trust and agencies, boards, commissions, authorities, departments and public
67 instrumentalities of the City shall be exempt from the provisions of Chapter 30B.
68 SECTION 12. (a) The City of Cambridge may, by ordinance, require the payment of an
69 employment and job training linkage fee for certain projects. The ordinance shall specify the
70 dollar amount of the linkage fee; the method by which it shall be increased from time to time;
71 and the types of uses to which it shall apply.
4 of 5

72 SECTION 13. The Board shall at least annually, and additionally upon request of the City
73 Manager, file a written report with the City Manager of its activities for the reporting year and an
74 accounting of its receipts and expenditures.
75 SECTION 14. This act shall take effect upon its passage.
