In the One Hundred and Ninety-Fourth General Court 
(2025-2026) 
_______________ 
 
An Act further regulating the Gloucester Fisheries Commission. 
 
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority 
of the same, as follows: 
 
1  SECTION 1. Chapter 260 of the acts of 1956 is hereby amended by striking out sections 
2 1 and 2 and inserting in place thereof the following 2 sections:-  
3  Section 1. There is hereby created a body politic and corporate to be known as the 
4 Gloucester Fisheries Commission, which shall be a public instrumentality for the purposes of this 
5 act and shall have an official seal as determined by the membership of the commission.  
6  Section 2. The commission shall be set up and sponsored by the city of Gloucester to 
7 investigate, advocate and recommend measures for the promotion, preservation and protection of 
8 the Gloucester fishing industry. 
9  SECTION 2. Said chapter 260 is hereby further amended by striking out section 3, as 
10 most recently amended by chapter 338 of the acts of 1984, and inserting in place thereof the 
11 following section:-  

2 of 2 12  Section 3. The commission shall consist of 7 members, 1 of whom shall be the mayor or 
13 a designee and 6 of whom shall be appointed by the mayor and confirmed by the city council for 
14 3-year terms. The mayor may appoint, subject to confirmation by the city council, 2 alternate 
15 members. The initial members of the commission shall serve staggered terms. The members of 
16 the commission shall be residents of the city of Gloucester and shall be knowledgeable of the 
17 fishing industry. The chair and vice chair shall be elected by a majority vote of the members 
18 present and voting thereon. 
19  SECTION 3. Said chapter 260 is hereby further amended by striking out section 3A, as 
20 most recently amended by section 2 of chapter 361 of the acts of 1980, and inserting in place 
21 thereof the following section:-  
22  Section 3A. The mayor may appoint an executive director who shall receive such 
23 compensation as determined by the mayor. 
24  SECTION 4. This act shall take effect upon its passage.
