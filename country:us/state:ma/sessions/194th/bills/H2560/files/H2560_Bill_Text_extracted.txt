An Act related to amending birth certificates.
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority
of the same, as follows:
1 Moves to amend MGL Chapter 46, Section 13 by adding the following section:
2 (l) Upon receipt of a certified copy of an order of a court of competent jurisdiction
3 changing the name of a person born in this state and upon request of such person or such
4 person’s parents, guardian, or legal representative, the commissioner or the registrar of vital
5 statistics of the town in which the vital event occurred shall amend the birth certificate to show
6 the new name by a method prescribed by the department.
