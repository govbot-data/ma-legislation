In the One Hundred and Ninety-Fourth General Court
(2025-2026)
_______________
An Act to authorize the town of Lexington to prohibit or restrict the application of second
generation anticoagulant rodenticides.
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority
of the same, as follows:
1 SECTION 1. Purpose
2 The purpose of this Act is to allow the Town of Lexington to prohibit or restrict the use
3 of Second-Generation anticoagulant rodenticides under a future bylaw approved by Town
4 Meeting.
5 SECTION 2.Notwithstanding Chapter 132B of the General Laws or any other general or
6 special law to the contrary, the Town of Lexington may, by bylaw, prohibit or restrict the
7 application of second-generation anticoagulant rodenticides within the Town of Lexington,
8 including the application of such pesticides by licensed commercial applicators as defined in 333
9 C.M.R. 10.00.
10 SECTION 3. This Act shall take effect upon passage.
