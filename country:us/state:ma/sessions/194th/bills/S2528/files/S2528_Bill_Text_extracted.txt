In the One Hundred and Ninety-Fourth General Court
(2025-2026)
_______________
An Act authorizing the town of Marblehead to amend chapter 37 of the acts of 2004: Parking
violations in the town of Marblehead.
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority
of the same, as follows:
1 SECTION 1. Notwithstanding Section 1 of Chapter 37 of the Acts of 2004 the Select
2 Board may establish fines related to parking violations during a snow emergency at no more than
3 $100 if paid within 21 days; no more than $105, if paid thereafter.
4 SECTION 2. this Act shall take effect upon its passage.
