By Mr. Rush, a petition (accompanied by bill, Senate, No. 2496) of Michael F. Rush for
legislation that the flag of the commonwealth shall be flown at half-staff honoring the passing of

The Commonwealth of Massachusetts

In the One Hundred and Ninety-Fourth General Court
(2025-2026)

An Act to recognize service to our Commonwealth and Nation.
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority
of the same, as follows:
1 Section 6A of Chapter 2 of the General Laws is hereby amended by inserting after (g),
2 the following:-
3 "(h) On the death of an actively serving service member from the U.S. Army, U.S. Air
4 Force, U.S. Navy, U.S. Marine Corp, U.S. Space Force, U.S. Coast Guard, the Reserve
5 Component, or the National Guard from the Commonwealth."
