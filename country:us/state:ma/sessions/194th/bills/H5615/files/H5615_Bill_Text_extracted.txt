An Act validating a conveyance of land and authorizing the recording of a confirmatory deed in 
the city of Marlborough. 
 
 Whereas, The deferred operation of this act would tend to defeat its purpose, which is to 
authorize forthwith the recording of a confirmatory deed by the commonwealth of a certain 
parcel of land in the city of Marlborough, therefore it is hereby declared to be an emergency law, 
necessary for the immediate preservation of the public convenience. 
 
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority 
of the same, as follows: 
 
1  SECTION 1. Section 1 of chapter 170 of the acts of 2024 is hereby amended by striking 
2 out subsection (b) and inserting in place thereof the following subsection:-  
3  (b)(1) The conveyance to be confirmed pursuant to subsection (a) relates to a parcel of 
4 land in the city of Marlborough, in Middlesex county, containing 1,000 square feet, more or less, 
5 as described in a deed on file with the division of capital asset management and maintenance 
6 from the commonwealth acting through its metropolitan district commission to Pierina Monti 
7 dated May 22, 1958. 
8  (2) Said parcel of land is bounded and described as follows:  

2 of 3 9  Beginning at a stone bound set at the northeasterly corner of the granted premises and in 
10 the boundary line between land of the grantee and land now or formerly of Lincoln H. and Ruth 
11 I. Breault, as shown on a plan entitled, “Commonwealth of Massachusetts, Metropolitan District 
12 Commission, Water Division, Land in Marlboro to be conveyed to Pierina Monti, February 
13 1958, Harold J. Toole, Director of Water Division and Chief Water Supply Engineer”. 
14  THENCE the line runs south 4º 25’ west by said land of the grantee 111 feet to a stone 
15 bound at land of the commonwealth;  
16  THENCE north 5º 35’ west by said land of the commonwealth of Massachusetts 104 feet 
17 to a point at land now or formerly of Lincoln H. and Ruth I Breault;  
18  THENCE northeasterly by a line curving to the right with a radius of 435 feet by said 
19 land of Lincoln H. and Ruth I. Breault 20 feet to the point of beginning.  
20  (3) Said parcel of land containing 1,000 square feet, more or less, and being shown on the 
21 plan pursuant to paragraph (2) of subsection (b). 
22  SECTION 2. Said chapter 170 is hereby further amended by adding the following 3 
23 sections:- 
24  SECTION 3. Notwithstanding the absence of a recorded deed or any defect in execution 
25 or delivery, the conveyance of the parcel of land, described in section 1, from the commonwealth 
26 to Pierina Monti, dated May 22, 1958, is hereby validated, confirmed and ratified and shall be 
27 deemed to have been validly made and effective as of May 22, 1958. Said conveyance shall have 
28 the same force and effect as if a deed had been properly executed, delivered and recorded at the 

3 of 3 29 time of the original transaction, notwithstanding any defects, irregularities or omissions in the 
30 original conveyance or the failure to record the same. 
31  SECTION 4. The Middlesex southern district registry of deeds is hereby authorized and 
32 directed to accept for recording the confirmatory deed authorized in section 1, together with any 
33 supporting documentation relating to the May 22, 1958 conveyance including a copy of the deed 
34 dated May 22, 1958 intending to convey the parcel of land described in subsection (b) of section 
35 1, notwithstanding any requirement of law, rule or regulation that would otherwise prevent such 
36 recording. Upon recording the confirmatory deed and supporting documentation, said 
37 confirmatory deed shall be deemed to relate back to and shall be effective as of May 22, 1958, 
38 and shall serve as conclusive evidence of the commonwealth’s conveyance of said parcel to 
39 Pierina Monti and her heirs, successors and assigns. 
40  SECTION 5. Upon the recording of the confirmatory deed authorized herein, the title to 
41 said parcel shall be deemed to have been vested in Pierina Monti as of May 22, 1958, and to 
42 have passed through any successive conveyances or other acts of succession to the current owner 
43 of record, free and clear of any claims by the commonwealth arising from the absence of a 
44 recorded deed for the 1958 conveyance. This act shall be conclusive evidence of the validity of 
45 said conveyance and shall cure any defects in the chain of title arising from the failure to record 
46 the original 1958 conveyance.
