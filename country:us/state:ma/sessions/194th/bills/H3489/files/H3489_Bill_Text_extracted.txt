An Act authorizing borrowing by cities and towns at zero percent from the Commonwealth for
green energy sources.
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority
of the same, as follows:
1 Chapter 44 of the General Laws is hereby amended by inserting after section 8C the
2 following section:-
3 Section 8D. A city or town may borrow from the commonwealth at zero per cents funds
4 for the expenses of studying or creating green energy sources of power within such city or town.
