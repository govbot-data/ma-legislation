An Act to protect victims of crimes and the public.
Whereas, The deferred operation of this act would tend to defeat its purpose, which is to
strengthen the protections for victims of crime and the public of statutes under which the judicial
system decides questions relating to the pre-trial release of dangerous persons, therefore it is
hereby declared to be an emergency law, necessary for the immediate preservation of the public
safety and convenience.

of the same, as follows:
