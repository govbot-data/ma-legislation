An Act providing prescription drug cost reimbursements to elderly governmental retirees.
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority
of the same, as follows:
1 Section 1. Section 2 of chapter 32A of the General Laws, as appearing in the 2012
2 Official Edition, is hereby amendment by inserting after the word "withheld", in line 126, the
3 following words:-; provided, however, that the term "elderly governmental retiree" shall include
4 any retired employee age 70 or over who has been retired for 5 years.
5 Section 2. Said chapter 32A, as so appearing, is hereby further amended by inserting after
6 section 10B the following section:-
7 Section 10C. For any elderly governmental retiree of any municipality that provides
8 prescription drug benefits, such retiree shall be entitled to reimbursement from the State Retirees
9 Benefits Trust Fund to be used exclusively to reduce the cost of prescription drugs for such
1 of 2

10 retiree. The reduction of the cost of such prescription drugs shall be no less than 25 per cent of
11 any co-payment amount paid by an elderly governmental retiree for such prescription drugs. The
12 trustee of the fund shall establish the reimbursement methodology for such payment in
13 conjunction with any participating municipality.
14 Section 3. Section 24 of said chapter 32A, as so appearing, is hereby amended by
15 inserting after the word "board", in line 29, the following words:-; provided that, a municipality
16 providing prescription drug coverage to an elderly governmental retiree shall participate in the
17 fund using the procedures set forth in section 10C
