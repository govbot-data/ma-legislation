SENATE . . . . . . . . . . . . . . No. 3142
Senate, June 23, 2026 -- Substituted as a new draft (Senator Creem) for the Senate Bill relative to
removing the term hearing impaired from the general laws (Senate, No. 2120).
The Commonwealth of Massachusetts
_______________
In the One Hundred and Ninety-Fourth General Court
(2025-2026)
_______________
An Act removing the term hearing impaired from the General Laws.
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority
of the same, as follows:
1 SECTION 1. Section 196 of chapter 6 of the General Laws, as appearing in the 2024
2 Official Edition, is hereby amended by striking out, in line 17, the words “hearing–impaired
3 individual” and inserting in place thereof the following words:- person who is deaf or hard of
4 hearing.
5 SECTION 2. Section 23 of chapter 32A of the General Laws, as so appearing, is hereby
6 amended by striking out, in line 17, the words “hearing impaired ear” and inserting in place
7 thereof the following words:- ear that is deaf or hard of hearing.
8 SECTION 3. Section 1 of chapter 129 of the General Laws, as so appearing, is hereby
9 amended by striking out, in line 20, the words “hearing impaired” and inserting in place thereof
10 the following words:- hard of hearing
11 SECTION 4. Section 4 of chapter 151B of the General laws, as most recently amended
12 by section 276 of chapter 102 of the acts of 2026, is hereby further amended by striking out, in
1 of 3

13 lines 296, 302 and 314, the words “hearing impaired” and inserting in place thereof, in each
14 instance, the following words:- deaf or hard of hearing or has any other disability.
15 SECTION 5. Said section 4 of said chapter 151B, as so amended, is hereby further
16 amended by striking out, in lines 306 and 307, 362 and 363 and 370, the words “hearing
17 impairment” and inserting in place thereof, in each instance, the following words:- because such
18 person is deaf or hard of hearing.
19 SECTION 6. Said section 4 of said chapter 151B, as so amended, is hereby further
20 amended by striking out, in lines 364, 372 and 382 and 383, the words “blindness or hearing
21 impairment” and inserting in place thereof, in each instance, the following words:- such person
22 being blind or deaf or hard of hearing.
23 SECTION 7. Said section 4 of said chapter 151B, as so amended, is hereby further
24 amended by striking out, in lines 380 and 381, the words “, hearing impairment”.
25 SECTION 8. Said section 4 of said chapter 151B, as so amended, is hereby further

27 hard of heard or because.
