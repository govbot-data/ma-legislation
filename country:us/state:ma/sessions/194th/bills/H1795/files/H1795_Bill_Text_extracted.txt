An Act relative to prejudgment interest rates.
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority
of the same, as follows:
1 SECTION 1. Section 6B of chapter 231 of the General Laws, as appearing in the 2022
2 Official Edition, is hereby amended by striking out, in lines 5 and 6, the words “at the rate of
3 twelve per cent per annum” and inserting in place thereof the following:- calculated at a weekly
4 average one-year constant maturity treasury yield, as published by the Board of Governors of the
5 Federal Reserve System, for the calendar week preceding date of the judgment.

7 2022 Official Edition, is hereby amended by striking out, in lines 4 and 5, the words “at the rate
8 of twelve per cent per annum” and inserting in place thereof the following:- calculated at a
9 weekly average one-year constant maturity treasury yield, as published by the Board of
10 Governors of the Federal Reserve System, for the calendar week preceding date of the judgment.
