An Act relative to the endowment match program.
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority
of the same, as follows:
1 SECTION 1. Section 15E of chapter 15A, as appearing in the 2022 Official Edition, is
2 hereby amended by inserting the following sentence at the end of the second paragraph:- A
3 community college shall be eligible to receive said match for any private donations received by
4 its foundation, regardless of whether said funds are endowed or contributed to a capital outlay
5 program.
6 SECTION 2. Said section 15E of said chapter 15A, as so appearing, is hereby further
7 amended by striking out, in line 21, the word “Private” and inserting in place thereof the
8 following words:- Except for the community colleges, private.
9 SECTION 3. Said section 15E of said chapter 15A, as so appearing, is hereby further
10 amended by adding the following paragraph:-
11 Not less than $30,000,000 shall be expended annually for the purposes of continuing the
12 implementation of this section to encourage private fundraising by the commonwealth’s public
13 institutions of higher education for the endowment and capital outlay programs of those
1 of 2

14 institutions; provided, that the board of higher education shall implement this program in a
15 manner which ensures that each institution shall have an opportunity to secure matching funds
16 from this item; provided further, that $10,000,000 shall be allocated to the university of
17 Massachusetts segment; provided further, that $10,000,000 shall be allocated to the state
18 university segment; provided further, that $10,000,000 shall be allocated to the community
19 college segment; provided further, that if any funds allocated herein for disbursement to the
20 community college segment shall be unused, the remaining funds shall be made available to the
21 state university segment; provided further, that if any funds allocated herein for disbursement to
22 the state university segment shall be unused, the remaining funds shall be made available to the
23 university of Massachusetts segment.
24 SECTION 4. Sections 1 and 2 shall take effect immediately upon passage.
