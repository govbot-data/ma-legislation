An Act relative to compulsory full-day kindergarten.
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority
of the same, as follows:
1 SECTION 1. Section 1B of chapter 69 of the General Laws, as appearing in the 2022
2 Official Edition, is hereby amended by striking out, in lines 117-120, inclusive, the twenty-first
3 paragraph and inserting in place thereof the following paragraph:
4 The board shall establish the permissible and mandatory ages for school attendance
5 provided that each child be required to attend a full-day educational program at kindergarten age.
6 SECTION 2. Section 1 of chapter 76 of the General Laws, as so appearing, is hereby
7 amended by inserting after the word “education”, in line 2, the following words:
8 “and any child enrolled in full-day kindergarten”
