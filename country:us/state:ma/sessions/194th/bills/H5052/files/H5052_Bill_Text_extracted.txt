An Act relative to town meeting accessibility.
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority
of the same, as follows:
1 Section 12 of Chapter 686 of the Acts of 1970 is hereby amended by adding the
2 following new paragraph -
3 (h) The Town Meeting shall be accessible by those Town Meeting Members who wish to
4 attend, either in person or remotely via a video conferencing platform, with a maximum of
5 fifteen (15) members attending remotely. This communications platform will allow users to
6 connect using an internet connection and a supported device. Members of these meetings shall be
7 able to be recognized by the Moderator or Chair, and their vote, where applicable, be recorded
8 via the electronic communications platform.
