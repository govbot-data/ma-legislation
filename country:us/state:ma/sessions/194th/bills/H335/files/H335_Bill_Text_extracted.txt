By Representatives Chan of Quincy and Uyterhoeven of Somerville, a petition (accompanied by
bill, House, No. 335) of Tackey Chan, Erika Uyterhoeven and others for legislation to require
that real estate brokers fees be paid by lessors. Consumer Protection and Professional Licensure.
The Commonwealth of Massachusetts
_______________
In the One Hundred and Ninety-Fourth General Court
(2025-2026)
_______________
An Act to promote equity among lessors.
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority
of the same, as follows:
1 SECTION 1. Section 87DDD1/2 of chapter 112 of the General Laws, as appearing in the
2 2022 Official Edition, is hereby amended by adding the following sentence: - Any fee shall only
3 be paid by the party, lessor or tenant who originally engaged and entered into a contract with the
4 licensed broker or salesman.
