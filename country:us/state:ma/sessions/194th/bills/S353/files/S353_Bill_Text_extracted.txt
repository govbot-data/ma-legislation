An Act to prevent discrimination in interscholastic athletic and extracurricular activities.
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority
of the same, as follows:
1 Chapter 71B of the General Laws, as appearing in the 2022 Official Edition, is hereby
2 amended by inserting after section 17 the following section:-
3 Section 18. Notwithstanding any general or special law to the contrary, any school age
4 child with a disability shall not be precluded from participating in any school-sponsored
5 interscholastic athletic or extracurricular activity.
