HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES, March 9, 2026.
The committee on Public Service, to whom were referred so much of
the recommendations of the Department of the State Treasurer (House, No.
35) as relates to the SMART Plan (House, No. 39); so much of the
recommendations of the Department of the State Treasurer (House, No. 35)
as relates to expanding access to retirement savings (House, No. 41); so
much of the recommendations of the Department of the State Treasurer
(House, No. 35) as relates to the Massachusetts State Employees Retirement
System (House, No. 42); and so much of the recommendations of the
Department of the State Treasurer (House, No. 35) as relates to requiring
public pension fund divestment from ammunition and firearms (House, No.
49), reports recommending that the accompanying order (House, No. 5193)
ought to be adopted.
For the committee,
DANIEL J. RYAN.
