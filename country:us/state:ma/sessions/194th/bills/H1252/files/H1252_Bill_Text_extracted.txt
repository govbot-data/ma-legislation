An Act relative to gender identity in insurance. 
 
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority 
of the same, as follows: 
 
1  SECTION 1.  Section 1 of chapter 175 of the General Laws, as appearing in the 2020 
2 Official Edition, is hereby amended by inserting after the definition for 'Foreign company'', the 
3 following definition:- 
4  “Gender identity” shall mean a person's gender-related identity, appearance or behavior, 
5 whether or not that gender-related identity, appearance or behavior is different from that 
6 traditionally associated with the person's physiology or assigned sex at birth. Gender-related 
7 identity may be shown by providing evidence including, but not limited to, medical history, care 
8 or treatment of the gender-related identity, consistent and uniform assertion of the gender-related 
9 identity or any other evidence that the gender-related identity is sincerely held as part of a 
10 person's core identity; provided, however, that gender-related identity shall not be asserted for 
11 any improper purpose.
