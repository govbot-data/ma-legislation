An Act limiting motor vehicle dealer documentation fees. 
 
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority 
of the same, as follows: 
 
1  Chapter 90 of the General Laws, as appearing in the 2022 Official Edition, is hereby 
2 amended by inserting after Section 62 the following section:- 
3  Section 63 (A). A motor vehicle dealer licensed by the Registry of Motor Vehicles may 
4 impose a fee of no more than four hundred dollars ($400.00) for the preparation of various 
5 paperwork associated with the sale, financing, leasing, insurance, liens, warranties, federal and 
6 state disclosures and other procedures associated with the sale, leasing and financing of vehicles 
7 obtained or provided by the dealership on vehicle transactions in excess of two thousand five 
8 hundred dollars ($2500.00). Any motor vehicle dealer who charges a documentary preparation 
9 fee in connection with the sale of a motor vehicle for less than two thousand five hundred dollars 

2 of 2 10 ($2500.00) shall be subject to the imposition of a fine and/or the suspension or revocation of 
11 their license, determined by the Registry of Motor Vehicles.
