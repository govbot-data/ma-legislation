An Act further regulating procurements by the city of Cambridge. 
 
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority 
of the same, as follows: 
 
1  SECTION 1. Notwithstanding chapter 30B of the General Laws or any other general or 
2 special law to the contrary, the City of Cambridge (“Cambridge”) may, for the procurement of a 
3 supply or service in the amount of $20,000 or less, use sound business practices (as defined in 
4 M.G.L. c. 30B, § 2) so long as the business awarded the contract is a disadvantaged businesses, 
5 diverse businesses, or small and local business enterprises, as certified by the supplier diversity 
6 office, by the City of Boston (“Boston”), by Cambridge by virtue of its compact (which may be 
7 amended from time to time) with Boston, or by virtue of certification by Cambridge, customarily 
8 providing the supply or service. Cambridge shall meet the requirements of section 3 of chapter 
9 30B, for those instances where contracts result from a purchase of $10,000 or more. 
10  SECTION 2. Notwithstanding chapter 30B of the General Laws or any other general or 
11 special law to the contrary, the procurement officer of Cambridge may, for the procurement of a 
12 supply or service in· the amount of $20,000 or greater, but not more than the small procurement 

2 of 2 13 threshold as defined by the Commonwealth of Massachusetts Operational Services Division, 
14 seek written quotations through a procurement officer from not fewer than three (3) 
15 disadvantaged businesses, diverse businesses or small and local business enterprises, as certified 
16 by the supplier diversity office, by Boston, by Cambridge by virtue of its compact (which may be 
17 amended from time to time) with Boston, or by virtue of certification by Cambridge, customarily 
18 providing the supply or service. The procurement officer shall record: (i) the names and 
19 addresses of all persons from whom quotations were sought; (ii) the specifications used for the 
20 procurement; (iii) the names of the persons submitting quotations; and 
21  (iv) the date and amount of each quotation. The recorded information shall be retained in 
22 the file required pursuant to section 3 of said chapter 30B. The procurement officer shall award 
23 the contract to the eligible and responsible bidder offering the needed quality of supply or service 
24 at the lowest quotation. After awarding a contract under this section, Cambridge shall publish 
25 notice of such award on Cambridge's website or other appropriate notification platform. 
26  SECTION 3. The provisions of this act are severable and if any provision, or portion 
27 thereof, should be held to be unconstitutional or otherwise invalid by any court of competent 
28 jurisdiction, such unconstitutionality or invalidity shall not affect the remaining provisions, 
29 which remain in full force and effect. 
30  SECTION 4. This act shall take effect upon its passage.
