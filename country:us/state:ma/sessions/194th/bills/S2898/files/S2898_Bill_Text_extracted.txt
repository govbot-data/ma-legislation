An Act improving campaign finance reporting by state ballot question committees.
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority
of the same, as follows:
1 SECTION 1. Clause (4) of subsection (a) of section 18 of chapter 55 of the General
2 Laws, as appearing in the 2024 Official Edition, is hereby amended by striking out, in lines 60
3 to 64, inclusive, the words “, independent expenditure PACs organized pursuant to section 18A
4 and political committees organized under the provisions of section 5 to favor or oppose a
5 question submitted to the voters, if the question appears on the ballot at the state election” and
6 inserting in place thereof the following words:- and independent expenditure PACs organized
7 pursuant to section 18A.
8 SECTION 2. Said subsection (a) said section 18 of said chapter 55, as so appearing, is
9 hereby further amended by striking out clause (5).
10 SECTION 3. Subsection (a) of section 19 of said chapter 55, as so appearing, is hereby
11 amended by inserting after the word “candidates”, in line 7, the following words:- , the treasurer
12 of each statewide ballot question committee,.
1 of 2

13 SECTION 4. Paragraph (2) of subsection (b) of said section 19 of said chapter 55, as so
14 appearing, is hereby amended by adding the following sentence:;- In addition, the treasurer of a
15 statewide ballot question committee shall also include the following information: (i) a list of all
16 in-kind contributions of more than $50 received as of the last day of the preceding month and
17 since the last statement, including an alphabetical list of names and addresses of each person
18 making such in-kind contribution, the date received, the type of in-kind contribution and the
19 value of the in-kind contribution; and (ii) a list of new liabilities incurred as of the last day of the
20 preceding month, including the name and address of the person to whom the liability exists,
21 together with a clear statement of purpose for which the liability was incurred.
22 SECTION 5. Section 22 of said chapter 55, as so appearing, is hereby amended by
23 striking out the third paragraph and inserting in place thereof the following paragraph:-
24 If the question is a statewide ballot question, the report shall be filed with the director as
25 follows: (1) by the fifth day of each month covering the preceding month until the sixtieth day
26 before the election; (2) on or before the fifth and twentieth day of each month, complete as of the
27 preceding first and fifteenth day of the month, from the sixtieth day before the election until the
28 election; (3) November 20 following the election, complete as of the fifteenth day of the month;
29 and (4) January 20 of each year, complete as of December 31 of the prior year, until all declared
30 liabilities of that person or corporation, association, organization or other group of persons have
31 been discharged.
