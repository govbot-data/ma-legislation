An Act to ensure equitable representation of required parent advisory councils to school
committees.
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority
of the same, as follows:
1 Notwithstanding any general or special law, rule or regulation to the contrary, each
2 school district in Massachusetts, being required by Massachusetts Law, following the criteria
3 specifically outlined in and Chapter 71A and regulation 603 CMR 14.09 (1-5), and Chapter 71B,
4 and regulations 603 CMR 28.07(4) and 603 CMR 28.03 (1)(a)(4), respectively, to support
5 English Language Learner Parent Advisory Councils and Special Education Parent Advisory
6 Councils, shall provide an ex-officio, nonvoting seat on the school committee to representatives
7 from the school district’s ELLPAC and SEPAC, without the right to attend executive sessions
8 unless such right is expressly granted by the district's school committee.
1 of 2

9 The ELLPAC and SEPAC shall elect one representative each from their membership
10 annually, and in accordance with their bylaws, and shall notify the school committee of their
11 selection within 30 days of election. Said ELLPAC and SEPAC representatives shall be subject
12 to all school committee rules and regulations and shall serve without compensation.
2 of 2
