This report describes grant activities related to funds allocated from Chapter 9 of the Acts of 202 5, line 
item 7061 -9650 , which are being coordinated with those related to line item 7061 -0028  Social Emotional 
Learning and Mental Health Grant. The combined grant aims to build the capacity of schools  and districts 
(including charter schools and educational collaboratives ) to do one or more of the following:  
• Pilot universal mental health screenings for students in kindergarten to grade 12;  
• Develop comprehensive integrated multi -tiered systems for student, family, and educator social -
emotional and or/mental health supports; and  
• Build sustainable infrastructure to facilitate integrated coordination between the school and 
community -based and/or providers.

I am pleased to submit this report to the Executive Office of Administration and 
Finance and to the House and Senate Committees on Ways and Means , Supporting 
Healthy Alliances Reinforcing Education (SHARE) , pursuant to Chapter 9 of the Acts 
of 202 5, line item 7061 -9650 , signed into law July 4, 202 5, that reads in part:  
 
“…provided further, that such grants may also be expended to support increased professional 
development opportunities for public school employees to identify students in need of mental 
and behavioral health support; provided further, that not later than Dece mber 30, 2025, the

further, that the report shall be provided to the senate president, the speaker of the house of 
representatives, the house and senate com mittees on ways and means and the joint committee 
on education …” 
 
This line item helps advance the Department of Elementary and Secondary Education’s 
(Department) Educational Vision , and DESE's  Whole Student strategic objective  in 
particular.  Through this strategic objective, DESE partners with districts, schools, and 
programs to cultivate systems to support the whole student and foster joyful, healthy, 
and supportive learning environments so that all students feel valued, connected, 
nourished , and ready to learn.  Focus areas in this objective include  implement ing multi -
tiered systems of support (MTSS) that help all students progress academically, with 
their social emotional and behavioral development, in their physical and mental health 
and wellbeing, and with a strong sense of belonging and connection.  This funding  is 
critical for schools  and districts  (including  charter schools and educational 
collaboratives ) as they build comprehensive systems of integrated student support  to 
create spaces that  allow students to engage in learning and to design environments that 
are welcoming . The  supports  and resources  implemented are particularly important as 
schools  and districts  (including charter schools and  educational collaboratives ) continue 
to see students ’ social and emotional, behavioral, and mental health , and educator  
health and wellness concerns.    
 
Additionally, t he efforts funded through this line item , and supplemented  and 
coordinated with funds through the Social Emotional Learning Grants line item  7061 -
0028, help further the ability of communities across the Commonwealth to maximize  
coordination with service providers  and community health agencies  to establish more 
comprehensive continuums of care. These funds help to increase student access to 
behavioral and mental health services, including grant -funded services, delivered in 
partnership with community -based providers .

 Introduction  
 
The Department of Elementary and Secondary Education (Department) respectfully 
submits this Report to the Legislature: Supporting Healthy Alliances Reinforcing 
Education (SHARE) Fiscal Year 2026 , pursuant to Chapter 9 of the Acts of 2025, line 
item 7061 -9650 , signed into law July 4, 2025:  
 
“For the Supporting Healthy Alliances Reinforcing Education (SHARE) grant program to 
provide an integrated student wellness grant program to assist schools with addressing 
nonacademic barriers to student success; provided, that grants shall be used to supp ort 
school districts establishing an infrastructure to facilitate integrated coordination of school 
and community -based resources including, but not limited to, social services, mental health 
and behavioral health resources; provided further, that not less  than $500,000 shall be 
awarded by the department to schools and school districts serving high percentages of low -
income students; provided further, that said supports may include funding to assist public 
school districts in contracting with licensed commu nity-based health care service providers, 
including mental and behavioral health providers; provided further, that said program shall be 
administered by the department of elementary and secondary education in coordination with 
the executive office of healt h and human services; provided further, that the department shall 
prioritize applications for such services that are submitted by school districts whose 
applications are consistent with infrastructure and coordination efforts linking schools to 
community -based resources in accordance with item 7061 -9612 of section 2 of chapter 154 
of the acts of 2018; provided further, that such support grants may be expended to assist 
school districts in connecting students with community -based services to maximize 
coordin ation with service providers and establish more comprehensive continuums of care; 
provided further, that such grants may also be expended to support increased professional 
development opportunities for public school employees to identify students in need o f mental 
and behavioral health support; provided further, that not later than December 30, 2025, the 
department shall issue a report outlining all student support efforts funded by this item; 
provided further, that the report shall be provided to the senat e president, the speaker of the 
house of representatives, the house and senate committees on ways and means and the 
joint committee on education; provided further, that for the purposes of this item, 
appropriated funds may be expended for programs or activ ities during the summer months; 
provided further, that any unexpended funds in this item shall not revert but shall be made 
available for the purposes of this item until August 28, 2026; and provided further, that 
notwithstanding any general or special law  to the contrary, any funds distributed from this 
item to a city, town or regional school district shall be deposited with the treasurer of such 
city, town or regional school district and held in a separate account and shall be expended by 
the school commi ttee of such city, town or regional school district without further 
appropriation, notwithstanding any general or special law to the contrary”  
 
This report outlines efforts to date to establish grants with public school districts  
(including charter schools and  educational collaboratives ), and contrac ts with vendors  to 
provide professional development pursuant to this line item.  
 
The Department continues to work collaboratively with the Executive Office of Health 
and Human Services (EOHHS), including the Departments of Public Health and Mental 

 
2 Health (DPH and DMH), to leverage existing and planned initiatives and to promote 
complementary and supplemental activities.  
 
The Supporting Students’ Social Emotional Learning, Behavioral and Mental Health, 
and Wellness through Multi -Tiered Systems of Support  (SEL  and Mental Health) 
continuation grant was  awarded  in September  2025 and provides opportunities for 
educators to participate in professional development (PD), technical assistance , and 
coaching to help identify students in need of services and supports. It also allows 
grantees to partner with community agencies and vendors to bring more resources into 
schools  and districts  (including charter schools and  educational collaboratives ). This 
grant funding has also created opportunities for grantees to learn and network with each 
other around topics related to mental and behavioral  health , SEL , and general health 
and wellness to support the whole student. More details about  the  2026 professional 
learning support s provided have been shared with grantees  and are available via 
DESE’s  Mental and Behavioral Health and Wellness Supports web page . 
 
Note that details related to the screening pilot component of the FY2025 grant program, 
required by line item 7061 -9650, are included in the Fiscal Year 2025 Social Emotional 
Learning Grants legislative report  (due September 3, 2025) . Similarly, details regarding 
the screening pilot component of the FY2026 grant programs will be included in the 
Fiscal Year 2026 Social Emotional Learning Grants legislative report  (due September 1, 
2026) . 
Selection of Participating Districts  
In September 2025, the Department awarded  the fiscal year  (FY) 2026 Fund Code ( FC) 
0311 continu ation  grant  (see Appendix for a list of grantees) . Fifty-eight  (58) ou t of the 
sixty (60)  eligible  grantees (those awarded FY 2025 competitive grants ) reapplied for 
continuation grant s to support activities to increase student access to behavioral and 
mental health services  and build comprehensive, multi -tiered systems of support . Two 
districts opted out of the grant , stating decreased staff capacity to support continu ing. 
The $500,000 from this  line item  is supplemented with line item 7061 -0028  to provide a 
total of approximately $3.5 million to support grantees , representing a significant 
decrease (nearly half) from FY  2025 competitive awards . One hundred percent of all 
grantees supported with line item 7061 -9650  serve a higher percentage of high needs 
students than the state average , as do  more than 60 percent of all grantees .
