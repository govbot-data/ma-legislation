An Act establishing a sick leave bank for Edward Suber, an employee of the department of youth
services.
Whereas, The deferred operation of this act would tend to defeat its purposes, which are
to establish forthwith a sick leave bank for a certain employee of the Massachusetts Department
of Youth Services, therefore it is hereby declared to be an emergency law, necessary for the
immediate preservation of the public convenience.
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority
of the same, as follows:
1 Notwithstanding any general or special law to the contrary, the Massachusetts
2 Department of Youth Services shall establish a sick leave bank for Edward Suber, an employee
3 of the Department. Any employee of the Department of Youth Services may voluntarily
4 contribute one or more sick, personal, or vacation days to the sick leave bank for use by Edward
5 Suber. If Edward Suber terminates employment with the Department or requests to dissolve the
6 sick leave bank, any remaining time in the sick leave bank shall be transferred to the Department
7 of Youth Service paid leave bank. Sick leave bank days shall not be used for absences unrelated
8 to the illness or disability that necessitated the establishment of the sick leave bank as determined
9 by the Department.
