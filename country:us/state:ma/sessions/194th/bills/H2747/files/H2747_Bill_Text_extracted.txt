An Act relative to the definition of Group 4 of the state retirement system.
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority
of the same, as follows:
1 The definition of Group 4, as set forth in Chapter 32, Section 3, paragraph (2),
2 subparagraph (g) is hereby amended by adding thereto the words:— the secretary and
3 undersecretaries of public safety, provided they had previously served in a Group 3 or 4
4 designated position for no less than 24 consecutive months.
