The Commonwealth of Massachusetts

HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES, May 11, 2026.
The committee on Election Laws, to whom were referred the petition
(accompanied by bill, House, No. 4262) of Samantha Montaño (with the
approval of the mayor and city council) relative to ranked choice voting in
the city of Boston; and the petition (accompanied by bill, House, No.
4916) of Kenneth I. Gordon (by vote of the town) for legislation to
authorize ranked choice voting in the town of Bedford, reports (under the
provisions of House Rule 27) recommending that the accompanying order
(House, No. 5413) ought to be adopted.
For the committee,
DANIEL J. HUNT.

HOUSE . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . No. 5413
The Commonwealth of Massachusetts
House of Representatives, May 11, 2026.
