An Act relative to healthcare proxies.
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority
of the same, as follows:
1 Section 2 of Chapter 201D of the General Laws, as appearing in the 2020 Official
2 Edition, is hereby amended by adding after the second paragraph the following:-
3 “In the event that a previously designated healthcare agent, healthcare proxy, or alternate
4 is not available, willing, or competent to make decisions, the attending physician may designate
5 another willing physician to make health care treatment decisions as a patient's proxy decision-
6 maker if:
7 (A) The attending physician has a made a reasonable effort to contact the patient’s proxy
8 decision-maker;
9 (B) The attending physician has obtained an independent determination of the patient's
10 lack of decisional capacity by another physician; by an advanced practice nurse who has
1 of 2

11 collaborated about the patient with a licensed physician either in person, by telephone, or
12 electronically; or by a court;
13 (C) The attending physician or his or her designee has consulted with and obtained a
14 consensus on the proxy designation with the medical ethics committee of the health care facility
15 where the patient is receiving care; and
16 (D) The identity of the physician designated as proxy decision-maker is documented in
17 the medical record.
18 Nothing in this section should be construed as authorizing physician assisted suicide.”
