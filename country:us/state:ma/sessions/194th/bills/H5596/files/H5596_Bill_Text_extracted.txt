HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES, July 23, 2026.
The committee on Revenue, to whom were referred the petition
(accompanied by bill, Senate, No. 1944) of Thomas Egan and Sean Reid
for legislation to exempt private pension income from taxation; the
petition (accompanied by bill, Senate, No. 1945) of John J. Cronin for
legislation to impose an excise tax on oral nicotine products; the petition

Senate, No. 1959) of Sal N. DiDomenico for legislation relative to the
