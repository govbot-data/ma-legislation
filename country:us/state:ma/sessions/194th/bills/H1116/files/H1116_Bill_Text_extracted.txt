An Act relative to sales tax reimbursement.
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority
of the same, as follows:
1 Chapter 175 of the General Laws is hereby amended by inserting after section 113O the
2 following section:-
3 Section 113O½. For purposes of this section, an insurance payment received for a motor
4 vehicle that has been stolen and not recovered shall be treated as a trade-in credit when applied
5 toward the purchase of a replacement motor vehicle; provided, that: (i) the stolen vehicle has
6 been reported to law enforcement and remains unrecovered by the time of the replacement
7 vehicle purchase; and (ii) the insurance payment is applied toward the purchase of the
8 replacement vehicle. The insured shall provide to the seller of the replacement motor vehicle
9 documentation from the insurance company indicating the amount of the insurance payment and
10 confirmation that the payment was for a stolen and unrecovered vehicle, which shall be sufficient
11 documentation for the purposes of this section and for the purposes of any excise tax. The sales
12 price of the replacement vehicle subject to tax shall be the difference between the total sales
