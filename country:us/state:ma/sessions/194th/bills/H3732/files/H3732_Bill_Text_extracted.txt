1 SECTION 1. Section 1 of Mass General Laws Chapter 159B is amended by adding the
2 following definitions:
3 “Third Party Delivery Provider”, a delivery service on which a customer can view items
4 for sale and place an online order for the delivery or same-day pick-up of food or eligible retail
5 goods, operated by a party that does not own the food service establishment.
6 “Third Party Delivery Driver”, any person contracted by a third party delivery provider,
7 for compensation, to deliver goods over irregular routes, to and from various points within the
8 commonwealth, not based upon a predetermined schedule, and from different establishments.
9 “Third Party Delivery Identification Number” - a unique identification number assigned
10 by the division to each third-party delivery driver that is physically located on the driver’s
11 vehicle, in public view. The third party delivery identification number shall be used for the
12 purposes of driver identification, data reporting and enforcement of fines on the third-party

13 delivery provider that the driver was completing at the time of the violation by municipal
14 authorities and the division.
15 “Delivery Order” - the process by which a customer places an order for food or other
16 items through a service provider, which coordinates for the food or other items to be picked up
17 from a restaurant or store by a third party delivery driver who delivers the order to the
18 customer’s specified address.

102 SECTION 3. Said chapter 159B is hereby further amended by adding the following
103 section:-
104 Section 24. (a) When a third party delivery driver is approved by a third party delivery
105 provider to be contracted for the delivery of goods, the third party delivery provider shall register
106 this approval with the division. The division will generate a unique third party delivery
107 identification number associated with each third party delivery driver who is contracting with
108 third party delivery providers and compile the third party delivery identification numbers in an
109 identification number registry. The format of the registry shall be determined by the division.
110 The third party identification number shall be assigned to each individual third party delivery
111 driver, this number shall be unique to that driver. If applicable, the third party identification
112 number shall be consistent across each third party delivery provider with whom the third party
113 delivery driver is in contract. The division will compile a list of each third party delivery
114 identification number in their identification number registry. Each third party delivery
115 identification number shall be:
116 (i) (A) unique so that no third party delivery identification number is duplicated within
117 the division’s identification number registry; (B) associated with an individual third party
118 delivery driver contracted to deliver goods for third party delivery providers; and (C) located on
119 the third party delivery driver’s vehicle in a place which is observable to the public whenever an
120 individual third party delivery driver delivery order request.
