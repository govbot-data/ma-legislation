Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority
of the same, as follows:
1 SECTION 1. Subsection (b) of section 37O of chapter 71 of the General Laws, as
2 appearing in the 2020 Official Edition, is hereby amended by striking out the second paragraph
3 and inserting in place thereof the following paragraph:-
4 Retaliation against a person who: (i) reports any instance of bullying, (ii) provides
5 information during an investigation of bullying, (iii) witnesses or has reliable information about
6 bullying or (iv) provides bullying incident data in a quarterly report to the department under
7 subsection (k) shall be prohibited.

50 (o) The department shall establish an anti-bullying officer pilot program to help schools
51 experiencing significant bullying incidents. The program shall assess bullying in schools based
52 on the quarterly reporting of bullying incident data required under subsection (k) and assign anti-
53 bullying officers to the 5 schools experiencing the most incidents of bullying, based on the
54 quarterly reporting, to help with the schools significant bullying problems. Anti-bullying officers
55 duties at the school they are assigned to shall include, but not be limited to: responding,
56 investigating and reporting incidents of bullying at the school.
