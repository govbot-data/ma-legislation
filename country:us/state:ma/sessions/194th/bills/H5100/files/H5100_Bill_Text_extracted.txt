HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES, February 11, 2026.
The committee on Veterans and Federal Affairs, to whom was referred
the petition (accompanied by bill, House, No. 3834) of Mark J. Cusack
relative to voluntary contribution check-off boxes on municipal tax bills for
support of local veterans’ agents, reports recommending that the
accompanying order (House, No. 5100) ought to be adopted.
For the committee,
JOSEPH W. MCGONAGLE, JR.

HOUSE . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . No. 5100
The Commonwealth of Massachusetts
House of Representatives, February 11, 2026.
1 Ordered, That the committee on Veterans and Federal Affairs be authorized to sit during
2 a recess of the General Court to make an investigation and study of House document numbered
3 3834, relative to voluntary contribution check-off boxes on municipal tax bills for support of
4 local veterans’ agents.
5 Said committee shall report to the General Court the results of its investigation and study
6 and its recommendations, if any, together with drafts of legislation necessary to carry such
7 recommendations into effect, by filing the same with the Clerk of the House of Representatives
8 on or before December 31, 2026.
