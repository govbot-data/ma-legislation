2  SECTION XXXX. Chapter 15D of the general laws, as appearing in the 2024 official 
3 edition, is hereby amended by inserting after section 13 the following section:- 
4  Section 13A. (a) The department of early education and care, in consultation with the 
5 department of elementary and secondary education, shall develop and administer the High 
6 Quality Pre-Kindergarten Expansion grant program to support the phased development of high 
7 quality, district-wide, full-day pre-kindergarten programs for children between the ages of 2 
8 years and 9 months and the age a child becomes eligible for kindergarten in the school district 
9 where he or she resides. The grants shall be used to bring to scale a high quality, mixed delivery 
10 early education system in order to reduce the achievement gap and improve third grade reading 
11 scores. 
12  (b) The departments shall establish requirements for grant applicants which shall include, 
13 but not be limited to the following: (1) a tracking system for preschool students in the program 
14 using the state student identifier system; (2) a child to instructor ratio of no more than 10 to 1; (3) 
15 tactics to engage families in the enrollment process and implementation of the plan required 

2 of 5 16 under subsection (c), and methods to ensure a high level of enrollment by families with 3 and 4 
17 year olds; (4) teacher qualification and evaluation system requirements, and on-going 
18 professional development requirements; (5) a full school-day program ; (6) a class size of no 
19 more than 20 preschool students;  (7) inclusion of children with disabilities; (8) instructional staff 
20 salaries and benefits comparable to the corresponding district salaries and benefits for 
21 kindergarten to high school staff; (9) developmentally and linguistically appropriate instruction; 
22 (10) evidenced based curricula; (11) a learning environment aligned with the state early learning 
23 and development standards; (12) on-site access to comprehensive services for children, or 
24 convenient access to such services off site; (13) partnership with, and promotion of, community 
25 service providers for families to support children’s learning and development; (14) evidenced 
26 based health and safety standards;  (15) a timetable for phasing-in of facilities, staff 
27 development, and other systems to achieve district-wide implementation; (16) program 
28 evaluation and data collection systems to ensure continuous improvement; (17) a description of 
29 the physical structures for the education of the students; (18) a plan to improve or sustain, as 
30 appropriate, the development of literacy skills from preschool through third grade. 
31  Among proposals satisfying the requirements of this subsection, priority in grant awards 
32 shall be given first to districts that have received grants through the department’s commonwealth 
33 preschool partnership or the federal preschool expansion grants initiative. The department shall 
34 also develop and publish a metric for prioritizing the awarding of grants to qualified applicants 
35 based on: their accountability level as determined by the department of elementary and 
36 secondary education; their third grade reading proficiency scores; and their percentage of 
37 students qualifying as economically disadvantaged. Nothing herein shall require the department 

3 of 5 38 to award a grant to a district, regardless of priority status, that it has determined does not meet 
39 one or more of the requirements of this subsection. 
40  (c) Each recipient of the grant shall establish a plan to implement the requirements set 
41 forth in subsection (b) and any other requirements as may be prescribed by the board of early 
42 education and care. Each superintendent shall appoint a local governing council to implement 
43 and oversee all aspects of the plan; provided however, that the superintendent may utilize a pre-
44 existing council for this purpose. Districts shall provide on a quarterly basis reports to the 
45 department of early education and care on all aspects of the plan. The district and the local 
46 council shall implement the plan as approved within the period of time approved by the 
47 department.  Failure to satisfactorily implement the plan within the approved period of time shall 
48 result in the termination of the plan and reversion of the grant funds to the department.  
49  (d) The board of early education and care shall promulgate regulations to implement the 
50 provisions of this act within six months of the effective date including guidelines for membership 
51 to local governing councils in each recipient district and evaluation criteria required in the 
52 quarterly reports. A grant awarded pursuant to this subsection shall require that recipients 
53 undertake ongoing evaluations of the implementation of the plan and all its aspects. 
54  The department of early education and care shall, subject to appropriation, establish a 
55 dedicated team to provide support, technical assistance, and oversight of programs established 
56 under this section. The department shall annually evaluate the effectiveness of programs 
57 established under this section, both for feedback and accountability for grant recipients and to 
58 inform the replication of such programs throughout the commonwealth. 

4 of 5 59  SECTION XXXX. Chapter 70 of the general laws, as appearing in the 2024 official 
60 edition, is hereby amended by adding after Section 2 the following section:- 
61  “Section 2A. (a) As used in this section, the following words shall, unless the context 
62 clearly requires otherwise, have the following meanings:- 
63  “Boards”, the board of elementary and secondary education and the board of early 
64 education and care. 
65  “Departments”, the department of elementary and secondary education and the 
66 department of early education and care.  
67  “Early education”, full-day education and care programming for pre-school and 
68 kindergarten-aged students. 
69  (b) School districts in the Commonwealth may, by December 31, 2027, submit to the 
70 departments a plan to provide for ongoing access to age-appropriate early education for all 
71 students residing within the district. Such a plan shall include, where relevant and feasible, the 
72 use, expansion, and quality improvement of any previously existing public and private early 
73 education delivery systems, including those programs developed and implemented pursuant to 
74 section 13A of chapter 15D. No plan submitted to the departments pursuant to this section shall 
75 include user fees for attendance. 
76  The Departments shall promulgate joint regulations to provide for the process of 
77 submission, review, and approval of such plans. The departments shall also promulgate joint 
78 regulations to provide for monitoring of quality and fidelity of implementation of a district’s plan 
79 and a process of probationary status and potential revocation of approval in the event of poor 

5 of 5 80 implementation. The departments shall provide notice of said regulations to the clerks of the 
81 House and Senate, the chairs of the joint committee on education, and the chairs of the joint 
82 committee on ways and means, at least 90 days before approval by the boards. 
83  Districts who do not submit such a plan by the aforementioned date may submit a plan 
84 after said date. However, any such district may be prioritized after districts whose plans were 
85 received by said date. 
86  (c) For all districts whose plan has been approved by the department, the following 
87 definition shall replace the corresponding definition in section 2 of this chapter, while all non-
88 corresponding definitions in said section 2 shall continue to apply:- 
89  “Pre-school enrollment”, the number of students enrolled in pre-school programs; 
90 provided, however, that in any district in which pre-school students funded by the district attend 
91 school for a full day, the foundation pre-school enrollment used to calculate the foundation 
92 enrollment, staff, and budget amounts described in this section shall be two times the number 
93 that would be otherwise used for said calculations. 
94  SECTION 3. Section 2 of said chapter 70, as so appearing, is hereby further amended by 
95 inserting after the word “students”, in line 425, the following words:- “, to the extent that 
96 kindergarten and pre-school students in a district are attending a half-day program. If a district is 
97 operating approved full-day pre-school or kindergarten programs, students in such full-day 
98 programs shall be counted at one hundred percent of the preceding year’s actual number of low-
99 income kindergarten and pre-school students.”
