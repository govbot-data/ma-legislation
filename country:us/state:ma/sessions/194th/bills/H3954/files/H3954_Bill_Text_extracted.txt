An Act relative to xylitol labeling.
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority
of the same, as follows:
1 The fourth paragraph of section 187 of chapter 94 of the General Laws, as appearing in
2 the 2022 Official Edition, is hereby amended by inserting after clause Fourteenth the following
3 clause:-
4 Fifteenth, if it contains xylitol, unless the label or labeling of such food contains a
5 warning specifying the toxic effects of xylitol for dogs if ingested.
