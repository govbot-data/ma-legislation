An Act to bring child support home. 
 
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority 
of the same, as follows: 
 
1  SECTION 1. Chapter 18 of the General Laws is hereby amended by striking section 18A 
2 and inserting in place thereof the following new section:- 
3  Section 18A. (a)(1). The department shall impose the sanction required by federal law on 
4 any recipient of public assistance funded under Title IV-A of the Social Security Act who, 
5 without good cause, does not cooperate with the IV-D agency specified in chapter 119A to 
6 establish paternity or to establish, modify, or enforce a child support order on behalf of a child 
7 for whom the recipient receives such public assistance. An applicant or recipient may contest 
8 such sanction at a hearing before the department, but shall have the burden to prove cooperation 
9 with the IV–D agency by a preponderance of the evidence. 
10  (2) The IV–D agency shall determine whether an applicant or recipient has cooperated, 
11 and is continuing to cooperate, with the IV–D agency. An applicant or recipient shall be deemed 
12 not to have cooperated if the applicant or recipient fails, without such good cause, to furnish a 
13 sworn statement setting forth sufficient verifiable information about the noncustodial parent or, if 

2 of 3 14 more than one person may be the noncustodial parent, about each such person. Such information 
15 shall include the name and the social security number of the noncustodial parent, or the name of 
16 the noncustodial parent and at least two of the following items for the noncustodial parent: date 
17 of birth; address; telephone number; name and address of employer; names of parents; and the 
18 manufacturer, model and license number of any motor vehicle owned by the noncustodial parent. 
19 An applicant or recipient who knowingly provides false information to the IV–D agency shall be 
20 subject to prosecution for perjury.(3) The department shall determine that a recipient has good 
21 cause for not cooperating with the IV-D agency for purposes of this section if: 
22  (A) The child was conceived as a result of incest or rape; 
23  (B) Proceedings for the adoption of the child are pending or under consideration; 
24  (C) Cooperation risks discouraging the noncustodial parent or other relatives of the child 
25 from maintaining a relationship with the child or providing emotional or other support; 
26  (D) Cooperation risks physical or emotional harm to or retaliation against the child or the 
27 relative with whom the child resides; or 
28  (E) Cooperation is otherwise not in the child’s best interest. 
29  SECTION 2. Section 2 of chapter 118 of the General Laws is hereby amended by 
30 inserting at the end of the first paragraph the following:- 
31  Beginning July 1, 2027, the first $100 per month collected for one child in a family who 
32 is a recipient of benefits under this chapter and the first $200 per month collected for two or 
33 more children in a family who are recipients of benefits under this chapter shall be paid to the 
34 family and disregarded in determining the family’s countable income. 

3 of 3 35  SECTION 3. Section 2 of chapter 119A of the General Laws is hereby amended by 
36 inserting at the end of subsection (b) the following:- Pursuant to Title IV, Part D of the Social 
37 Security Act, in the case of former recipients of assistance, the first $100 per month collected for 
38 one child in a family who is a recipient of benefits under this chapter and the first $200 per 
39 month collected for two or more children in a family who are recipients of benefits under this 
40 chapter shall be paid to the family by the IV-D agency and disregarded in determining the 
41 family’s countable income for all support payments collected, including payments on arrears 
42 assigned to the state and payments collected through the federal tax refund offset.
