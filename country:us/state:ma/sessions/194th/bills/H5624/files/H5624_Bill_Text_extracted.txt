An Act establishing a Medal of Fidelity license plate. 
 
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority 
of the same, as follows: 
 
1  SECTION 1. Section 67B of chapter 33 of the General Laws, as appearing in the 2024 
2 Official Edition, is hereby amended by adding the following paragraph:-  
3  Qualifying next of kin of a fallen service member may apply to the adjutant general for a 
4 medal of fidelity, in a form prescribed by the adjutant general. There shall be within the 
5 application for the medal of fidelity a notice that informs applicants of their eligibility to receive 
6 the medal of fidelity license plate, pursuant to section 2 of chapter 90, free of charge upon 
7 approval of the application. The notification shall read as follows: “IF YOU RECEIVE THE 
8 MEDAL OF FIDELITY, YOU ARE ENTITLED TO RECEIVE THE MEDAL OF FIDELITY 
9 LICENSE PLATE FREE OF CHARGE” and shall include a link to the online application 
10 established by the registrar of motor vehicles.  
11  SECTION 2. Section 2 of chapter 90 of the General Laws, as so appearing, is hereby 
12 amended by inserting after the twenty-third paragraph the following paragraph:-  

2 of 2 13  The registrar shall furnish without charge to owners of private passenger motor vehicles 
14 who have been awarded the medal of fidelity pursuant to section 67B of chapter 33 upon 
15 presentation of satisfactory evidence of such status, as determined by the registrar, distinctive 
16 registration plates for 1 private passenger motor vehicle owned and principally used by such 
17 recipient, bearing on the left side an image of the medal and ribbon and bearing the words 
18 “MEDAL OF FIDELITY” along the bottom. The registrar, in consultation with the adjutant 
19 general of the Massachusetts national guard, shall notify all past recipients of the medal of 
20 fidelity, established pursuant to said section 67B of said chapter 33, of their eligibility for the 
21 medal of fidelity license plate through a letter, which shall include the application for the medal 
22 of fidelity license plate.  
23  SECTION 3. Not later than 6 months after the medal of fidelity license plate is designed 
24 and in circulation, the registrar of motor vehicles, in consultation with the adjutant general of the 
25 Massachusetts national guard shall notify past recipients of the medal of fidelity concerning their 
26 eligibility for the medal of fidelity license plate.
