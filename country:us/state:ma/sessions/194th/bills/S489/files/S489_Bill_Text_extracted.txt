An Act establishing a statewide volunteer program to assist seniors. 
 
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority 
of the same, as follows: 
 
1  SECTION 1.  Section 40 of chapter 19A of the General Laws, as appearing in the 2014 
2 Official Edition, is hereby amended by inserting the following section:- 
3  Section 41.  The secretary shall, subject to appropriation or the receipt of gifts, grants, 
4 and awards from any public or private entity pursuant to section 7 of chapter 19A, establish a 
5 statewide volunteer program for the purpose of assisting seniors with snow removal, property or 
6 home maintenance services. The department shall have the power to adopt rules and regulations 
7 to administer this program which shall include, but not be limited to: (1) coordinating among 
8 agencies of the commonwealth and other organizations using community service and 
9 volunteerism as a strategy to assist with this program; (2) establishing a registry and registration 
10 of volunteer personnel who are available to provide services; (3) establishing procedures for 

2 of 2 11 matching and placing individuals with seniors; (4) encouraging the corporate community of the 
12 commonwealth to become an active partner in the support, advocacy and promotion of 
13 community service and volunteerism relative to this program; and (5) establishing a system of 
14 volunteer incentives to assist with recruiting and registration of volunteers.
