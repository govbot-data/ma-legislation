_______________
An Act relative to reducing limited liability company filing and annual report fees.
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority
of the same, as follows:
1 SECTION 1. Section 12 of chapter 156C of the General Laws, as appearing in the 2024
2 Official Edition, is hereby amended by striking out Section 12(d) and inserting in place thereof
3 the following section:-
4 (d) The fee for the filing of the certificate of organization required by subsection (a) shall
5 be one hundred and fifty dollars. The fee for the filing of the annual report required by
6 subsection (c) shall be fifty dollars. Such fees shall be paid to the state secretary at the time the
7 certificate of organization or the annual report is filed.
8 SECTION 2. The secretary of the commonwealth shall take all necessary steps to
9 implement the changes in filing and annual report fees required by this act, including updating
10 regulations, forms, and filing systems.
11 SECTION 3. This act shall take effect on January 1, 2027.
