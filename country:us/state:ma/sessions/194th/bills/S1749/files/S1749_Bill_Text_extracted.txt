An Act relative to emergency response in an active shooter or hostile event situation. 
 
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority 
of the same, as follows: 
 
1  Chapter 6A of the General Laws is hereby amended by inserting after section 18V the 
2 following section:- 
3  Section 18W. (a) Definitions 
4  Active Shooter/Hostile Events Response (ASHER) Program: A community-based 
5 approach to preparedness, mitigation, response, and recovery from an ASHER incident, 
6 including public and private partnerships, emergency management, the medical community, 
7 emergency responders, and the public. 
8  Department: The Executive Office of Public Safety Secretary; the Secretary of Public 
9 Safety  

2 of 3 10  (b) The department shall adopt the National Fire Protection Association’s NFPA 3000 as 
11 the standard guideline for active shooter or hostile event situations in the Commonwealth. 
12  (c) The department, in conjunction with state, county, municipal, and regional law 
13 enforcement, fire services, emergency medical services, and emergency response agencies, 
14 develop and implement standard training requirements to achieve an integrated preparedness, 
15 response, and recovery for active shooter or hostile event situations. 
16  (d) There shall be within the department an Active Shooter or Hostile Event Situation 
17 (ASHER) Executive Council, consisting of the Secretary or their designee; Fire Marshall or their 
18 designee; the Colonel of the State Police or their designee,  a medical director appointed by the 
19 OEMS medical director; the director of the office of preparedness and emergency management 
20 at the department of public health, or a designee; and 10 members appointed by the Secretary, or 
21 their designees, as follows: 1 each from the Professional Fire Fighters of Massachusetts, the 
22 Massachusetts Ambulance Association, the Fire Chiefs Association of Massachusetts, the 
23 International Association of EMTs and Paramedics, the Massachusetts Chiefs of Police, the 
24 Massachusetts Coalition of Police, the State Police Association of Massachusetts,  a 
25 representative of the state 911 department and 2 members at large. 
26  The Secretary shall appoint the chair of the executive council. Each appointed member of 
27 the executive council shall serve a term of 3 years, or until a successor is appointed and 
28 qualified. At least one-half of the total appointed members of executive council shall constitute a 
29 quorum for the transaction of business. Appointees shall serve without compensation. 
30  (e) The executive council shall meet, at a minimum, once quarterly, and shall assist in 
31 coordinating the efforts of all persons and agencies in the state concerned with the emergency 

3 of 3 32 response in an active shooter or hostile event situation, and shall render advice on the 
33 development of the emergency response in an active shooter or hostile event situation. 
34  The executive council, in consultation with the department, shall submit an annual report 
35 to the Secretary and to the Clerks of the House of Representatives and Senate, on or before June 
36 30.  
37  (f) It shall be the duty of the department to plan, guide, assist, coordinate and regulate the 
38 development of an emergency response plan in an active shooter or hostile event situation. 
39  The department shall be the lead state agency for development and recommendations on 
40 emergency response to an active shooter or hostile event situation in the Commonwealth. The 
41 department shall have the authority to: 
42  (1) direct and coordinate a program for planning, developing, maintaining, expanding, 
43 improving and upgrading emergency response in an active shooter or hostile event situation. 
44  (2) establish minimum standards and criteria for all elements of emergency response in 
45 an active shooter or hostile event situation, taking into consideration relevant standards and 
46 criteria developed or adopted by nationally recognized agencies or organizations, and the 
47 recommendations of interested parties. 
48  (3) define and approve the minimum standards for training programs on emergency 
49 response in an active shooter or hostile event situation in this state.
