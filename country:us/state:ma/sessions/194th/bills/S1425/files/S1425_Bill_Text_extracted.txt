An Act further regulating the amendment of a conservation restriction in the town of Hanson. 
 
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority 
of the same, as follows: 
 
1  SECTION 1. Chapter 133 of the acts of 2022 is hereby amended by striking out section 3 
2 and inserting in place thereof the following section:- 
3  Section 3. As consideration for the change in the conservation restriction authorized in 
4 section 1, the town of Hanson shall, pursuant to section 3 of chapter 40 of the General Laws, 
5 transfer the care, custody and control of a certain 25.405, more or less, acre parcel identified as 
6 the town of Hanson assessors’ map 49, parcel 1-1A and a 3,922, more or less, square foot parcel 
7 identified as assessors’ map 105, parcel 6 from the board of selectmen to the conservation 
8 commission for the purpose of wildlife, recreation and forestry. 
9  SECTION 2. This act shall take effect upon its passage.
