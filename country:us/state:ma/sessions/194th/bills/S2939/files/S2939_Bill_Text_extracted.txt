An Act relative to the appointment of retired police officers as special police officers in the town 
of Dighton. 
 
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority 
of the same, as follows: 
 
1  SECTION 1. Notwithstanding any general or special law to the contrary, the Board of 
2 Selectmen in the town of Dighton may appoint, as the Police Chief considers necessary, POST 
3 certified retired police officers as special police officers to perform police details or police duties 
4 arising therefrom or during the course of the police detail work, whether or not related to the 
5 detail work. Such retired police officers shall have been regular police officers and voluntary 
6 retired based on superannuation. The special police officers shall not be subject to the maximum 
7 age restrictions applied to regular police officers under Chapter 32 of the General Laws but shall 
8 not be eligible to serve as special police officers if they have reached the age of 70. Such special 
9 police officer shall pass a medical examination by a physician or other certified professional 
10 chosen by the Town to determine that the officer is capable of performing the essential duties of 

2 of 3 11 special police officer, the cost of which shall be borne by the special police officer, prior to 
12 performing police details. 
13  SECTION 2. Special police officers appointed pursuant to this act shall not be subject to 
14 chapter 31 of the General Laws, Section 85H and 85H1/2 of chapter 32 of the General Laws, 
15 sections 99A, 100 and 111F of chapter 41 of the General Laws, or chapter 150E of the General 
16 Laws. Special police officers appointed pursuant to this act shall be subject to chapter 151A of 
17 the General Laws. 
18  SECTION 3. Special police officers appointed under this act shall, when performing the 
19 duties under section 1, have the same power to make arrests and to perform other police 
20 functions as do regular police officers of the town of Dighton. 
21  SECTION 4. Special police officers shall be re-appointed annually, subject to removal by 
22 the Board of Selectmen at any time with 14 days’ written notice except in cases where the Police 
23 Chief determines that immediate action is necessary to protect the public safety. Upon request, 
24 the Police Chief shall provide reasons for removal in writing. 
25  SECTION 5. Special police officers appointed pursuant to this act shall be subject to the 
26 rules and regulations, policies and procedures and requirements of the Police Department and 
27 Police Chief of the town of Dighton, including restrictions on the type of detail assignment, 
28 requirements regarding medical examinations to determine continued capacity to perform the 
29 duties of a special police officer, requirements for training, requirements for firearm licensing 
30 and qualification and requirements regarding uniforms and equipment. Compliance with all 
31 requirements shall be a no cost to the Town. Special Police officers shall not be subject to section 
32 96B of chapter 41 of the General Laws. 

3 of 3 33  SECTION 6. Special police officers appointed under this act shall be sworn before the 
34 Town Clerk of the town of Dighton who shall keep a record of all such appointments. 
35  SECTION 7. Appointment as a special police officer pursuant to this act shall not entitle 
36 any officer to assignment to any specific detail or type of detail. 
37  SECTION 8. Retired police officers serving as special police officers under this act shall 
38 be subject to the limitations on hours worked and on payments to retired town employees 
39 pursuant to paragraph (b) of section 91 of chapter 32 of the General Laws. 
40  SECTION 9. This act shall take effect upon its passage.
