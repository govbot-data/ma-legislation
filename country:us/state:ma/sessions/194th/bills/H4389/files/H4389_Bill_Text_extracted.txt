An Act directing the city of Boston Police Department to waive the maximum age requirement 
for police officers for Angel G. Rivera. 
 
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority 
of the same, as follows: 
 
1  SECTION 1.  Notwithstanding any general or special law to the contrary, including the 
2 provisions of Chapter 43 of the Act of 2007, the City of Boston Police Department shall waive 
3 the maximum age requirement for original appointment of police officers for Angel G. Rivera. If 
4 Angel G. Rivera meets all other requirements set forth by the Boston Police Department, he shall 
5 be eligible to have his name certified for original appointment to the position of police officer.  
6  SECTION 2.  The provisions of this act shall take effect upon its passage.
