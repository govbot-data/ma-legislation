HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES, April 8, 2026.
The committee on Transportation, to whom were referred the petition
(accompanied by bill, House, No. 3705) of Bradley H. Jones, Jr., and
others relative to the Massachusetts Bay Transportation Authority; and the
petition (accompanied by bill, House, No. 3707) of Bradley H. Jones, Jr.,
and others relative to the procurement of rolling stock for the use of public
transportation by the Massachusetts Bay Transportation Authority, reports
recommending that the accompanying order (House, No. 5329) ought to
be adopted [Representative DeCoste of Norwell dissents].
For the committee,
JAMES ARCIERO.
