HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES, January 15, 2026.
The committee on Financial Services, to whom was referred so much of
the recommendations of the Department of the State Treasurer (House, No.
35) as relates to establishing a Massachusetts Baby Bonds program (House,
No. 48), reports recommending that the accompanying order (House, No.
4929) ought to be adopted.
For the committee,
JAMES M. MURPHY.

The Commonwealth of Massachusetts
House of Representatives, January 15, 2026.
1 Ordered, That the committee on Financial Services be authorized to sit during a recess of
2 the General Court to make an investigation and study of House document numbered 48, relative
3 to establishing a Massachusetts Baby Bonds program.
4 Said committee shall report to the General Court the results of its investigation and study
5 and its recommendations, if any, together with drafts of legislation necessary to carry such
6 recommendations into effect, by filing the same with the Clerk of the House of Representatives
7 on or before December 31, 2026.
