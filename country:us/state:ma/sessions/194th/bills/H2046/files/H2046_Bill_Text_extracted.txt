An Act relative to pill press machines. 
 
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority 
of the same, as follows: 
 
1  Section 32G of Chapter 94C of the General Laws, as appearing in the 2020 Official 
2 Edition, is hereby amended by inserting the following new subsection:  
3  (1) It shall be unlawful for any person to possess, purchase, deliver, sell, or possess 
4 with intent to sell or deliver a tableting machine, an encapsulating machine, drug molds, or 
5 controlled substance counterfeiting materials knowing, intending, or having reasonable cause to 
6 believe that it will be used to manufacture a controlled substance or counterfeit controlled 
7 substance. Any person who violates this subsection, or violates any regulation adopted by the 
8 Board of Registration in Pharmacy relative to the use or licensing of such equipment, shall be 
9 guilty of a felony punishable by a sentence of not less than 1 year and not more than 5 years in 
10 the state prison or county house of correction or by a fine of up to $25,000, or both such fine and 
11 imprisonment. 

2 of 2 12  (2) The Board of Registration in Pharmacy shall adopt rules and regulations for 
13 standards of compliance for the use of and licensing of pill press machines for the legal use of 
14 the any of the said devices in this section. 
15  (3) The Board of Registration in Pharmacy may require registration of pill presses or 
16 similar equipment to be promulgated by the Board.
