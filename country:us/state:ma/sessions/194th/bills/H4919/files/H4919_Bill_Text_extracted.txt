The Commonwealth of Massachusetts 
 
________________________________________ 
 
HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES, February 17, 2026. 
 The committee on Children, Families and Persons with Disabilities, to 
whom were referred the petition (accompanied by bill, House, No. 217) of 
David F. DeCoste, Joseph D. McKenna and Colleen M. Garry relative to 
providing resources and support for pregnant and parenting families; the 
petition (accompanied by bill, House, No. 237) of Brandy Fluker-Reid, 
Samantha Montaño and Daniel Cahill that the Executive Office of Health 
and Human Services be authorized to establish a direct care worker 
medication administration program registry; the petition (accompanied by 
bill, House, No. 240) of William C. Galvin relative to licensure for the use 
of graduated electronic decelerators to alter behavior of persons with 
disabilities; the petition (accompanied by bill, House, No. 245) of Danielle 
W. Gregoire and James B. Eldridge for legislation to prohibit the use of 
procedures which cause physical pain or deny a reasonable humane 
existence to persons with disabilities; the petition (accompanied by bill, 
House, No. 255) of Michael P. Kushmerek, Natalie M. Higgins and James 
B. Eldridge relative to family child care homes; the petition (accompanied 
by bill, House, No. 274) of Frank A. Moran for legislation to establish a 
bill of rights for individuals experiencing homelessness; and the petition 
(accompanied by bill, House, No. 294) of Christopher J. Worrell relative 
to surveillance in out-of-home care facilities, reports recommending that 
the accompanying order (House, No. 4919) ought to be adopted. 
 
For the committee, 
 
JAY D. LIVINGSTONE.
