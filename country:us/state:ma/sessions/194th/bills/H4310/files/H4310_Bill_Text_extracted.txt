(accompanied by bill, House, No. 4310) of David M. Rogers and William N. Brownsberger (by

In the One Hundred and Ninety-Fourth General Court
(2025-2026)
_______________
An Act authorizing the town of Belmont to prohibit or restrict the application of second-
generation anticoagulant rodenticides.
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority
of the same, as follows:
1 Section 1. Notwithstanding any general or special law to the contrary, the Town of
2 Belmont is hereby authorized to enact and enforce local regulations, bylaws, or ordinances
3 prohibiting or restricting the use of second-generation anticoagulant rodenticides within the
4 boundaries of the Town, including but not limited to their use on public and private properties.
5 Section 2. This act shall take effect upon its passage.
