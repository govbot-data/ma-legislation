An Act supporting maternal and pediatric health. 
 
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority 
of the same, as follows: 
 
1  SECTION 1. Chapter 32A of the General Laws is hereby amended by adding the 
2 following section:- 
3  Section 33. Any coverage offered by the commission to an active or retired employee of 
4 the commonwealth insured under the group insurance commission shall provide coverage for a 
5 prenatal pediatric visit. The term ‘prenatal pediatric visit’ shall mean an appointment between a 
6 pregnant person and a pediatrician to provide a foundation on which to build a positive, family-
7 pediatric professional partnership as recommended by the American Academy of Pediatrics. 
8  SECTION 2. Chapter 118E of the General Laws, as appearing in the 2020 Official 
9 Edition, is hereby amended by inserting after section 10Q, the following section:- 
10  Section 10R. (a) The division and its contracted health insurers, health plans, health 
11 maintenance organizations, behavioral health management firms and third-party administrators 
12 under contract to a Medicaid managed care organization or primary care clinician shall provide 

2 of 3 13 coverage for a prenatal pediatric visit. The term ‘prenatal pediatric visit’ shall mean an 
14 appointment between a pregnant person and a pediatrician to provide a foundation on which to 
15 build a positive, family-pediatric professional partnership as recommended by the American 
16 Academy of Pediatrics. 
17  (b) The division shall provide notice to its members regarding the coverage required by 
18 this section. The notice shall be in writing and prominently positioned in any literature or 
19 correspondence sent to members and shall be transmitted to members within calendar year when 
20 annual information is made available to members, or in any other mailing or communication to 
21 members.
