An Act relative to building permits and job site safety.
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority
of the same, as follows:
1 Chapter 143 of the General Laws is here by amended by adding the following section:-
2 Section 102. A licensed construction supervisor or a registered design professional who is
3 granted a building permit shall be responsible for the overall safety on a job site and shall ensure
4 that all safety requirements at a job site are followed and implemented, including the
5 requirements set forth in 29 C.F.R. 1926 and the state building code pursuant to this section.
