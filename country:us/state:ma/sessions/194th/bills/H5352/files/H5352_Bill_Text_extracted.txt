HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES, April 13, 2026.
The committee on Housing, to whom were referred the petition
(accompanied by bill, House, No. 1470) of David Biele relative to
establishing an advisory commission to study the best practices and safety
protocols of state subsidized senior and non-elderly disabled housing; the
petition (accompanied by bill, House, No. 1474) of Daniel Cahill and Sean
Reid relative to affordable housing in certain municipalities; the petition
(accompanied by bill, House, No. 1475) of Tackey Chan relative to
manufactured housing; the petition (accompanied by bill, House, No. 1479)
of Mike Connolly for legislation to establish a statewide housing policy
simulator to model the impacts of existing and potential housing policies
and conditions in the Commonwealth; the petition (accompanied by bill,
House, No. 1480) of Mike Connolly for legislation to authorize
supplemental financing of housing first programs, state public housing
renovations, and other housing programs and to make related changes in
certain laws; the petition (accompanied by bill, House, No. 1483) of Manny
Cruz relative to the use of credit reporting for rent-subsidized tenants; the
petition (accompanied by bill, House, No. 1489) of David F. DeCoste for
legislation to establish a savings program to assist rental tenants in the
purchase of a home; the petition (accompanied by bill, House, No. 1490) of
David F. DeCoste relative to the Affordable Homes Act; the petition
(accompanied by bill, House, No. 1491) of David F. DeCoste relative to
statewide public housing applications and the centralized waitlist system;
the petition (accompanied by bill, House, No. 1492) of David F. DeCoste
relative to expanding the definition of affordable housing to include
manufactured homes; the petition (accompanied by bill, House, No. 1494)
of David F. DeCoste relative to rental assistance programs; the petition
(accompanied by bill, House, No. 1497) of Michelle M. DuBois relative to
the stabilization of rents in cities and towns facing distress in the housing
market; the petition (accompanied by bill, House, No. 1498) of Michelle M.
DuBois relative to homelessness; the petition (accompanied by bill, House,
No. 1499) of Patricia A. Duffy relative to standards for group homes; the

legislation to improve municipal equity within the emergency assistance
shelter program; the petition (accompanied by bill, House, No. 1503) of
Paul K. Frost, Joseph D. McKenna and others relative to the emergency
housing assistance program and universal background checks; the petition
(accompanied by bill, House, No. 1504) of Paul K. Frost, David F. DeCoste
and Joseph D. McKenna relative to the inclusion of mobile homes in the
definition of affordable housing; the petition (accompanied by bill, House,
No. 1505) of Paul K. Frost, David F. DeCoste and Donald R. Berthiaume,
Jr., relative to emergency housing assistance residency requirements; the
petition (accompanied by bill, House, No. 1506) of Paul K. Frost and others
relative to prioritizing Massachusetts residents on the waitlist for
emergency housing assistance; the petition (accompanied by bill, House,
No. 1507) of William C. Galvin for legislation to limit rent increases and
create a rental arbitrator within the office of the Attorney General; the
petition (accompanied by bill, House, No. 1508) of John R. Gaskey relative
to repealing the emergency housing assistance program; the petition
(accompanied by bill, House, No. 1509) of Carmine Lawrence Gentile,

G. Honan relative to multifamily housing construction; the petition

voting in condominiums; the petition (accompanied by bill, House, No.
1527) of Bradley H. Jones, Jr., and others relative to public housing

1528) of Bradley H. Jones, Jr., and others relative to public housing
restrictions; the petition (accompanied by bill, House, No. 1529) of Bradley

Bradley H. Jones, Jr., and others for an investigation by a special
commission (including members of the General Court) relative to the

petition (accompanied by bill, House, No. 1533) of Bradley H. Jones, Jr.,

1536) of Bradley H. Jones, Jr., and others relative to prioritization for the
emergency assistance program; the petition (accompanied by bill, House,

1540) of Patrick Joseph Kearney relative to eligibility for the emergency

House, No. 1549) of Joseph D. McKenna relative to cities and towns
sheltering individuals for certain state or federal housing agencies; the

the Department of Housing and Community Development provide for
programs, policies, guidelines, priorities and preferences for the housing of
formerly incarcerated persons reentering communities; the petition
(accompanied by bill, House, No. 1555) of David K. Muradian, Jr., relative
to the display of the flag of the United States by certain condominium
owners; the petition (accompanied by bill, House, No. 1556) of Norman J.
Orrall relative to expanding the definition of affordable housing to include
manufactured homes; the petition (accompanied by bill, House, No. 1557)
of Sean Reid, Homar Gómez and Tara T. Hong for legislation to create an
office of tenant protections in the Executive Office of Housing and Livable
Communities; the petition (accompanied by bill, House, No. 1558) of David
M. Rogers, Christine P. Barber and others relative to the promotion of
affordable housing; the petition (accompanied by bill, House, No. 1563) of
Lindsay N. Sabadosa that the Housing Appeals Committee be authorized to
develop an application to remove deed riders for property owners who
purchased homes through the Homeowner Opportunity Program; the
petition (accompanied by bill, House, No. 1567) of Thomas M. Stanley
relative to fair housing assessments for large-scale housing developments;
the petition (accompanied by bill, House, No. 1569) of Chynah Tyler that
senior housing facilities provide security services to ensure the safety and
security of residents; the petition (accompanied by bill, House, No. 1573)
of David T. Vieira relative to short term rental inspections; the petition
(accompanied by bill, House, No. 1575) of Bud L. Williams relative to
reducing damage caused by the current foreclosure crisis in the historic

districts of the Commonwealth; the petition (accompanied by bill, House,
No. 1576) of Christopher J. Worrell and others for legislation to create a
program for the development and construction of permanently affordable
homeownership opportunities for low and moderate income buyers; the
petition (accompanied by bill, House, No. 1578) of Steven George Xiarhos,
Bradley H. Jones, Jr., and Joseph D. McKenna relative to background
checks for individuals in Massachusetts housing programs; the petition
(accompanied by bill, House, No. 1579) of Steven George Xiarhos, Donald
R. Berthiaume, Jr., and Joseph D. McKenna relative to emergency shelter
assistance for commonwealth residents; the petition (accompanied by bill,
House, No. 2255) of Mike Connolly relative to local approval of
inclusionary zoning; the petition (accompanied by bill, House, No. 2347) of
Andres X. Vargas, Tara T. Hong and Vanna Howard relative to zoning
ordinances and taxation of land owned by religious sects or denominations
for multifamily housing; the petition (accompanied by bill, House, No.
3934) of Michelle L. Badger (by vote of the town) for legislation to establish
a Plymouth land bank in the town of Plymouth; the petition (accompanied
by bill, House, No. 3988) of Kip A. Diggs for legislation to establish the
Barnstable home center for housing resources; the petition (accompanied
by bill, House, No. 3990) of David Henry Argosky LeBoeuf and Russell E.
Holmes relative to tenants and small property owners; the petition
(accompanied by bill, House, No. 3996) of Michelle L. Badger relative to
zoning restrictions for solar systems; the petition (accompanied by bill,
House, No. 4063) of Joan Meschino and Carmine Lawrence Gentile relative
to increases of the annual rent of affordable housing units and for rental unit
and project oversight; the petition (accompanied by bill, House, No. 4145)
of Mike Connolly (with the approval of the city council) relative to broker
fees in the city of Cambridge; and the joint petition (accompanied by bill,
House, No. 4228) of Susannah M. Whipps and Joanne M. Comerford (by
vote of the town) that the town of Athol be authorized to regulate rents for
the use or occupancy of manufactured housing parks in said town, reports
(under the provisions of House Rule 27) recommending that the
accompanying order (House, No. 5352) ought to be adopted.
For the committee,
RICHARD M. HAGGERTY.
