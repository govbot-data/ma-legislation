An Act establishing a fund for the Western Massachusetts Hospital.
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority
of the same, as follows:
1 Chapter 29 of the General Laws is hereby amended by inserting after section 2JJJJJJ the
2 following section:-
3 Section 2KKKKKK. (a) There shall be established and set up on the books of the
4 commonwealth a separate fund known as the Western Massachusetts Hospital Fund, to be
5 administered by the commissioner of public health. There shall be credited to the fund: (i)
6 appropriations or other money authorized or transferred by the general court and specifically
7 designated to be credited to the fund; (ii) funds from public and private sources, including, but
8 not limited to, gifts, grants and donations; and (iii) any interest earned on the assets of the fund.
9 (b) Amounts credited to the fund may be expended without further appropriation by the
10 commissioner of public health for any purpose related to the operation of the Western
11 Massachusetts hospital.
