An Act relative to Massachusetts General Laws chapter 32: retirement and pensions.
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority
of the same, as follows:
1 Section 1: Notwithstanding any general or special law to the contrary, Section 1 of
2 Chapter 32 in the 2020 Official Edition is hereby amended by inserting in line 633 after the
3 phrase following "regular compensation"; Regular compensation shall include all overtime paid
4 to any employee as defined by this chapter, who was required to, or volunteered to, work outside
5 their home during the COVID 19 pandemic state of emergency that commenced on March 10,
6 2020 until the conclusion of the COVID 19 state of emergency.
7 The state human resources administrator shall compile a list of all employees eligible for
8 overtime under this chapter and transmit it to the state retirement board.
