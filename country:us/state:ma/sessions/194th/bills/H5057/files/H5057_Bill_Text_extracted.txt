HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES, February 9, 2026.
The committee on the Judiciary, to whom was referred the petition
(accompanied by bill, House, No. 1651) of Marjorie C. Decker, Mike
Connolly and Patricia D. Jehlen that the executive office of the Trial Court
relocate the Cambridge district court from the city of Medford to the city

No. 5057) ought to pass.
For the committee,

HOUSE . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . No. 5057
The Commonwealth of Massachusetts
_______________
In the One Hundred and Ninety-Fourth General Court
(2025-2026)
_______________
An Act requiring the relocation of the Cambridge District Court.
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority
of the same, as follows:
1 SECTION 1. Notwithstanding any general or special law to the contrary, the court
2 administrator of the executive office of the trial court shall, not later than December 31, 2026,
3 relocate the Cambridge district court, also known as the third district court of eastern Middlesex,
4 from the city of Medford to the city of Cambridge.
5 SECTION 2. Within 90 days of the effective date of this act, the executive office of the
6 trial court shall file a report with the clerks of the house of representatives and senate and the
7 chairs of the joint committee on the judiciary outlining the timeline for the relocation of the
8 Cambridge district court.
