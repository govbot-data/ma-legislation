An Act relative to replacement plans.
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority
of the same, as follows:
1 Section 115A of Chapter 164 of the general laws, as so appearing, is hereby amended by
2 adding, in the first paragraph after the words "in accordance with law", the following:-; provided
3 however, that a gas company or municipal lighting plant may petition the department for
4 approval of an alternate gas meter replacement plan and the department may approve such an
5 alternate gas meter replacement plan and the department may approve such an alternate plan
6 upon a determination that the plan is consistent with the public interest.
