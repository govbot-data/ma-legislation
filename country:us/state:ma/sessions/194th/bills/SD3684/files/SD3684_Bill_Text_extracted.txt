SUBJECT: February 2026 UNEMPLOYMENT INSURANCE TRUST FUND 
INFORMATION  
 
The following information relates to the reporting on the Unemployment Insurance  
Trust Fund (“UI Trust Fund”) for the month of January 2026 .  
 
As of January 31,  2026, the preliminary Massachusetts UI  Trust Fund ending balance 
was $ 1.27 billion.  
 
Year -to-date through January 31, 202 6, preliminary employer contributions  totaled  
$62.10 million. 
 
Year -to-date through January  31, 202 6, DUA paid $ 240.39 million in benefits for regular 
UI for  89,882 initial and continuing claims . 
 
This update contains information related only to actual dollars collected or disbursed. On 
January 17, 2025, the Commonwealth and the U.S. Department of Labor entered into an agreement that fully resolved any outstanding liability related to pandemic 
unemployment insurance overdraws as a result of federal UI programs established 
during the COVID -19 pandemic. The impact of this agreement is reflected in the January 
2026 projections contained in the Unemployment Insurance Trust Fund Quarterly 

Outlook Report. The Commonwealth’s first payment under the agreement was  made on 
December 1, 2025.  
 
The UI Trust Fund data is also available at Mass.gov/UITrustFund in the form of data 
visualizations spanning from January  2025  through January 2026.
