An Act establishing a charter for the town of Orange. 
 
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority 
of the same, as follows: 
 
1  The following shall be the charter for the Town of Orange:-        
2  PREAMBLE   
3  We the people of the Town of Orange, Massachusetts, in order to reaffirm the customary  
4 and traditional liberties of the people with respect to the conduct of our local government and to  
5 take the fullest advantages inherent in the home rule amendment of the constitution of the  
6 Commonwealth and Massachusetts General Laws Chapter 43B, do hereby adopt the following 
7 home rule  Charter for this Town. It is the intention of this document that it reflects a gender 
8 nonspecific approach. Any references to masculine gender are intended to reflect both masculine 
9 and feminine gender, and should not be otherwise construed.   
10  CHAPTER 1 – POWERS OF THE TOWN   
11  Section 1: Incorporation   

2 of 33 12  1-1-1 The present Town of Orange, Massachusetts, within its territorial limits as now or 
13 may hereafter be established by law, is hereby reincorporated and continued as a body politic and 
14 corporate under the name: Town of Orange.   
15  Section 2: Scope of Town Powers   
16  1-2-1  The Town shall possess, exercise, and enjoy all powers possible under the 
17 constitution and laws of the Commonwealth of Massachusetts as fully and completely as though 
18 they were expressly enumerated in this Charter.   
19  Section 3: Form of Government   
20  1-3-1  This Charter provides for the Open Town Meeting-Select Board-Town Manager 
21 form of government.   
22  Section 4: Construction of Charter   
23  1-4-1  The powers of the Town under this Charter, shall be construed liberally in favor of 
24 the  Town, and the specific mention of particular powers in the Charter shall not be construed  as 
25 limiting in any measure the general powers of the Town as stated in this charter.   
26  Section 5: Intergovernmental Relations   
27  1-5-1   Consistent with any constitutional or statutory provisions, the Town may exercise 
28 any of its powers or perform any of its functions, and may participate in the financing thereof, 
29 jointly or in cooperation, by contract or otherwise with any one or more municipalities,  with any 
30 civil division, subdivision, or agency of the Commonwealth, with any other state, or with the 
31 United States government.   

3 of 33 32  CHAPTER 2 – TOWN MEETING   
33  Section 1: Organization   
34  2-1-1   The legislative powers of the Town shall be exercised by a Town Meeting, open 
35 to all registered voters of the Town.   
36  2-1-2   The Quorum for the conduct of business at Town Meetings shall be a minimum of 
37 seventy-five (75) of the  Town’s registered voters.   
38  2-1-3   Upon a successful challenge of the presence of a quorum, a smaller number of 
39 voters than the established quorum shall, at the discretion of the Moderator, adjourn any such 
40 meeting forthwith to a stated date, time, and place.   
41  2-1-4    In all procedural matters the Town Meeting shall follow the latest revised edition 
42 of Town Meeting Time as the procedural handbook, except as otherwise provided by 
43 Massachusetts General Laws, established practices, or this Charter.   
44  2-1-5    Rules of parliamentary procedure for Town Meetings shall be summarized in 
45 simplified form in a document prepared by the Moderator and the Town Clerk and shall be made 
46 available for distribution to those requesting it, and also posted on the town website.   
47  Section 2: Powers and Responsibilities   
48  2-2-1  The Town Meeting shall consider and act upon, with or without amendments, all 
49 proposed operating and capital improvement budgets, bond issues, and other financial proposals 
50 of the Town.   

4 of 33 51  2-2-2   The Town Meeting shall possess and may exercise all powers possible under 
52 general laws. 
53  Section 3: Procedures   
54  2-3-1  The Annual Town Meeting shall be held according to town bylaw on the second 
55 Monday of May, to consider and adopt an annual operating and capital budget, and to act on 
56 financial and other matters. The meeting shall be continued on other days if necessary, until all 
57 articles in the warrant shall have been acted upon. The Select Board shall insert in the warrant for 
58 the Annual Town Meeting all subjects, the insertion of which shall have been requested of them 
59 in writing by ten (10) or more registered voters of the Town, and in the warrant for a Special 
60 Town Meeting, all subjects the insertion of which shall have been requested of them in writing 
61 by one hundred (100) registered voters or by ten (10) per cent of the total number of registered 
62 voters of the Town, whichever number is less.  
63   2-3-2  A Special Town Meeting may be called by the Select Board, and shall be called 
64 by said Board upon request, in writing, of at least two hundred (200) of the Town’s registered 
65 voters. The Select Board shall call a Special Town Meeting upon request, in writing, of two 
66 hundred (200) registered voters or of twenty (20) percent of the total number of registered voters 
67 of the Town, whichever number is less; such meeting shall be held not later than forty-five (45) 
68 days after the receipt of such request, and the Select Board shall insert in the warrant therefore all 
69 subjects the insertion of which shall have been requested by said petition.  
70  2-3-3   All proposed operating expenditures shall be included in a single, omnibus-type 
71 article in the Town Meeting warrant. In addition, all regular proposed capital improvement  
72 expenditures shall be included in an omnibus-type article.   

5 of 33 73  2-3-4   The Select Board shall prepare the warrant for all Town Meetings.   
74  2-3-5   The warrant for the Annual Town Meeting shall be closed sixty (60) days prior to 
75 the date of the Town Meeting and the date for the closing of the warrant for any other Town 
76 Meeting shall be at the discretion of the Select Board. The warrant for every Town Meeting shall 
77 be posted on the town website and also posted in at least four (4) public places within the Town 
78 at least fourteen (14) days prior to the date of the Town Meeting.    
79  2-3-6  Articles involving an expenditure of Town funds shall not be voted on by the 
80 Town  Meeting unless the Finance Committee shall have given due consideration to the articles. 
81 The  Chair of the Finance Committee, or his/her designated representative, shall be present at the 
82 Town Meeting to provide verbal explanations of the Committee recommendations.   
83  2-3-7 Articles involving planning, zoning, subdivision control, land acquisition, 
84 conservation,  extension of sewer lines, and all other matters relating to the environment shall not 
85 be  voted on by the Town Meeting unless the Finance Committee and/or the Planning Board 
86 shall have given due consideration to the articles. The Chair of the respective boards, or their 
87 designated representatives, shall be present at the Town Meeting to provide verbal explanations 
88 of the board’s recommendations.     
89   2-3-8  The order of consideration of articles as printed in the warrant may be changed 
90 only by a majority vote of the Town Meeting or at the discretion of the moderator. The decisions 
91 of the moderator take precedence.   
92  2-3-9   No proposed ordinance or bylaw which has been unfavorably acted upon by a 
93 Town Meeting shall be considered by the Town Meeting within two (2) years after the date of 

6 of 33 94 such unfavorable action unless the adoption of such proposed ordinance or bylaw is 
95 recommended in the final report of the Planning Board and/or the Select Board.   
96  2-3-10 At least fourteen (14) days prior to the date of any Town Meeting, a notice thereof 
97 shall be made available by the Select Board. Said notice shall include the date, time and location 
98 of the Town Meeting and a description of ways to obtain copies of the warrant. A copy of the 
99 warrant shall be emailed or otherwise delivered by the Select Board to the Moderator, 
100 Department Heads, Town Counsel, and to each member of the Finance Committee and Planning 
101 Board. Any registered voter may file a written request annually with the Town Clerk to have a 
102 copy of the warrant mailed or delivered by the Select Board to their residence.    
103  2-3-11 Any ten (10) registered voters of the Town  may secure, by written petition to the 
104 Select Board, the inclusion of an article in the warrant for the Annual Town Meeting, and one 
105 hundred (100) registered voters may secure, by written petition to the Select Board, the inclusion 
106 of an article for any Special Town Meeting. 
107  Section 4: Presiding Officer   
108  2-4-1   A Moderator shall be elected for a term of three (3) years and shall preside at all 
109 sessions of the Town Meeting. In the absence of the Moderator and Deputy Moderator, the Town 
110 Meeting shall, as its first act, elect a temporary Moderator by a majority vote. The Town Clerk 
111 shall preside until such election has taken place, in accordance with Section 14 of Chapter 39 of 
112 the Massachusetts General Laws.  
113  2-4-2  The Moderator shall have the power to declare a secret written ballot on any 
114 motion under any article, when the Moderator deems such action to be in the best interests of the 
115 Town.   

7 of 33 116  2-4-3  The Moderator shall have the power to declare that a required two-thirds vote has 
117 been achieved by means of a voice vote.  
118  2-4-4   The Moderator shall appoint: (a) seven (7) members of the Finance Committee; 
119 (b) a Deputy  Moderator for a one-year term who shall preside in the Moderator’s absence; (c) 
120 three (3) members of the Sewer and Wastewater Commission; (d) the designated number of 
121 Orange members of the Franklin County Technical School Committee; and (e) any Ad Hoc 
122 Committee created by the Town Meeting, unless another appointing authority shall be specified 
123 in the article establishing such Committee.  
124  Section 5: Compulsory Attendance   
125  2-5-1   All Town Officials, including elected officials, the Chairs of Boards, 
126 Commissions, and Committees, and Department Heads or their duly designated representatives, 
127 are expected to attend all Annual Town Meeting and Special Town Meeting sessions.   
128  CHAPTER 3 – SELECT BOARD   
129  Section 1: The Select Board   
130  3-1-1   A Select Board of five (5) members shall be elected at large for three-year 
131 overlapping terms. The regular election for the Office of Select Board shall be held in 
132 accordance with Chapter 7 of this Charter.   
133  3-1-2   Vacancies in the Office of Select Board shall be filled by special elections in 
134 accordance with provisions of general laws.  
135  Section 2: General Powers   

8 of 33 136  3-2-1  All executive powers of the Town, except as otherwise provided in this Charter, 
137 shall be vested in the Select Board. The Board shall exercise control over Town affairs by 
138 recommending major courses of action to the Town Meeting.   
139  3-2-2  The Board shall have the power to enact rules and regulations relating to the 
140 conduct of  Town elections and for the establishment of Town policies not otherwise governed 
141 by  statute or this Charter, provided, however, that whenever an appropriation shall be  necessary 
142 to implement such action, the vote of the Board shall be effective only if such  appropriation 
143 shall be made by the Town Meeting.   
144  3-2-3  The Board shall establish written policies and procedures to be adopted within six 
145 months of the appointment of a Town Manager. These policies and procedures shall cover the 
146 functions and duties of the Select Board, Town Manager, and other Officers, Boards, and 
147 Commissions appointed by the Select Board or Town Manager, not previously specified in the 
148 Charter. In the event of conflict between these policies and procedures and the Charter, the 
149 Charter prevails.   
150  3-2-4   The policies and procedures shall be reviewed and updated within ninety (90) 
151 days of each annual election.  
152  Section 3: Powers of Appointment   
153  3-3-1  The Board shall have the power to appoint: (a) a Town Manager as provided in 
154 Chapter  5 of this Charter; (b) three (3) members of a Board of Registrars of voters for three-year 
155 overlapping terms; (c) five (5) members of a Zoning Board of Appeals for overlapping terms of 
156 such length and so arranged that the term of at least one member shall expire each year, and three 
157 (3) associate members in a like manner; (d) Election Officers.         

9 of 33 158  CHAPTER 4 – ELECTED OFFICERS AND TOWN BOARDS   
159  Section 1: General Provisions  
160  4-1-1   The Officers and Boards to be elected by vote of the Town shall be: Town Clerk,  
161 Moderator, Board of Library Trustees, Select Board, Board of Health, Water  Commission, 
162 Cemetery Commission, Housing Authority, Elementary School  Committee; the elected Orange 
163 members of the Mahar Regional School Committee; Soldiers’ Memorial Trustees; and  
164 Constables for three-year overlapping terms.    
165  4-1-2  Boards and Commissions established or continued under this chapter shall perform 
166 their functions and duties in accordance with the constitution, general laws, and this Charter.  
167  4-1-3.  During the term for which a person is elected, and for one (1) year following 
168 expiration of their term, no Officer or member of any Board or Commission established under 
169 this chapter shall be eligible to accept any appointed, paid Town position placed under the 
170 jurisdiction of the respective Commission or Board vacated by the Officer or member.   
171  Section 2: Vacancies   
172  4-2-1 Except as otherwise provided, vacancies in elected Town Boards established under 
173 this chapter shall be filled by the Select Board, together with remaining members of the 
174 respective Board, in accordance with the provisions of Massachusetts General Laws Chapter 41, 
175 Section 11.  
176  Section 3: School Committees   

10 of 33 177  4-3-1  The elected Orange members of the Mahar Regional School Committee shall be 
178 elected for three-year overlapping terms, in accordance with the terms of the Mahar Regional 
179 School District agreement.  
180  4-3-2 The elected members of the Orange Elementary School Committee shall be elected 
181 for three- year overlapping terms.  
182  4-3-3  The Mahar Regional School Committee and the Orange Elementary School 
183 Committee are expected to submit to the Town Manager the budget as adopted by the Committee 
184 no later than February 1 of each year, to enable the Town Manager to consider the effects of the 
185 schools’ requested assessments upon the total Town budget.   
186  Section 4: Library Trustees   
187  4-4-1   A Board of Library Trustees of six (6) members shall be elected for three-year 
188 overlapping  terms.   
189  4-4-2  The Board shall be responsible for the administration and operation of the Town 
190 Library, acquisition of books, journals, periodicals, and other materials relating to the library 
191 function, and the promulgation of library rules and regulations.        
192  Section 5: Housing Authority   
193  4-5-1  There shall be a Housing Authority of five (5) members, one (1) of whom shall be 
194 appointed under authority of the Commonwealth and four (4) of whom shall be elected. All 
195 members shall serve five-year overlapping terms.   
196  Section 6: Town Clerk   

11 of 33 197  4-6-1 The Town Clerk and the Board of Registrars shall recommend the appointment of 
198 Election Officers to the Select Board, and shall carry out such duties as are, and may be 
199  provided by general laws, by this Charter and by vote of the Town Meeting. In the event 
200  the Town votes sometime in the future to make the position of Town Clerk appointed, the 
201 Town Clerk shall be appointed by the Town Manager and approved by a majority of the full 
202 Select Board.  
203  CHAPTER 5 – THE TOWN MANAGER   
204  Section 1: Appointment   
205  5-1-1   The Select Board, by an affirmative vote of at least four (4) members, shall 
206 appoint a Town Manager for an indefinite term to serve at its pleasure.   
207  Section 2: Qualifications   
208  5-2-1 The Office of the Town Manager shall be filled based on educational, executive, 
209 and administrative qualifications and experience. The educational qualifications shall consist of 
210 at least a bachelor’s degree, preferably in public administration, granted by an accredited degree 
211 granting college or university, or professional experience that shall include at least three (3) 
212 years of prior full-time, compensated executive service in public or business administration.   
213  5-2-2 In selecting a Town Manager, the Select Board shall conduct a search for 
214 candidates, including an advertisement in a current publication serving the municipal 
215 management profession. The Town Manager shall not have held an elective Office in the Town 
216 of Orange within (2) two years immediately prior to appointment.   
217  Section 3: Powers and Duties   

12 of 33 218  5-3-1 The Town Manager shall be the Chief Administrative Officer of the Town and shall 
219 be directly responsible to the Select Board for the performance of the duties of Town Manager. 
220 The Select Board shall be responsible for setting the salary of the Town Manager, within the 
221 limits of the appropriation provided by Town Meeting.   
222  5-3-2 The Town Manager shall devote their full working time to the duties of the Office; 
223 and shall not become a candidate for or hold any elective Office during their term of 
224 appointment.                 
225  5-3-3   The Town Manager shall attend all meetings of the Select Board, except when 
226 excused, and shall have the right to speak but not to vote.   
227  5-3-4 The Town Manager will keep the Select Board fully informed regarding 
228 departmental operations, general problems, administrative actions, and any other matters of 
229 concern or importance, and to this end shall make recommendations as appropriate and submit 
230 written periodic reports to the Select Board.   
231  5-3-5   The Town Manager shall be responsible for negotiating contracts with employee 
232 bargaining units, with final review and approval by the Select Board.   
233  5-3-6 The Town Manager shall be responsible for the oversight of the Divisions of (a) 
234 Finance and Administration, (b) Public Safety, (c) Public Services, and (d) The Departments of 
235 Highway, Parks and Public Spaces, Cemetery, Sanitation, Tree Warden, Water, and Sewer and 
236 Wastewater. At such time that the Town of Orange takes action to create a Division of Public 
237 Works, the above Departments shall become the Division of Public Works and oversight shall be 
238 the responsibility of the Town Manager.  

13 of 33 239  5-3-7 The Town Manager shall be the Chief Procurement Officer with the authority to 
240 designate procurement officers for all Town Offices and departments, in accordance with 
241 Massachusetts General Laws Chapter 30 B Section 19.  The Town Manager may, subject to the 
242 approval of the Select Board, establish, reorganize, consolidate, or abolish any Department, 
243 Board, Commission, Committee, Office, or Agency under their direction and supervision, except 
244 as prohibited by general laws.   
245  5-3-8 The Town Manager shall have the authority to hire, discipline, and terminate Town 
246 employees, the authority to appoint employees for terms of one to three years, and the authority 
247 to discipline or remove appointed employees. The following exceptions to the preceding 
248 provisions shall apply: employees appointed or hired by the Library Trustees, the Water 
249 Commission, the Airport Commission, the School Committees, , the Orange Economic 
250 Development & Industrial Corp., and the Housing Authority, or those employees or officers, 
251 whether appointed or hired, as specifically otherwise provided for in this Charter. The Town 
252 Manager shall also have the authority to appoint or hire a Town Counsel with approval of a 
253 majority of the full Select Board.   
254  5-3-9 The Town Manager shall have the authority to appoint, discipline, or remove 
255 members of such Boards, Commissions and Committees except as otherwise specifically 
256 provided for in this Charter.  
257  5-3-10 The Town Manager shall perform such other duties as may be required by this 
258 Charter, bylaws, policy and procedures, vote of the Town Meeting, or vote of the Select Board.   
259  Section 4: Removal  

14 of 33 260  5-4-1   During the first six months after the initial appointment, the Select Board may 
261 remove or discipline the Town Manager by an affirmative vote of at least four members.    
262  5-4-2  After the Town Manager has been in office longer than six months, the Select 
263 Board may remove or discipline the Town Manager pursuant to provisions in the Massachusetts 
264 Open  Meeting Law, provided that no such removal shall be adopted within ninety (90) days 
265 following any Town election that has resulted in a change in the incumbents on the Board and 
266 provided further that such removal shall be by an affirmative vote of at least four  (4) members. 
267  5-4-3   A copy of all notices, requests, and motions adopted by the Select Board, and a 
268 transcript of the public hearing, if one is held, shall be filed in the Office of the Town Clerk, and 
269 shall be a public record.   
270  Section 5: Resignation   
271  5-5-1   The Town Manager shall provide the Select Board with sixty (60) days written 
272 notice of their intention to resign.   
273  Section 6: Acting Town Manager  
274  5-6-1   The Select Board shall designate a qualified person to exercise the powers and 
275 perform the duties of the Town Manager during any temporary vacancy caused by the Town 
276 Manager’s absence, suspension, removal or resignation. The appointment shall be for a period 
277 not to exceed ninety (90) days, and it may be renewed for additional periods not to exceed ninety 
278 (90) days. Members of the Select Board and members of their families, including the spouse and 
279 children of a Select Board member and a parent, brother, sister, nephew or niece of a Select 
280 Board member and their spouse, shall be ineligible to serve in this capacity.   

15 of 33 281  CHAPTER 6 – TOWN ADMINISTRATIVE ORGANIZATION 
282  Section 1: General   
283  6-1-1 The administrative functions of the Town government shall be performed within 
284 the  organizational framework of three Divisions: (a) a Division of Finance and Administration, 
285 (b) a Division of  Public Safety, (c) a Division of Public Services, and (d) the Departments of 
286 Highway, Parks and Public Spaces, Cemetery, Tree Warden, Sanitation,  Water, and Sewer and 
287 Wastewater. At such time that the Town of Orange takes action to create a Division of Public 
288 Works, the above Departments shall be carried out within the Division of Public Works.     
289            
290  6-1-2 Responsibility for the functions administered within the three Divisions and the 
291 Departments shall be vested with the Town Manager, except as provided in section 3-2-1. 
292  6-1-3 The Town Manager shall designate those Divisions to be supervised by a director 
293 and those, if any, to be supervised by a manager. If a manager is designated to act as director of 
294 one or more Divisions, the Town Manager shall serve in such dual capacity without additional 
295 compensation. A Division director, other than the manager, may be designated as head of one or 
296 more Departments within the respective Divisions, should any such Departments be established 
297 under section 5-3-8 of this Charter.   
298  Section 2: Division of Finance and Administration    
299  6-2-1 The administrative functions of assessment, tax collections, receipts, 
300 disbursements, and other functions of a fiscal nature, as well as permits, licensing and elections, 
301 shall be carried out within a Division of Finance and Administration.   

16 of 33 302  Section 3: Division of Public Safety  
303  6-3-1   The administrative functions of Emergency Management, Fire, Police, Public 
304 Health, Public  Safety in general, Inspection Services, and such other public safety services as 
305 may be assigned by this Charter or by bylaw shall be carried out within a Division of Public 
306 Safety.   
307  Section 4: Division of Public Services   
308  6-4-1 The administrative functions of general Town services, recreation, the Town 
309 library, veterans’ services, and such other public services as may be assigned by this Charter or 
310 by bylaw shall be carried out within a Division of Public Service.  
311  Section 5: Division of Public Works   
312  6-5-1   At such time that the Town of Orange takes action to create a Division of Public 
313 Works, the administrative functions of the Departments of Highway, Parks and Public Spaces, 
314 Cemetery, Sanitation, Tree Warden, Water, and Sewer and Wastewater and such other public 
315 works functions as may be assigned by this Charter or by bylaw shall be carried out within a 
316 Division of Public Works.   
317  6-5-2 The Moderator shall appoint three (3) members of the Sewer and Wastewater 
318 Commission. Each member shall be a resident of the Town, and the Commission shall include 
319 one (1) member with extensive experience in the field of public works or engineering, and one 
320 (1) member with extensive experience in the field of finance or accounting, and one (1) member 
321 at large, with three-year staggered terms. The Commission shall prepare an annual report to be 
322 delivered to the Town Manager.   

17 of 33 323  Section 6: Personnel System  
324  6-6-1   All appointments and promotions of Town Officers and employees shall be made 
325 based on merit and fitness demonstrated by an examination and/or other evidence of competence.   
326  6-6-2  The Town Manager, except as otherwise provided by general laws, shall propose  
327 personnel policies and procedures to include: (a) the classification of all positions, based on the 
328 duties, responsibilities and authority of each position, with adequate provisions for  
329 reclassification of any position whenever warranted by changed circumstances; (b) a salary and 
330 pay plan for all positions; (c) methods of determining the merit and fitness of  candidates for 
331 appointment and promotion; (d) policies and procedures regulating  reduction in force and 
332 removal of employees; (e) hours of work, attendance regulations,  and provisions for sick, 
333 vacation, military, family medical and other leave; (f) policies  and procedures governing 
334 persons holding provisional appointments; (g) policies regarding in-service training programs; 
335 (h) grievance procedures including procedures for the hearing of such grievances; and (i) such 
336 other practices and procedures as may be  necessary for the administration of the personnel 
337 system. The proposed rules shall be presented for discussion and comment to the Select Board 
338 convened in a public session. Upon vote of the Select Board, the policies shall become 
339 immediately effective. A copy of such policies shall be filed for record purposes with the Town 
340 Clerk and shall be made available to all Town employees. Said policies may, from time to time, 
341 be amended or revised following the same procedure of adoption stated above.   
342  CHAPTER 7 – ELECTIONS   
343  Section 1: Town Election   

18 of 33 344  7-1-1 The regular election for all Town Offices shall be held on the first Monday of 
345 March.   
346  Section 2: Elections to be Nonpartisan   
347  7-2-1   All elections of Town Officers shall be nonpartisan, and election ballots for Town 
348 Offices shall be printed without any party mark, emblem, or designation whatsoever.  
349  Section 3: Eligibility of Town Voters   
350  7-3-1   Any registered voter of the Town shall be eligible for election to any elective 
351 Office or Board of the Town.   
352  Section 4: Time of Taking Office.   
353  7-4-1 Any person duly elected to any Office or Board shall take up the duties of their 
354 Office  immediately following certification, provided that they first shall have been sworn to the 
355 faithful performance of their duties by the Town Clerk.   
356  Section 5: Recall of Elected Offices   
357  7-5-1   Any holder of an elective Office in the Town may be recalled by the registered 
358 voters of said Town as herein provided.   
359  7-5-2   Any one hundred and fifty (150) registered voters of the Town may initiate a 
360 recall  petition by filing with the Town Clerk an affidavit containing the name of the official  
361 sought to be recalled and a statement of the grounds for such a recall. Said Town Clerk shall 
362 thereupon deliver to such voters making the affidavit copies of petition blanks demanding such 
363 recall, copies of which printed forms said Town Clerk shall keep available. Such blanks shall be 

19 of 33 364 issued by said Town Clerk, with said Town Clerk’s signature and official seal attached thereto. 
365 Such petition shall be dated, shall be addressed to the Select Board and shall contain the names 
366 of all persons to whom they are issued, the name of the person whose recall is sought, and the 
367 grounds for such recall as stated in the affidavit, and they shall demand the election of a 
368 successor in said Office. A copy of such petition shall be entered in a record book to be kept in 
369 the Office of said Town Clerk. Said recall petition shall be returned and filed with said Town 
370 Clerk within thirty (30) days after the filing of the affidavit, the designated petition shall have 
371 been signed only by registered voters of the town and shall have been signed by at least ten (10) 
372 percent of the number of the registered voters who shall add their signatures and the street 
373 number of their residences.  
374  The Town Clerk shall, within three (3) business days of receipt of the recall petition, 
375 submit such petition to the registrars of voters in said Town, and said registrars shall, within five 
376 (5) business days, certify thereon the number of signatures which are names of registered voters 
377 of said Town.   
378  7-5-3   If the recall petition shall be found and certified by the Town Clerk, said Town 
379 Clerk shall submit the same with the Town Clerk’s certificate to the Select  Board within five (5) 
380 working days and said Select Board shall, within five (5) working days,  give written notice of 
381 the receipt of the certificate to the official sought to be recalled and shall, if the official does not 
382 resign within seven (7) days thereafter, order an election to be held on a date fixed by them not 
383 less than sixty (60) days and not more than one hundred and  twenty (120) days after the date of 
384 the Town Clerk’s certification that a sufficient petition has been filed; provided, however, that if 
385 any other Town election is to occur within sixty (60) to ninety (90) days after the date of the 
386 certificate, said Select Board shall postpone the  recall election to the date of such other election.    

20 of 33 387  7-5-4   An official sought to be removed may be a candidate to succeed themselves and, 
388 unless they request otherwise in writing, the Town Clerk shall place their name on a ballot 
389 without nomination.   
390  The nomination of other candidates, the publication of the warrant for the removal 
391 election, and the conduct of the same shall be in accordance with the provisions of law relating to 
392 elections, unless otherwise provided in this section.    
393  7-5-5 The incumbent shall continue to perform the duties of their Office until the recall
