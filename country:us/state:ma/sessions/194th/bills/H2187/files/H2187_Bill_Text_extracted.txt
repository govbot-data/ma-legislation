By Representative Vaughn of Wrentham, a petition (accompanied by bill, House, No. 2187) of
Marcus S. Vaughn that the Department of Unemployment Assistance online unemployment
benefits portal have a status bar on every individual’s profile displaying the progress of the
applicant’s claim. Labor and Workforce Development.
The Commonwealth of Massachusetts
_______________
In the One Hundred and Ninety-Fourth General Court
(2025-2026)
_______________
An Act relative to modernizing the unemployment benefits online portal.
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority
of the same, as follows:
1 Section 22 of chapter 151A of the General Laws, as appearing in the 2022 Official
2 Edition, is hereby amended by inserting after the second sentence the following:-
3 The department of unemployment assistance shall maintain, as part of its online
4 unemployment benefits portal, a status bar on every individual’s profile displaying the progress
5 of the applicant’s claim. The department shall determine which milestones in the review process
6 shall be displayed.
