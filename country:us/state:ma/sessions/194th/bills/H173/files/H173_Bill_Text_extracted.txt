An Act relative to the regulation of Delta-9 THC.
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority
of the same, as follows:
1 SECTION 1. Section 1 of Chapter 94G of the General Laws, as appearing in the 2022
2 Official Edition, is hereby amended by inserting the following definitions:
3 Delta-9 Tetrahydrocannabinol (Delta-9 THC), The chemical compound found in the
4 cannabis plant that is primarily responsible for its psychoactive effects, commonly referred to as
5 THC, regardless of concentration level in cannabis or cannabis products.
6 Delta-9 THC Products, Any product containing Delta-9 THC, regardless of whether the
7 product is derived from marijuana or hemp, that is intended for human consumption, including
8 but not limited to edibles, concentrates, tinctures, oils, or other consumables.
9 SECTION 2. Chapter 94G of the General Laws, as so appearing, is hereby amended by
10 inserting after Section 3 the following section:
1 of 3

11 Section 3A. Regulation of Delta-9 Tetrahydrocannabinol Products by the Cannabis
12 Control Commission
13 (a) The Cannabis Control Commission shall be responsible for the regulation, licensure,
14 and oversight of all Delta-9 THC products within the Commonwealth of Massachusetts,
15 irrespective of whether such products are derived from marijuana or hemp.
16 (b) No person shall manufacture, distribute, or sell any Delta-9 THC product in the
17 Commonwealth unless such product is licensed and registered with the Cannabis Control
18 Commission in accordance with this chapter.
19 (c) The Cannabis Control Commission shall establish and enforce rules, regulations, and
20 standards governing Delta-9 THC products, including, but not limited to: product safety
21 standards, including the testing of Delta-9 THC concentration levels in cannabis products, to
22 ensure compliance with all applicable public health and safety standards; packaging and labeling
23 requirements to clearly state the Delta-9 THC content, appropriate serving sizes, safety warnings,
24 and any other necessary consumer information; licensing requirements for producers,
25 manufacturers, distributors, and retailers of Delta-9 THC products; and advertising and
26 marketing restrictions to prevent any promotional activities that target or appeal to minors or that
27 would otherwise pose a risk to public health.
28 (d) The Cannabis Control Commission may issue fines or penalties for non-compliance
29 with the regulations established under this section and may suspend or revoke licenses for
30 egregious violations.
31 SECTION 3. Section 6 of Chapter 94G of the General Laws, as appearing in the 2022
32 Official Edition, is hereby amended by inserting after subsection (b) the following subsection:
2 of 3

33 (c) Notwithstanding any general or special law to the contrary, the Cannabis Control
34 Commission shall have the authority to regulate all cannabis-related products, including Delta-9
35 THC products, regardless of their origin, including marijuana or hemp, to ensure that they are
36 produced, distributed, and consumed safely in accordance with state laws.
37 SECTION 4. Chapter 94G of the General Laws, as so appearing, is hereby amended by
38 adding after section 8 the following section:
39 Section 8A. Annual Report on Delta-9 THC Product Oversight
40 (a) The Cannabis Control Commission shall submit an annual report to the clerks’ office
41 of the house of representatives and the senate on the status of Delta-9 THC product regulation,
42 including but not limited to the number of licenses issued, the volume of Delta-9 THC products
43 sold, and any public health concerns or issues identified.
44 (b) The report shall also include recommendations for any changes or improvements to
45 the regulation and oversight of Delta-9 THC products in the Commonwealth.
46 SECTION 5. All Delta-9 THC products in circulation at the time of enactment of this
47 legislation that do not comply with the newly established regulatory framework shall have a
48 period of no less than 180 days from the effective date of this act to come into compliance with
49 the Cannabis Control Commission’s licensing and regulatory requirements.
50 SECTION 6. This act shall take effect on the first day of the month following 90 days
51 after passage.
