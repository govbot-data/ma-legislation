HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES, March 12, 2026. 
 The committee on Labor and Workforce Development, to whom were 
referred the petition (accompanied by bill, House, No. 2095) of Michelle 
M. DuBois relative to non-disclosure agreements relating to a claim of 
discrimination, non-payment of wages or benefits, retaliation, harassment 
or violation of public policy in employment; the petition (accompanied by 
bill, House, No. 2176) of Priscila S. Sousa and Manny Cruz relative to 
employer disclosure of immigration status to conceal violation of state 
wage, benefit, or tax laws; and the petition (accompanied by bill, House, 
No. 4208) of Angelo J. Puppolo, Jr., relative to damages for employees 
aggrieved by violations of the Massachusetts Wage Act, reports 
recommending that the accompanying order (House, No. 5209) ought to be 
adopted. 
 
For the committee, 
 
PAUL MCMURTRY.
