An Act regarding free expression.
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority
of the same, as follows:
1 SECTION 1. Chapter 71 of the General Laws is hereby amended by inserting after
2 section 82 the following 3 sections:-
3 Section 82A. (a) A determination that materials are appropriate for inclusion in a school
4 library by a school library teacher licensed by the department or, in the absence of such school
5 library teacher, by the school employee responsible for the selection and display of library
6 materials shall: (i) be age appropriate; (ii) serve an educational purpose; and (iii) be based on the
7 teacher’s or employee’s professional training and not on personal, political or doctrinal views.
8 For the purposes of this section a school library shall mean a library within a school department
9 of a city or town, a regional school, an independent vocational school or a vocational school or
10 agricultural school operated by a county.
11 (b) No challenged material selected for inclusion in a school library collection pursuant to
12 subsection (a) shall be removed except upon a determination and vote by the school committee
1 of 7

13 or board of trustees of a charter school following: (i) notice; (ii) a public hearing; and (iii) a
14 finding by a review committee of school personnel, appointed by: (A) the school superintendent,
15 in consultation with the school committee; or (B) in the case of a charter school, the board of
16 trustees, that the material is devoid of any educational, literary, artistic, personal or social value
17 or is not age appropriate for any child who attends the school.. A school committee shall only
18 consider a challenge to material in a school library from an individual who is a staff member
19 employed by a school overseen by the school committee, a parent or guardian of a student
20 currently enrolled in such a school or a student currently enrolled in such a school.
21 (c) A decision of the school committee or the board of trustees of a charter school to
22 remove materials from a school library may be challenged by a student, parent, guardian or the
23 author or creator of the materials through a petition for a writ of mandamus in the supreme
24 judicial court or superior court.
25 (d) Nothing in this section shall preclude school employees responsible for school library
26 content from deciding to remove materials that have become outdated or to accommodate new
27 materials; provided, however, that the decision to remove materials shall not made based on
28 personal, political or doctrinal views.
29 Section 82B. Each school district, charter school and local education agency shall
30 establish a written policy for its school library for: (i) the selection of school library materials,
31 including textbooks, instructional materials, books or other library resources; (ii) the use of
32 library materials and library facilities; and (iii) responding to challenges requesting to remove or
33 restrict library materials. The written policy shall: (i) be in accordance with standards adopted by
34 the American Library Association; (ii) be posted on the school district, charter school or local
2 of 7

35 education agency’s website; and (iii) provide that if material selected by the school library
36 teacher or responsible school employee is the subject of a challenge requesting to remove or
37 restrict its use, such material shall remain on the shelves of the school library pending a vote of
38 the school committee or the board of trustees of a charter school made during the process for
39 responding to such challenge in accordance with subsection (b) of section 82A. The board of
40 library commissioners for the commonwealth through its affiliate, the Massachusetts Library
41 System, shall: (i) make resources available to help school committees or other administrative
42 authority of a school library create or modify written policies for the selection of library
43 materials and the use of materials and facilities in accordance with the standards adopted by the
44 American Library Association; and (ii) make sample policies available on its website.
45 Annually, school districts, charter schools and local education agencies shall submit a
46 report to the department, which shall include information on all challenges requesting to remove
47 or restrict educational materials from its schools during the previous school year.
48 Section 82C. No school employee shall lose the employee’s department-issued license or
49 professional certification or be dismissed, disciplined, placed on probation, involuntarily
50 transferred, be otherwise subjected to an adverse employment action, fined or imprisoned for the
51 selection of library materials when the selection is made in good faith and in accordance with the
52 policy adopted pursuant to section 82B.
