An Act to prevent medical debt by restoring health safety net eligibility levels.
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority
of the same, as follows:
1 SECTION 1. Section 65 of chapter 118E, as so appearing in the General Laws, is hereby
2 amended by inserting the following words in subsection (b)(iii) after the words “determine the
3 eligibility of uninsured and underinsured patients for reimbursable health services”:-
4 , pursuant to subsection (c) of this section,
5 SECTION 2. Section 65 of chapter 118E, as so appearing in the General Laws, is hereby
6 amended by inserting after subsection (b) the following subsection:-
7 (c) The office shall develop eligibility criteria for uninsured or underinsured patients in
8 accordance with, but not limited to, the following guidelines:
9 (i) Uninsured or underinsured patients, who meet all other eligibility criteria as
10 determined by the office, up to 400 percent of the federal poverty level, as calculated pursuant to
11 the regulations of the executive office, shall be eligible;
1 of 2

12 (ii) eligible patients whose income exceeds 200 percent of the federal poverty level, but
13 does not exceed 400 percent of the federal poverty level, may be subject to a deductible; and
14 (iii) eligibility shall be retroactive to at least 180 days prior to receipt of an application for
15 an eligible individual.
16 Nothing in this subsection shall be construed to limit the office’s ability to establish
17 patient eligibility at income levels higher than those specified herein and/or to provide greater
18 retroactive coverage than required by this paragraph.
19 SECTION 3. The health safety net office, established under section 65 of chapter 118E,
20 in consultation with the office of Medicaid, shall convene a task force composed of experts that
21 shall include, but is not limited to, a consumer advocate representative, a hospital representative,
22 a community health center representative, and a health insurance plan representative to
23 investigate and study the adequacy of the financing of the Health Safety Net Trust Fund
24 established under section 66 of chapter 118E, including but not limited to the fund’s shortfall, the
25 amounts paid to the fund by hospitals, ambulatory surgical centers, and surcharge payors
26 established under sections 67 and 68 of chapter 118E, reimbursements made from the fund to
27 hospitals and community health centers established under section 69 of chapter 118E, and
28 transfers to and from the fund.
29 All appointments shall be made not later than 30 days after the effective date of this
30 legislation. The task force shall meet not later than 60 days after the effective date of this
31 legislation.
32 Not later than 1 year after the effective date of this legislation, the task force shall report
33 to the general court the result of its investigation and study and its recommendations.
