Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority

1 SECTION 1. Section 17I of chapter 32A of the General Laws, as so appearing in the
2 2022 Official Edition, is hereby amended by striking out subsection (b) and inserting in place
3 thereof the following subsection:-
4 (b) For the purposes of this section the following words shall, unless the context clearly
5 requires otherwise, have the following meanings:
6 “Orthosis”, a device: (i) used to support, align, correct or prevent deformities of the body,
7 which may be used to eliminate, control or assist motion at a joint or body part; and (ii)
8 appropriately used in a person’s home or any setting in which normal life activities take place in
9 the community.

10 “Prosthetic device”, an artificial limb device to replace, in whole or in part, an arm or leg
11 including a device that is designed exclusively for athletic purposes.
12 SECTION 2. Said section 17I of said chapter 32A, as so appearing, is hereby further
13 amended by adding the following subsection:-
14 (g) The commission shall provide coverage for prosthetic devices and orthotic devices,
15 including those designed, custom-built or fitted for a specific enrollee, for the performance of
16 physical activities or for an athletic purpose, as applicable, to maximize the enrollee’s ability to
17 ambulate, run, bike and swim and to maximize upper limb function. The coverage required
18 pursuant to this subsection shall include the repair or replacement of a prosthetic or orthotic
19 device for the performance of physical activities or for an athletic purpose.

30 (b) The division shall provide coverage for prosthetic and orthotic devices including the
31 repair or replacement of prosthetic or orthotic devices to eligible MassHealth members under the
32 same terms and conditions that apply to other durable medical equipment. The coverage required
33 by this section shall be subject to the terms and conditions applicable to other benefits.
34 (c) The division shall provide coverage for prosthetic devices and orthotic devices,
35 including those designed, custom-built or fitted for a specific enrollee, for the performance of
36 physical activities or for an athletic purpose, as applicable, to maximize the enrollee’s
37 ability to ambulate, run, bike and swim and to maximize upper limb function. The coverage
38 required pursuant to this subsection shall include the repair or replacement of a prosthetic or
39 orthotic device for the performance of physical activities or for an athletic purpose.
40 (d) Eligible MassHealth members shall be required to provide a written prescription
41 signed by a licensed physician or an independent nurse practitioner. The prescription must be
42 written on the prescriber's prescription form and must include the following information:
43 (i) the member's name and address;
44 (ii) the member’s MassHealth identification number;
45 (iii) specific identification of the prescribed item;
46 (iv) medical justification for the use of the item, including the member’s diagnosis;
47 (v) the prescriber's address and telephone number; and
48 (vi) the date on which the prescription was signed by the prescriber.
