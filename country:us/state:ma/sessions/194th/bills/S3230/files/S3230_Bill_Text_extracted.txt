An Act authorizing the Massachusetts Department of Transportation to acquire interests in 
certain portions of land owned by the town of Andover which were acquired by the town for 
Article XCVII purposes. 
 
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority 
of the same, as follows: 
 
1  SECTION 1. Notwithstanding any general or special law to the contrary, the 
2 Massachusetts Department of Transportation may acquire from the town of Andover, acting by 
3 and through its conservation commission, for highway purposes interests in certain portions of 
4 land owned by the conservation commission of the town of Andover and identified as permanent 
5 easement parcel numbers 20-E-1 and 20-PUE-1-T on a plan of land entitled “Massachusetts 
6 Department of Transportation Highway Division Plan And Profile Of Tewksbury Street Over 
7 CSX Railroad (Bridge No. A-09-015) In The Town of Andover, Essex County, Preliminary 
8 Right of Way Plans”. The impact to the protected land to be acquired by the Massachusetts 
9 Department of Transportation is limited to permanent easements of approximately 704 square 
10 feet for guy wire placement, guardrail removal and bridge special slope paving. 

2 of 2 11  SECTION 2. The secretary of energy and environmental affairs may waive the 
12 requirement that suitable replacement land be provided by any entity acquiring an interest in land 
13 protected by Article XCVII of the Amendments to the Constitution of the Commonwealth 
14 pursuant to clause (B) of the last sentence of subsection (a) of section 5A of chapter 3 of the 
15 General Laws. As reasons for such waiver, the land transferred pursuant to this act is of 
16 insignificant natural resource and recreation value, is less than 2,500 square feet in area and the 
17 benefit serves a significant public interest. 
18  SECTION 3. The Massachusetts Department of Transportation shall assume all costs 
19 associated with engineering, surveys, appraisals, deed preparation and other expenses necessary 
20 to execute the conveyances authorized by this act.
