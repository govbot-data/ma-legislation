RESOLUTIONS CALLING ON THE PRESIDENT OF THE UNITED STATES TO RELEASE 
CONTINGENCY FUNDS FOR THE SUPPLEMENTAL NUTRITION ASSISTANCE 
PROGRAM FOR THE DURATION OF THE ONGOING FEDERAL GOVERNMENT 
SHUTDOWN. 
 
1  WHEREAS, THE SUPPLEMENTAL NUTRITION ASSISTANCE PROGRAM IS 1 OF 
2 THE NATION’S MOST EFFECTIVE AND EFFICIENT FOOD SECURITY PROGRAMS, 
3 PROVIDING CRITICAL SUPPORT TO MORE THAN 40 MILLION UNITED STATES 
4 RESIDENTS, INCLUDING 1 MILLION RESIDENTS OF THE COMMONWEALTH; AND 
5  WHEREAS, ANY DISRUPTION IN SUPPLEMENTAL NUTRITION ASSISTANCE 
6 PROGRAM BENEFITS, EVEN BRIEF, WOULD THREATEN THE FOOD SECURITY AND 
7 WELL-BEING OF RESIDENTS OF THE COMMONWEALTH, DISPROPORTIONATELY 
8 BURDENING SENIORS, CHILDREN AND INDIVIDUALS LIVING WITH DISABILITIES; 
9 AND 
10  WHEREAS, MORE THAN 5,500 RETAILERS IN THE COMMONWEALTH 
11 PARTICIPATE IN THE SUPPLEMENTAL NUTRITION ASSISTANCE PROGRAM, WITH 
12 PURCHASES ACCOUNTING FOR UP TO 20 PER CENT OF ALL GROCERY SPENDING 

2 of 3 13 IN SOME STORES, SUCH THAT AN INTERRUPTION IN BENEFITS WOULD 
14 DESTABILIZE LOCAL GROCERS, UNDERMINE JOBS AND CAUSE CASCADING 
15 HARM TO FARMERS, FISHERS AND FOOD PRODUCERS; AND 
16  WHEREAS, EMERGENCY FOOD PROVIDERS ACROSS THE COMMONWEALTH 
17 HAVE ALREADY REPORTED SHARP INCREASES IN DEMAND AND WHILE THE 
18 LEGISLATURE AND ADMINISTRATION HAVE EXPANDED INVESTMENTS IN STATE-
19 BASED ANTI-HUNGER INITIATIVES, INCLUDING THE MASSACHUSETTS 
20 EMERGENCY FOOD ASSISTANCE PROGRAM, UNIVERSAL SCHOOL MEALS AND 
21 THE HEALTHY INCENTIVES PROGRAM, THE COMMONWEALTH CANNOT ABSORB 
22 THE $210,000,000 PER MONTH IN FEDERAL SUPPLEMENTAL NUTRITION 
23 ASSISTANCE PROGRAM PAYMENTS THAT WOULD BE LOST IF NOT FUNDED 
24 DURING THE ONGOING FEDERAL GOVERNMENT SHUTDOWN; AND 
25  WHEREAS, FOR EVERY 1 MEAL PROVIDED BY A FOOD BANK, THE 
26 SUPPLEMENTAL NUTRITION ASSISTANCE PROGRAM  PROVIDES 9 MEALS, 
27 UNDERSCORING THE IRREPLACEABLE ROLE OF FEDERAL SUPPORT IN 
28 PREVENTING HUNGER AND HARDSHIP; NOW, THEREFORE BE IT 
29  RESOLVED, THAT THE MASSACHUSETTS SENATE HEREBY URGES THE 
30 PRESIDENT OF THE UNITED STATES TO USE ALL EXISTING AUTHORITY TO 
31 SUPPLEMENT AND TRANSFER AVAILABLE FUNDS TO SUSTAIN SUPPLEMENTAL 
32 NUTRITION ASSISTANCE PROGRAM BENEFITS THROUGHOUT THE DURATION OF 
33 THE ONGOING FEDERAL GOVERNMENT SHUTDOWN; AND BE IT FURTHER 

3 of 3 34  RESOLVED, THAT THE MASSACHUSETTS SENATE CALLS UPON THE 
35 MASSACHUSETTS CONGRESSIONAL DELEGATION TO ADVOCATE FOR AND 
36 SUPPORT IMMEDIATE FEDERAL ACTION TO ENSURE THE CONTINUATION OF 
37 SUPPLEMENTAL NUTRITION ASSISTANCE PROGRAM BENEFITS; AND BE IT 
38 FURTHER 
39  RESOLVED, THAT COPIES OF THESE RESOLUTIONS BE TRANSMITTED 
40 FORTHWITH BY THE CLERK OF THE SENATE TO THE PRESIDENT OF THE UNITED 
41 STATES AND TO EACH MEMBER OF THE MASSACHUSETTS CONGRESSIONAL 
42 DELEGATION.
