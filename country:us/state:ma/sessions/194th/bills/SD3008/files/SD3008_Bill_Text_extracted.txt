SENATE DOCKET, NO. 3008 FILED ON: 6/26/2025
Senate, June 26, 2025 -- (Filed by Ms. Creem) Order relative to the membership and matters
currently before the joint committee on Agriculture and the joint committee on Elder Affairs.
The Commonwealth of Massachusetts
Senate, June 26, 2025.
_______________
In the One Hundred and Ninety-Fourth General Court
(2025-2026)
_______________
1 Ordered, That all matters that are currently before the joint committee on Agriculture
2 established under the order establishing the temporary rules of the two branches for the 2025-
3 2026 legislative session are hereby transferred to the joint committee on Agriculture and
4 Fisheries; and
5 Be it further ordered, that all matters currently before the joint committee on Elder Affairs
6 established under the order establishing the temporary rules of the two branches for the 2025-
7 2026 legislative session are hereby transferred to the joint committee on Aging and
8 Independence; and
9 Be it further ordered, that the current members of the joint committee on Agriculture so
10 established shall hereby be the members of the joint committee on Agriculture and Fisheries and
11 the current members of the joint committee on Elder Affairs so established shall hereby be the
12 members of the joint committee on Aging and Independence.
