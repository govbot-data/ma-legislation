An Act relative to the Foundation Budget Review Commission.
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority
of the same, as follows:
1 Notwithstanding any general or special law to the contrary The Foundation Budget
2 Review Commission prescribed by Ch. 70, S. 4 of the General Laws shall convene not later than
3 January 2, 2026, provided that, in addition to the review of the items identified in said section,
4 the commission shall also consider the challenges presented for school districts with declining
5 enrollment, the increased costs associated with social-emotional learning and mental health, the
6 costs associated with special education, and the methodology employed to calculate required
7 local contribution by municipalities, provided further that, in establishing the scope of its
8 operations, the commission shall conduct no less than 3 public hearings in geographically diverse
9 locations in the commonwealth, which shall accommodate remote electronic participation. The
10 commission shall file a final report, including legislative recommendations, no later than
11 September 30, 2026, with the clerks of the House and Senate, the House and Senate Committees
12 on Ways and Means, the Joint Committee on Education, the Department of Elementary and
1 of 2

13 Secondary Education, and the Executive Office of Education provided that the commission may
14 file interim reports from time to time in the same manner.
