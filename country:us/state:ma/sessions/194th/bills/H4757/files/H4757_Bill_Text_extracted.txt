An Act promoting housing opportunities and access.
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority
of the same, as follows:
1 SECTION 1. Section 1A of chapter 40A of the General Laws is hereby amended by
2 adding the following 3 definitions:-
3 “Excluded Land”, land defined by reference to the ownership, use codes, use restrictions,
4 and hydrological characteristics in MassGIS and consists of the following: (i) All Publicly-
5 owned land, except for Lots or portions of Lots determined to be Developable public land; (ii)
6 All rivers, streams, lakes, ponds and other surface waterbodies; (iii) All wetland resource areas,
7 together with a buffer zone around wetlands and waterbodies equivalent to the minimum setback
8 required by 310 CMR 15.00: The State Environmental Code, Title 5: Standard Requirements for
9 the Siting, Construction, Inspection, Upgrade and Expansion of On-site Sewage Treatment and
10 Disposal Systems and for the TransPort and Disposal of Septage; (iv) Protected open space and
11 recreational land that is legally protected in perpetuity (for example, land owned by a local land
12 trust or subject to a conservation restriction), or that is likely to remain undeveloped due to
13 functional or traditional use (for example, cemeteries); (v) All Public rights-of-way and Private
14 rights-of-way; (vi) Privately-owned land on which development is prohibited to protect private
1 of 5

15 or public water supplies, including, but not limited to, Zone I wellhead protection areas and Zone
16 A surface water supply protection areas; (vii) Privately-owned land used for educational or
17 institutional uses such as a hospital, prison, electric, water, wastewater or other utility, museum,
18 or private school, college or university. If privately owned land that would otherwise be excluded
19 is no longer being used for such educational or institutional uses, EOHLC may determine that
20 such land no longer being so used is Developable Land.
21 “Calculable developable land”, land on which multi-family housing can be permitted and
22 constructed, consisting of (a) all privately-owned land except Lots or portions of Lots that meet
23 the definition of Excluded land; and (b) Developable public land.
24 "Developable land area'', that area within an approved smart growth district that can be
25 feasibly developed into residential or mixed use development determined in accordance with
26 regulations of the department. Developable land area shall not include: (1) land area that is
27 already substantially developed, including existing parks and dedicated, perpetual open space
28 within such substantially developed portion; (2) open space designated by the city or town as
29 provided in section 6; or (3) areas exceeding one-half acre of contiguous land that are unsuitable
30 for development because of topographic features or for environmental reasons, such as wetlands.
31 SECTION 2. Section 9 of chapter 40A, as so appearing, is hereby amended by inserting
32 after the second paragraph the following paragraph:-
33 In a city or town where less than 10% of the housing units are located in multi-family
34 structures, the city or town shall establish one or more multifamily zoning districts consisting of
35 1.5% of all calculable developable land. The land within the multifamily zoning district(s)
36 should adhere to the definition of “developable land area” as defined in Section 1A of this
2 of 5

37 chapter. Zoning ordinances or by-laws shall establish a housing density for by-right multifamily
38 development in such zoning districts of not less than twenty (20) dwelling units per acre. As used
39 herein, “multifamily housing” means apartment or condominium units in buildings which
40 contain or will contain more than three (3) such units.
41 SECTION 3. (a) Section 9 of chapter 40A, as so appearing, is hereby amended by
42 striking out, in the fifth paragraph, the words “open space residential developments or”.
43 (b) Section 9 of chapter 40A, as so appearing, is hereby amended by striking out the
44 sixth paragraph and inserting in place thereof the following paragraph:-
45 Notwithstanding any provision of this section to the contrary, zoning ordinances or by-
46 laws shall provide that open space residential development shall be permitted by right in
47 residential zoning districts at the density permitted in the zoning district in which the property is
48 located upon review and approval by a planning board pursuant to the applicable provisions of
49 sections 81K to 81GG, inclusive, of chapter 41 and in accordance with its rules and regulations
50 governing subdivision control. Zoning ordinances and by-laws shall not require the submission
51 of a plan showing a standard subdivision complying with the otherwise applicable requirements
52 of the ordinance or by-laws as a condition precedent to the approval of an open space residential
53 development plan.

60 SECTION 4. (a) The twelfth paragraph of Section 9 of chapter 40A, as so appearing, is
61 hereby amended by deleting the words “a two-thirds vote of boards with more than five
62 members, a vote of at least four members of a five member board, and a unanimous vote of a
63 three member board” and inserting in place thereof the following words:- the concurring vote of
64 a majority of the members then in office.

67 members, and a concurring vote of four members of a board consisting of five members” and

70 SECTION 5. All cities and towns in the commonwealth shall determine their calculable
71 developable land by December 1st, 2026.
