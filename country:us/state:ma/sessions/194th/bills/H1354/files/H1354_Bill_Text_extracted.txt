23  SECTION 6. Chapter 118E of the General Laws, as so appearing, is hereby amended by 
24 adding the following new section:- 
25  Section 68A. Supplemental MCO Assessment to Fund Shortfall 
26  (a) No later than April 1 of the year preceding the start of the fund fiscal year, the 
27 executive office shall estimate the projected total reimbursable health services provided by acute 
28 hospitals and community health centers and emergency bad debt costs, the total funding 
29 available and any projected shortfall after adjusting for reimbursement payments to hospitals and 
30 community health centers. If a shortfall in revenue is estimated to exist in any fund fiscal year to 
31 cover projected costs for reimbursement of health services, the executive office shall allocate 50 

3 of 4 32 percent of that shortfall amount to managed care organizations subject to an assessment in 
33 section 68 of Chapter 118E and that are not Medicaid managed care organizations. 
34  (b) The executive office shall allocate the supplemental shortfall assessment in a manner 
35 consistent with the methods promulgated by the executive office to determine the managed care 
36 liability defined in section 68, with the exception that the supplemental shortfall assessment shall 
37 only be assessed to managed care organizations that are not Medicaid managed care 
38 organizations. 
39  (c) The supplemental shortfall assessment shall be paid to the Health Safety Net Trust 
40 Fund, established in section 66, by managed care organizations subject to the assessment on a 
41 monthly basis and shall be assessed on all managed care organization services subject to 
42 assessment. The executive office shall finalize the supplemental surcharge amount for each fund 
43 fiscal year using best available data no later than 60 days after the fund fiscal year end. 
44  (d) The secretary of health and human services shall implement the supplemental 
45 shortfall assessment described in this section and shall promulgate regulations necessary to 
46 support implementation of said assessment. In promulgating such regulations, the secretary of 
47 health and human services shall, at a minimum: (i) establish set assessment rates for the 
48 supplemental shortfall assessment; (ii) establish any necessary reporting requirements for 
49 managed care organizations; (iii) establish an appropriate mechanism for enforcing each 
50 managed care organization’s supplemental shortfall assessment liability to the Health Safety Net 
51 Trust Fund, established in section 66, if a managed care organization rendering managed care 
52 organization services subject to assessment does not make a scheduled payment to the Health 
53 Safety Net Trust Fund; (iv) specify an appropriate mechanism for determination and payment of 

4 of 4 54 a managed care organization’s supplemental shortfall assessment liability to the Health Safety 
55 Net Trust Fund; (v) identify the managed care organization services subject to the supplemental 
56 shortfall assessment; (vi) specify an appropriate mechanism for the determination of a managed 
57 care organization’s liability in cases of merger or transfer of ownership; and (vii) specify an 
58 appropriate mechanism by which any amounts paid by a managed care organization in excess of 
59 its supplemental shortfall assessment may be refunded or otherwise credited to the managed care 
60 organization. 
61  SECTION 7. Section 69 of Chapter 118E of the General Laws, as so appearing, is hereby

65 that reflects each hospital's proportional financial requirement for reimbursements from the fund, 
66 including, but not limited to, the establishment of a graduated reimbursement system and under 
67 any additional regulations promulgated by the office.”
