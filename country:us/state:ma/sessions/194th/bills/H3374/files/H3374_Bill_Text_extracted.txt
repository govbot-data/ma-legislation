An Act relative to Chapter 30B procurement through RFPs and cooperative contracts. 
 
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority 
of the same, as follows: 
 
1  SECTION 1. Section 4 of chapter 30B of the General Laws, as appearing in the 2022 
2 Official Edition, is hereby amended by striking out subsection (a) and inserting in place thereof 
3 the following subsection:- 
4  (a) Except as permitted pursuant to this section and section 7, for the procurement of a 
5 supply or service for a governmental body in the amount of $10,000 or greater, but not more than 
6 $50,000, a procurement officer shall seek written quotations from no fewer than 3 persons 
7 customarily providing the supply or service; provided, however, that for the procurement of a 
8 supply or service for a municipal or regional school district such amount shall be $10,000 or 
9 greater, but not more than $100,000. The procurement officer shall record: (1) the names and 
10 addresses of all person from whom quotations were sought, (2) the purchase description used for 

2 of 3 11 the procurement, (3) the names of the persons submitting quotations and (4) the date and amount 
12 of each quotation. Such information shall be retained in the file required pursuant to section 3. A 
13 governmental body may require that any procurement for a governmental body in an amount of 
14 not more than $50,000 be subject to section 5 or section 6; provided, however, that any 
15 procurement for a municipal or regional school district such amount shall be not more than 
16 $100,000. 
17  SECTION 2. Section 4 of chapter 30B of the General Laws is hereby amended by 
18 striking out subsection (b) and inserting in place thereof the following subsection:- 
19  (b) The procurement officer shall award the contract to the responsible person offering 
20 the needed quality of supply or service at the lowest quotation unless the procurement officer 
21 elected to procure the supply or service subject to section 6. If the supply or service was procured 
22 subject to section 6, the procurement officer shall award the contract pursuant to section 6 
23 subsection (g). 
24  SECTION 3. Section 6 of said Chapter 30B is hereby amended in paragraph (a) by 
25 inserting after the figure “$50,000” the following words: “except as permitted pursuant to section 
26 4 subsection (a),” 
27  SECTION 4. Section 22 of said Chapter 30B is hereby amended by striking out Section 
28 22 in its entirety and inserting in place thereof the following section:- Section 22. A public 
29 procurement unit may participate in, sponsor, conduct or administer a cooperative purchasing 
30 agreement for the procurement of supplies and services bid in accordance with Massachusetts 
31 General Law Chapter 30B with public procurement units or external procurement activities in 
32 accordance with an agreement entered into between the participants. The public procurement 

3 of 3 33 unit conducting the procurement of supplies and services shall do so in a manner that constitutes 
34 a full and open competition.
