An Act establishing Getting to Zero Week.
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority
of the same, as follows:
1 The governor shall annually issue a proclamation setting apart December first through the
2 eighth as Getting to Zero Week to increase public awareness, understanding, and recognition of
3 our communities’ shared commitment to end the HIV and AIDS epidemic in the Commonwealth
4 by getting to zero new cases, zero HIV-related deaths, and zero stigma. The proclamation shall
5 recommend that the week in December be observed in an appropriate manner by the people.
