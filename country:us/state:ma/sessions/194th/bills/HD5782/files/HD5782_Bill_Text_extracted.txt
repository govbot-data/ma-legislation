RE: Prior Year Deficiency Report—FY2026, Quarter 2
Dear Chair Michlewitz and Chair Rodrigues:
In accordance with Item 1599-2040 of Section 2B of Chapter 9 of the Acts of 2025, the Office of
the Comptroller hereby submits the attached Paid Prior Year Deficiency Report (the Report) on
prior year deficiencies to the Senate and House Committees on Ways and Means.
The Report contains information regarding payment of prior year deficiencies through
chargebacks to Fiscal Year 2026 funds.
Through the second quarter of Fiscal Year 2026, as detailed in the attached report, a total of
$3,445,834.54 was spent in prior year deficiencies for 32 departments. Of that amount,
$1,961,006.82 was incurred in accounts that had sufficient funds (the department reverted funds
to the General Fund at the end of Fiscal Year 2025) and $1,484,827.72 was from accounts that
had insufficient funds (the department did not revert funds to the General Fund at the end of
Fiscal Year 2025). Whether there were sufficient or insufficient funds, the funding for prior year
deficiencies is recouped from each department’s current year appropriation.
