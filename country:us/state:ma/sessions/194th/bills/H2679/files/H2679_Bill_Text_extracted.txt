An Act relative to fire protection systems for buildings and structures. 
 
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority 
of the same, as follows: 
 
1  SECTION 1. Section 4 of Chapter 22D, as so appearing, is hereby amended by inserting, 
2 in line 80, after the word “code”, the following paragraph:- 
3  “The provisions of the fire safety code shall apply to all buildings and structures, other 
4 than the State House, including buildings and structures owned, operated, or controlled by the 
5 Commonwealth, its agencies, departments, boards, commissions, or political subdivisions. The 
6 State Fire Marshal or his/her designee shall enforce the State Fire Code in all state owned or 
7 controlled buildings.”

11  This shall not include standards for fire protection or life safety systems, as defined in 
12 Section 1 of Chapter 148, installed in new or substantially modified buildings in accordance with 
13 regulations prescribed by the Board of Fire Prevention Regulations.

20  SECTION 5. Section 1 of Chapter 148 of the Massachusetts General Laws, as so 
21 appearing in the 2012 Official Edition, is hereby amended by inserting, between the definitions

23  Fire Protection and Life Safety Systems, any equipment or system relating to fire 
24 protection, fire detection, fire warning, fire suppression or carbon monoxide alarms installed in 
25 any occupancy  defined in 780 CMR or as otherwise defined by the Board of Fire Prevention 
26 Regulations through regulation.
