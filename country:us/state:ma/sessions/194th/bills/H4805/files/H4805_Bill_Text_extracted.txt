HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES, December 8, 2025. 
 The committee on Municipalities and Regional Government, to whom 
were referred the petition (accompanied by bill, House, No. 4382) of 
Marcus S. Vaughn (by vote of the town) that the town of Plainville be 
authorized to appoint a finance committee; the petition (accompanied by 
bill, House, No. 4383) of Marcus S. Vaughn (by vote of the town) that the 
town of Plainville be authorized to temporarily appoint alternate or 
associate members to certain town boards; the petition (accompanied by 
bill, House, No. 4384) of Marcus S. Vaughn (by vote of the town) that the 
town of Plainville be authorized to change the town administrator title to 
town manager; the petition (accompanied by bill, House, No. 4385) of 
Marcus S. Vaughn (by vote of the town) relative to the select board of the 
town of Plainville; and the petition (accompanied by bill, House, No. 
4386) of Marcus S. Vaughn (by vote of the town) relative to the budget 
process of the town of Plainville, reports recommending that the 
accompanying bill (House, No. 4805) ought to be pass. 
 
For the committee, 
 
JACK PATRICK LEWIS.
