Senate, June 1, 2026 -- Text of the Senate amendment (Senator Brownsberger) to the House Bill 
relative to parking enforcement in the city of Cambridge (House, No. 4231). 
 
The Commonwealth of Massachusetts 
 
_______________ 
In the One Hundred and Ninety-Fourth General Court 
(2025-2026) 
_______________ 
 
1  SECTION 1. Notwithstanding any general or special law to the contrary, the city of 
2 Cambridge may issue notices of violation of any rule, regulation, order, ordinance or by-law 
3 established by the city regulating the parking of motor vehicles within the city through the use of 
4 automated license plate readers or similar parking monitoring technologies. The issuance of such 
5 notices of violation shall be conducted under the procedures prescribed in section 20A of chapter 
6 90 of the General Laws. Photographs or data of parking violations detected by such readers or 
7 similar technologies and transmitted to the transportation department of the city of Cambridge 
8 shall constitute a cognizance of a violation for purposes of said section 20A of said chapter 90. 
9 Issuance of notices of violation pursuant to this act shall require the provision of evidence that an 
10 alleged violation occurred in the form of photographs of the violation, monitoring data or other 
11 evidence of the violation from said department or such readers or similar technologies authorized 
12 under this act. 
13  SECTION 2. This act shall take effect upon its passage.
