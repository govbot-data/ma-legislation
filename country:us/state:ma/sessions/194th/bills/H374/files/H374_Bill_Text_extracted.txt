An Act providing for tenant agents.
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority
of the same, as follows:
1 Chapter 112 of the General Laws is hereby amended by striking out section 87DDD½
2 and inserting in place thereof the following section:- Section 87DDD½ . No person shall engage
3 in the business of finding dwelling accommodations for prospective tenants for a fee unless such
4 person is a licensed broker or salesperson as defined in section 87PP.
5 A licensed real estate broker or salesperson may solely contract with a tenant or
6 prospective tenant to locate or rent residential or commercial real property for a tenant or find a
7 tenant for a property and present an offer to lease to the landlord or landlord's agent and
8 negotiate on behalf of the tenant.
