Election Laws. 
 
The Commonwealth of Massachusetts 
 
_______________ 
In the One Hundred and Ninety-Fourth General Court 
(2025-2026) 
_______________ 
 
An Act relative to election laws. 
 
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority 
of the same, as follows: 
 
1  SECTION 1. Section 47C of chapter 51 of the General Laws, as appearing in the 2022 
2 Official Edition, is hereby amended by inserting at the end of the first paragraph the following:- 
3  The state secretary shall honor requests for standard voter registration and voter history 
4 information in a timely fashion, with said information provided no later than one week after such 
5 request is made. During early voting periods, the state secretary shall maintain an updated list of 
6 registered voters who have cast early ballots, whether in person or by mail, and shall transmit 
7 this information to state party committees, via electronic mail, on a daily basis.  
8  SECTION 2. Section 25B of chapter 54 of the General Laws, as appearing in the 2022 
9 Official Edition, is hereby amended by inserting after subsection (k) the following subsection:- 
10  (l) Not less than four times per year, the state secretary shall provide state party 
11 committees with a list of registered voters who have requested a vote by mail ballot.
