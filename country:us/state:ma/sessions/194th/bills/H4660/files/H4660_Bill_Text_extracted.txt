An Act relative to a certain parcel of land in the town of Norton.
Whereas, The deferred operation of this act would tend to defeat its purpose, which is to
provide forthwith a conveyance of land in the town of Norton, therefore it is hereby declared to
be an emergency law, necessary for the immediate preservation of the public convenience.
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority
of the same, as follows:
1 SECTION 1. Notwithstanding section 5A of chapter 3 of the General Laws, section 20 of
2 chapter 6C of the General Laws, section 7E of chapter 81 of the General Laws or any other
3 general or special law to the contrary, the secretary of transportation, or a designee, may convey
4 to the town of Norton for redevelopment purposes through a release deed certain parcels of land
5 in the town of Norton, owned by the Massachusetts Department of Transportation and taken for
6 highway purposes pursuant to an order of taking dated May 16, 1973 and recorded with the
7 Bristol northern district registry of deeds in book 1635, page 64.
8 The land to be conveyed pursuant to this act is identified as: (i) a portion of Parcel ID 11-
9 16 to be determined by the secretary of transportation or a designee; (ii) Parcel ID 11-14-01; and
10 (iii) Parcel ID 11-15, all on the town of Norton assessor’s records and in an order of taking dated
11 May 16, 1973, recorded in the Bristol northern district registry of deeds in book 1635, page 64
1 of 2

12 and as shown on a plan of land dated May 16, 1973 in plan book 139, page16 recorded in said
13 registry of deeds. Prior to finalizing the transaction or making the conveyance authorized in this
14 act, the Massachusetts Department of Transportation shall determine the exact boundaries of the
15 parcels following completion of a survey.
16 SECTION 2. The consideration for the conveyance authorized in this act shall be the full
17 and fair market value of the parcel as determined by the Massachusetts Department of
18 Transportation’s office of real estate and economic development. The parcels shall be conveyed
19 in its present condition, as is, where is and with all faults, through the Massachusetts Department
20 of Transportation’s standard form release deed. The town of Norton may sell or lease the parcels
21 described in section 1 for redevelopment. Upon the town of Norton selling or leasing any portion
22 of the parcels, the proceeds from such sale or lease shall be conveyed to the commonwealth to
23 meet the full and fair market value of such parcels.
24 SECTION 3. Notwithstanding any general or special law to the contrary, the purchaser of
25 the land shall be responsible for all costs and expenses associated with the conveyance
26 authorized in this act including, but not limited to, all costs associated with any engineering,
27 surveys, appraisals and deed preparation as such costs may be determined by the secretary of the
28 Massachusetts Department of Transportation.
