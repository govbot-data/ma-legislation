2 of 6 16  "(c) Nothing in this section, chapter 128A, chapter 128C, or any other general or special 
17 law shall be construed to authorize the commission to approve, license, regulate, permit, or 
18 otherwise allow historical horse racing, wagering on a historical horse race, or the operation of a

23  SECTION _. Section 1 of chapter 128A of the General Laws is hereby amended by 
24 inserting after the definition of “Commission” the following 3 definitions:- 
25  "“Historical horse race”, any horse race, whether running horse or harness, that was 
26 previously conducted at a licensed pari-mutuel facility, concluded with official results, and 
27 concluded without scratches, disqualifications, or dead-heat finishes. 
28  “Historical horse racing”, wagering on a historical horse race, whether conducted by 
29 means of an electronic, electrical, mechanical, computerized, video, mobile, account-wagering, 
30 or other device, terminal, system, or platform, and whether the race is displayed by replay, 
31 animation, video recording, graphical representation, or any other method. 
32  “Historical horse racing terminal”, any electronic, electrical, mechanical, computerized, 
33 video, or other device, terminal, machine, equipment, system, or platform that allows, facilitates, 
34 displays, processes, records, or resolves a wager on historical horse racing, including any device 
35 that depicts or simulates a game of chance, slot machine, or video game in connection with a 
36 historical horse race." 

3 of 6 37  SECTION _. Section 5 of said chapter 128A is hereby amended by adding the following 
38 paragraph:- 
39  No pari-mutuel or certificate system of wagering authorized by this section shall include, 
40 be construed to include, or be used to conduct historical horse racing, wagering on a historical 
41 horse race, or wagering by means of a historical horse racing terminal. Historical horse racing 
42 shall not be deemed wagering on the speed or ability of horses performing in races held or 
43 conducted by a licensee at a racing meeting. 
44  SECTION _. Section 5C of said chapter 128A is hereby amended by inserting after the 
45 definition of “account wagering” the following sentence:- 
46  "Account wagering shall not mean or include historical horse racing, wagering on a 
47 historical horse race, wagering by means of a historical horse racing terminal, or any televised, 
48 video, computer, mobile, or electronic screen depicting a video game of chance or slot machine 
49 in connection with a historical horse race." 
50  SECTION _. Said chapter 128A is hereby further amended by inserting after section 5C 
51 the following section:- 
52  "Section 5D. Historical horse racing prohibited. 
53  (a) Notwithstanding any general or special law to the contrary, no racing meeting 
54 licensee, racing licensee, simulcasting licensee, account-wagering provider, gaming licensee, 
55 gaming establishment, or other person shall conduct, operate, host, manage, advertise, promote, 
56 facilitate, offer, accept, place, process, or settle a wager on historical horse racing or on a 
57 historical horse race. 

4 of 6 58  (b) No person shall own, lease, possess, install, maintain, make available, or use a 
59 historical horse racing terminal for wagering in the commonwealth. 
60  (c) Historical horse racing shall not be considered horse racing, a racing meeting, live 
61 racing, simulcast wagering, account wagering, pari-mutuel wagering, or wagering on horse races 
62 under this chapter, chapter 128C, section 7 of chapter 4, section 5B of chapter 271, section 17A 
63 of chapter 271, or any other general or special law. 
64  (d) A license, approval, regulation, order, plan of operation, condition, contract, or other 
65 authorization issued or approved by the commission or any other governmental body shall be 
66 void to the extent it purports to authorize historical horse racing, wagering on a historical horse 
67 race, or use of a historical horse racing terminal. 
68  (e) A violation of this section shall constitute cause for suspension, revocation, or 
69 nonrenewal of any license issued under this chapter or chapter 128C. The commission may 
70 impose a civil administrative penalty of not more than $10,000 for each violation, and each day 
71 on which historical horse racing is conducted or a historical horse racing terminal is made 
72 available for wagering shall constitute a separate violation."
