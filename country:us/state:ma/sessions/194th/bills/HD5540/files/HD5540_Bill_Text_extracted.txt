C. Legislative Reference:  
Section 35PPP. (a) There shall be a Wellfleet Hollow State Campground Trust Fund to be used, 
without further appropriation, for the long- term preservation and maintenance of Wellfleet 
Hollow State Campground in the town of Wellfleet. Any balance remaining in the fund at the 

close of a fiscal year shall not revert to the General Fund but shall remain available for 
expenditure in subsequent fiscal years. No expenditure made from the fund shall cause the fund 
to become deficient at any point during a fiscal year  
(b) The Department of Conservation and Recreation shall impose a surcharge of $5 upon each 
fee charged and collected for admission to camping in Wellfleet Hollow State Campground for 
out-of-state residents. The additional money collected from the surcharge  shall be deposited into 
the Wellfleet Hollow State Campground Trust Fund.  
 
(c) An annual report, which shall include projects undertaken, expenditures made, and income received by the fund, shall be submitted to the clerks of the house of representatives and senate and to the house and senate committees on ways and means not later than December 31.
