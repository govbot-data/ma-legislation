70  (c1/2) Notwithstanding any other provision of this section or any other general or special 
71 law to the contrary, if the National Labor Relations Act is repealed or amended so that it no

75 enjoins the enforcement of the National Labor Relations Act, holds said act or any provision

77 commonwealth of the labor-management relations of an employer, employee, bargaining unit, 
78 industry or trade in the commonwealth, this section shall apply to such employer, employee, 
79 bargaining unit, industry or trade. If an act of Congress expressly authorizes the commonwealth 

5 of 9 80 to regulate the labor-management relations of an employer, employee, bargaining unit, industry 
81 or trade in the commonwealth, this chapter shall apply to such employer, employee, bargaining 
82 unit, industry or trade. Application of this chapter pursuant to this section shall commence on the 
83 date on which the applicable repeal, amendment, order, judgment or act of Congress takes effect 
84 and shall extend only to the extent that such application is not preempted by federal law. A 
85 collective bargaining agreement in effect immediately before such date shall remain in effect 
86 according to its terms and be enforceable under this chapter..  
87  Notwithstanding any other provision of this section or any other general or special law to 
88 the contrary, in the event that the National Labor Relations Board, in its discretion under 29 
89 U.S.C. § 164(c)(1), declines to assert jurisdiction over any labor dispute involving a class or 
90 category of employers, the department shall, pursuant to 29 U.S.C. § 164(c)(2), assert 
91 jurisdiction over the same. In the event that the National Labor Relations Board seeks to cede 
92 jurisdiction to the commonwealth to prevent any person from engaging in any unfair labor 
93 practice, pursuant to 29 U.S.C. § 160(a), the department shall agree to accept such jurisdiction. 
94 In the event that the National Labor Relations Board or a court of law determines that a class or 
95 category of employers is not subject to the National Labor Relations Act, the department shall 
96 assert jurisdiction over such class or category of employers.  
97  For purposes of this section, the transition date applicable to a bargaining unit shall be the 
98 date on which this chapter first applies to the employer of such unit pursuant to the preceding 
99 paragraphs. The department shall, upon application filed not later than 12 months after the 
100 transition date and without an election, certify as the exclusive bargaining representative of such

102 National Labor Relations Board as the exclusive representative of such unit, or was recognized 

6 of 9 103 in writing by the employer as such representative, where such certification or recognition

105  The board, or by designation, the department, shall establish rules and procedures for the

109 the petition is filed with the department.  All existing terms and conditions of employment 
110 between a formerly National Labor Relations Board-certified exclusive bargaining representative 
111 and an employer shall remain in full force and effect through the department’s verification 
112 process. 
113  Notwithstanding any other provision of this section, when no other labor organization has 
114 been and currently is lawfully recognized as the exclusive representative of the employees in an 
115 appropriate bargaining unit, the commission shall certify to the parties, in writing, and the 
116 employer shall recognize as the exclusive representative for the purposes of collective bargaining 
117 of all employees in the bargaining unit, a labor organization which has received a written

122  (i) the labor organization and the employer shall agree upon a neutral to conduct a

7 of 9 125 commission shall act as the neutral. The neutral shall verify the labor organization’s majority

129  (ii) within 7 days after receiving a copy of the labor organization’s petition for written 
130 majority authorization, an employer may file a petition with the department requesting an 
131 election by secret ballot to determine whether the labor organization shall be the exclusive

133  SECTION 128O. Said chapter 150A  is hereby further amended by striking out section 
134 6A, as so appearing, and inserting in place thereof the following section:- 
135  Section 6A. An employee who is required as a condition of employment to be a member 
136 in good standing of a labor organization may file with the department a charge alleging that: (i) 
137 although eligible for membership, the employee has been unfairly denied admission to, or 
138 unfairly suspended or expelled from membership in, such organization for reasons other than 
139 malfeasance in office or non-payment of regular initiation fees, dues or assessments; and (ii) the 
140 labor organization has requested, or is about to request, their employer to discipline against the 
141 employee because of the employee’s failure to maintain membership in good standing in such 
142 organization; provided, that such charge shall be filed not more than 15 days after notice of such 
143 request has been given to the employee by the labor organization. Upon filing of such charge, the 
144 department may issue and cause to be served upon the labor organization a complaint stating the 
145 charge in that respect and containing a notice of hearing. The notice shall be given and the 
146 subsequent proceedings shall be conducted in the manner provided in section 6. If upon 

8 of 9 147 reviewing all the evidence, the department determines that the employee was unfairly denied 
148 admission to membership in such organization, or that such discipline: 
149  (i) was imposed by the labor organization in violation of its constitution and by-laws;  
150  (ii) was imposed without a fair trial, including an adequate hearing and opportunity to 
151 defend;  
152  (iii) was not warranted by the offense, if any, committed by the employee against the 
153 labor organization;  
154  (iv) is not consistent with the established public policy of the commonwealth; or 
155  (v) was requested, or about to be requested, by the labor organization, notwithstanding 
156 the employee’s payment in full of all applicable agency service fees in lieu of membership. 
157  Then the department shall state its determinations and issue and cause to be served on the 
158 labor organization an order requiring it to either to admit or restore the employee to membership 
159 in good standing together with full voting rights or refrain from seeking to bring about any 
160 discipline against the employee in their employment because the employee is not a member in 
161 good standing and return to the employee such union dues and assessments as may have been 
162 collected from the employee during the period of the employee’s suspension or expulsion from 
163 the union; provided, however, that if the department does make such a determination after 
164 hearing, it shall enter an order dismissing the charge filed by the employee. 
165  Nothing contained in this section or in section 4 shall require a labor organization as a 
166 condition of making or enforcing a contract requiring membership therein as a condition of 
167 employment to accord to non-participants in an insurance plan the right to vote on questions 

9 of 9 168 pertaining thereto or to grant local organizations voting rights in a convention proportionate to 
169 their membership.
