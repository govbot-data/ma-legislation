An Act relative to contractor rental equipment.
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority
of the same, as follows:
1 Paragraph (f) of section 6 of chapter 64H of the General Laws, as appearing in the 2022
2 edition, is amended by adding at the end of said paragraph the following:-
3 "Contractors renting said construction vehicles, equipment, and machinery, including but
4 not limited to office trailers, storage containers, portable restrooms, and vehicles, from a rental
5 vendor shall be required to provide at time of rental a Contractor’s Sales Tax Exempt Purchase
6 Certificate issued by the department of revenue that includes the following: name of exempt
7 organization; exempt number, or in the alternative, the exempt entity’s ST-2; the purchasing
8 contractor or subcontractor’s name; the contract/subcontract date and estimated date of
9 completion (if no estimated date of completion is provided, certificate is valid for a maximum of
10 24 months from the contract date); the contractor’s signature and title; and the location and
1 of 2

11 description of the exempt project. A rental vendor accepting the Contractor’s Sales Tax Exempt
12 Purchase Certificate from the contractor shall be held harmless from collection of the sales/use
13 tax if accepted in good faith. In this paragraph the following words shall have the following
14 meaning: “Rental Vendor” includes a rental business that rents equipment and other tangible
15 personal property in the state under rental agreements for 365 days or less, or under open-ended
16 agreements. “Purchaser” means a contractor or subcontractor that is renting from a rental vendor
17 equipment to be used on a tax exempt project in the state pursuant to this paragraph.
18 “Contractor’s Sales Tax Exempt Purchase Certificate” means an exemption form that includes
19 the information provided above. “Good faith” means the acceptance of a completed tax exempt
20 purchase certificate under this paragraph."
