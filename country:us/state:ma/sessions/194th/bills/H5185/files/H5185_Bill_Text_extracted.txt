The Commonwealth of Massachusetts
________________________________________
HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES, March 9, 2026.
The committee on Tourism, Arts and Cultural Development, to whom
were referred the petition (accompanied by bill, House, No. 3586) of
David Biele relative to procurement services for artists; the joint petition
(accompanied by bill, House, No. 3589) of Leigh Davis for legislation to
establish a choreographer laureate of the Commonwealth; the petition
(accompanied by bill, House, No. 3593) of Hadley Luddy for legislation to
create the position of poet laureate of the Commonwealth; and the petition
(accompanied by bill, House, No. 3600) of Thomas P. Walsh relative to
investments in tourism and visitation marketing and promotion, reports
recommending that the accompanying order (House, No. 5185) ought to
be adopted.
For the committee,
SEAN GARBALLEY.

HOUSE . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . No. 5185
The Commonwealth of Massachusetts
House of Representatives, March 09, 2026.
1 Ordered, That the committee on Tourism, Arts and Cultural Development be authorized
2 to sit during a recess of the General Court to make an investigation and study of House
3 documents numbered 3586, 3589, 3593 and 3600, relative to artists, choreographer laureate, poet
4 laureate and tourism marketing.
5 Said committee shall report to the General Court the results of its investigation and study
6 and its recommendations, if any, together with drafts of legislation necessary to carry such
7 recommendations into effect, by filing the same with the Clerk of the House of Representatives
8 on or before December 31, 2026.
