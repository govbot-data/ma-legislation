An Act relative to continuing education requirements for any person holding a license as a real 
estate broker or salesman. 
 
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority 
of the same, as follows: 
 
1  Section 87XX1/2 of chapter 112 of the General Laws is hereby amended by inserting at 
2 the end of the first paragraph the following two sentences:- 
3  The board may authorize any courses or programs for continuing education credit which 
4 satisfy the provisions of this section.  The board may adopt such rules and regulations as it deems 
5 necessary for the continuing professional education required for renewal of licenses.
