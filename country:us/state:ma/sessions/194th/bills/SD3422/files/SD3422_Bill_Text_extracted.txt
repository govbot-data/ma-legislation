Dear Honorable Clerks Hurley and Carroll:
This is a submission ofa plan for expenditures from the County Technological Fund for the
Norfolk County Registry ofDeeds, and a request for referral ofthis plan to the Committee on
Ways and Means and Post Audit and Oversight.
The proposed plan is consistent with the Registry's submitted IT profile, the provisions of
Section 4 ofChapter 4 of the Acts of2003, and M.G.L. Chapter 29, Section 2KKK for the
purposed oftechnological improvements at the Norfolk County Registry ofDeeds utilizing
systems interoperable with other like systems used by other registries. In preparing this plan I
have consulted with the Commonwealth's Chieflnformation Officer and the State Secretary, as
per the attached letters.
The specific proposed expenditures, totaling $750,000.00 are for:
Back Indexing ofRegistry of Deeds Records $650,000.00
Redundant Back Up ofLand Records/Indexes $100,000.00
Detail including descriptions and proposed component costs is attached. All services (and
equipment, where applicable) are to be obtained pursuant to the provisions ofM.G.L. Ch. 30B.
