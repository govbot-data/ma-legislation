An Act to establish a carbon fee and cash-back program. 
 
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority 
of the same, as follows: 
 
1  SECTION 1. (a) There shall be a fee imposed on the carbon content of all fossil fuels 
2 sold in the commonwealth. These fees shall be collected for the purpose of reimbursing all 
3 individuals who pay state taxes to the commonwealth in the form of an annual cash-back.  
4  (b) The department of energy resources shall promulgate regulations upon passage of this 
5 act.
