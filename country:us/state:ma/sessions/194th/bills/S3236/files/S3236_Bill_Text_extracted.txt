An Act authorizing the Massachusetts Department of Fish and Game to convey easements over 
certain parcels of land. 
 
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority 
of the same, as follows:

62  SECTION 4. (a) The value of the easements described in sections 1 and 3 and the value 
63 of the property interest to be conveyed to the commonwealth pursuant to section 2 shall be 
64 determined by an independent appraisal prepared in accordance with the usual and customary 
65 professional appraisal practice by a qualified appraiser commissioned by the commissioner of 
66 capital asset management and maintenance. The commissioner may, in consultation with the 
67 commissioner of fish and game, accept an appraisal of said easements and property interest 
68 conducted prior to the effective date of this act. The commissioner of capital asset management 
69 and maintenance shall submit the appraisal to the inspector general for his review and comment. 
70 The inspector general shall review and approve the appraisal, and the review shall include an 
71 examination of the methodology used for the appraisal. The inspector general shall prepare a 
72 report of his review and file the report with the commissioner for submission by the 
73 commissioner to the house and senate committees on ways and means and the joint committee 
74 on state administration and regulatory oversight. The commissioner shall submit copies of the 
75 appraisals, and the inspector general’s report to the house and senate committees on ways and 
76 means and the joint committee on state administration and regulatory oversight before the 
77 execution of the conveyances. 
78  (b) Algonquin Gas Transmission, LLC shall compensate the commonwealth in an amount 
79 greater than, or equal to, the full and fair market value, or the value in use of the easements 

5 of 5 80 authorized in sections 1 and 3, whichever is greater, as determined by the independent appraisals. 
81 The commonwealth shall not be obligated to pay any consideration to Algonquin Gas 
82 Transmission, LLC if the appraised value of any parcels or interests conveyed to the 
83 commonwealth exceeds the value of the easements authorized in section 1 and 3. All money paid 
84 to the commonwealth by Algonquin Gas Transmission, LLC as a result of the conveyance of the 
85 easements authorized by this act shall be deposited in the Biodiversity Fund established by 
86 section 35D ½ of chapter 10 of the General Laws, to be used for future land acquisition by the 
87 department of fish and game. 
88  (c) If the independent appraisal shows that the fair market value or value in use, 
89 whichever is greater, of the interests described in section 1 are not equal to or greater than the 
90 fair market value or value in use, whichever is greater, of the property interest to be conveyed to 
91 the commonwealth pursuant to section 2, then Algonquin Gas Transmission, LLC shall 
92 compensate the commonwealth in an amount equal to or greater than this difference in monetary 
93 value. All money paid to the commonwealth by Algonquin Gas Transmission, LLC as a result of 
94 the conveyance of the easements authorized by this act shall be deposited in the Biodiversity 
95 Fund established by section 35D ½ of chapter 10 of the General Laws, to be used for future land 
96 acquisition by the department of fish and game. 
97  SECTION 5. Algonquin Gas Transmission LLC shall be responsible for all costs 
98 associated with engineering, surveys, appraisals, document preparation and other expenses 
99 deemed necessary by the commissioner of capital asset management and maintenance to effect 
100 the conveyances authorized by this act.
