An Act relative to veteran employment opportunities. 
 
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority 
of the same, as follows: 
 
1  Chapter 115 of the General Laws, as appearing in the 2022 Official Edition, is hereby 
2 amended by inserting after Section 2C the following:-   
3  Section 2D. Program to further enhance employment opportunities and outcomes among 
4 veterans  
5  The commissioner shall authorize reimbursement to Veterans for costs associated with 
6 taking municipal civil service exams. The commissioner shall authorize no more than three 
7 reimbursements per Veteran, provided further, no reimbursement shall be authorized if the 
8 Veteran fails a subsequent exam. The commissioner shall engage with interested stakeholders 
9 and organizations that provide services to veterans in the development of the program.
