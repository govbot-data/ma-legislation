316 Section 8.1 RECALL
317 A holder of an elected office in the town of Sandwich may be recalled therefrom by the
318 qualified voters of the town as provided in this section for reasons which shall include, but are
319 not limited to, the following: embezzlement; influence peddling; refusal to comply with clause
320 Twenty-sixth of section 7 of chapter 4 of the General Laws (protection of information in public
16 of 20

321 records), sections 10 and 10A of chapter 66 of the General Laws (public records requests),
322 sections 18 to 25, inclusive, of chapter 30A of the General Laws (open meeting law) or chapter
323 268A of the General Laws (conflict of interest) or any rules and regulations thereto and the by-
324 laws of the town of Sandwich that pertain to the same; destruction or alteration of public records;
325 nepotism; conviction for a felony; failure to perform the duties of the elected office; or other
326 willful acts of omission or commission which betray the public trust.
327 SECTION 8.2 RECALL PROCESS
328 (a) Any 10 qualified voters of the town of Sandwich may file with the town clerk an
329 affidavit containing the name of the officer sought to be recalled and a statement of the grounds
330 for recall. The town clerk shall thereupon deliver to the voters making such affidavit a sufficient
331 number of copies of petition blanks demanding such recall, printed forms of which the town
332 clerk shall keep on hand. The blanks shall be issued by the town clerk with the town clerk’s
333 signature and official seal attached thereto and shall be dated and addressed to the select board of
334 the town. Said blanks shall contain the name of the person to whom issued, the number of blanks
335 to be issued, the name of the person sought to be recalled, the office from which removal is
336 sought, the grounds for recall as stated in the affidavit and shall demand the election of a
337 successor to such office. A copy of the petition shall be entered in a record book to be kept in the
338 office of the town clerk. The recall petition shall be returned and filed with the town clerk within
339 20 days after the filing of the affidavit. The petition, before being returned and filed, shall be
340 signed by 25 per cent of qualified voters in the town and to every signature shall be added the
341 place of residence of the signer, giving both the street and number. The recall petition shall be
342 submitted to the town clerk not later than 3:00 p.m. on the Friday preceding the day which it
17 of 20

343 must be filed, to the registrars of voters in the town, and the registrars shall forthwith certify
344 thereon the number of signatures which are names of voters of the town.
345 (b) If the petition is found and certified by the town clerk to be sufficient, the town clerk
346 shall submit the petition with the town clerk’s certification to the select board, without delay, and
347 the select board shall forthwith give written notice of receipt of the certificate to the officer
348 against whom recall is being sought. If the officer does not resign within 5 days of such notice,
349 the select board shall order an election to be held on a date fixed by them not less than 60 days
350 and not more than 90 days after the date of the town clerk's submission of the petition to the
351 select board; provided, however, that if another town election is to occur within 100 days after
352 the date of the certificate, the select board may, in their discretion, postpone the holding of the
353 recall election to the date of such other election. If a vacancy occurs in the office sought to be
354 recalled after a recall election has been so ordered, the election shall nevertheless proceed as
355 provided herein.
356 (c) Any officer sought to be removed may be a candidate to succeed themself and the
357 town clerk shall place their name on the ballot without nomination, unless such officer requests
358 in writing not to have their name on the ballot. The nomination of other candidates, the
359 publication of the warrant for the recall election and the conduct of the same shall all be in
360 accordance with the provisions of law relating to elections, unless otherwise provided by this
361 charter.
362 (d) The incumbent shall continue to perform the duties of the office until the recall
363 election. If reelected, the incumbent shall continue in the office for the remainder of the
364 unexpired term, subject to recall as before, except as provided in this charter. If not reelected in
18 of 20

365 the recall election, the incumbent shall be deemed removed upon the qualification of their
366 successor, who shall hold office for the remainder of the unexpired term. If the successor fails to
367 qualify within 5 days after receiving notification of their election, the incumbent shall thereupon
368 be deemed removed and the office vacant.
369 (e) Ballots used in a recall election shall submit the following propositions in the order
370 indicated: For the recall of (name of officer) Against the recall of (name of officer) Immediately
371 at the right of each proposition, there shall be a square in which the voter, by making a cross
372 mark (X), may vote for either of the propositions. Under the proposition shall appear the word
373 "Candidates", the directions to voters required by section 42 of chapter 54 of the General Laws,
374 beneath this the names of candidates nominated as hereinbefore provided. If a majority of the
375 votes cast upon the question of recall is in the affirmative, the candidate receiving the highest
376 number of votes shall be declared elected; provided, however, that at least 40% of those entitled
377 to vote shall have voted. If a majority of votes on the question is in the negative, the ballot for
378 candidates need not be counted.
379 (f) No recall petition shall be filed against an officer within 6 months after they take
380 office, nor, in case of an officer subject to a recall election and not recalled thereby, until at least
381 6 months after the election at which their recall was submitted to the voters.
