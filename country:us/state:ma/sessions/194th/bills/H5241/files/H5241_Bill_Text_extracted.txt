HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES, March 16, 2026.
The committee on Agriculture and Fisheries, to whom were referred
the petition (accompanied by bill, House, No. 114) of Nicholas A.
Boldyga relative to the regulation and availability of cottage foods; the
petition (accompanied by bill, House, No. 122) of Rodney M. Elliott and
James Arciero that the Division of Animal Health be authorized to
regulate the preservation of existing pet cemeteries; and the petition
(accompanied by bill, House, No. 140) of Erika Uyterhoeven and Jacob R.
Oliveira relative to cottage food entrepreneurs, reports (under the
provisions of House Rule 27) recommending that the accompanying order
(House, No. 5241) ought to be adopted.
For the committee,
AARON L. SAUNDERS.
