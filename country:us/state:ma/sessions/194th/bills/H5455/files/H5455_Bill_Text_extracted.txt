An Act regulating the operation of medical spas.
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority
of the same, as follows:
1 SECTION 1. As used in this section, the following word shall, unless the context clearly
2 requires otherwise, have the following meaning:-
3 “Department”, the department of public health
4 SECTION 2. Not later than December 31, 2027, the department shall prepare a report
5 summarizing an update on the progress of the implementation of recommendations issued by the
6 medical spa task force report as established in chapter 81 of acts 2006. This report shall include,
7 but not be limited to, (i) an analysis of the report’s recommendations, (ii) whether the department
8 took any action on the recommendations, and; (iii) determine if an updated report is necessary.
9 Said report shall be submitted to the joint committee on public health and the joint committee on
10 consumer protection and professional licensure.
