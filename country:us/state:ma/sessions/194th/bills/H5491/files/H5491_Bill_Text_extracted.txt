An Act implementing the recommendations of the Walsh-Kennedy Commission Report. 
 
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority 
of the same, as follows: 
 
1  SECTION 1. Chapter 148 of the General Laws is hereby amended by inserting after 
2 section 10H the following 2 sections:- 
3  Section 10I. All certifications and training programs for cutting, welding and hot works 
4 processes shall be performed using a program approved by the state fire marshal that includes: (i) 
5 a thorough and accessible electronic database maintained by the department of fire services that 
6 can be used to check a worker’s status; (ii) multi-lingual in-class offerings; (iii) identity integrity 
7 safeguards; (iv) in-person initial training; and (v) subsequent annual continued education 
8 program either online or in person. 
9  Section 10J. The department of fire services shall, in consultation with the department of 
10 occupational licensure, or any other executive agency or department deemed necessary, establish 
11 a system of public notification and recording of non-compliance with regulations and statues 
12 pertaining to cutting, welding and hot works processes. The system may include quarterly 

2 of 3 13 publications, an online database, an automated notification system to alert property owners and 
14 communication with insurance companies. 
15  SECTION 2. Said chapter 148 is hereby further amended by striking out section 34 and 
16 inserting in place thereof the following section:- 
17  Section 34. (a) Except as otherwise provided, any person violating any provision of this 
18 chapter shall be liable to a fine of $100, or, in case of a continuing offense after notice of such 
19 violation, to a fine of not more than $100 for every day during which the violation continues. 
20  (b) Notwithstanding subsection (a), any violation that pertains to cutting, welding or hot 
21 works processes shall be subject to the following for said violation: (i) for a first offense, a fine 
22 of not more than $1,000; (ii) for a second offense, a fine of not more than $2,000 or 
23 imprisonment in a house of correction for not more than a year, or both; (iii) for a third or 
24 subsequent offense, a fine of not more than .025 per cent of the total project value, or $10,000, 
25 whichever is greater, or imprisonment in the house of correction for up to 2.5 years, or both. 
26 Pursuant to this subsection, a judge may take into account the size of the project on which the 
27 violation occurred and levy additional fines if the total cost of said project is such that the fine is 
28 inadequate to deter continued offenses. 
29  SECTION 3. Said chapter 148 is hereby further amended by striking out section 34B and 
30 inserting in place thereof the following section:-   
31  Section 34B. (a) For the purposes of this section, the following words shall, unless the 
32 context clearly requires otherwise, have the following meanings: 

3 of 3 33  “Criminal negligence”, repeated, reckless and knowing failure to comply with this 
34 chapter or regulations promulgated thereunder and failure to perceive a substantial and 
35 unjustifiable risk that a result would occur from said failure. The risk shall be of such nature and 
36 degree that failure to perceive it constitutes a gross deviation from the standard of care that a 
37 reasonable person would observe in the situation.  
38  “Serious bodily injury”, bodily injury that results in a permanent disfigurement, loss or 
39 impairment of a bodily function, limb or organ, or a substantial risk of death. 
40  (b)(1) Any person who wantonly or recklessly violates the state building code or state fire 
41 code and thereby causes serious bodily injury to any person shall be punished by a fine of not 
42 more than $250,000 or by imprisonment in the state prison for not more than 5 years or in a 
43 house of correction for not more than 2 1/2 years, or both a fine and imprisonment.  
44  (2) Any person who wantonly or recklessly violates the state building code or state fire 
45 code and thereby causes death to any person shall be punished by a fine of not more than 
46 $500,000 or by imprisonment in the state prison for not more than 7 years or in a house of 
47 correction for not more than 5 years, or both a fine and imprisonment.  
48  (c) Any person found to have acted with criminal negligence may be determined to be 
49 criminally liable for damage or death stemming from repeated and reckless non-compliance with 
50 requirements set forth in the state building code or state fire code.
