An Act relative to data collection on below threshold wells.
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority
of the same, as follows:
1 SECTION 1. Section 20 of Chapter 21G shall be amended to insert the following: - Local
2 boards of health or other permitting bodies that oversee local permitting of wells in
3 municipalities shall provide annually by December 31st, a report of the below-threshold wells
4 permitted to the Department of Environmental Protection (DEP). Data should include, but not be
5 limited to: Location/address of well; primary use (i.e., residential, irrigation, commercial,
6 farming); and year of permit issuance.
7 SECTION 2. DEP shall use this data to estimate withdrawals from below-threshold wells
8 at a sub-basin scale. Estimated below threshold well withdrawals shall be included in estimates
9 of water withdrawals by basin and considered when DEP is issuing water withdrawal permits.
10 SECTION 3. Local Boards of Health or other appropriate local permitting authority shall
11 implement water conservation measures for these below-threshold wells during drought
1 of 2

12 consistent with the municipality’s current drought conditions and water use restrictions or in
13 identified flow depleted basins.
14 SECTION 4. Chapter 21G Section 4 shall be amended to strike the first sentence and
15 replace it with “The withdrawal volume threshold to be applied pursuant to sections five and
16 seven shall be twenty thousand gallons per day.”
