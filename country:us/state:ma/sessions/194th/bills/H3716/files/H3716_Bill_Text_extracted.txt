An Act relative to electronic scanning of identification.

of the same, as follows:
1 SECTION 1. Chapter 90 of the General Laws is hereby amended by inserting after
2 section 8M the following section
3 Section 8N. Businesses in the Commonwealth may not only retrieve information from
4 consumers by electronically scanning their ID, but the information may also be allowed to be
