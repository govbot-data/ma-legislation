By Mr. Oliveira, a petition (accompanied by bill, Senate, No. 814) of Jacob R. Oliveira for
legislation to give patients a 60 day notice of any increase to out of pocket health insurance costs.
Financial Services.
The Commonwealth of Massachusetts
_______________
In the One Hundred and Ninety-Fourth General Court
(2025-2026)
_______________
An Act relative to health insurance coverage notification.
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority
of the same, as follows:
1 Section 6 of chapter 176O, as so appearing, is hereby amended by adding after the words
2 “denied enrollment” the following:- For any change to ongoing medical costs that will increase
3 the out of pocket costs to the patient, including costs related to prescription drugs, medical
4 supplies or other treatment, a patient must be given at least a 60 notice before those changes take
5 effect.
