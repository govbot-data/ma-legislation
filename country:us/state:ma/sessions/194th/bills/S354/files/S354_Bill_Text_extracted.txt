An Act establishing funds to provide for increases in English language learners in school
districts.
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority
of the same, as follows:
1 SECTION 1. Chapter 71A of the General Laws, as appearing in the 2022 Official
2 Edition, is hereby amended by adding the following section:-
3 Section 13. (a) Subject to appropriation, for each fiscal year commencing with the fiscal
4 year that this section is enacted, any school district that has experienced at least a 20-student
5 increase over the prior fiscal year in the number of students who are classified as English
6 learners shall receive an additional cost reimbursement from the Commonwealth for such
7 English learners in the current fiscal year. This reimbursement shall be equal to the positive
8 difference, if any, between: (i) the number of English learners on October 1 of the prior fiscal
9 year and (ii) the number of English learners students for the current fiscal year as measured by
1 of 2

10 the census of such students on October 1 of that fiscal year, an shall be in an amount for each
11 such student equal to the amount of reimbursement provided for in Chapter 70 for an English
12 learner for that school district.
13 (b) A school district eligible for additional cost reimbursement shall receive an advance
14 allotment payment from the Commonwealth as calculated by subsection (a), no later than the
15 close of the first monthly accounting period in such fiscal year. Said additional cost
16 reimbursements shall be kept in a separate fund by the state treasurer and shall be disbursed by
17 the state treasurer at the direction of the commissioner of the board of education to the school
18 district. Reimbursement to a school district pursuant to this section shall be in addition to any
19 other reimbursement or a grant pursuant to Title III of Public Law 107-110.
