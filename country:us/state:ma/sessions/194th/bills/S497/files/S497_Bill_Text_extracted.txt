An Act clarifying the role of governor’s councillor on the ballot.
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority
of the same, as follows:
1 SECTION 1. Section 43A of chapter 54 of the general laws is hereby amended by
2 inserting after the word “congressman” the following word:- governor’s.
3 SECTION 2. Section 150 of said chapter 54 is hereby amended by inserting after the
4 word “each,” in line 4, the following word:- governor’s.
5 SECTION 3. Said section 150 of said chapter 54 is hereby further amended by inserting
6 after the word “one,” in line 5, the word:- governor’s.
