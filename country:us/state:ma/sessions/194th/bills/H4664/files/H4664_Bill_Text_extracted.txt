An Act relative to notice of activation of the Massachusetts National Guard within the
Commonwealth.
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority
of the same, as follows:
1 An act strengthening the governor's authority to limit outside un-federalized military
2 activities within the state.
3 Chapter 33 of the General Laws is hereby amended by adding the following section:-
4 Section 140. (a)(1) The governor, acting as commander-in-chief may provide conditions
5 under which any out of state military units, that have not been federalized under Title 10 of the
6 United States Code, may be permitted to operate within the borders of the commonwealth for the
7 purpose of doing military duty. For the purposes of this section the term “military units” shall
8 mean armed forces, as defined in 10 U.S.C § 101, the United States Army National Guard or the
9 United States Air National Guard.
10 (2) Such military units shall not be permitted to operate in Massachusetts, absent the prior
11 written authorization of the governor and the forthwith acknowledgment of control and
12 conditions of such operation of the governor as commander-in-chief of such military units.
1 of 3

13 (3) Upon providing military units permission to operate in the commonwealth, the
14 governor shall deliver written notice to members of the house of representatives and the senate of
15 the conditions of any such operation including its scope, purpose and duration.
16 (b) Any military units or individual troops purporting to conduct operations within the
17 commonwealth without such authority and acknowledgment shall be subject to the civil and
18 criminal laws of the commonwealth.
19 (c) Nothing in this section shall prohibit or interfere with the interstate deployment of
20 National Guard units into the commonwealth pursuant to any existing interstate compact or
21 agreement.
22 (d)(1) Upon receiving credible information that the President of the United States or any
23 federal agent has communicated, directly or indirectly, an actual or proposed mobilization
24 affecting military units within, into or from the Commonwealth, the governor shall, within 24
25 hours, notify all members of the House of Representatives and the Senate. Such notification shall
26 include, to the extent known: (i) the scope of mobilization; (ii) its stated purpose; (iii) anticipated
27 duration; (iv) the source and nature of the information received; and (v) any limitations on the
28 reliability or completeness of such information.
29 (2) The governor shall provide supplemental notification within 24 hours upon: (i)
30 discovering that any material aspect of the initial notification was inaccurate or incomplete; (ii)
31 receiving new information regarding the mobilization; or (iii) learning of material changes to the
32 scope, purpose, or duration of the mobilization. Any supplemental notification shall explicitly
33 identify information that supersedes or corrects a prior notification and shall include the date and
34 time of all related prior notifications.
2 of 3

35 (3) The duty to provide supplemental notifications under paragraph (2) shall terminate
36 upon the earliest of: (i) the complete withdrawal or demobilization of all affected military units;
37 or (ii) 90 days after initial notification, subject to legislative extension by joint resolution of the
38 general court.
