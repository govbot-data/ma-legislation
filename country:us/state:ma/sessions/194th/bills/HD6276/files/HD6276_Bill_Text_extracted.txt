An Act authorizing the town of Rutland to grant one additional license for the sale of all
alcoholic beverages to be drunk off the premises.
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority
of the same, as follows:
1 SECTION 1. (a) Notwithstanding section 17 of chapter 138 of the General Laws or any
2 other general or special law to the contrary, the licensing authority of the town of Rutland may
3 grant an additional license for the sale of all alcoholic beverages to be drunk off the premises
4 under section 15 of said chapter 138 to Varahi Foods Inc., d/b/a Rutland Market Place, located at
5 26 Pommogusett Road. The license shall be subject to all provisions of said chapter 138 except
6 said section 17.
7 (b) The licensing authority shall not approve the transfer of a license granted pursuant to
8 this act to any other location, but it may grant the license to a new applicant at the same location
9 if the applicant files with the licensing authority a letter from the department of revenue and a
10 letter from the department of unemployment assistance indicating that the license is in good
11 standing with those departments and that all applicable taxes, fees, and contributions have been
12 paid.
1 of 2

13 (c) If a licensee terminates or fails to renew the license granted under this section or any
14 such license is cancelled, revoked or no longer in use, it shall be returned physically, with all of
15 the legal rights, privileges and restrictions pertaining thereto, to the licensing authority which
16 may, within 3 years after such return, then grant the license to a new applicant at the same
17 location and under the same terms and conditions as specified in this section, otherwise such
18 license shall dissolve.
19 (d) The license granted under this act shall be issued within 1 year after the effective date
20 of this act; provided, however, that if the license is originally granted within that time period, it
21 may be granted to a new applicant under subsections (b) and (c) thereafter.
22 SECTION 2. This act shall take effect upon its passage.
