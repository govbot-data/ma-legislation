An Act relative to the Massachusetts Credit Union Share Insurance Corporation. 
 
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority 
of the same, as follows: 
 
1  SECTION 1. Section 5 in section 1 of chapter 294 of the acts of 1961, as amended by 
2 section 1 of chapter 306 of the acts of 1973, is hereby further amended by striking out the second 
3 paragraph and inserting in place thereof the following paragraph:- 
4  The board of directors shall meet at least quarterly. A majority of the directors shall
