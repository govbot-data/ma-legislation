_______________ 
 
An Act relative to bake sales, farmer’s markets, or community festivals by political party 
committees. 
 
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority 
of the same, as follows: 
 
1  Section 1 of chapter 55 of the General Laws, as appearing in the 2022 Official Edition, is 
2 hereby amended by inserting at the end of the definition of “contribution” the following sentence 
3 in line 72:- 
4  It shall not include any payments in the aggregate of less than $10,000 per calendar year 
5 made by individuals for food, beverages, or goods sold by a political party committee organized 
6 pursuant to G.L. c. 52, §§2 and 3 for a nominal price per item at events such as bake sales, 
7 farmer’s markets, or community festivals even if the purchase price exceeds the cost of the goods 
8 sold.
