By Representative Berthiaume of Spencer and Senator Durant, a joint petition (subject to Joint
Rule 12) of Donald R. Berthiaume, Jr., for legislation to establish a sick leave bank for Kathleen
Rodrigue, an employees of the Chief Medical Examiner's office. Public Service.
The Commonwealth of Massachusetts
_______________
In the One Hundred and Ninety-Fourth General Court
(2025-2026)
_______________
An Act establishing a sick leave bank for Kathleen Rodrigue.
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority
of the same, as follows:
1 Section 1. Notwithstanding any general or special law to the contrary, the Chief Medical
2 Examiner's office shall establish a sick leave bank for Kathleen Rodrigue, an employee of the
3 Department. Any employee of the Department may voluntarily contribute 1 or more sick,
4 personal or vacation days to the sick leave bank for use by Kathleen Rodrigue. If Kathleen
5 Rodrigue terminates employment with the Department or requests to dissolve the sick leave
6 bank, any remaining time in the sick leave bank shall be transferred to the extended illness leave
7 bank. Sick leave bank days shall not be used for absences unrelated to the illness or disability
8 that necessitated the establishment of the sick leave bank as determined by the Department.
9 Section 2. This act shall take effect upon its passage.
