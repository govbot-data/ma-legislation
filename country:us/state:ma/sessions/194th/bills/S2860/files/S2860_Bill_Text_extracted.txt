1 of 4 SENATE  .  .  .  .  .  .  .  .  .  .  .  .  .  .  No. 2860 
Senate, December 11, 2025 -- Text of the Senate amendment (Senator Barrett) to the House Bill 
increasing the age limit for retired police officers serving as special police officers in the town of 
Weston (House, No. 4031). 
 
The Commonwealth of Massachusetts 
 
_______________ 
In the One Hundred and Ninety-Fourth General Court 
(2025-2026) 
_______________ 
 
1  SECTION 1. Chapter 74 of the acts of 2020 is hereby repealed. 
2  SECTION 2. Notwithstanding any general or special law, rule or regulation to the 
3 contrary, the town manager of the town of Weston may appoint, at the recommendation of the 
4 chief of police and as the chief of police deems necessary, retired police officers as special police 
5 officers to perform police details or any police duties arising from police details or during the 
6 course of police detail work, whether or not related to the detail work. Such retired police 
7 officers shall have been regular full-time police officers who voluntarily retired based upon 
8 superannuation. No further deductions shall be made from the regular compensation of such 
9 retired police officers under chapter 32 of the General Laws for any service subsequent to 
10 reaching 65 years of age and, upon retirement, such retired police officers shall receive a 
11 superannuation retirement allowance equal to that which they would have been entitled had they 
12 retired at 65 years of age. Special police officers appointed pursuant to this act shall not be 
13 subject to the maximum age restrictions applied to regular police officers under said chapter 32; 
14 provided, however, that such special police officers shall not be eligible to serve as special police 
15 officers after reaching 70 years of age. Prior to appointment pursuant to this act, a special police 

2 of 4 16 officer shall provide certification to the town that they are covered by personal health insurance 
17 and shall pass a medical examination by a physician chosen by the town to determine that the 
18 officer is capable of performing the essential duties of a special police officer, the cost of which 
19 shall be borne by the special police officer.  
20  SECTION 3. Special police officers appointed pursuant to this act shall not be subject to 
21 chapter 31 of the General Laws, section 99A of chapter 41 of the General Laws or chapter 150E 
22 of the General Laws; provided, however, that they shall be subject to chapter 151A of the 
23 General Laws. A special police officer appointed pursuant to this act shall comply with all 
24 requirements of chapter 6E of the General Laws, including: (i) maintaining certification and 
25 good standing with the Massachusetts Peace Officer Standards and Training Commission; and 
26 (ii) complying with all annual in-service and other training requirements mandated by the 
27 municipal police training committee. 
28  SECTION 4. When performing the duties under section 1, special police officers 
29 appointed pursuant to this act shall have the same power to make arrests and to perform other 
30 police functions as do regular police officers of the town of Weston. 
31  SECTION 5. Special police officers appointed pursuant to this act shall subject to 
32 removal by the town manager at any time with 14-day written notice. 
33  SECTION 6. Special police officers appointed pursuant to this act shall be subject to the 
34 rules and regulations, policies, procedures and requirements of the police department and the 
35 chief of police of the town of Weston, including: (i) restrictions on the type of detail 
36 assignments; (ii) requirements regarding medical examinations to determine continuing 
37 capability to perform the duties of a special police officer; (iii) requirements for training; (iv) 

3 of 4 38 requirements for firearms qualifications and licensing; and (v) requirements regarding uniforms 
39 and equipment; provided, however, that the cost of compliance with all requirements shall be at 
40 no cost to the town of Weston.  
41  SECTION 7. Special police officers appointed pursuant to this act shall be sworn before 
42 the town clerk of the town of Weston, who shall keep a record of all such appointments. 
43  SECTION 8. Special police officers appointed pursuant to this act shall be subject to 
44 sections 100 and 111F of chapter 41 of the General Laws. The amount payable under said 
45 section 111F of said chapter 41 shall be calculated by averaging the amount earned over the prior 
46 52 weeks as a special police officer working police details or averaged over such lesser period of 
47 time for any officer designated as a special police officer less than 52 weeks before the 
48 incapacity. In no event shall payment under said section 111F of said chapter 41 exceed, in any 
49 calendar year, the limitation on earning contained in paragraph (b) of section 91 of chapter 32 of 
50 the General Laws. Payment under said section 111F of said chapter 41 shall terminate when a 
51 physician designated by the town determines that the disability no longer exists or when the 
52 special police officer reaches 70 years of age. Special police officers appointed pursuant to this 
53 act shall not be subject to or eligible for any benefits under section 85H or 85H1/2 of said 
54 chapter 32. 
55  SECTION 9. Appointment as a special police officer pursuant to this act shall not entitle 
56 a special police officer to assignment to any detail. Special police officers shall be paid the detail 
57 rate applicable to regular full-time officers but shall not be entitled to any other town benefits. 

4 of 4 58  SECTION 10. Retired police officers serving as special police officers pursuant to this 
59 act shall be subject to the limitations on hours worked and on payments to retired town 
60 employees under paragraph (b) of section 91 of chapter 32 of the General Laws. 
61  SECTION 11. This act shall take effect upon its passage.
