32 SECTION 7. Section 1 of chapter 255B of the General Laws, as so appearing, is hereby
33 amended by striking out the definition of “holder” and inserting in place thereof the following 3

35 “Holder”, the retail seller of the motor vehicle under or subject to a retail instalment
36 contract or lease agreement or, if the contract is purchased by a financing agency or other
37 assignee, the sales finance company or other assignee.

60 SECTION 10. Said chapter 255B is hereby further amended by striking out section 20A,
61 as so appearing, and inserting in place thereof the following section:-
62 Section 20A. (a) An agreement of the parties in a retail instalment contract or lease
63 agreement defining default is enforceable only to the extent that the default is material and
64 consists of the buyer’s or lessee’s failure to make at least 1 installment as required by the
65 agreement or the occurrence of an event which substantially impairs the value of the collateral or
66 leased vehicle.
67 (b) After a default by a buyer or lessee under a consumer credit transaction or lease
68 agreement, the secured creditor or lessor may not bring an action against the buyer or lessee,
69 proceed against the collateral or the leased vehicle until the secured creditor or lessor gives the
70 buyer or lessee the notice described in this section. Such notice shall be deemed to be delivered
71 when delivered to the debtor or when mailed to the debtor at the debtor’s address last known to
72 the secured creditor or lessor. If a buyer or lessee cures a default after receiving notice and again
73 defaults, the secured creditor or lessor shall give another notice before bringing an action or
74 proceeding against the collateral or the leased vehicle with respect to the subsequent default, but
4 of 7

75 no notice shall be required in connection with a subsequent default if, within the period
76 commencing on the date of the consumer credit transaction or lease agreement subject to this
77 section and the date of the subsequent default, the debtor has cured a default after notice not less
78 than 3 times.
79 (c) Notice given pursuant to this section shall be in writing and shall be given to the
80 buyer or lessee not less than 10 days after the default and shall conspicuously state the rights of
81 the buyer or lessee upon default in substantially the following form:
82 The heading shall read:—“Rights of Defaulting Buyer or Lessee under the Massachusetts
83 Motor Vehicle Installment Sales Act.”
84 The body of the notice shall read:—“You may cure your default in (describe transaction
85 in a manner enabling buyer or lessee to identify it) by paying to (name and address of secured
86 creditor or lessor) (amount due) before (date which is at 21 days after notice is mailed). If you
87 pay this amount within the time allowed, you are no longer in default and may proceed with the
88 transaction as though no default had occurred.
89 If you do not cure your default by the date stated above, the creditor or lessor may sue
90 you to obtain a judgment for the amount of the debt or, if applicable, may take possession of the
91 collateral or leased vehicle.
92 If the creditor or lessor takes possession of the collateral or leased vehicle, if any, you
93 may get it back by paying the full amount of your debt plus any reasonable expenses incurred by
94 the creditor or lessor if you make the required payment within 20 days after the creditor or lessor
95 takes possession.”
5 of 7

96 (d) During the 21-day period after delivery of the notice pursuant to this section, the
97 secured creditor or lessor shall not, because of that default, accelerate the unpaid balance of the
98 obligation, bring action against the buyer or lessee or proceed against the collateral or the leased
99 vehicle.
100 (e) Unless the secured creditor or lessor has first notified the buyer or lessee that the
101 secured creditor or lessor has elected to accelerate the unpaid balance of the obligation because
102 of default, brought action against the buyer or lessee or proceeded against the collateral or leased
103 vehicle, the buyer or lessee may cure a default consisting of a failure to pay money by tendering
104 the amount of all unpaid sums due at the time of tender, without acceleration, plus any unpaid
105 delinquency or deferral charges. Such a cure shall restore the buyer or lessee to the buyer’s or
106 lessee’s rights under the agreement as though the default had not occurred subject to subsection
107 (b).
108 SECTION 11. Section 20B of said chapter 255B, as so appearing, is hereby amended by
109 striking out subsections (a) to (c), inclusive, and inserting in place thereof the following 3
110 subsections:-
111 (a) Subject to this section and section 20A, a secured creditor or lessor under a consumer
112 credit transaction or lease agreement may take possession of collateral or a leased vehicle. In
113 taking possession, the secured creditor or lessor under a consumer credit transaction or lease
114 agreement may proceed without a prior hearing only if: (i) the default is material and consists of
115 the debtor’s failure to make at least 1 payment as required by the agreement or the occurrence of
116 an event which substantially impairs the value of the collateral; and (ii) possession can be
6 of 7

117 obtained without use of force, without breach of peace and, unless the debtor consents to an entry
118 at the time of such entry, without entry on property owned by or rented to the debtor.
119 (b) Except as provided in subsection (a), a secured creditor or lessor under a consumer
120 credit transaction or lease agreement may proceed against collateral or a leased vehicle only after
121 a prior hearing. In any proceeding where possession of the collateral is part of the relief sought
122 by a holder, no court shall allow a secured creditor or lessor to take possession of collateral or a
123 leased vehicle until the right of the secured creditor or lessor to take possession has been
124 determined at a hearing at which the buyer or lessee has an opportunity to be heard, having been
125 notified in writing of said hearing not less than 7 days in advance thereof.
126 (c) The buyer or lessee under a consumer credit transaction or lease agreement may
127 redeem the collateral from the holder at any time within 20 days of the secured creditor’s or
128 lessor’s taking possession of the collateral or thereafter until the secured creditor or lessor has
129 either disposed of the collateral, entered into a contract for its disposition or gained the right to
130 retain the collateral in satisfaction of the buyer’s or lessee’s obligation.
