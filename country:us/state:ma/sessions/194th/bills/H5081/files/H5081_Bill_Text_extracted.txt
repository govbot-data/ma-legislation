HOUSE . . . . . . . . No. 5081
The Commonwealth of Massachusetts
________________________________________
HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES, February 17, 2026.
The committee on Consumer Protection and Professional Licensure, to
whom were referred the petition (accompanied by bill, House, No. 339) of
Tackey Chan relative to funeral directors; the petition (accompanied by
bill, House, No. 355) of Mark J. Cusack relative to animal telehealth; the
petition (accompanied by bill, House, No. 368) of Christopher Richard
Flanagan relative to the representation of the Board of Registration of
Cosmetology and Barbering; the petition (accompanied by bill, House,
No. 371) of Paul K. Frost and Joseph D. McKenna relative to bans on the
sale of legal consumer products by local boards of health; the joint petition
(accompanied by bill, House, No. 372) of William C. Galvin and Michael
D. Brady (by vote of the town) that the town of Avon be authorized to
grant one additional license for the sale of all alcoholic beverages not to be
drunk on the premise; the petition (accompanied by bill, House, No. 373)
of William C. Galvin relative to the financing of motor vehicle purchases;
the petition (accompanied by bill, House, No. 376) of Colleen M. Garry
for legislation to restrict sales of certain items and products at flea
markets, so-called; the petition (accompanied by bill, House, No. 385) of
Christopher Hendricks relative to civil actions for unfair or deceptive
practices; the petition (accompanied by bill, House, No. 392) of Steven S.
Howitt relative to funeral homes and charitable foundations; the petition
(accompanied by bill, House, No. 393) of Steven S. Howitt and David F.
DeCoste relative to out-of-state purchases at auto auctions; the petition
(accompanied by bill, House, No. 394) of Steven S. Howitt relative to the
displaying of motor fuel prices based on the method of payment; the
petition (accompanied by bill, House, No. 396) of Steven S. Howitt
relative to background checks for bidders at auto auctions; the petition
(accompanied by bill, House, No. 397) of Steven S. Howitt relative to
warranty for motor vehicles after certain sales; the petition (accompanied
by bill, House, No. 398) of Steven S. Howitt and Lindsay N. Sabadosa
relative to disclosure notice in the right to repair motor vehicle data law;
the petition (accompanied by bill, House, No. 415) of Patrick Joseph

Kearney for legislation to ban purchases and consumption of Russian
made products; the petition (accompanied by bill, House, No. 426) of Jack
Patrick Lewis and others for legislation to regulate the selling of animal
tested cosmetics; the petition (accompanied by bill, House, No. 446) of
James M. Murphy for legislation to further regulate wildlife rehabilitators;
the petition (accompanied by bill, House, No. 463) of Lindsay N.
Sabadosa relative to warnings on pumps or other dispensing devices from
which motor fuel is sold; the petition (accompanied by bill, House, No.
470) of Jeffrey Rosario Turco and Hadley Luddy relative to stores offering
digital coupons; and the petition (accompanied by bill, House, No. 472) of
Marcus S. Vaughn relative to motor vehicle dealership transparency,
reports recommending that the accompanying order (House, No. 5081)
ought to be adopted.
For the committee,
TACKEY CHAN.
