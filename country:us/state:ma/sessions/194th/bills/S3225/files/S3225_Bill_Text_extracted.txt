2 of 15 15  “CHAPTER 90L. AUTOMATED ROAD SAFETY ENFORCEMENT PROGRAM. 
16  Section 1. For the purposes of this chapter, the following words shall have the following 
17 meanings unless the context clearly requires otherwise: 
18  "Automated road safety camera system", an automated motor vehicle sensor device that 
19 produces digital photographs, video or other images of a motor vehicle that commits a speed 
20 camera enforceable violation at the location where the automated motor vehicle sensor device is 
21 installed. 
22  "Department", the Massachusetts Department of Transportation. 
23  "Enforcing authority", (i) the municipal entity designated by the city manager in a city 
24 with a Plan D or E form of government, the mayor in all other cities or the select board in a 
25 town; (ii) the department; or (iii) such other person, contractor or entity as the municipal entity or 
26 the department may designate to supervise and coordinate the administration of speed camera 
27 enforceable violations under this chapter. 
28  "Registrar", the registrar of motor vehicles. 
29  "Secretary", the secretary of the Massachusetts Department of Transportation. 
30  "Social and racial equity", efforts, policies, standards, processes and any other functions 
31 of government intended to ensure that patterns of discrimination against and disparities of race, 
32 ethnicity or socioeconomic status, whether intentional or unintentional, are neither reinforced nor 
33 perpetuated and to prevent the emergence and persistence of foreseeable future patterns of 
34 discrimination against or disparities of race, ethnicity or socioeconomic status. 

3 of 15 35  "Speed camera enforceable violation", exceeding the posted speed limit in violation of 
36 section 17, section 17C, section 17D or section 18 of chapter 90, by: (i) 11 miles per hour or 
37 more in a school zone established by a city or town pursuant to section 2 of chapter 85, when a 
38 school zone speed limit is in effect; or (ii) 11 miles per hour or more in an active construction or 
39 work zone, or a designated safety zone established pursuant to section 18B of said chapter 90. 
40  Section 2. (a) There shall be within the department’s highway division an automated road 
41 safety enforcement program. The department's highway division shall establish by regulation the 
42 requirements, standards and processes for participation in the automated road safety enforcement 
43 program for municipalities and issue a procurement pursuant to which enforcing authorities may 
44 obtain road safety camera systems and other related services to implement speed camera 
45 enforcement of violations. An enforcing authority may install automated road safety camera 
46 systems as a means of promoting traffic safety. The automated road safety camera systems may 
47 be placed: (i) by a municipality along any school zone established by the city or town pursuant to 
48 section 2 of chapter 85; (ii) by a municipality along any safety zone established by the city or 
49 town as provided in section 18B of chapter 90; or (iii) by the department in any active 
50 construction or work zone. 
51  Nothing in this section shall be construed as limiting the authority of law enforcement to 
52 issue citations for speeding violations pursuant to section 2 of chapter 90C. 
53  (b) An enforcing authority shall not employ more than 1 automated road safety camera 
54 system per 5,000 residents as measured by using the most recent census data. Plans for locating 
55 automated road safety camera systems shall be approved by the select board in a town, or by the 
56 city council and the mayor in a city. Nothing in this paragraph shall limit the department from 

4 of 15 57 employing an automated road safety camera system in a construction or work zone, irrespective 
58 of the number of automated road safety camera systems in use in the city or town in which the 
59 work zone exists. 
60  (c) Annually, not later than December 1, a participating city or town shall transmit a 
61 report to the department detailing each automated road safety camera system located in the city 
62 or town during the previous fiscal year. The report shall be in the form and manner determined 
63 by the department and shall include, but not be limited to: (i) a list of the locations of the 
64 automated road safety camera system in the city or town; (ii) the number of fines and warnings 
65 issued for each separate location; (iii) the number of fines and warnings successfully contested 
66 for each separate location's speed camera enforceable violations; (iv) an analysis of speed and 
67 crash data at each separate location; and (v) a description of any other public safety impacts of 
68 the city or town's participation in the automated road safety enforcement program. The 
69 department shall post all reports received pursuant to this section on its website. 
70  Section 3. (a) The fines and warnings imposed for speed camera enforceable violations, 
71 as accrued in a 2-year look-back period, shall be as follows: (i) a warning for a first violation and 
72 (ii) either: (A) $25 for a second or subsequent violation or (B) $100 for a second or subsequent 
73 violation of driving at a speed of 25 miles per hour or more over the posted speed limit; 
74 provided, however, that if subclause (B) applies, then the fine under subclause (A) shall not 
75 apply. Enforcing authorities shall be responsible for tracking subsequent violations to ascertain 
76 the appropriate fee under this section. 
77  (b) Except as provided in section 4, the registered owner of a motor vehicle shall be liable 
78 for the fine; provided, however, that a registered owner of a motor vehicle shall not be liable for 

5 of 15 79 the fine imposed under this program for a speed camera enforceable violation if the operator of 
80 the motor vehicle was issued a citation for the violation in accordance with section 2 of chapter 
81 90C. 
82  (c) A certificate, or a facsimile thereof, based upon inspection of photographs, video or 
83 other images and data produced by an automated road safety camera system and sworn to or 
84 affirmed by the enforcing authority shall be prima facie evidence of the facts contained therein. 
85  (d) Notice of a speed camera enforceable violation issued by an enforcing authority under 
86 the automated road safety enforcement program shall not be: (i) made part of the official record 
87 of the person upon whom such liability is imposed as provided in section 27 of chapter 90; or (ii) 
88 a conviction of a moving violation of the motor vehicle laws for the purpose of determining a 
89 surcharge on a motor vehicle premium pursuant to section 113B of chapter 175. 
90  (e) An enforcing authority may hire and designate personnel as necessary or contract for 
91 services to implement the automated road safety enforcement program through the procurement 
92 conducted by the highway division provided in subsection (a) of section 2. Any such contract 
93 shall include a provision that all data collected from automated road safety camera systems is 
94 confidential and the exclusive property of the contracting enforcement authority, and shall 
95 prohibit the contractor from using, disclosing, selling or permitting access to data collected by an 
96 automated road safety camera system except as necessary to process speed camera enforceable 
97 violations and conduct reporting in accordance with this chapter. 
98  (f)(1) The enforcing authority shall provide a notice of violation to the registered owner 
99 of a motor vehicle that is identified in photographs, video or other images produced by an 
100 automated road safety camera system as evidence of a speed camera enforceable violation 

6 of 15 101 pursuant to the automated road safety enforcement program. The notice shall include, but not be 
102 limited to: (i) a copy of the photographs produced by the automated road safety camera system 
103 and any other data showing the vehicle in the process of a speed camera enforceable violation; 
104 (ii) the registration number and state of issuance of the vehicle; (iii) the date, time and location of 
105 the alleged speed camera enforceable violation; (iv) the specific speed camera enforceable 
106 violation charged; (v) the speed limit and the actual speed of the vehicle; (vi) instructions for 
107 payment of the fine imposed pursuant to subsection (a); (vii) instructions on how to appeal the 
108 speed camera enforceable violation in writing and to obtain a hearing; and (viii) an affidavit form 
109 approved by the enforcing authority for the purposes of making a written appeal pursuant to 
110 subsection (i). 
111  (2) In the case of a violation involving a motor vehicle registered in the commonwealth, 
112 the enforcing authority shall mail the notice of violation within 14 days of the violation to the 
113 address of the registered owner of the motor vehicle as listed in the records of the registrar. If a 
114 motor vehicle is registered under the laws of another state or country, the notice of violation shall 
115 be mailed within 21 days of the violation to the address of the registered owner as listed in the 
116 records of the official in the state or country that has charge of the registration of the motor 
117 vehicle. If the address is unavailable, it shall be sufficient for the enforcing authority to mail a 
118 notice of violation to the official in the state or country that has charge of the registration of the 
119 motor vehicle. 
120  (3) The notice of violation shall be sent by first class mail. A manual or automatic record 
121 of mailing processed by or on behalf of the enforcing authority in the ordinary course of business 
122 shall be prima facie evidence thereof and shall be admitted as evidence in any judicial or 
123 administrative proceeding as to the facts contained therein. 

7 of 15 124  (g) A registered owner of a motor vehicle shall not be liable for a speed camera 
125 enforceable violation under this program if the: (i) operator of the motor vehicle was operating 
126 an emergency vehicle; (ii) violation was necessary to allow the passage of an emergency vehicle; 
127 (iii) violation was incurred during a period of time in which the motor vehicle was reported to 
128 the police department of any state, city or town as having been stolen and had not been recovered 
129 before the time the violation occurred; (iv) operator of the motor vehicle was operating the motor 
130 vehicle under a rental or lease agreement and the registered owner of the motor vehicle is a rental 
131 or leasing company and has complied with section 4; (v) operator of the motor vehicle was 
132 issued a citation for the violation in accordance with section 2 of chapter 90C; or (vi) violation 
133 was necessary to comply with any other law or regulation governing the operation of a motor 
134 vehicle. 
135  (h) A registered owner of a motor vehicle to whom a notice of violation has been issued 
136 pursuant to this program may admit responsibility for the violation and pay the fine provided 
137 therein in accordance with the instructions in the notice of violation. Payment of the established 
138 fine shall operate as the final disposition of a speed camera enforceable violation; provided, 
139 however, that payment by a registered owner of a motor vehicle shall operate as the final 
140 disposition of the violation as to any other registered owner of the same motor vehicle for the 
141 same violation. 
142  (i) Not more than 60 days after notice of a speed camera enforceable violation is given 
143 pursuant to this chapter, a registered owner of a motor vehicle may contest responsibility for the 
144 violation in writing by mail or online. The registered owner shall provide the enforcing authority 
145 with a signed affidavit, in a form approved by the enforcing authority, stating the: (i) reason for 
146 disputing the violation; (ii) full legal name and address of the registered owner of the motor 

8 of 15 147 vehicle; and (iii) full legal name and address of the operator of the motor vehicle at the time the 
148 violation occurred. The registered owner may include signed statements from witnesses, 
149 including the names and addresses of witnesses, supporting the registered owner's defense. Not 
150 more than 21 days after receipt of the signed affidavit, the enforcing authority or the hearing 
151 officer shall send the decision of the hearing officer, including the reasons for the outcome, by 
152 first class mail to the registered owner. If the registered owner is found responsible for the 
153 violation, the registered owner shall pay the fine in the manner described in subsection (h) not 
154 more than 14 days after the issuance of the decision or request further judicial review pursuant to 
155 section 14 of chapter 30A. 
156  (j) In lieu of contesting responsibility for a violation in writing or online pursuant to 
157 subsection (i) and not more than 60 days after a violation under this program, a registered owner 
158 of the motor vehicle may request a hearing in accordance with the instructions in the notice of 
159 violation to contest responsibility for a speed camera enforceable violation. A hearing request 
160 shall be made in writing by mail or online. Upon receipt of a hearing request, the enforcing 
161 authority shall schedule the matter before a hearing officer. The hearing officer may be an 
162 employee of the enforcing authority or such other person as the enforcing authority may 
163 designate. Written notice of the date, time and place of the hearing shall be sent by first class 
164 mail to each registered owner of the motor vehicle. The hearing shall be informal, the rules of 
165 evidence shall not apply and the decision of the hearing officer shall be final subject to judicial 
166 review pursuant to section 14 of chapter 30A. Not more than 21 days after the hearing, the 
167 enforcing authority or the hearing officer shall send the decision of the hearing officer, including 
168 the reason for the outcome, by first class mail to the registered owner. If the registered owner is 
169 found to be responsible for the speed camera enforceable violation, the registered owner shall 

9 of 15 170 pay the fine in the manner described in subsection (h) not more than 14 days after the issuance of 
171 the decision or request further judicial review pursuant to said section 14 of said chapter 30A. 
172  (k) Subject to any limitations the department may impose by regulation or by agreement 
173 with the registrar, the enforcing authority may notify the registrar when a Massachusetts resident 
174 and registered owner of a motor vehicle to whom a notice of a speed camera enforceable 
175 violation has been issued: (i) fails to contest the responsibility for a violation pursuant to 
176 subsection (i) or subsection (j) and fails to pay the fine in the notice in accordance with 
177 subsection (h) within 60 days of the violation; or (ii) is found responsible for the violation and 
178 does not pay the fine in accordance with subsection (h) and the registrar shall not renew the 
179 vehicle's registration. The enforcing authority may, not later than 2 years after the initial fine 
180 issuance was made, transmit such notice of non-payment to the registrar, in such form and 
181 containing such information as required by the registrar; provided, however, that no notice shall 
182 be transmitted to the registrar under this section at a time when there is pending, before either the 
183 enforcing authority or a court, a duly filed appeal of the fine. Upon receipt of such notification of 
184 nonpayment the registrar shall place the matter on record and not renew the registration of the 
185 motor vehicle to which a notice of a speed camera enforceable violation has been issued to the 
186 registered owner, nor allow an exchange of the registration of such vehicle nor issue a new 
187 registration of such vehicle to the person to whom the unpaid fine was assessed until after notice 
188 from the enforcing authority that the matter has been disposed of in accordance with the 
189 requirements herein. Upon such notification of nonpayment to the registrar, an additional $20 
190 charge payable to the registrar of motor vehicles shall be assessed against the registered owner of 
191 said vehicle to be collected by the enforcing authority to be transferred to the registry of motor 
192 vehicles as part of the non- renewal process. It shall be the duty of the enforcing authority to 

10 of 15 193 notify the registrar that such matters have been disposed of in accordance with the requirements 
194 herein; provided however, that a certified receipt of full and final payment from the enforcing 
195 authority shall also serve as a legal notice to the registrar that the matter has been resolved. The 
196 registrar shall approve such forms as they deem necessary to implement this section and said 
197 forms shall be printed and used by the enforcing authorities. 
198  Section 4. (a) Notwithstanding section 3, if the registered owner of a motor vehicle is a 
199 person or entity engaged in the business of leasing or renting motor vehicles and the motor 
200 vehicle was operated under a rental or lease agreement at the time of the speed camera 
201 enforceable violation, this section shall be applicable and the registered owner shall not be liable 
202 for any unpaid fines if the registered owner has complied with the requirements of this section. 
203  (b) The enforcing authority shall provide notice in writing of each speed camera 
204 enforceable violation to the registered owner of a motor vehicle if a motor vehicle owned by the 
205 registered owner is involved in a speed camera enforceable violation. 
206  (c) Not more than 45 days after the violation, the registered owner shall furnish to the 
207 enforcing authority, in writing, the name and address of the lessee or rentee of the motor vehicle 
208 at the time of the speed camera enforceable violation, the lessee's or rentee's driver's license 
209 number, the state that issued the driver's license and the lessee's or rentee's date of birth. 
210  (d) Upon receipt of the information required under subsection (c), the enforcing authority 
211 shall issue a notice of a speed camera enforceable violation to the lessee or rentee in the form 
212 prescribed by section 3 and the lessee or rentee shall be liable for the violation. 
213  (e) Subject to any limitations the department may impose by regulation or by agreement 
214 with the registrar, the enforcing authority may notify the registrar as provided in subsection (k) 

11 of 15 215 of section 3 if the lessee or rentee to whom a notice of violation has been issued: (i) fails to 
216 contest the responsibility for a speed camera enforceable violation pursuant to either subsection 
217 (i) or subsection (j) of section 3 and fails to pay the fine in the notice in accordance with 
218 subsection (h) of said section 3 within 90 days of the violation; or (ii) is found responsible for the 
219 violation and does not pay the fine in accordance with said subsection (h) of said section 3; 
220 provided, however, that if the vehicle owner furnished the information under subsection (c), such 
221 non-renewal shall only apply to the license or right to operate of the lessee or rentee and not the 
222 registration of the vehicle. 
223  Section 5. (a) An enforcing authority shall install a reasonable distance away from each 
224 road safety camera system an unobstructed sign notifying the public that an automated road 
225 safety camera system is in use. 
226  (b) An enforcing authority shall make a public announcement and conduct a public 
227 awareness campaign concerning its use of automated road safety camera systems beginning not 
228 less than 60 days before the first such automated road safety camera system is put into use; 
229 provided, however, that an enforcing authority may install but shall not activate automated road 
230 safety camera systems during the 60-day time period; provided further, that no further public 
231 awareness campaign shall be required for additional automated road safety camera systems that 
232 may be added in the participating city or town. 
233  Section 6. (a) The compensation paid to the manufacturer or vendor of an automated road 
234 safety camera system shall not be based on the number of speed camera enforceable violations 
235 issued or the revenue generated by the automated road safety camera system. 

12 of 15 236  (b) Not less than every 90 days, a city or town that adopts this chapter, or their designee, 
237 shall inspect the automated road safety camera system to verify that the automated road safety 
238 camera system is correctly calibrated. Not less than annually, an independent professional 
239 engineer registered in the commonwealth or an independent laboratory shall verify that the 
240 automated road safety camera system are correctly calibrated. Prior to the installation of an 
241 automated road safety camera system in a work zone, the department or their designee shall 
242 inspect the automated road safety camera system to verify that the automated road safety camera 
243 system is correctly calibrated. 
244  Section 7. (a) An automated road safety camera system shall only retain photographs, 
245 video or other images when a speed camera enforceable violation occurs. Photographs and video 
246 shall be destroyed not more than 48 hours after the final disposition of a speed camera 
247 enforceable violation. 
248  (b) A photograph, video or other image taken pursuant to this chapter shall not be 
249 discoverable in any judicial or administrative proceeding, other than in a proceeding held 
250 pursuant to this chapter, without a court order. A photograph or video taken pursuant to this 
251 chapter shall not be admissible in any judicial or administrative proceeding, other than in a 
252 proceeding to adjudicate liability for a violation of this chapter, without a court order. A court 
253 shall not order a release of a photograph or video taken pursuant to this chapter unless the 
254 photograph or video establishes or undermines a finding of a moving violation and the speed 
255 camera enforceable violation is material as to a finding of civil or criminal liability. 
256  (c) Photographs, video or other images and other personal identifying information 
257 collected pursuant to this chapter shall not be a public record under clause Twenty-sixth of 

13 of 15 258 section 7 of chapter 4 or chapter 66. Each enforcing authority shall maintain the confidentiality 
259 of all information including, but not limited to, photographs or other recorded images and credit 
260 and account data, relative to the registered vehicle or registered owner of the vehicle subject to a 
261 speed camera enforceable violation. Such information shall be used for enforcement purposes 
262 only with respect to speed camera enforceable violations under this chapter. 
263  (d) An automated road safety camera system shall not be utilized to take a frontal view 
264 photograph of a motor vehicle operator committing a speed camera enforceable violation or 
265 other occupants of the vehicle. A frontal view photograph of a motor vehicle committing a speed 
266 camera enforceable violation taken by an automated road safety camera system that captures the 
267 operator or occupants of the vehicle shall not be discoverable or admissible in any judicial or 
268 administrative proceeding and shall not be used as the basis for a speed camera enforceable 
269 violation under this chapter. To the extent practicable, additional efforts shall be made to ensure 
270 that photographs produced by an automated road safety camera system shall not be used to 
271 identify the vehicle operator, the passengers or the contents of the vehicle. The use of facial 
272 recognition technology in conjunction with an automated road safety camera system is 
273 prohibited. 
274  (e) A city or town or a manufacturer or vendor of an automated road safety camera 
275 system may not use, disclose, sell or permit access to data collected by an automated road safety 
276 camera system except as necessary to process speed camera enforceable violations and fulfill 
277 reporting requirements in accordance with this chapter. 
278  Section 8. An enforcing authority may recover costs reasonably related to the 
279 implementation and operation of an automated road safety camera system including, but not 

14 of 15 280 limited to, costs associated with: (i) purchasing, maintaining and operating the automated road 
281 safety camera system; (ii) issuing notices of speed camera enforceable violations; (iii) holding 
282 hearings for appeals of speed camera enforceable violations; (iv) notifying the registrar of a 
283 failure to pay a fine under this program; (v) fulfilling reporting requirements in accordance with 
284 this chapter; and (vi) collecting a fine; provided, however, that net revenues collected by 
285 participating cities and towns pursuant to this program shall be deposited in the Massachusetts 
286 Transportation Trust Fund established in section 4 of chapter 6C. 
287  Section 9. A city or town shall not implement this program unless the city or town has 
288 submitted a plan for the implementation of automated road safety camera systems to the 
289 department and the department has approved the plan. The plan submitted to the department 
290 shall include, but shall not be limited to, data and analysis of the traffic and safety history of the 
291 locations where automated road safety camera systems are proposed to be located, the discussion 
292 of social and racial equity impacts of the plan and steps the municipality shall take to ensure 
293 social and racial equity in the implementation of the plan. 
294  Nothing in this section shall limit the number of work zones in which the department may 
295 install automated road safety camera systems.”; and
