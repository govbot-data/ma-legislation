An Act relative to delivery network companies.
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority
of the same, as follows:
1 SECTION 1. The general laws are hereby amended by inserting after chapter 159B the
2 following chapter:-
3 Chapter 159B1/2.
4 DELIVERY NETWORK COMPANIES
5 Section 1.
6 Definitions.
7 For the purposes of this chapter the following terms shall have the following meanings:
8 “Delivery driver”, a person making deliveries of property as arranged through a delivery
9 network company.
1 of 4

10 “Delivery network company”, a private entity doing business in the Commonwealth
11 which uses a digital network to facilitate the delivery of property to consumers by delivery
12 drivers.
13 “Delivery driver account”, an account, profile, or other credential which allows a person
14 to use a delivery network company’s network to conduct deliveries as a delivery driver.
15 "Identifying apparel", a jacket, vest, or other wearing apparel on the upper part of a
16 delivery driver's body as the outermost garment while making deliveries the back of which shall
17 display the word “delivery” in lettering not less than two inches in height in a color that contrasts
18 with the color of the garment; provided that any garment prescribed by a municipality for
19 delivery drivers shall be considered adequate identifying apparel.
20 “Motorized delivery vehicle”, any vehicle used for the delivery of property on behalf of a
21 private entity that is driven, drawn or assisted by a motor of any kind, including, without
22 limitation, electric and internal combustion motors, provided, that a motorized delivery vehicle
23 shall not mean a commercial motor vehicle as defined in section 2 of chapter 159B.
24 “Registrar”, the Registry of Motor Vehicles.
25 Section 2.
26 No delivery driver shall make deliveries on any public way without a valid driver’s
27 license or license to operate a motorcycle, regardless of whether the vehicle would require a
28 license to be operated for a purpose other than making deliveries as arranged through a delivery
29 network company. Making deliveries in violation of this paragraph shall constitute a violation of
30 section 10 of chapter 90.
2 of 4

31 Any person found to be in violation of this section shall be subject to the penalties
32 imposed by section 20 of chapter 90.
33 Section 3.
34 No delivery driver shall operate a motorized delivery vehicle on any way without
35 wearing identifying apparel unless they are fully enclosed within a vehicle. Any person
36 convicted of violating this section shall be subject to a civil fine of not more than $250 dollars.
37 Section 4.
38 A delivery network company shall only authorize a person to create a delivery driver
39 account and make deliveries using a motorized delivery vehicle if the person has a valid license
40 to operate the intended motorized delivery vehicle pursuant to section 2 of this chapter.
41 No delivery network company shall allow a person to create a delivery driver account if
42 the person has been convicted of the following:
43 Operating without a valid license in the past 5 years prior to their application; or
44 Operating without valid registration in the past 2 years prior to their application.
45 Delivery network companies shall contact the Registrar not less than every 6 months to
46 verify the licenses associated with all delivery driver accounts. If any license is found to be
47 invalid, the corresponding delivery driver account shall be immediately suspended until the
48 license is again valid.
49 The Registrar shall promulgate all rules and regulations necessary for the implementation
50 of this chapter.
3 of 4

51 SECTION 2. Section 31 of chapter 90 of the general laws, as appearing in the 2022
52 official edition, is hereby amended in the first sentence by inserting after the word “trailer” the
53 following text:- or motorized delivery vehicle as defined in section 1 of chapter 159B1/2.
54 SECTION 3. Section 1 of chapter 90C of the general laws is hereby amended by
55 inserting in line 22, after the words “chapter 90,”, the following text:- and a motorized delivery
56 vehicle as defined in section 1 of chapter 159B1/2.
