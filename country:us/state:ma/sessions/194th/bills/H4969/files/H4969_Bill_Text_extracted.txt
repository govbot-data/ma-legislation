The Commonwealth of Massachusetts

HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES, February 2, 2026.
The committee on Consumer Protection and Professional Licensure, to
whom were referred the petition (accompanied by bill, House, No. 345) of
Tackey Chan relative to the size of alcoholic beverage containers; the
petition (accompanied by bill, House, No. 350) of Rob Consalvo relative
to private labels of distilled spirits; the petition (accompanied by bill,
House, No. 381) of Kenneth I. Gordon relative to the use retailer-backed
coupons by licensees authorized to sell alcoholic beverages at retail for
off-premises consumption; the petition (accompanied by bill, House, No.
430) of William F. MacGregor relative to modernizing charitable
organization fundraising opportunities; the petition (accompanied by bill,
House, No. 439) of Paul McMurtry relative to alcohol purchasing
discounts; and the petition (accompanied by bill, House, No. 467) of
Priscila S. Sousa relative to water rates for tenants, reports recommending
that the accompanying order (House, No. 4969) ought to be adopted.
For the committee,
TACKEY CHAN.

HOUSE . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . No. 4969
The Commonwealth of Massachusetts
House of Representatives,
1 Ordered, That the committee on Consumer Protection and Professional Licensure be
2 authorized to sit during a recess of the General Court to make an investigation and study of
3 House documents numbered 345, 350, 381, 430, 439 and 467, relative to consumer protection
4 and professional licensure matters.

6 and its recommendations, if any, together with drafts of legislation necessary to carry such
7 recommendations into effect, by filing the same with the Clerk of the House of Representatives
8 on or before December 31, 2026.
