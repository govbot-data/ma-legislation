HOUSE . . . . . . . . No. 5423
The Commonwealth of Massachusetts
________________________________________
HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES, May 14, 2026.
The committee on Public Service, to whom were referred the joint
petition (accompanied by bill, House, No. 4698) of Donald R. Berthiaume,
Jr., and Peter J. Durant relative to the retirement benefits of Dale R. Kiley,
a retired state trooper; the joint petition (accompanied by bill, House, No.
4742) of Aaron L. Saunders and Joanne M. Comerford (by vote of the
town) that the town of Pelham be authorized to continue the employment
of Gary L. Thomann as police chief in said town; the petition
(accompanied by bill, House, No. 4747) of Antonio F. D. Cabral relative
to benefit payments by the New Bedford Police Association; the petition
(accompanied by bill, House, No. 4977) of Michelle L. Badger relative to
a transfer of retirement benefits for Valeria D'Orazio an associate
professor at the Massachusetts Maritime Academy; and the petition
(accompanied by bill, House, No. 5159) of Brian M. Ashe for legislation
to authorize the Massachusetts Teachers’ Retirement System to grant
credible service to Sara Scibelli, reports (under the provisions of House
Rule 27) recommending that the accompanying order (House, No. 5423)
ought to be adopted.
For the committee,
DANIEL J. RYAN.
