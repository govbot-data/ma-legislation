An Act to connect veterans with earned benefits. 
 
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority 
of the same, as follows: 
 
1  SECTION 1. Section 3 of said chapter 276A, as so appearing, is hereby amended by 
2 striking out the first paragraph and inserting in place thereof the following paragraph:-   
3  The probation officers of a district or municipal court, or an official designee of such a 
4 probation officer, when gathering information in accordance with section 85 of chapter 276, shall 
5 also screen each defendant for the purpose of enabling the judge at arraignment to consider the 
6 eligibility of the defendant for diversion to a program. The probation officers or an official 
7 designee shall also confirm the defendant's status as a veteran or as a person on active service in 
8 the armed forces of the United States and shall determine if the defendant has previously been 
9 diverted pursuant to clause (ii) of subsection (c) of section 4. Any person identified as a veteran, 
10 as defined in said clause, shall be provided with a list of contact information for the Veteran’s 

2 of 2 11 Administration, the local Veteran Justice Officer, their local Veteran Service Officer and the 
12 Veterans Administration Department of Veteran Services Suicide Hotline.   
13  SECTION 2. The first paragraph of section 3 of chapter 276A, as so appearing, is hereby 
14 amended by adding after the first paragraph the following paragraph:  
15  Once a veteran is identified by the indigency verification process, the office of probation 
16 shall refer said veteran to the Veteran Justice Officer for determination of Federal Benefits.
