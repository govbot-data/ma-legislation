An Act to fairly fund early voting.
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority
of the same, as follows:
1 SECTION 1. Section 25B of Chapter 54 of the General Laws is hereby amended by
2 inserting at the end thereof the following new subsection:-
3 “(r) The Commonwealth shall reimburse each city and town to the full extent of the
4 amounts expended for mandated early voting procedures and operations. The state treasurer shall
5 annually, on or before July 1st of the year following the election for which reimbursement is
6 being sought, pay to cities and towns, the sums required for such reimbursement and approved
7 by the secretary of state pursuant to section 2 herein. Each city and town may establish an Early
8 Voting Reimbursement Fund. Reimbursements made by the commonwealth pursuant to this
9 section may be deposited into the fund and may carry forward for 1 fiscal year.”
1 of 2

10 SECTION 2. Section 25B of Chapter 54 of the General Laws is hereby amended by
11 inserting in subsection (o) the following at the end thereof:- “including the means to seek,
12 process and approve reimbursement subject to subsection (r)”.
