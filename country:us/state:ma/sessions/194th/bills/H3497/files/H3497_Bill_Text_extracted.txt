An Act relative to a renewable portfolio standard review.
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority
of the same, as follows:
1 The department of energy resources shall, in consultation with the department of
2 environmental protection, conduct a review and analysis of the renewable portfolio standard
3 program in the commonwealth. The review should consider the effectiveness of the program to
4 date and identify ways to make improvements to further facilitate the development of affordable
5 renewable energy in the commonwealth. A report summarizing these findings and
6 recommendations shall be submitted to the joint committee on telecommunications, utilities and
7 energy, and the clerks of the house and senate no later than 12 months from the effective date of
8 this legislation.
