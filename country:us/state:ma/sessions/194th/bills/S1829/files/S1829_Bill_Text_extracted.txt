An Act relative to certain licensed pipefitters and refrigeration technicians.
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority
of the same, as follows:
1 Section 3 of Chapter 32 of the General Laws, as appearing in the 2020 Official Edition, is
2 hereby amended by inserting in line 315, after the clause "employees of the Massachusetts Port
3 Authority who are employed as licensed electricians, utility technicians, steam engineers, watch
4 engineers, boiler operators," the following words:- "licensed pipefitters and refrigeration
5 technicians,".
