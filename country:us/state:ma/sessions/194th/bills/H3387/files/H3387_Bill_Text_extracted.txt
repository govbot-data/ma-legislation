An Act relative to expedited referrals by legislators.
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority
of the same, as follows:
1 Chapter 6 of the General Laws is hereby amended by adding the following section:-
2 Section 219. Notwithstanding any general or special law to the contrary, a state agency
3 shall disclose to a state legislator or an authorized staff person of a state legislator, who is
4 reaching out to the agency on behalf of a constituent, personal information about the constituent
5 to the extent necessary for the state legislator or authorized staff person to provide assistance on
6 the behalf of the constituent; provided, however, that the state agency shall not require an
7 authorization for release of information from the constituent or state legislator unless otherwise
8 required by federal law.
