An Act prohibiting the aquaculture of any species of octopus for the purpose of human 
consumption. 
 
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority 
of the same, as follows: 
 
1  Chapter 130 of the General Laws is hereby amended by inserting after section 106 the 
2 following section:- 
3  Section 107. (a) For the purposes of this section, the following words shall, unless the 
4 context clearly requires otherwise, have the following meanings:- 
5  “Aquaculture”, waters used primarily and directly in the commercial cultivation of 
6 aquatic organisms including finfish, mollusk or crustacean in a controlled environment. 
7  “Octopus”, a cephalopod mollusk with a soft body and 8 limbs belonging to the order 
8 Octopoda and does not include wild-caught octopuses or octopuses propagated, cultivated, 
9 maintained, reared or harvested only for research purposes unrelated to commercial production. 
10  “Wild-caught‘ or “wild-captured" any species of octopus harvested from its natural 
11 marine environment. 

2 of 2 12  (b) No person shall engage in the aquaculture of any species of octopus for the purpose of 
13 human consumption. 
14  (c) No business entity shall sell, possess or transport any species of octopus that is the 
15 result or product of aquaculture. 
16  (d) Whoever violates any provision of this section or any rule or regulation adopted 
17 pursuant thereto, shall be subject, upon an order of a court, to a civil penalty not to exceed 
18 $1,000 per day of the violation, and each day during which the violation continues shall 
19 constitute an additional, separate and distinct offense. Any civil penalty imposed pursuant to this 
20 section may be collected with costs in a summary proceeding pursuant to chapter 30A or may be 
21 collected in a civil action commenced by the commissioner. In addition to any penalties, costs or 
22 interest charges, the superior court may assess against the violator the amount of economic 
23 benefit accruing to the violator from the violation. 
24  (e) Nothing in this section shall prevent any: (i) person from engaging in the wild-capture 
25 of any species of octopus for the purpose of human consumption; and (ii) business entity from 
26 selling, possessing or transporting any species of octopus that is the result or product of being 
27 wild-caught. 
28  (f) The division may adopt rules and regulations as may be necessary to implement the 
29 provisions of this section. 
30  (g) Any species of octopus that are wild caught and land in the commonwealth shall be 
31 regulated under 322 CMR due to octopus being shipped and marketed throughout the country, 
32 regulations governing their transport, handling and sale are necessary to differentiate between 
33 aquaculture raised octopus product from wild caught octopus.
