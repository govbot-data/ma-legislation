An Act regarding municipal zoning powers. 
 
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority 
of the same, as follows: 
 
1  Section 3 of Chapter 40A of the General Laws is hereby amended by striking the 
2 following language: 
3  "No zoning ordinance or by-law shall prohibit or unreasonably regulate the installation of 
4 solar energy systems or the building of structures that facilitate the collection of solar energy, 
5 except where necessary to protect the public health, safety or welfare."
