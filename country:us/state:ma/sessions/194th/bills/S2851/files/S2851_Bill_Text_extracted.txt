50 following section:-
51 Section 290. Natural Organic Reduction
3 of 4

52 A corporation may operate a natural organic reduction facility to engage in the practice of
53 natural organic reduction through the employment of natural organic reduction operators. Said
54 corporations shall receive operating licensure from the Division of Occupational Licensure. The
55 Board of Registration in Embalming and Funeral Directing, in consultation with the Division of
56 Occupational Licensure and the Department of Public Health, shall promulgate rules and
57 regulations for the administration of natural organic reduction facility and operator licenses
58 which shall include operational standards, personnel qualifications, environmental safeguards,
59 and testing for pathogens and toxic materials.
60 A natural organic reduction facility shall not be bound by any ownership interest
61 threshold that exists for licensed funeral establishments.
