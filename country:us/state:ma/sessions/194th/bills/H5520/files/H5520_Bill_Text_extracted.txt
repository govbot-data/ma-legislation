To the Honorable Senate and House of Representatives, 
 
 Pursuant to the provisions of Article II, Section 8, Paragraph 1, Clause (2) of the 
Amendments to the Constitution, as appearing in Article LXXXIX of said Amendments, I am 
filing for your consideration the attached legislation entitled “An Act exempting George Patrinos 
from the maximum retirement age for the position of firefighter in the Town of Dudley.” 
 I am filing this special legislation at the request of the Town of Dudley’s Town 
Administrator and Fire Chief, with support from Dudley’s legislative delegation.   
 This legislation would exempt George Patrinos, a 64-year-old call firefighter in the Town 
of Dudley, from the mandatory retirement age of 65 as contemplated under M.G.L. c. 32, § 1. As 
a call firefighter, Mr. Patrinos acts as a part-time emergency responder who augments full-time 
personnel by responding to calls only in the event of an ongoing incident. Granting this 
exemption would preserve continuity of public safety services in the town while imposing no 
additional burden on the Commonwealth.   
 Mr. Patrinos’ 65th birthday is on October 20th, 2026. I therefore respectfully urge your 
prompt consideration of this bill so that Mr. Patrinos may continue serving as an active call 
firefighter before he reaches the mandatory retirement age. Timely action will ensure no 
interruption in the experienced emergency response support he provides to the Dudley Fire 
Department.   
        Respectfully submitted, 
 
 
Maura T. Healey, 
        Governor

An Act exempting George Patrinos from the maximum age requirement age for the position of 
firefighter in the town of Dudley. 
 
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority 
of the same, as follows: 
 
1  Section 1: (a) Notwithstanding chapter 32 of the General Laws or any other general or 
2 special law to the contrary, George Patrinos, firefighter in the town of Dudley, may continue to 
3 serve in that position until he reaches the age of 70; provided, however, that he is mentally and 
4 physically capable of performing the duties of such position. The select board of the town of 
5 Dudley may, at its own expense, require George Patrinos to undergo an annual examination by 
6 an impartial physician designated by the town to determine his capability to remain in service.    
7  (b) No further deductions shall be made from the regular compensation of George 
8 Patrinos under said chapter 32 for any service subsequent to his reaching 65 years of age and, 
9 upon retirement, George Patrinos shall receive a superannuation retirement allowance equal to 
10 that which he would have been entitled had he retired at 65 years of age, if any.    
11  Section 2: This Act shall take effect upon its passage.
