An Act establishing an economic development special revenue account for the town of
Hopkinton.
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority
of the same, as follows:
1 SECTION 1. (a) Notwithstanding section 53 of chapter 44 of the General Laws or any
2 other general or special law to the contrary, the town of Hopkinton shall establish in the town
3 treasury a special revenue account to be known as the Economic Development Special Revenue
4 Fund, into which shall be deposited certain receipts comprised of the total local meals tax
5 received annually by the town under subsections (b) and (c) of section 2 of chapter 64L of the
6 General Laws. The fund shall be used to support economic development projects, activities,
7 operations and administration in the town of Hopkinton, including those that stimulate economic
8 growth, attract and retain businesses, and improve infrastructure.
9 (b) Notwithstanding any general or special law to the contrary, the amount of the local
10 meals tax collected under said section 2 of said chapter 64L by the town for the fiscal year
11 beginning July 1, 2026 and each fiscal year thereafter shall be credited to the Economic
1 of 3

12 Development Special Revenue Fund and shall be subject to further appropriation by a majority
13 vote of the town meeting.
14 (c) Notwithstanding any general or special law to the contrary, any interest accruing on
15 any amount on deposit in the Economic Development Special Revenue Fund shall be credited to
16 the general fund of the town of Hopkinton.
17 (d) Nothing in this act shall affect amounts distributed in any fiscal year to the town from
18 the Local Aid Fund.
19 (e) If, by a majority vote of the town meeting pursuant to section 4B of chapter 4 of the
20 General Laws, the town revokes its acceptance of the local meals tax collected under said section
21 2 of said chapter 64L, the town shall decide by a 2/3 vote of the town meeting whether the
22 Economic Development Special Revenue Fund shall cease to have effect. If 2/3 of the town
23 meeting votes that the Economic Development Special Revenue Fund shall cease to have effect,
24 all unexpended and uncommitted amounts on deposit in the Economic Development Special
25 Revenue Fund as of the date of the vote to revoke the local meals tax shall be credited to the
26 general fund of the town on the first day of the calendar quarter following 30 days after the date
27 of the revocatory vote; provided, however, that if 2/3 of the town meeting does not vote that the
28 Economic Development Special Revenue Fund shall cease to have effect, the Economic
29 Development Special Revenue Fund shall continue to have effect and all unexpended and
30 uncommitted amounts on deposit in the Economic Development Special Revenue Fund as of the
31 date of the vote to revoke the local meals tax collected under said section 2 of said chapter 64L
32 shall be subject to further appropriation by a majority vote of the town meeting.
2 of 3

33 (f) The town may vote to close the Economic Development Special Revenue Fund by a
34 2/3 vote of the town meeting. The vote to close the Economic Development Special Revenue
35 Fund shall designate: (i) that the Economic Development Special Revenue Fund shall cease to
36 have effect; (ii) that all unexpended and uncommitted amounts on deposit in the Economic
37 Development Special Revenue Fund as of the date of the vote shall immediately be credited to
38 the general fund of the town; and (iii) that the local meals tax that is received annually by the
39 town under said section 2 of said chapter 64L and credited to the Economic Development
40 Special Revenue Fund as set forth in subsections (b) and (c) shall be credited to the general fund
41 of the town.
42 SECTION 2. The treasurer of the town of Hopklnton shall, until such time as the
43 Economic Development Special Revenue Fund ceases to exist, provide an annual financial report
44 to the town, which shall be publicly posted on or before October 1 of each year. The annual
45 financial report shall specifically include: (i) fund revenues; (ii) fund receipts; (iii) the amount of
46 grants made from the fund; (iv) the specific amounts of loans made by the find and to whom
47 such loans were made; and (v) the balance of money in the fund at the end of each fiscal year.
48 SECTION 3. This act shall take effect upon its passage.
