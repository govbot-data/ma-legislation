An Act relative to the diverse recruitment and reform of the Massachusetts State Police.
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority
of the same, as follows:
1 Section 22C of the General Laws is hereby amending by adding the following new
2 section:
3 Section XX. There is hereby established a career incentive pay program offering base
4 salary increases to regular full-time members of the state police department as an incentive for
5 furthering the education of current officers and recruiting diverse candidates to the state police
6 through future recruitment and the cadet program. This section shall not apply to officers
7 currently receiving career incentive pay for educational degrees under any other program or law.
8 Police career incentive base salary increases shall be predicated on the accumulation of
9 points earned in the following manner: one point for each semester hour credit earned toward a
1 of 2

10 baccalaureate or an associate degree; sixty points for an associate degree; one hundred and
11 twenty points for a baccalaureate degree; and one hundred and fifty points for a degree of master
12 or for a degree in law. All semester credits and degrees shall be earned in an accredited
13 educational institution and shall be credited for the purpose of determining points under this
14 section, notwithstanding the date of appointment of an individual to a position described in the
15 first paragraph of this section. Only graduates of: (1) an accredited degree program offered by
16 an institution of higher learning in order to ensure diverse educational backgrounds of current
17 and future officers, or (2) accredited law schools and who have passed the Massachusetts bar
18 examination shall be eligible for the police career incentive pay program.
19 Base salary increases authorized by this section shall be granted in the following manner:
20 a ten per cent increase for sixty points, a twenty per cent increase for one hundred and twenty
21 points, and a twenty-five per cent increase for one hundred and fifty points so accumulated.
