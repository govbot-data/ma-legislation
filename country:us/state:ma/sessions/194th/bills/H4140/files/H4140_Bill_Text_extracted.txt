An Act relative to non-civil service public safety personnel in the town of Wellesley.
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority
of the same, as follows:
1 SECTION 1. Notwithstanding section 99A of chapter 41 of the General Laws, or any
2 other general or special law to the contrary, the town of Wellesley may increase the 15-mile
3 residency limit contained in said section 99A of said chapter 41 for members of the regular
4 Wellesley police and fire departments. Provided, that any increase in the residency limit shall be
5 subject to a collective bargaining agreement negotiated in accordance with chapter 150E of the
6 General Laws.
7 SECTION 2. This act shall take effective upon its passage.
