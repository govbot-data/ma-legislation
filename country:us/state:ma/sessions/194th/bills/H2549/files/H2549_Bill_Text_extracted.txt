An Act relative to fluoride in water warnings.
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority
of the same, as follows:
1 Chapter 111 of the General Laws, as appearing in the 2022 Official Edition, is hereby
2 amended by inserting after section 8C the following section:-
3 Section 8D. The annual Consumer Confidence Report on water quality shall include the
4 following language whenever the fluoride in drinking water is at or above 0.3 ppm:
5 The offspring of pregnant persons and bottle-fed babies are at risk of developmental
6 neurotoxicity and damaged teeth from fluoride levels at or above 0.3 ppm;
7 Diabetics and those with kidney, thyroid, or inflammatory conditions are at risk of
8 worsening their conditions from consumption of waters at or above 0.3 ppm.
