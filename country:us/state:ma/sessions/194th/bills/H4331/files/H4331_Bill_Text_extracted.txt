3 of 9 31  Section 3. (a) A debtor is insolvent if, at a fair valuation, the sum of the debtor’s debts is 
32 greater than the sum of the debtor’s assets.  
33  (b) A debtor that is generally not paying the debtor’s debts as they become due other than 
34 as a result of a bona fide dispute is presumed to be insolvent. The presumption imposes on the 
35 party against which the presumption is directed the burden of proving that the nonexistence of 
36 insolvency is more probable than its existence.  
37  (c) Assets under this section shall not include property that has been transferred, 
38 concealed, or removed with intent to hinder, delay, or defraud creditors or that has been 
39 transferred in a manner making the transfer voidable under this chapter.  
40  (d) Debts under this section shall not include an obligation to the extent it is secured by a 
41 valid lien on property of the debtor not included as an asset.

79  SECTION 20. Said chapter 109A is hereby amended by striking out section 9, as so 
80 appearing, and inserting in place thereof the following section:-  
81  Section 9. (a) A transfer or obligation is not voidable under paragraph (1) of subsection 
82 (a) of section 5 against a person that took in good faith and for a reasonably equivalent value 
83 given the debtor or against any subsequent transferee or obligee.  
84  (b) To the extent a transfer is avoidable in an action by a creditor under paragraph (1) of 
85 subsection (a) of section 8, the following rules apply:  
86  (1) Except as otherwise provided in this section, the creditor may recover judgment for 
87 the value of the asset transferred, as adjusted under subsection (c), or the amount necessary to 
88 satisfy the creditor’s claim, whichever is less. The judgment may be entered against:  
89  (i) the first transferee of the asset or the person for whose benefit the transfer was made; 
90 or  
91  (ii) an immediate or mediate transferee of the first transferee, other than:  

6 of 9 92  (A) a good-faith transferee that took for value; or  
93  (B) an immediate or mediate good-faith transferee of a person described in clause (A).  
94  (2) Recovery pursuant to paragraph (1) of subsection (a) or (b) of section 8 of or from the 
95 asset transferred or its proceeds, by levy or otherwise, is available only against a person 
96 described in clause (i) or (ii) of paragraph (1).  
97  (c) If the judgment under subsection (b) is based upon the value of the asset transferred, 
98 the judgment shall be for an amount equal to the value of the asset at the time of the transfer, 
99 subject to adjustment as the equities may require.  
100  (d) Notwithstanding voidability of a transfer or an obligation under this chapter, a good-
101 faith transferee or obligee is entitled, to the extent of the value given the debtor for the transfer or 
102 obligation, to:  
103  (1) a lien on or a right to retain an interest in the asset transferred;  
104  (2) enforcement of an obligation incurred; or  
105  (3) a reduction in the amount of the liability on the judgment.  
106  (e) A transfer is not voidable under paragraph (2) of subsection (a) of section 5 or section 
107 6 if the transfer results from:  
108  (1) termination of a lease upon default by the debtor when the termination is pursuant to 
109 the lease and applicable law; or  
110  (2) enforcement of a security interest in compliance with Article 9 of chapter 106, other 
111 than acceptance of collateral in full or partial satisfaction of the obligation it secures.  

7 of 9 112  (f) A transfer is not voidable under subsection (b) of section 6:  
113  (1) to the extent the insider gave new value to or for the benefit of the debtor after the 
114 transfer was made, except to the extent the new value was secured by a valid lien;  
115  (2) if made in the ordinary course of business or financial affairs of the debtor and the 
116 insider; or  
117  (3) if made pursuant to a good-faith effort to rehabilitate the debtor and the transfer 
118 secured present value given for that purpose as well as an antecedent debt of the debtor.  
119  (g) The following rules determine the burden of proving matters referred to in this 
120 section:  
121  (1) A party that seeks to invoke subsection (a), (d), (e) or (f) has the burden of proving 
122 the applicability of that subsection.  
123  (2) Except as otherwise provided in paragraphs (3) and (4), the creditor has the burden of 
124 proving each applicable element of subsection (b) or (c).  
125  (3) The transferee has the burden of proving the applicability to the transferee of clause 
126 (A) or (B) of clause (ii) of paragraph 1 of subsection (b).  
127  (4) A party that seeks adjustment under subsection (c) has the burden of proving the 
128 adjustment.  
129  (h) The standard of proof required to establish matters referred to in this section is by a 
130 preponderance of the evidence.

139  Section 11. (a) In this section, the following rules determine a debtor’s location:  
140  (1) A debtor who is an individual is located at the individual’s principal residence.  
141  (2) A debtor that is an organization and has only one place of business is located at its 
142 place of business.  
143  (3) A debtor that is an organization and has more than one place of business is located at 
144 its principal place of business.  
145  (b) A claim for relief under this chapter is governed by the local law of the jurisdiction in 
146 which the debtor is located when the transfer is made or the obligation is incurred.  
147  Section 12. Unless otherwise required by the provisions of this chapter, the principles of 
148 law and equity, including the law merchant and the law relating to principal and agent, estoppel, 
149 laches, fraud, misrepresentation, duress, coercion, mistake, insolvency, or other validating or 
150 invalidating cause, shall supplement its provisions.

163  SECTION 25. This Act: (a) applies to a transfer made or obligation incurred on or after 
164 the effective date of the amendments; (b) does not apply to a transfer made or obligation incurred 
165 before the effective date of the amendments; and (c) does not apply to a right of action that has 
166 accrued before the effective date of the amendments. A transfer is made and an obligation is 
167 incurred at the time provided in section 6 of chapter 109A.
