An Act relative to certain hunting zones.
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority
of the same, as follows:
1 SECTION 1. Section 70 of Chapter 131 of the Massachusetts General Laws is hereby
2 amended by inserting after the last paragraph the following new paragraph;
3 "Notwithstanding the provisions of this section, or any other section under chapter 131,
4 the director may, with the approval of the board, may establish a season for deer with the use of a
5 rifle, revolver or pistol. Such seasons may be established in appropriate deer hunting zones as
6 determined by the director after assessing considerations including, but not limited to, human
7 population and housing densities."
