An Act relative to internet service provider data.
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority
of the same, as follows:
1 Chapter 93 of the General Laws, as appearing in the 2022 Official Edition is hereby
2 amended by inserting after section 114 the following new section:-
3 “Section 115. Notwithstanding any other general or special law to the contrary, no
4 telecommunications or internet service provider that has entered into a franchise agreement,
5 right-of-way- agreement, or other contract with the commonwealth of Massachusetts or a
6 political subdivision, or that uses facilities that are subject to such agreements, even if it is not a
7 party to the agreement, or otherwise operates in the commonwealth of Massachusetts may
8 collect, use, disclose or otherwise disseminate, personal information from a customer resulting
9 from the customer’s use of the telecommunications or internet service provider without express
10 written approval from the customer. No such telecommunication or internet service provider
1 of 2

11 shall add an additional surcharge for customers that do not provide their express written
12 approval, and said providers shall not refuse to provide services to a customer on the grounds
13 that the customer has not approved collection, use, disclosure or other forms of dissemination of
14 the customer’s personal information.
15 A telecommunication or internet service provider shall prohibit the sale of a customer’s
16 personally identifiable information unless the customer expressly, in writing, authorizes the
17 provider to disclose this information, which the customer may revoke, in writing, at any time.”
