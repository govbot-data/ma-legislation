The Commonwealth of Massachusetts
________________________________________
HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES, February 17, 2026.
The committee on Higher Education, to whom was referred the
petition (accompanied by bill, House, No. 1439) of Daniel J. Hunt for
legislation to provide full student representation on the University of
Massachusetts Board of Trustees; and the petition (accompanied by bill,
House, No. 1445) of Bradley H. Jones, Jr., and others for an investigation
by a special task force (including members of the General Court) relative
to the economic and educational impacts of establishing public private
partnerships between higher educational institutions and private
corporations, reports recommending that the accompanying order (House,
No. 5106) ought to be adopted.
For the committee,
DAVID M. ROGERS.

HOUSE . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . No. 5106
The Commonwealth of Massachusetts
House of Representatives,
1 Ordered, That the committee on Higher Education be authorized to sit during a recess of
2 the General Court to make an investigation and study of House documents numbered 1439 and
3 1445, relative to student representation and public private partnerships.
4 Said committee shall report to the General Court the results of its investigation and study
5 and its recommendations, if any, together with drafts of legislation necessary to carry such
6 recommendations into effect, by filing the same with the Clerk of the House of Representatives
7 on or before December 31, 2026.
