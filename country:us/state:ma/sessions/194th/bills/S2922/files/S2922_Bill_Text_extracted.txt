An Act authorizing the Division of Capital Asset Management and Maintenance to grant
easements to NSTAR electric company d/b/a Eversource Energy in return for NSTAR releasing
or modifying easements for the benefit of the Commonwealth.
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority
of the same, as follows:
1 SECTION 1. Notwithstanding sections 32 to 37, inclusive, of chapter 7C of the General
2 Laws or any other general or special law to the contrary, the commissioner of capital asset
3 management and maintenance may, in consultation with the commissioner of the department of
4 conservation and recreation, convey an easement to NSTAR Electric Company d/b/a Eversource
5 Energy, its successors and assigns, solely for the purposes of: (1) clearing and keeping cleared of
6 trees, structures and buildings; (2) constructing, maintaining, accessing, operating, replacing,
7 repairing, patrolling and removing underground and overhead electric transmission and
8 distribution lines and equipment; associated ground wires and telecommunication wires or cables
9 solely for electric infrastructure monitoring; and poles, structures, conduits or other
10 appurtenances and fixtures; and (3) constructing and maintaining substation fixtures and
1 of 4

11 appurtenant facilities for the transmission of electricity. The conveyance shall be subject to such
12 additional conditions and restrictions as the commissioner of capital asset management and
13 maintenance, in consultation with the commissioner of conservation and recreation, may
14 determine.
15 The easement is anticipated to be located in an area more particularly shown as “20’
16 Wide Permanent Easement to be Located within the Hatched Area. Final Location to be
17 Determined by Survey of the Centerline of Duct Bank Installation” on a progress print plan of
18 land, entitled “Easement Plan, Magazine Beach Charles River & Dr. Paul Dudley White Bike
19 Path in Boston and Cambridge, Massachusetts, Suffolk and Middlesex Counties” dated August
20 28, 2025 and on file with the division of capital asset management and maintenance. The exact
21 boundaries and location of the easement shall be determined by the commissioner of capital asset
22 management and maintenance, in consultation with the commissioner of conservation and
23 recreation. The property is currently under the care and control of the department of conservation
24 and recreation for conservation and recreation purposes.
25 SECTION 2. (a) In consideration for the easement authorized in section 1, NSTAR
26 Electric Company d/b/a Eversource Energy shall compensate the commonwealth through: (i) the
27 transfer of land or an interest in land to the department of conservation and recreation, with a
28 value equal to or greater than the full and fair market value of the easement described in said
29 section 1, or its value in use as proposed, whichever is greater, as determined by an independent
30 appraisal; (ii) a sum of money equal to the full and fair market value of the easement or its value
31 in use as proposed, whichever is greater, as determined by an independent appraisal; or (iii) some
32 combination thereof. The commonwealth shall not be obligated to pay any consideration to
33 NSTAR Electric Company d/b/a Eversource Energy if the appraised value of any parcels or
2 of 4

34 interests conveyed under this subsection exceeds the value of the easement in section 1. Any
35 funds received shall be deposited in the Conservation Trust established in section 1 of chapter
36 132A of the General Laws.
37 (b) To ensure no-net-loss of lands protected for natural resource purposes, and as all or a
38 portion of the consideration for the conveyance authorized in section 1 and calculated in
39 accordance with subsection (a), the commonwealth may accept the conveyance of a parcel of
40 land located at 0 Thompson Road in the town of Wendell. The parcel is approximately 53.725
41 acres and is more particularly shown on a plan entitled, “Plan of Land, Thompson Road,
42 Wendell, Massachusetts” and dated December 4, 2023, which plan is on file with the division of
43 capital asset management and maintenance. The land shall be permanently held and managed
44 for conservation and recreation purposes by the department of conservation and recreation.
45 SECTION 3. The value of the easement described in section 1 and the value of any
46 property interests to be conveyed to the commonwealth pursuant to section 2 shall be determined
47 by an independent appraisal prepared in accordance with the usual and customary professional
48 appraisal practice by a qualified appraiser commissioned by the commissioner of capital asset
49 management and maintenance. The commissioner of capital asset management and maintenance
50 shall submit any appraisals to the inspector general for review and comment. The inspector
51 general shall review and approve any appraisals and the review shall include an examination of
52 the methodology utilized for the appraisals. The inspector general shall prepare a report of such
53 review and file the report with the commissioner of capital asset management and maintenance
54 for submission by the commissioner of capital asset management and maintenance to the house
55 and senate committees on ways and means and the joint committee on state administration and
56 regulatory oversight. The commissioner shall submit copies of the appraisals and the inspector
3 of 4

57 general’s report to the house and senate committees on ways and means and the joint committee
58 on state administration and regulatory oversight not less than 15 days before the execution of the
59 instrument effecting the grant of easement described in section 1.
60 SECTION 4. No instrument granting the easement described in section 1 shall be valid
61 unless the instrument provides that the easement shall be used solely for the purposes described
62 in this act. The grant of easement shall stipulate that: (1) the easement shall terminate if the
63 property ceases to be used for the express purposes set forth in the instrument granting the
64 easement, upon such terms and conditions as the commissioner of capital asset management and
65 maintenance, in consultation with the department of conservation and recreation, may determine,
66 following notice of such to the grantee by the division of capital asset management and
67 maintenance and a failure by the grantee to cure the violation to the satisfaction of the division of
68 capital asset management and maintenance; and (2) for installation, maintenance, repair or other
69 work performed in the easement area, the easement holder shall restore the surface condition to
70 the equivalent or better condition as determined by the department of conservation and
71 recreation.
72 SECTION 5. NSTAR Electric Company d/b/a Eversource Energy shall be responsible
73 for all costs associated with engineering, surveys, appraisals, document preparation and other
74 expenses deemed necessary by the commissioner of capital asset management and maintenance
75 to effect the conveyances authorized by this act.
