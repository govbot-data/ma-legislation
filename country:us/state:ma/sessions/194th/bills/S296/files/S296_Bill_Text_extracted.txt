1  SECTION 1. Section 3C of chapter 23A, as appearing in the 2022 Official Edition, is 
2 hereby amended by adding the following 2 subsections:-  
3  (e) Notwithstanding subsections (b) and (c), the EACC shall, by guideline or regulation,

5 national headquarters and executive operations within the commonwealth to hire and retain new,
