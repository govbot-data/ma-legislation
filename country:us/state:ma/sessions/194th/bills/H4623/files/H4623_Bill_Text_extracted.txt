An Act increasing access to ABA services by recognizing assistant level providers. 
 
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority 
of the same, as follows: 
 
1  Section 10H of Chapter 118E of the General Laws, as appearing in the 2022 Official 
2 Edition, is hereby amended by striking out the first paragraph and inserting in place thereof the 
3 following paragraph:- 
4  "Section 10H. Subject to the availability of federal financial participation, the division 
5 shall cover medically necessary treatments for persons younger than 21 years old who are 
6 receiving medical coverage underthis chapter and who are diagnosed with an autism spectrum 
7 disorder by a licensed physician or a licensed psychologist. If federal funds are available to the 
8 commonwealth, said coverage shall include, but shall not be limited to, services for applied 
9 behavior analysis rendered by Behavior Technicians, Licensed Assistant Applied Behavior 
10 Analysts and Licensed Applied Behavior Analysts supervised by a licensed applied behavior 
11 analyst and dedicated and non-dedicated augmentative and alternative communication devices, 
12 including, but not limited to medically necessary tablets; provided, however, that the division 

2 of 2 13 shall also provide coverage for augmentative and alternative communication devices not eligible 
14 for federal funds if the total cost incurred by the division for a device that is not eligible for 
15 federal funds is not more than the commonwealth's share of a comparable device that is eligible 
16 for federal funds."
