An Act requiring that broker fees in the city of Cambridge be paid by the party who solicits a 
licensed broker. 
 
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority 
of the same, as follows: 
 
1  SECTION 1. Notwithstanding any general or special law to the contrary, in the city of 
2 Cambridge, any person who performs real estate brokerage activities and is licensed or registered 
3 pursuant to sections 87PP to 87DDD1/2, inclusive, of chapter 112 of the General Laws may 
4 solely contract with a prospective tenant to find for rent residential or commercial real estate 
5 property for a tenant and present an offer to lease to the landlord or landlord’s agent and 
6 negotiate on behalf of the tenant or may solely contract with a landlord or landlord’s agent to 
7 find a tenant for a property. Any fee shall only be paid by the party, lessor or tenant who 
8 originally engaged and entered into a contract with the licensed broker or salesperson. 
9  SECTION 2. This act shall take effect upon its passage.
