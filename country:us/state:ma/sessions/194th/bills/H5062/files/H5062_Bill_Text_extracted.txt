An Act concerning equitable allocation of recovery proceeds for subrogation claims. 
 
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority 
of the same, as follows: 
 
1  SECTION 1. Section 70A of chapter 111 of the General Laws, as appearing in the 2024 
2 Official Edition, is hereby amended by inserting after the second sentence the following 
3 sentence:-  
4  Any attorney’s fees and costs incurred in enforcing liability obtaining such judgment, 
5 settlement or compromise shall be divided between the health maintenance organization, 
6 hospital, medical or dental service corporation and the injured person in proportion to the amount 
7 received by them from any such judgment, settlement or compromise. If the settlement or 
8 judgment, or funds available to satisfy the judgment, are less than the amount of the injured 
9 person’s total damages, the court may, after a hearing and evaluation of the injured party’s total 
10 damages, reduce the amount of the lien.  
11  SECTION 2. Said chapter 111 of the General Laws, as so appearing, is hereby further 
12 amended by inserting after section 70D the following section:-  

2 of 3 13  Section 70D ½. Notwithstanding the provisions of sections 70A to 70D, inclusive, and 
14 any contractual term to the contrary, no health maintenance organization or group or individual 
15 medical or disability insurer that has provided benefits for covered services to an injured person 
16 shall have a lien, right of reimbursement, subrogation claim or claim of recoupment upon any 
17 recovery or sum had,  collected or to be collected, whether by judgment, settlement or 
18 compromise, from another person as damages on account of such injuries, for more than the 
19 proportionate share of said recovery or sum subject to its lien, right of reimbursement, 
20 subrogation claim or claim of recoupment which the amount of benefits so provided for covered 
21 services bears to the total value of the injured person’s damages.  
22  The entity which provided benefits or the injured person may petition the court in which 
23 any case seeking damages on account of such injuries is pending, or in a court in which such 
24 case could be properly filed had settlement not been attained before commencement of suit, for a 
25 determination of the reasonableness of the settlement and the fair allocation of amounts payable 
26 thereunder, including any attorneys’ fees or costs incurred obtaining any recovery or sum from a 
27 third party as damages on account of such injuries. A hearing on such petition shall adhere to the 
28 same procedural requirement as provided in section 15 of chapter 152.  
29  SECTION 3. Chapter 231 of the General Laws, as so appearing, is hereby amended by 
30 inserting after section 140C ½ the following section:-  
31  Section 140D. In any action in which an injured person enters into a settlement with, or 
32 obtains a judgment from a third party, and benefits for such person’s injuries have been paid 
33 under chapter 152, and the injured person and the insurer paying said benefits do not agree to the 
34 amount each is entitled to recover out of such settlement or judgment, the court or authority 

3 of 3 35 authorized to approve settlements under section 15 of chapter 152 shall determine a just and 
36 reasonable apportionment thereof in accordance with this section; provided, however, if the 
37 settlement or judgment, or funds available to satisfy the judgment, are less than the amount of the 
38 injured person’s total damages, the court or other authority authorized to approve settlements 
39 under section 15 of chapter 152 may  after a hearing and evaluation of the injured party’s total 
40 damages reduce the amount of the lien. Except in the case of a final judgment, the injured party 
41 may withdraw consent to settlement if dissatisfied with the apportionment by the court.
