HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES, November 6, 2025.
The committee on Consumer Protection and Professional Licensure, to
whom were referred the petition (accompanied by bill, House, No. 317) of
James Arciero, Colleen M. Garry and Paul McMurtry relative to
professional licensure; the petition (accompanied by bill, House, No. 326)
of David Biele and others relative to pipefitting licensure; the petition
(accompanied by bill, House, No. 328) of Daniel Cahill relative to the sale
of wines and malt beverages by food stores; the petition (accompanied by
bill, House, No. 329) of Daniel Cahill for legislation to further regulate the
issuance liquor licenses; the petition (accompanied by bill, House, No. 353)
of Mark J. Cusack relative to retail alcohol license caps; the petition
(accompanied by bill, House, No. 370) of Paul K. Frost relative to removing
liquor license caps in cities and towns; the petition (accompanied by bill,
House, No. 382) of Richard M. Haggerty relative to exempting certain
school stadiums from plumbing regulations; the petition (accompanied by
bill, House, No. 408) of Bradley H. Jones, Jr., and others relative to cider;
the petition (accompanied by bill, House, No. 412) of Bradley H. Jones, Jr.,
and others relative to the issuance of alcoholic beverage licenses to certain
felons; the petition (accompanied by bill, House, No. 422) of David Henry
Argosky LeBoeuf for legislation to establish a board of registration of Asian
bodywork therapy; the petition (accompanied by bill, House, No. 429) of
Marc T. Lombardo relative to continuing education requirements for
electricians; the petition (accompanied by bill, House, No. 437) of Joseph
D. McKenna and Jacob R. Oliveira relative to returning liquor license
control to cities and towns; the petition (accompanied by bill, House, No.
456) of Sean Reid relative to the transportation of alcoholic beverages; the
petition (accompanied by bill, House, No. 459) of John H. Rogers relative
to the board of registration in podiatry within the board of registration in
medicine; the petition (accompanied by bill, House, No. 460) of Daniel J.
Ryan relative to construction safety standards; the petition (accompanied by
bill, House, No. 464) of Amy Mah Sangiolo, Carmine Lawrence Gentile
