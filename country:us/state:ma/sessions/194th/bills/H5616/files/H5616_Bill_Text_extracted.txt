An Act authorizing the Massachusetts Department of Transportation to take easements over 
certain land located in the city of Woburn and the town of Burlington held in trust by the city of 
Boston for the Mary P. C. Cummings Testamentary Trust for highway purposes. 
 
 Whereas, The deferred operation of this act would tend to defeat its purpose, which is to 
authorize forthwith the commonwealth, acting by and through its department of transportation to 
take certain easements for highway purposes, to support the intersection improvement project 
being undertaken by the commonwealth at the intersection of Cambridge road at South Bedford 
street and Bedford road in the city of Woburn and the town of Burlington, therefore it is hereby 
declared to be an emergency law, necessary for the immediate preservation of the public 
convenience. 
 
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority 
of the same, as follows: 
 
1  SECTION 1. Notwithstanding any general or special law to the contrary, the 
2 commonwealth, acting by and through the Massachusetts Department of Transportation, may 
3 take for highway purposes, certain parcels of land located in the city of Woburn and the town of 
4 Burlington, held in trust by the city of Boston’s Treasury department, being temporary 
5 construction areas of 4,603 square feet and permanent easements of 8,917 square feet and shown 
6 on a plan of land entitled “Massachusetts Department of Transportation Highway Division 
7 Intersection Improvement Project Cambridge Road at South Bedford Street and Bedford Road in 
8 the city and town of Woburn/Burlington, Middlesex County Preliminary Right of Way Plans”, 

2 of 2 9 dated May 27, 2026, to be filed with the chief engineer of the highway division of the 
10 Massachusetts Department of Transportation and recorded with the Middlesex north registry of 
11 deeds. 
12  SECTION 2. In anticipation of and as planned mitigation for the takings described in 
13 section 1 and to ensure a no-net-loss of land subject to Article 97 of the Amendments to the 
14 Constitution of the Commonwealth, during the May 25, 2025 approval of a special permit to 
15 Jumbo Self Storage, LLC - Jumbo Capital Management for land being located at 7 Wheeler road 
16 in the town of Burlington and shown on the town’s assessor’s Map 52 as parcels 4 and 6, the 
17 town of Burlington required that land be set aside for future improvements to the intersection of 
18 Cambridge road at South Bedford street and Bedford road to satisfy Article 97. The town of 
19 Burlington further accepted a conservation restriction from Wheeler Self Storage, LLC on the 
20 Wheeler road property containing approximately 9 acres, recorded with the Middlesex south 
21 registry of deeds at book 78014, page 433. As described in conditions G and H of a special 
22 permit decision granted by the town of Burlington’s planning board and recorded with the 
23 Middlesex south registry of deeds at book 71169 and page 469, said conveyance was specifically 
24 intended to be mitigation for the transfer described in section 1 and the Mary Cummings Trust, 
25 by and through the city of Boston, does not oppose the mitigation proposed by the Massachusetts 
26 Department of Transportation. 
27  SECTION 3. The Massachusetts Department of Transportation shall assume all costs 
28 associated with engineering, surveys, appraisals, deed preparation and other expenses necessary 
29 to execute the takings authorized by this act.
