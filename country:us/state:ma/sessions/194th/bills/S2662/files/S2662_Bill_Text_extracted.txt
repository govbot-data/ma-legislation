An Act expanding wheelchair warranty protections for consumers with disabilities.
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority
of the same, as follows:
1 SECTION 1. Chapter 93 of the General Laws, as appearing in the 2022 Official Edition,
2 is hereby amended by inserting after section 107 the following new section:-
3 Section 107A. (a) As used in this section the following words shall have the following
4 meanings unless the context clearly indicates otherwise:
5 "Authorized wheelchair dealer", any company doing business in the state selling or
6 leasing wheelchairs, including complex rehabilitation technology wheelchairs.
7 "Complex rehabilitation technology wheelchair", a specialized, medically necessary
8 manual or powered wheelchair individually configured for the user with specialized equipment
9 that requires evaluation, configuration, fitting, adjustment, programming and long-term
10 maintenance and repair services.
1 of 8

11 "Consumer", the purchaser or lessee of a wheelchair, including a complex rehabilitation
12 technology wheelchair, irrespective of whether the purchase or lease of the wheelchair is funded
13 in whole or in part by the consumer or privately or publicly funded health insurance.
14 "Timely repair", as soon as practicable but not later than ten business days after the date
15 of request for repair from a consumer that makes the wheelchair available and any prior
16 authorization required from an insurer has been acquired; provided, any time spent waiting for
17 prior authorization from an insurer or for delivery of necessary parts ordered for the repair by an
18 authorized wheelchair dealer acting in compliance with subsection (d) shall not be included in
19 the ten business days.
20 "Wheelchair", a manual or motorized wheeled device that enhances the mobility or
21 positioning of an individual with a disability and includes a complex rehabilitation technology
22 wheelchair.
23 (b) An authorized wheelchair dealer shall timely repair a wheelchair, including a complex
24 rehabilitation technology wheelchair, sold or leased by such dealer in the state. An authorized
25 wheelchair dealer who sells or leases a complex rehabilitation technology wheelchair in the state
26 shall provide timely repair of such wheelchair at a consumer's home upon request.
27 (c) An authorized wheelchair dealer shall maintain an electronic mail address and a
28 phone line for consumer repair requests that are accessible each business day and capable of
29 receiving and recording messages. The authorized wheelchair dealer shall respond to a request
30 for wheelchair repair not later than one business day after the date of request, and order parts for
31 a repair not later than three business days after assessing the need for the repair or after receiving
32 prior authorization from an insurer for the repair.
2 of 8

33 (d) Manufacturers or other authorized wheelchair dealers shall fill all repair and
34 replacement orders for wheelchairs pursuant to this section from their own inventory or have a
35 written subcontract for the purchase of items necessary to fill repair and replacement orders;
36 provided, however, that the subcontract shall be in writing and contain, at a minimum: (i) names,
37 addresses, phone numbers and contact information for both entities; (ii) the contract term start
38 and end dates; (iii) a description of the wheelchairs covered under the subcontract and the cost of
39 each item; (iv) signatures of both parties, including signature dates and position titles; (v) an
40 established credit limit that is reasonable, based on the value of the products and services to be
41 provided by the contractor; and (vi) a provision requiring shipping of parts, whenever feasible,
42 by overnight mail.
43 (e) (1) Nothing contained in this section shall be deemed to limit any rights or remedies
44 available to a consumer under any other law. Any waiver by a consumer of rights under this
45 section shall be void.
46 (2) In addition to pursuing any other remedy, a consumer may bring an action to recover
47 for damages caused by a violation of this section. The court shall award a consumer who prevails
48 in such an action twice the amount of any pecuniary loss, together with costs, disbursements and
49 reasonable attorney fees, and any equitable relief that the court deems is appropriate.
50 (f) The attorney general shall promulgate such rules and regulations as deemed necessary
51 for the interpretation, implementation, administration, and enforcement of this section. Such
52 authority shall be in addition to the attorney general's authority to promulgate rules and
53 regulations under section 2 of chapter 93A.

54 SECTION. 2. Chapter 118E of the General Laws, as so appearing, is hereby amended by
55 inserting after section 10Q the following section:-
56 Section 10R. For purposes of this section, “complex rehabilitation technology
57 wheelchair” shall mean a specialized, medically necessary manual or powered wheelchair
58 individually configured for the user with specialized equipment that requires evaluation,
59 configuration, fitting, adjustment, programming and long-term maintenance and repair services.
60 The division and its contracted health insurers, health plans, health maintenance
61 organizations, behavioral health management firms and third-party administrators under contract
62 to a Medicaid managed care organization or primary care clinician plan shall not require a
63 preauthorization for any repair of a complex rehabilitation technology wheelchair unless the
64 original prescription is more than five years old.

138 SECTION 13. Section 107 of chapter 93 of the General Laws shall be amended by
139 inserting after section 107(C)(5) the following sections:
140 “(D) If a wheelchair covered by a manufacturer’s warranty has a nonconformity to which
141 the warranty is applicable and is made available by the consumer to the manufacturer or a
142 customized wheelchair dealer for the repair of the nonconformity, and if such repair is
143 reasonably expected by the manufacturer to be longer than ten days, or if ten business days have
144 lapsed before completion of the repair, the manufacturer shall offer, or coordinate with an
145 authorized wheelchair dealer to offer to the consumer, for the duration of the repair period,
146 directly and at no cost to the consumer, as selected by the consumer and as soon as reasonably
147 possible: (A) a temporary loaner wheelchair; or (B) reimbursement for the cost incurred by the
148 consumer for renting a wheelchair meeting the requirements of a temporary loaner wheelchair,
149 while the original wheelchair is assessed and repaired.”
