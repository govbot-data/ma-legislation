An Act relative to pharmacists as healthcare providers. 
 
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority 
of the same, as follows: 
 
1  Chapter 94C, as appearing in the 2022 Official Edition, is hereby amended by inserting, 
2 after section 19F, the following section:- 
3  Section 19G. (a) Notwithstanding any general or special law to the contrary, a pharmacist 
4 may order, test, screen, and treat the following health conditions: influenza, streptococcal 
5 infections, COVID-19 or any other emerging and existing public health threats identified by the 
6 department if permitted by an order, rule, or regulation.  
7  b) The department shall establish standard procedures for pharmacists to test, screen and 
8 initiate treatment for qualified health conditions. 
9  (c) A pharmacist who orders, tests, screens, or treats health conditions under this section 
10 may use any test that may guide clinical decision making that is waived under the federal 

2 of 2 11 Clinical Laboratory Improvement Amendments of 1988, or the federal rules adopted thereunder, 
12 or any established screening procedure that is established via a statewide protocol. 
13  (d) A pharmacist may delegate the administrative and technical tasks of performing a test 
14 waived by the federal Clinical Laboratory Improvement Amendments of 1988 to a pharmacy 
15 technician acting under the supervision of the pharmacist. 
16  (e) This section prohibits the denial of reimbursement under health benefit plans for 
17 services and procedures performed by a pharmacist that are within the scope of the pharmacist’s 
18 license and would be covered if the services or procedures were performed by a physician, an 
19 advanced practice nurse, or physician's assistant. An insurance carrier or pharmacy benefit 
20 manager shall develop adequate provider and consult codes as authorized by this act for 
21 pharmacy compensation.
