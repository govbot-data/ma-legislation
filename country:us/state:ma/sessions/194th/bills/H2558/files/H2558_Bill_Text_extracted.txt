In the One Hundred and Ninety-Fourth General Court
(2025-2026)
_______________
An Act relative to prescription eyewear.
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority
of the same, as follows:
1 Notwithstanding any general or special law to the contrary, any licensed physician or
2 registered optometrist registered to issue written prescriptions in the Commonwealth who are
3 issuing a prescription for eyeglasses or contact lenses, shall include the patient’s pupillary
4 distance on the script. No additional fees may be imposed for including such information on the
5 script. The board of registration in optometry shall issue promulgations to carry out the
6 requirements of this section.
