An Act proclaiming April 25th of each year to be Parental Alienation Awareness Day. 
 
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority 
of the same, as follows: 
 
1  WHEREAS, Parental Alienation is described as behavior by a parent, or an adult a child 
2 trusts, whether conscious or unconscious, that could create alienation in the relationship between 
3 a child and a parent; and 
4  WHEREAS, This negative behavior involves the manipulation of a child through a set of 
5 behaviors that can result in destroying the loving and warm relationship the child shares with a 
6 parent; and 
7  WHEREAS, The alienating behavior often involves one caregiver attempting to turn a 
8 child against another caregiver with a desired outcome of persuading the child that they should 
9 exclude the other caregiver or parent from their life permanently; and 
10  WHEREAS, Methods of behavior typically include constant badmouthing and/or 
11 belittling of the other parent, and limiting a parent's contact with the child; and 

2 of 2 12  WHEREAS, The manipulation can be mild and temporary but can also be extreme and 
13 ongoing, damaging a child and potentially leading to life-long consequences; and 
14  WHEREAS, Children who are alienated from one parent may experience increased 
15 anger, have heightened feelings of neglect, or learn a destructive pattern that they pass on to 
16 others; and 
17  WHEREAS, Parental Alienation deprives children of their right to be loved by and 
18 showing love for both of their parents and is seen mostly in cases of divorce or separation; and 
19  WHEREAS, Research has estimated that twenty-two million adults in the United States 
20 and almost four million children have been victimized by Parental Alienation; and 
21  WHEREAS, Parental Alienation Awareness Day was created to help raise awareness 
22 about this growing problem of mental and emotional child abuse; now, therefore be it  
23  RESOLVED, That the Commonwealth of Massachusetts hereby proclaims April 25th of 
24 each year to be "Parental Alienation Awareness Day" and urges all residents of Massachusetts to 
25 help stop the abuse of innocent children caught in the crossfire of this destructive behavior from 
26 the people they love.
