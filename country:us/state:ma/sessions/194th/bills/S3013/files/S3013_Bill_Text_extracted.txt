SENATE . . . . . . . . . . . . . . No. 3013
Senate, May 7, 2026 - Text of the Senate amendment (Senator Rausch) to the House Bill revising
the charter of the town of Millis, as amended (House, No. 3911).
The Commonwealth of Massachusetts
_______________
In the One Hundred and Ninety-Fourth General Court
(2025-2026)
_______________
1 SECTION 1. Section III-5 of article III of the charter of the town of Millis, which is on
2 file in the office of the archivist of the commonwealth, as provided in section 12 of chapter 43B
3 of the General Laws, is hereby amended by adding the following clause:-
4 k. Approve all payroll and expense warrants for payment of town funds; provided,
5 however, that such approval shall be sufficient authority to authorize payment of the same by the
6 town treasurer; provided further, that in the event of the absence of the town administrator, the
7 select board shall have the authority to approve such warrants; and provided further, that if a
8 vacancy exists in the office of the town administrator, the select board may approve all such
9 warrants or may delegate such authority to an interim town administrator.
10 SECTION 2. This act shall take effect upon its passage.
