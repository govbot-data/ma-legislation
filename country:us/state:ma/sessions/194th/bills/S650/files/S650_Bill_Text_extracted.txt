An Act banning the retail sale of dogs, cats, and rabbits in pet shops.
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority
of the same, as follows:
1 SECTION 1. Section 39A of chapter 129 of the General Laws, as appearing in the 2022
2 Official Edition, is hereby amended by inserting, in line 1, before the word “Every”, the
3 following letter:- (a)
4 SECTION 2. Section 39A of chapter 129 of the General Laws is hereby further amended
5 by striking out, in line 13, the word “section”, and inserting in place thereof the following word:-
6 subsection
7 SECTION 3. Section 39A of chapter 129 of the General Laws is hereby further amended
8 by inserting after subsection (a) the following 3 subsections:-
1 of 2

9 (b) No person engaged in the business of operating a pet shop for which a license must be
10 obtained pursuant to subsection (a); and are regulated under 330 CMR 12.00 shall sell or offer
11 for sale a dog, cat, or rabbit.
12 (c) Subsection (b) shall not be construed to prohibit a pet shop from offering space to an
13 animal rescue or shelter organization to showcase dogs, cats, or rabbits for adoption so long as
14 the pet shop does not have an ownership interest in the animals offered for adoption.
15 (d) Nothing in subsections (b) or (c) shall affect the authority of any town, city, or other
16 locality to regulate or prohibit the sale of dogs, cats, or rabbits.
17 SECTION 4. This act shall go into effect 90 days after its passage
