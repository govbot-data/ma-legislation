HOUSE . . . . . . . . No. 5240
The Commonwealth of Massachusetts
________________________________________
HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES, March 16, 2026.
The committee on Children, Families and Persons with Disabilities, to
whom were referred the petition (accompanied by bill, House, No. 216) of
Marjorie C. Decker, Lindsay N. Sabadosa and Samantha Montaño relative
to emergency housing assistance for children and families experiencing
homelessness; the petition (accompanied by bill, House, No. 260) of
David Henry Argosky LeBoeuf that the Executive Office of Housing and
Livable Communities conduct a study on the state of immigrant and
refugee resettlement infrastructure in the Commonwealth; and the petition
(accompanied by bill, House, No. 4194) of Colleen M. Garry relative to
the estimated rate of inflation for social service programs, reports (under
the provisions of House Rule 27) recommending that the accompanying
order (House, No. 5240) ought to be adopted.
For the committee,
JAY D. LIVINGSTONE.
