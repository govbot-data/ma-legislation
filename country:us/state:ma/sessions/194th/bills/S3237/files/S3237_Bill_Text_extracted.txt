An Act authorizing the town of Bourne to convey a right-of-way and easement. 
 
 Whereas, The deferred operation of this act would tend to defeat its purpose, which is to 
authorize forthwith the conveyance of certain easements on Joint Base Cape Cod to Algonquin 
Gas Transmission, LLC in exchange for a certain parcel of land in Bourne, therefore it is hereby 
declared to be an emergency law, necessary for the immediate preservation of the public 
convenience. 
 
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority 
of the same, as follows:

61  SECTION 4. (a) The value of the easements described in sections 1 and 3 and the value 
62 of the property interest to be conveyed to the commonwealth pursuant to section 2 shall be 
63 determined by an independent appraisal prepared in accordance with the usual and customary 
64 professional appraisal practice by a qualified appraiser commissioned by the commissioner of 
65 capital asset management and maintenance. The commissioner may, in consultation with the 
66 commissioner of fish and game, accept an appraisal of the easements and property interest 
67 conducted prior to the effective date of this act. The commissioner of capital asset management 
68 and maintenance shall submit the appraisal to the inspector general for review and comment. The 
69 inspector general shall review and approve the appraisal and the review shall include an 
70 examination of the methodology used for the appraisal. The inspector general shall prepare a 
71 report of the review and file the report with the commissioner of capital asset management and 
72 maintenance.  The commissioner shall submit copies of the appraisals and the inspector general’s 
73 report to the house and senate committees on ways and means and the joint committee on state 
74 administration and regulatory oversight before the execution of the conveyances.
