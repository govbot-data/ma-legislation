HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES, March 12, 2026. 
 The committee on Financial Services, to whom were referred the 
petition (accompanied by bill, House, No. 1108) of Daniel Cahill for 
legislation to establish portable benefit accounts for app-based-delivery 
drivers; the petition (accompanied by bill, House, No. 1116) of Rob 
Consalvo relative to the sales tax for replacement of certain stolen vehicles; 
the petition (accompanied by bill, House, No. 1163) of Danae Isbister 
relative to insurance stamps for motor vehicle liability policy certificates; 
the petition (accompanied by bill, House, No. 1201) of Bradley H. Jones, 
Jr., and others relative to insurance surcharges for driving with an expired 
inspection sticker; the petition (accompanied by bill, House, No. 1213) of 
Hannah Kane and others relative to motor vehicle insurance discounts for 
marijuana impairment education courses; the petition (accompanied by bill, 
House, No. 1251) of Jay D. Livingstone relative to transportation network 
company fees; and the petition (accompanied by bill, House, No. 1340) of 
Marcus S. Vaughn relative to moped liability insurance, reports 
recommending that the accompanying order (House, No. 5206) ought to be 
adopted. 
 
For the committee, 
 
JAMES M. MURPHY.
