An Act to provide notice to counsel of changes in a child’s placement and other events. 
 
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority 
of the same, as follows: 
 
1  SECTION 1. Section 29 of chapter 119 of the General Laws, as appearing in the 2020 
2 Official Edition, is hereby amended by inserting after the second paragraph the following 
3 paragraphs: 
4  No later than five business days before any non-emergency change in a child’s or a 
5 young adult’s placement or any non-emergency hospitalization, and no later than one business 
6 day after any emergency change in a child’s or a young adult’s placement or any emergency 
7 hospitalization, the department shall provide notice of the change in placement or hospitalization 
8 to the child’s or the young adult’s attorney. 

2 of 2 9  No later than the first business day thereafter, the department shall provide notice to a 
10 child’s attorney if it receives a report under section 51A and the child is the subject of the report. 
11 No later than the first business day thereafter, the department shall provide notice to a child’s 
12 attorney or a young adult’s attorney if it receives a report under section 51A that raises 
13 substantial questions regarding the suitability of the child’s or young adult’s current placement or 
14 any of the child’s or young adult’s service providers. 
15  No later than the first business day thereafter, the department shall provide notice to a 
16 child’s or young adult’s attorney whenever it becomes aware of: (1) the child or young adult 
17 being arrested; (2) the child’s or young adult’s potential involvement in any proceeding under 
18 this chapter or any criminal investigation or proceeding; (3) the child or young adult being 
19 suspended from school; or (4) the child or young adult being the subject of any proceeding that 
20 could result in his or her suspension or expulsion from school. 
21  SECTION 2. The department of children and families shall promulgate regulations to 
22 implement section 1 of this act no later than six months from the effective date of this act.
