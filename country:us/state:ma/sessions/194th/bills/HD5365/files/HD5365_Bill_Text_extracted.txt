An Act establishing an independent child protection and advocacy agency.
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority
of the same, as follows:
1 SECTION 1.
2 (a) Whereas:
3 1. Children and adolescents who are incapacitated, disabled, medically fragile, or
4 otherwise vulnerable are at increased risk of harm, neglect, exploitation, and deprivation of their
5 basic human rights.
6 2. Many of these children experience systemic failures that deny them access to
7 appropriate education, therapeutic care, and a quality of life consistent with the standards of the
8 Commonwealth.
9 3. An independent, nonpartisan, and oversight-oriented agency is required to ensure
10 accountability and protection for all incapacitated and at-risk minors.
1 of 6

11 (b) The purpose of this act is to establish an Independent Child Protection and Advocacy
12 Agency (ICPAA), whose mission shall be:
13 To serve as an overwatch and advocacy body protecting incapacitated children and
14 adolescents from individuals or systems that harm, exploit, or endanger their well-being through
15 negligence or abuse;
16 To safeguard the human rights of all children to appropriate education, safety, and quality
17 of life;
18 To investigate, intervene, and advocate when those rights are violated.

34 SECTION 3.
35 Chapter 119 of the General Laws, as appearing in the 2024 Official Edition, is hereby
36 amended by inserting after section 89 the following two sections:-
37 Section 90. Independent Child Protection and Advocacy Agency.
38 For the purposes of this section:-
39 a. “Incapacitated child” means a minor under the age of 18 who, due to physical,
40 developmental, cognitive, or emotional conditions, is substantially limited in self-care, decision-
41 making, or protection.
42 b. “Neglect” means the failure, by act or omission, to provide a child with necessary
43 care, supervision, education, medical attention, or safety.
44 c. “Advocacy” means the act of representing and defending a child’s legal,
45 educational, and human rights in any proceeding, system, or institution.
46 d. “Agency” means the Independent Child Protection and Advocacy Agency.
47 There is hereby established within the Commonwealth an autonomous public agency
48 known as the Independent Child Protection and Advocacy Agency (ICPAA). The Agency shall
49 not be subject to the supervision, control, or direction of any other department or office of the
50 Commonwealth, except as specifically provided by law. The Agency shall be funded through
51 annual appropriations by the General Court. The Agency may also receive public or private
3 of 6

52 grants, federal funds, or charitable donations consistent with its mission and in compliance with
53 state finance laws.
54 The Independent Child Protection and Advocacy Agency shall have the power to:
55 (a) Investigate reports, complaints, or patterns of abuse, neglect, exploitation, or rights
56 violations involving incapacitated or vulnerable children.
57 (b) Monitor and review the performance and compliance of state, municipal, and private
58 entities providing care, education, or residential services to children.
59 (c) Act as an independent advocate for a child’s best interests in administrative, civil, or
60 educational proceedings when conflicts of interest or lack of adequate representation exist.
61 (d) Access information from any state agency, department, or private facility necessary to
62 conduct investigations, subject to confidentiality laws.
63 (e) Refer matters to law enforcement or regulatory bodies when evidence of criminal or
64 administrative violations is found.
65 (f) Develop recommendations for policy, training, and systemic reform to strengthen
66 child protection and advocacy systems across the Commonwealth.
67 (g) Provide education and outreach to families, guardians, and community members
68 regarding the rights and protections of children with disabilities.
69 All records, investigative materials, and communications of the Agency shall be
70 confidential and exempt from public disclosure under Chapter 66, Section 10 of the General
4 of 6

71 Laws, except where release is necessary to protect a child’s welfare or as otherwise required by
72 court order.
73 The Agency shall coordinate, as necessary, with the Department of Children and
74 Families, the Department of Developmental Services, the Department of Elementary and
75 Secondary Education, and the Office of the Child Advocate. This coordination shall not diminish
76 the statutory responsibilities of those entities but shall enhance transparency, accountability, and
77 oversight.
78 Section 91.
79 Any state or private agency that fails to comply with an ICPAA investigation or refuses
80 to provide required documentation shall be subject to a civil penalty not to exceed $5,000 per
81 day of noncompliance.
