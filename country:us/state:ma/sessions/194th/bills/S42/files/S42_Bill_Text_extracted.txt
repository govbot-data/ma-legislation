An Act protecting against cyber ransom.
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority
of the same, as follows:
1 Chapter 7D of the General Laws as appearing in the 2022 Official Edition is hereby
2 amended in Section 7 by adding the following subsections:-
3 (e) No State agency, local government entity, or municipality shall submit payment or
4 otherwise communicate with an entity that has engaged in a cybersecurity incident on an
5 information technology system by encrypting data and then subsequently offering to decrypt that
6 data in exchange for a ransom payment.
7 (f) Any State agency or local government entity experiencing a ransom request in
8 connection with a cybersecurity incident shall report to and consult with the CIO.
