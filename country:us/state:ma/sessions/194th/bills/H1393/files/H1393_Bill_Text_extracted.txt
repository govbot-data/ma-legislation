HOUSE DOCKET, NO. 2559 FILED ON: 1/16/2025
HOUSE . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . No. 1393
By Representative Moran of Lawrence, a petition (accompanied by bill, House, No. 1393) of
Frank A. Moran and others that MassHealth make payments for certain graduate medical
education training. Health Care Financing.
The Commonwealth of Massachusetts
_______________
In the One Hundred and Ninety-Fourth General Court
(2025-2026)
_______________
An Act to promote graduate medical education.
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority
of the same, as follows:
1 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled,
2 and by the authority of the same, as follows:
3 Chapter 118E of the general laws is amended by adding at the end thereof, the following
4 new section:
5 Section 83. MassHealth shall make graduate medical education payments for post-
6 graduate residency and other training in community-based primary care, behavioral health, and
7 other areas of physician or provider shortage in community-based healthcare settings in the
8 Commonwealth. The payments may support community-based training for other health
9 professionals, including but not limited to, family medicine nurse practitioners, dentists, and
10 dental hygienists. Eligible providers for said payments shall include community health centers.
11 MassHealth shall seek to obtain the maximum amount of federal reimbursement for said
12 payments. MassHealth shall promulgate and revise any rules or regulations necessary to
1 of 2

13 implement this provision within 180 days of enactment. MassHealth will prioritize placements at
14 community-based settings and organizations that serve a high public payer mix. MassHealth
15 shall consult with the Massachusetts League of Community Health Centers, Inc. regarding
16 implementation of this section.
