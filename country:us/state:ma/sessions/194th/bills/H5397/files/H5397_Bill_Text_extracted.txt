HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES, May 11, 2026.
The committee on Financial Services, to whom was referred the
petition (accompanied by bill, House, No. 1188) of Natalie M. Higgins,
Christine P. Barber and others relative to healthcare coverage for certain
gender affirming care, reports (under the provisions of House Rule 27)
recommending that the accompanying order (House, No. 5397) ought to
be adopted.
For the committee,
JAMES M. MURPHY.
