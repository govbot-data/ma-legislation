An Act relative to the re-use of soil for large reclamation projects.
Whereas, The deferred operation of this act would tend to defeat its purpose, which is to
ensure the reuse of large volumes of soil for the reclamation of sand pits, gravel pits and quarries
poses no significant risk of harm to health, safety, public welfare or the environment, therefore it
is hereby declared to be an emergency law, necessary for the immediate preservation of the
public safety, health and convenience.
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority
of the same, as follows:
1 SECTION 1. Chapter 21E of the General Laws, as appearing in the 2022 Official Edition,
2 is hereby amended by inserting, after Section 22, the following new section:-
3 Section 23. (a) As used in this chapter the following words shall, unless the context
4 clearly requires otherwise, have the following meanings:-
5 “Approved Re-use”, the use of soil for the reclamation of a quarry, sand pit or gravel pit
6 under the conditions of this policy;

7 “Covered project”, any quarry, gravel pit, or sand pit reclamation project that receives, or
8 plans to receive, greater than 100,000 cubic yards of soil for the reclamation or filling of said
9 quarry, gravel pit, or sand pit.
10 “Filling operation”, the filling of sand pits, gravel pits, and quarries.
11 "Department'', the department of environmental protection.
12 "Hazardous material'', material including but not limited to, any material, in whatever
13 form, which, because of its quantity, concentration, chemical, corrosive, flammable, reactive,

17 otherwise managed. The term shall not include oil. The term shall also include all those
18 substances which are included under 42 USC Sec. 9601(14), but it is not limited to those
19 substances.
20 “Reportable release”, the release of oil or hazardous material into the environment which
21 requires notice pursuant to 310 CMR 40.000.

23 Department:
24 (i) of releases, and threats of release, of oil and/or hazardous material and Imminent
25 Hazards set forth in 310 CMR 40.0300; and/or
26 (ii) of changes in activities, uses and/or exposures set forth in 310 CMR 40.0020.

30 not include those substances which are included in 42 USC Sec. 9601(14).
31 “Release”, the placement, dumping, disposing or reuse of soil containing oil or hazardous

33 310 CMR 40.000.

58 (b) This act shall apply to any quarry, gravel pit, or sand pit reclamation project that
59 receives, or plans to receive, greater than 100,000 cubic yards of soil for the reclamation or
60 filling of said quarry, gravel pit, or sand pit.
61 (c) The department shall issue a site-specific approval, in the form of an administrative
62 consent order, to any filling operation that is a covered project.
63 The administrative consent order shall include the following: (1) implementation of a
64 detailed soil and fill management plan that specifies how material will be sampled, documented,
65 tracked, transported and managed as well as what materials are permitted and not permitted; (2)
66 detailed plans that specify how material will be managed at the reclamation project to prevent
67 nuisance conditions, such as noise, odor, litter, and dust; (3) a detailed stormwater management
4 of 6

68 plan to prevent impacts to sensitive receptors; (4) detailed wetlands impact provisions, including,
69 as applicable, a requirement to obtain an order of conditions, determination of applicability or
70 other approval or permit to proceed with the project as designed; (5) a plan for communicating
71 with the public and involving interested parties at key points in the implementation of the
72 reclamation project; (6) oversight by a qualified environmental professional and third party
73 inspection program; (7) knowledge of and intention to comply with all applicable laws and
74 regulations; and (8) stipulated penalties for noncompliance with the administrative consent order;
75 (9) certification, signed by an agent of the covered project, that the reuse of large volumes of soil
76 pose no significant risk of harm to health, safety, public welfare, or the environment and would
77 not create new releases or threats of releases of oil or hazardous materials.
78 (d) To be eligible for department approval, soil accepted by a covered project for use in a
79 filling operation shall not contain more than de minimus quantities of solid waste. The
80 placement, dumping, disposing or reuse of soil containing oil or hazardous material into the
81 environment shall constitute a release. Such dumping, disposing, or unapproved re-use of soil is
82 a reportable release requiring assessment and remediation.
83 (e) Filling operations conducted without the approval of the department shall be subject
84 to department enforcement for violations of rules governing solid waste management and oil or
85 hazardous material release pursuant to department regulations.
86 (f) A covered project that is not managed in compliance with the requirements of this act
87 shall be found to have caused, contributed to, or exacerbated a release of oil or hazardous
88 material and shall be subject to enforcement pursuant to section 277 of chapter 165 of the acts of
89 2014 and chapter 21E of the general laws. Any fill project that accepts any amount of soil,

90 whether pursuant to this act or otherwise, must ensure that the filling does not create new,

92 will not violate section 150A of chapter 111.
93 (d) Nothing in this act shall eliminate any local, state, or federal requirements that apply
94 to the management of soil, including any local, state, or federal permits or approvals necessary
