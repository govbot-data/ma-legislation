An Act providing for the exchange of certain park land in the town of Lexington.
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority
of the same, as follows:
1 SECTION 1. Notwithstanding any general or special law to the contrary, the select board
2 of the town of Lexington may, pursuant to a vote of the Lexington special town meeting on
3 November 3, 2025 and the requirements of section 15A of chapter 40 of the General Laws,
4 section 5A of chapter 3 of the General Laws, and Chapter 114 of the Acts of 1961, transfer the
5 care, custody and control of certain parcels of land currently under the care and control of the
6 select board of the town of Lexington for athletic fields to the school committee of the town of
7 Lexington for school purposes. The parcels of land that may be transferred under this section
8 comprise approximately eight acres, and are more particularly described in whole or in part in
9 two deeds recorded with the Middlesex south registry of deeds as Book 3953, Page 561 and
10 Book 3981, Page 109
11 SECTION 2. As a condition of any disposition of land pursuant to section 1, and to
12 ensure no net loss of land subject to Article 97 of the amendments to the constitution of the
