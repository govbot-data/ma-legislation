HOUSE . . . . . . . . No. 5049
The Commonwealth of Massachusetts
________________________________________
HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES, February 5, 2026.
The committee on Transportation, to whom were referred the petition
(accompanied by bill, House, No. 3618) of Bruce J. Ayers relative to the
flight of unmanned aircraft drones in the airspace over early, elementary
and secondary educational facilities; the petition (accompanied by bill,
House, No. 3625) of Luke Soojian relative to the permitting for removal or
transportation of refuse; the petition (accompanied by bill, House, No.
3663) of Brandy Fluker-Reid relative to the safe operation of drones in the
Commonwealth; the petition (accompanied by bill, House, No. 3671) of
Homar Gómez and others relative to Purple Heart registration plates; the
petition (accompanied by bill, House, No. 3797) of Marcus S. Vaughn
relative to providing for a MassPort airline regulation study; and the
petition (accompanied by bill, House, No. 3800) of Marcus S. Vaughn
relative to regulating drones, reports recommending that the
accompanying order (House, No. 5049) ought to be adopted.
For the committee,
JAMES ARCIERO.

HOUSE . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . No. 5049
The Commonwealth of Massachusetts
