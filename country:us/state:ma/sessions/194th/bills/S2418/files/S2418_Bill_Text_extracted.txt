An Act relative to improved headlight safety.
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority
of the same, as follows:
1 Section 7 of Chapter 90 is hereby amended by inserting:
2 All new automobiles sold in the Commonwealth after January 1, 2028, shall be equipped
3 with adaptive beam headlight technology, as authorized by the amendment to the Federal Motor
4 Vehicle Safety Standard No. 108 by the U.S. Department of Transportation’s National Highway
5 Traffic Safety Administration.
