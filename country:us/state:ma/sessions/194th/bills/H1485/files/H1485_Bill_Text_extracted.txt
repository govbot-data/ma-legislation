of the same, as follows:

7 “Housing Production Plan,” shall contain, at a minimum, the following elements covering

10 SECTION 3: Chapter 40B of the General Laws is hereby amended by adding the
11 following section after Section 21;
12 Section 21(a):
13 1. Comprehensive Housing Needs Assessment. The Housing Production Plan must
14 establish a strategic plan for municipal action with regards to housing, based upon a
15 comprehensive housing needs assessment that examines: The most recent available census data
16 of the municipality’s demographics and housing stock, together with a projection of future
17 population and housing needs, taking into account regional growth factors, that covers the entire
18 time period of the plan; development constraints and limitations on its current and future needs,
19 and the municipality’s plans to mitigate those constraints; and the capacity of the municipality’s
20 infrastructure to accommodate the current population and anticipated future growth, including
21 plans for enlargement or expansion of existing infrastructure systems to ensure that both current
22 and future needs are met.
23 2. Affordable housing goals. The Housing Production Plan shall address the matters
24 set out in the Department of Housing and Community Development’s guidelines, including: a
25 mix of types of housing, consistent with local and regional needs and feasible within the housing
26 market in which they will be situated, including rental, homeownership, and other occupancy
27 arrangements, if any, for families, individuals, persons with special needs, and the elderly; a
28 numerical goal for annual housing production, pursuant to which there is an increase in the
29 municipality’s number of Subsidized Housing Inventory Eligible Housing units by at least 0.50%
30 of its total units (as determined by the Department of Housing and Community Development)
2 of 5

31 during every calendar year included in the Housing Production Plan, until the overall percentage
32 exceeds the Statutory Minimum set forth in Section 20 of this Chapter.
33 3. Implementation Strategies. The Housing Production Plan shall address the matters
34 set out in Section 20 of this Chapter, including an explanation of the specific strategies by which
35 the municipality will achieve its housing production goal, and a schedule for implementation of
36 the goals and strategies for production of units, including all of the following strategies, to the
37 extent applicable: the identification of zoning districts or geographic areas in which the
38 municipality proposes to modify current regulations for the purposes of creating Subsidized
39 Housing Inventory Eligible Housing developments to meet its housing production goal; the
40 identification of specific sites for which the municipality will encourage the filing of
41 Comprehensive Permit applications; characteristics of proposed residential or mixed-use
42 developments that would be preferred by the municipality (examples might include cluster
43 developments, adaptive re-use, transit-oriented housing, mixed-use development, inclusionary
44 housing, etc.); municipally owned parcels for which the municipality commits to issue requests
45 for proposals to develop Subsidized Housing Inventory Eligible Housing; and /or participation in
46 regional collaborations addressing housing development.
47 4. Review and approval of Housing Production Plans. A Housing Production Plan
48 shall be adopted by the municipality’s planning board and its select board or city council,
49 following which the Chief Executive Officer may submit the Housing Production Plan to the
50 Department of Housing and Community Development for its approval. The Department of
51 Housing and Community Development shall conduct an initial 30-day completeness review, and
52 it will notify the municipality of any deficiency and offer an opportunity to remedy the
53 deficiency. Within 90 days after the Department of Housing and Community Development’s
3 of 5

54 finding that the Housing Production Plan is complete, the Department of Housing and
55 Community Development shall approve the Housing Production Plan if it meets the requirements
56 specified herein; otherwise, it shall disapprove the Housing Production Plan. The Department of
57 Housing and Community Development shall notify the municipality of its decision to either
58 approve or disapprove a Housing Production Plan in writing. If the Department of Housing and
59 Community Development disapproves a Housing Production Plan, the notification shall include
60 a statement of reasons for the disapproval. If the Department of Housing and Community
61 Development fails to mail notice of approval or disapproval of a Housing Production Plan within
62 90 days after its receipt, it shall be deemed to be approved. A municipality that originally
63 submitted a Housing Production Plan that had been disapproved may submit a new or revised
64 Housing Production Plan to the Department of Housing and Community Development at any
65 time.
66 a. A municipality may periodically amend its Housing Production Plan if the
67 Department of Housing and Community Development approves the amendment upon the finding
68 that the amended Housing Production Plan meets the requirements of this Chapter. The
69 Department of Housing and Community Development shall have the discretion to require the full
70 90-day review process for a major amendment to a Housing Production Plan. A Housing
71 Production Plan shall be updated and renewed within five years of the date of its approval by the
72 Department of Housing and Community Development, through the full 90-day review process
73 set forth above, or as the Department of Housing and Community Development may otherwise
74 require. The Department of Housing and Community Development may, at its sole discretion,
75 elect to treat a major amendment as a renewed Housing Production Plan.
4 of 5

76 5. Certification of municipal compliance. A municipality may request that the
77 Department of Housing and Community Development certify its compliance with an Housing
78 Production Plan if it has increased its number of Subsidized Housing Inventory Eligible Housing
79 units in an amount equal to or greater than its 0.50% production goal for that calendar year.
80 Subsidized Housing Inventory Eligible Housing units shall be counted for the purpose of
81 certification in accordance with the provisions for counting units under the Subsidized Housing
82 Inventory set forth in this Chapter and Department of Housing and Community Development
83 regulations. Requests for certification may be submitted at any time, and the Department of
84 Housing and Community Development shall determine whether a municipality is in compliance
85 within 30 days of receipt of the municipality’s request. If the Department of Housing and
86 Community Development determines the municipality is in compliance with its Housing
87 Production Plan, the certification shall be deemed effective on the date upon which the
88 municipality achieved its numerical target for the calendar year in question, in accordance with
89 the rules for counting units on the Subsidized Housing Inventory set forth in this Chapter and
90 Department of Housing and Community Development regulations.
91 a. A certification shall be in effect for a period of two years from its effective date.
92 If the Department of Housing and Community Development finds that the municipality has
93 increased its number of Subsidized Housing Inventory Eligible Housing units in a calendar year
94 by at least 1.0% of its total housing units, the certification shall be in effect for three years from
95 its effective date.
