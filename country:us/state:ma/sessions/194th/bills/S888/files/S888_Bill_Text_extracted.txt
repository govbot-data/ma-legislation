An Act increasing access to MassHealth dental providers. 
 
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority 
of the same, as follows: 
 
1  Chapter 118E of the General Laws as appearing in the 2022 Official Edition is hereby 
2 amended by inserting after Section 82 the following section:- 
3  Section 83. (a) Notwithstanding any general or special law to the contrary, and subject to 
4 appropriation, the secretary of health and human services shall provide MassHealth supplemental 
5 payments to eligible MassHealth-participating dental providers who render covered dental 
6 services to an additional ten or greater MassHealth beneficiaries age 21 and older  in a given year 
7 compared to the number of MassHealth beneficiaries they provided services to in the prior year. 
8  (b) The supplemental payment established in section (a) shall initially be set at $31 for 
9 the first year and shall be distributed to eligible dental providers as a rate add-on for each patient 

2 of 2 10 encounter in which a MassHealth-covered dental service is administered to an actively enrolled  
11 MassHealth beneficiary age 21 and older. The payment rate shall be subject to re-evaluation by 
12 the secretary of health and human services under Section 13C and 13D of this chapter. 
13  (c) The supplemental payment established in section (a) shall be available to providers 
14 located in ten specific municipalities that are designated by the secretary of health and human 
15 services as having a high number or proportion of MassHealth enrollees and low rates of 
16 utilization of dental services. 
17  (d) To be eligible for supplemental payments a dental provider must meet each of the 
18 following criteria: 
19  (1) Be a dentist or public health dental hygienist licensed in the state of Massachusetts. 
20  (2) Be enrolled with MassHealth as an approved billing provider. 
21  (3) Provide dental services to a minimum of ten additional MassHealth beneficiaries age 
22 21 and older than the number of MassHealth beneficiaries the dental provider provided services 
23 to in the previous year. 
24  (4) Provide dental services at a location in eligible municipalities, as determined by the 
25 secretary of health and human services under section (c). 
26  (e) MassHealth shall promulgate regulations to implement this section. These regulations 
27 shall define provider-eligible municipalities using available data on past year MassHealth 
28 enrollment and utilization.
