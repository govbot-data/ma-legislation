The Commonwealth of Massachusetts
________________________________________
HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES, March 9, 2026.
The committee on Transportation, to whom were referred the petition
(accompanied by bill, House, No. 3624) of John Barrett, III and Colleen
M. Garry for legislation to establish idling time limits for trains; the
petition (accompanied by bill, House, No. 3640) of Rob Consalvo relative
to access to certain data by private detectives and private investigators; the
petition (accompanied by bill, House, No. 3798) of Marcus S. Vaughn
relative to establishing school zone speed limits for locomotives; and the
petition (accompanied by bill, House, No. 4085) of Christopher Richard
Flanagan that the Registry of Motor Vehicles be authorized to issue
distinctive license plates for the establishment of a fund to support the arts,
reports recommending that the accompanying order (House, No. 5133)
ought to be adopted.
For the committee,
JAMES ARCIERO.
