An Act relative to naming the Winchester center station after Governor John A. Volpe.
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority
of the same, as follows:
1 SECTION I. The Massachusetts General Laws are hereby amended by inserting the
2 following new chapter:
3 The John A. Volpe Winchester Center Station Memorial Recognition Act
4 SECTION II – PURPOSE: Memorializing Governor John A. Volpe
5 John A. Volpe, a remarkable, and important individual in Massachusetts History, lived a
6 significant portion of his life, particularly most of his public life, in Winchester, Massachusetts,
7 and should be publicly recognized, within its boundaries. Among his actions, was speaking at the
8 actual original dedication of the elevated Winchester Center Station, having made the decision,
1 of 3

9 for the new format of the station, which has saved numerous uncounted lives, over many
10 decades.
11 Among many details, of an energetic, enthusiastic, and committed public life, are known
12 significant achievements:
13 • Governor of Massachusetts, for three terms
14 • United States Secretary of Transportation
15 • United States Ambassador to Italy
16 • Construction Contractor
17 • Navy Seabee
18 • Winchester Town Meeting Member
19 • Known National Figure
20 • Chair, first national commission on drunk driving, and driving safety
21 • Strong local Charity Interests, And Activities
22 • A wide range of personal stories, and individual interactions with numerous
23 people, over his entire life
24 SECTION III – MEMORIAL DETAILS
25 A Volpe Memorial Working Group (VMWG) shall be established, with representatives,
26 of the Town of Winchester, the MBTA, and the Winchester Volpe Memorial Group, to consider
27 how to carry out, proper memorialization of Governor John A. Volpe; signage, appropriate
2 of 3

28 design work, and suitable historical memorial information, in the formal recognition of Governor
29 John A. Volpe, at or near the Winchester Center Station.
30 The General Manager (GM) of the Massachusetts Bay Transportation Authority (MBTA)
31 shall name a Chair of the Volpe Memorial Working Group (VMWG); and the GM, and the
32 named Chair, shall assemble a committee of interested individuals, who shall include residents of
33 the Town of Winchester, and other appropriate individuals, to consider how best to memorialize
34 Governor John A. Volpe, for his contributions to Transportation, the Town of Winchester, and
35 other fields.
36 The Volpe Memorial Working Group, shall keep records of their meetings, and keep a
37 spirit of relevant ad hoc, energetic membership, as would befit Governor Volpe.
