Senate, October 30, 2025 -- Resolutions (filed by Senator Rodrigues) calling on the President of 
the United States to release contingency funds for the supplemental nutrition assistance program 
for the duration of the ongoing federal government shutdown 
 
The Commonwealth of Massachusetts 
 
_______________ 
In the One Hundred and Ninety-Fourth General Court 
(2025-2026) 
_______________ 
 
RESOLUTIONS CALLING ON THE PRESIDENT OF THE UNITED STATES TO RELEASE 
CONTINGENCY FUNDS FOR THE SUPPLEMENTAL NUTRITION ASSISTANCE 
PROGRAM FOR THE DURATION OF THE ONGOING FEDERAL GOVERNMENT 
SHUTDOWN. 
 
1  WHEREAS, ON OCTOBER 26, 2025, THE TRUMP ADMINISTRATION INFORMED 
2 STATES THROUGHOUT THE NATION THAT STARTING ON NOVEMBER 1, 2025, THE 
3 FEDERALLY-FUNDED SUPPLEMENTAL NUTRITION ASSISTANCE PROGRAM 
4 WOULD NOT BE AVAILABLE DUE TO THE ONGOING FEDERAL GOVERNMENT 
5 SHUTDOWN; AND 
6  WHEREAS, REPORTS FROM THE CENTER ON BUDGET AND POLICY 
7 PRIORITIES AND OTHERS INDICATE THAT THE TRUMP ADMINISTRATION HAS 
8 THE AUTHORITY TO ALLOCATE FUNDS FROM A FEDERALLY-ESTABLISHED 
9 CONTINGENCY FUND TO COVER SUPPLEMENTAL NUTRITION ASSISTANCE 
10 PROGRAM BENEFITS AND THE CONTINGENCY FUND CURRENTLY TOTALS OVER 
11 $5,000,000,000; AND 

2 of 2 12  WHEREAS, DURING THE 2018 SHUTDOWN, THE TRUMP ADMINISTRATION 
13 WAS ABLE TO ENSURE THE CONTINUITY OF SUPPLEMENTAL NUTRITION 
14 ASSISTANCE PROGRAM FUNDS TO STATES AND THE TRUMP ADMINISTRATION 
15 AT THE TIME OPINED THAT CONTINGENCY FUNDS COULD BE USED TO FUND THE 
16 SUPPLEMENTAL NUTRITION ASSISTANCE PROGRAM; AND 
17  WHEREAS, THE CONSEQUENCES OF DECIDING NOT TO FUND THE 
18 SUPPLEMENTAL NUTRITION ASSISTANCE PROGRAM WOULD EXTEND BEYOND 
19 INDIVIDUAL HOUSEHOLDS AND CAUSE A DISRUPTION IN SUPPLEMENTAL 
20 NUTRITION ASSISTANCE PROGRAM BENEFITS THAT WOULD DESTABILIZE THE 
21 COMMONWEALTH’S FOOD ECONOMY; AND 
22  WHEREAS, HUNGER AND HARDSHIP WILL RISE IMMEDIATELY AND 
23 MEASURABLY, DISPROPORTIONATELY BURDENING SENIORS, CHILDREN AND 
24 INDIVIDUALS LIVING WITH DISABILITIES; NOW THEREFORE BE IT 
25  RESOLVED, THAT THE MASSACHUSETTS SENATE CALLS ON THE 
26 PRESIDENT OF THE UNITED STATES TO RELEASE CONTIGENCY FUNDS FOR THE 
27 SUPPLEMENTAL NUTRITION ASSISTANCE PROGRAM THROUGH THE DURATION 
28 OF THE ONGOING FEDERAL GOVERNMENT SHUTDOWN; AND BE IT FURTHER 
29  RESOLVED, THAT A COPY OF THESE RESOLUTIONS BE TRANSMITTED 
30 FORTHWITH BY THE CLERK OF THE SENATE TO THE PRESIDENT OF THE UNITED 
31 STATES AND THE MASSACHUSETTS CONGRESSIONAL DELEGATION. 
 

1 of 1 SUBMITTED BY: 
SENATOR RODRIGUES
