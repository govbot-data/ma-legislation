An Act to further enhance behavioral health workforce development. 
 
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority 
of the same, as follows: 
 
1  SECTION 1. Notwithstanding any special or general law to the contrary, the Secretary of 
2 the Executive Office of Health and Human Services (EOHHS) shall direct the Commissioners of 
3 the Department of Mental Health (DMH), the Department of Youth Services (DYS), the 
4 Department of Children and Families (DCF), the Department of Public Health (DPH), and the 
5 Department of Personnel Administration, to update the official Job Classification Specifications 
6 to include Licensed Mental Health Counselors(LMHCs) and Licensed Supervised Mental Health 
7 Counselors (LSMHCs) to the full extent of their scopes’ of practice.  
8  SECTION 2. Said revisions to the Job Classification Specifications must be completed by 
9 September 1, 2025. All relevant behavioral health job postings for positions in the 
10 aforementioned Departments under the jurisdiction of EEOHS will reflect these revisions by 
11 September 1, 2025.
