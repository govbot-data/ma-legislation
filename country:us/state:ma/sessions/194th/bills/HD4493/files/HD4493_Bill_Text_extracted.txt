Dear Secretary Gorzkowicz  and the Committees on Ways and Means : 
In accordance with Sections 1A and 1B of Chapter 140 of the Acts of 2024  (the Fiscal Year 202 5 
General Appropriation Act), I hereby submit  the comparison of Actual Revenue Collections to 
Budget for the second  quarter of Fiscal Year 202 5. 
This report  detail s actual revenue collections based on amounts accounted for in the official 
books and records of the Commonwealth- MMARS, as of December  31, 2024.  Additional detail 
is available in various MMARS reports and will be furnished upon request.   Please let me know 
if we can provide additional information or answer any questions.  
Please n ote:  in preparing this report, the Office of the Comptroller (CTR)  identified 
discrepancies  in the tally of revenue and other figures included in Sections 1A and 1B of the 
Fiscal Year 2025 General Appropriations Act  (GAA) .  CTR  staff  brought this issue to the 
attention of  the Executive Office for Administration and Finance and the Committee s on Ways 
and Means ; we have been assured that a legislative correction will be included in a forthcoming 
supplemental appropriation bill . 
To produce a meaningful report  despite the issue , we used total s based on the individual items, 
rather than the total s reflected in the GAA.   We look forward to resolution of the underlying 
issue before the end of the fiscal year.  
If you have any questions or concerns about this report or about our approach to handling the 
math discrepancy , please let me know.
