An Act establishing the term of office for the town manager of the town of Bridgewater. 
 
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority 
of the same, as follows: 
 
1  SECTION 1. Article IV of the charter of the city known as the town of Bridgewater, 
2 which is on file with the state archivist, as provided by section 12 of chapter 43B of the General 
3 Laws, is hereby amended by striking out section 4-1 and inserting in place thereof the following 
4 section:- 
5  Section 4-1. Appointment, Qualifications, Term of Office 
6  The town council shall appoint by a majority vote of the full town council, a town 
7 manager for a term not to exceed three (3) years, which may be renewed for additional terms of 
8 up to three (3) years each upon a majority vote of the full town council, subject to the procedures 
9 for appointment and removal outlined in this article. The town manager shall be a person of 
10 proven administrative ability, especially qualified by education and training with prior 
11 experience as a city or town manager or an assistant city or town manager or the equivalent 
12 public or private sector level experience. The town council may from time to time establish 
13 additional qualifications as deemed necessary and appropriate. The town manager shall devote 

2 of 2 14 full-time to the duties of the office and shall not hold any other elective or appointive office in 
15 the town, nor shall the town manager engage in other business unless such business is approved 
16 in advance by a majority vote in public session of the town council. The town manager need not 
17 be a resident of the town, but must be a United States citizen. 
18  SECTION 2. This act shall take effect upon approval by a majority of the voters of the 
19 city known as the town of Bridgewater, but not otherwise.
