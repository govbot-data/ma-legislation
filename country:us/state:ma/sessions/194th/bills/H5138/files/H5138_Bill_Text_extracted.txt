An Act relative to consumer protections for low valued legal tender scarcity. 
 
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority 
of the same, as follows: 
 
1  SECTION 1.  Section 10A of chapter 255D of the General Laws, as appearing in the 
2 2024 Official Edition, is hereby amended by added at the end thereof of the following 2 
3 paragraphs:- 
4  In any case in which the total transaction amount, including any taxes or fees, ends with 1 
5 cent, 2 cents, 6 cents, or 7 cents as the final digit, the amount of cents in the sum shall be 
6 rounded down to the nearest amount divisible by 5 for any buyer seeking to make a payment or 
7 portion thereof with legal tender.  In any case in which the total transaction amount, including 
8 any taxes or fees, ends with 3 cents, 4 cents, 8 cents, or 9 cents as the final digit, the amount of 
9 cents in the sum shall be rounded up to the nearest amount divisible by 5 for any buyer seeking 
10 to make a payment or portion thereof with legal tender; provided, however, where the total 
11 transaction amount, including any taxes or fees, totals $0.01 or $0.02, such transaction may be 
12 rounded up to $0.05 for any buyer seeking to make a payment with legal tender.  

2 of 2 13  The foregoing paragraph shall not apply to goods or services where a buyer offers to use 
14 as total payment: electronic fund transfer, check, gift card, money order, credit card, or other 
15 similar instrument or method not legal tender.  
16  SECTION 2.  The office of consumer affairs and business regulation shall promulgate 
17 rules and regulations necessary to ensure that establishments provide adequate notice to buyers 
18 and maintain the necessary systems and protocols in place relative to the use of legal tender or 
19 other forms of payment for goods and services.
