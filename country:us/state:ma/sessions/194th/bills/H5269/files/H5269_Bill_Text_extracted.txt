HOUSE . . . . . . . . No. 5269
The Commonwealth of Massachusetts
________________________________________
HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES, March 23, 2026.
The committee on Economic Development and Emerging
Technologies, to whom were referred the petition (accompanied by bill,
House, No. 481) of Daniel Cahill for legislation to further regulate
category 2 sports wagering licenses; the petition (accompanied by bill,
House, No. 483) of Paul K. Frost relative to non-outcome based or
performance based wagers; the petition (accompanied by bill, House, No.
488) of Bradley H. Jones, Jr., and others relative to the Community
Preservation Trust Fund; the petition (accompanied by bill, House, No.
496) of Jeffrey N. Roy relative to the gaming industry of the
Commonwealth; and the petition (accompanied by bill, House, No. 4431)
of David K. Muradian, Jr., relative to internet gaming, reports
recommending that the accompanying order (House, No. 5269) ought to
be adopted.
For the committee,
CAROLE A. FIOLA.
