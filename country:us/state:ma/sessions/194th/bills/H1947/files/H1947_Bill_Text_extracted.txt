An Act relative to small landlords.
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority
of the same, as follows:
1 Section 7 of chapter 185C of the General Laws, as appearing in the 2022 Official Edition,
2 is hereby amended by adding the following paragraph:-
3 A landlord that is a limited liability company formed pursuant to chapter 156C, and
4 which owns and leases 10 or fewer residential dwelling units, may be represented in proceedings
5 under this chapter by one of its officers or managers, regardless of whether the officer or
6 manager is an attorney licensed to practice law; provided, that separate limited liability
7 companies that share any of the same officers or managers shall be considered a single limited
8 liability company for the purposes of calculating the number of properties owned and leased
9 pursuant to this section.
