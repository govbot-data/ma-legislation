Dear Senate Clerk Michael Hurley and House Clerk Timothy Carroll:
Per Chapter 32, Section 23 of the Massachusetts General Laws, the Pension Reserves Investment Trust
("PRIT" or the "PRIT Fund") shall not remain invested in securities of any company "which derives more
than 15 percent of its revenues from the sale tobacco produclerkcts." The Pension Reserves
Investment Management Board's ("PRIM") current tobacco divestment policy requires that 50% of
PRIT's tobacco restrictive holdings be divested within 6 months of the security being added to the
restriction list and 100% be divested within 12 months of the security being added. PRIM is also
required to report annually a listing of such tobacco investments "held by the Fund and their book value
as of the preceding December first" as PRIT divests from these companies.
Per Chapter 32 Section 23, as of December 1, 2025, PRIM reports that no restricted tobacco securities
were held in the PRIT Fund.
