In the One Hundred and Ninety-Fourth General Court

_______________
An Act modernizing the Official Poem of the Commonwealth.
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority
of the same, as follows:
1 Section 21 of Chapter 2 of the General Laws, as appearing in the 2024 Official Edition, is
2 hereby amended by striking out, in lines 1 and 2, the words: “‘Blue Hills of Massachusetts’
3 composed by Katherine E. Mullen of the town of Barre” and inserting in place thereof the
4 following words:- “‘Song of Massachusetts’ by Regie Gibson, the Commonwealth of
5 Massachusetts first Poet Laureate.”
