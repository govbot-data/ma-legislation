An Act authorizing the city of Taunton to utilize certain areas of conservation land for road 
improvements. 
 
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority 
of the same, as follows: 
 
1  SECTION 1. Notwithstanding any general or special law to the contrary, the city of 
2 Taunton, acting by and through its conservation commission, municipal council, and mayor, may 
3 take the necessary actions to transfer the following certain parcels of land held for conservation 
4 purposes and under the care, custody and control of the conservation commission to the care, 
5 custody and control of the mayor and municipal council for improvements to the public way 
6 known as Scadding Street: Parcel No. E-5, containing approximately 6,891 square feet; and 
7 Parcel No. D-2, containing approximately 586 square feet, all as shown on that certain set of 
8 road improvement plans entitled “Plan of Land in the City of Taunton Bristol County Showing 
9 Location of Permanent and Temporary Easements Taken by the City of Taunton” prepared by 
10 Nitsch Engineering, dated March 25, 2024, as planned under Massachusetts department of 
11 transportation Project No. 608616. Prior to completing said transfer, the city of Taunton and the 

2 of 3 12 Massachusetts department of transportation may make minor modifications to the plans to carry 
13 out the purposes of this Act. 
14  SECTION 2. Notwithstanding any general or special law to the contrary, the city of 
15 Taunton, acting by and through its conservation commission, municipal council, and mayor, may 
16 take the necessary actions to transfer the following certain parcels of land held for conservation 
17 purposes and under the care, custody and control of the conservation commission to the care, 
18 custody and control of the mayor and municipal council for improvements to the public way 
19 known as Rhode Island Road (also known as state highway route 79): Parcel T-X-1, containing 
20 approximately 42 square feet; and Parcel T-D-2, containing approximately 2,297 square feet, all 
21 as shown on that certain set of road improvement plans entitled “Massachusetts Department of 
22 Transportation Highway Division Plan and Profile of Route 79 (Myricks St/Rhode Island Rd) in 
23 the Town/City of Lakeville and Taunton Plymouth County and Bristol County Preliminary Right 
24 of Way Plans”, prepared by BETA Group, Inc., dated November 4, 2024, as planned under 
25 Massachusetts department of transportation Project No. 606715. Prior to completing said 
26 transfer, the city of Taunton and the Massachusetts department of transportation may make 
27 minor modifications to the plans to carry out the purposes of this Act. 
28  SECTION 3. In consideration of the transfers of land pursuant to sections 1 and 2, the 
29 city of Taunton and to ensure no net loss of land subject to Article XCVII of the amendments to 
30 the constitution of the commonwealth, acting by and through its mayor and municipal council, 
31 shall transfer the parcel of city-owned land located on Fisher Street, shown on city of Taunton 
32 assessor maps as Parcel 64-295, said parcel being more particularly shown and described as 
33 Parcel 12 on a certain plan of land entitled “Plan of Land Taunton, Mass. Owned by: 
34 Quequechan Development Corporation” dated May 2, 1972, Scale 1”=40’, recorded with the 

3 of 3 35 Bristol County Northern District Registry of Deeds in Book 131, Page 26, owned by the City of 
36 Taunton under Final Decree in Tax Lien Case dated February 18, 1984, recorded with said 
37 Registry in Book 2448, Page 81, said parcel containing approximately 23,858 square feet of land 
38 and presently held for general municipal purposes, to the care, custody and control of the 
39 conservation commission for the city of Taunton for conservation purposes. In the event that an 
40 independent appraisal of the fair market value or value in use, whichever is greater, determines 
41 that the monetary value of said parcel is not equal or greater than the fair market value or value 
42 in use, whichever is greater, of the land described in Section 1 and Section 2 of this act, then the 
43 difference in monetary value shall be deposited into a segregated account solely for the purposes 
44 of acquiring land for conservation purposes. Said parcel conveyed to the conservation 
45 commission of the city of Taunton pursuant to this section shall be subject to Article XCVII of 
46 the Amendments to the Constitution of the Commonwealth. 
47  SECTION 4. This act shall take effect upon passage.
