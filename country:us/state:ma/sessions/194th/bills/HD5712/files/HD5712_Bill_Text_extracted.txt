12) of Michael J. Soter, Joseph D. McKenna and Ryan C. Fattman that the Massachusetts 
Department of Transportation be authorized to designate the roundabout at the intersection of

(2025-2026) 
_______________ 
 
An Act designating a certain roundabout in the town of Uxbridge in honor of Officer Stephen A. 
LaPorta. 
 
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority 
of the same, as follows: 
 
1  The roundabout at the intersection of state highway route 146, exit 6 northbound, and 
2 state highway route 16, in the town of Uxbridge shall be designated and known as the Officer 
3 Stephen A. LaPorta memorial roundabout. The Massachusetts Department of Transportation 
4 shall erect and maintain suitable markers, including a memorial stone and plaque at the 
5 roundabout bearing the designation in compliance with the standards of the department. The 
6 Massachusetts Department of Transportation shall coordinate with the town of Uxbridge and the 
7 Uxbridge police department to determine the location, design and installation of the memorial 
8 stone and plaque at the roundabout.
