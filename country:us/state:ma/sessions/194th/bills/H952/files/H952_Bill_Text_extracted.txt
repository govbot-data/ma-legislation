23 SECTION 2. Chapter 92A½ of the General Laws is hereby amended in Section 1 by
24 inserting the following-
25 “Park”, an area which conserves unique natural and cultural values while focusing on the
26 provision of recreation or facilities maintenance. Parks may have existing modified environments
27 where use is intensively managed. The timber and other natural resources in Parks shall not be
28 sold, removed, or destroyed. Vegetation management shall be allowed to ensure public health
29 and safety; support recreational use; provide fire protection and prevention; remove invasive
30 species or pests; maintain or restore recreation sites; restore or protect natural vegetation
31 communities; maintain or stabilize soils, roads and trails, scenic vistas, agricultural fields, lawns,
32 turfs, and greens; or meet other regulatory requirements. The application of pesticides or
33 herbicides shall not be permitted unless there is a clear threat to public health and safety, as
34 determined by the Massachusetts Department of Public Health.
2 of 6

35 "Reserve", an area permanently protected to allow natural processes to prevail with
36 minimal human interference, which conserves intact ecosystems with the goals of ensuring the
37 recovery and protection of mature and old growth forest ecosystems, maintenance of connected
38 habitat blocks and species movement corridors to promote ecosystem resilience, conservation of
39 habitat for threatened and endangered species, long‐term carbon sequestration and storage,
40 nutrient cycling and soil formation, preservation of reference sites for scientific research,
41 comparative studies, and long-term monitoring, and opportunities for wild land recreation. No
42 timber or other natural resources shall be sold, removed, or destroyed, provided that active
43 management shall be allowed in circumstances where it is proven that such action is necessary to
44 control erosion and stabilize soils; for invasive species management, to maintain existing
45 agricultural fields, vistas, and hiking trails; or for public health, safety and welfare. Creation of
46 new fields, vistas, and wildlife openings is prohibited. The application of pesticides or herbicides
47 shall not be permitted unless there is a clear threat to public health and safety, as determined by
48 the Massachusetts Department of Public Health.
49 SECTION 3. Chapter 92A½ of the General Laws is hereby amended in Section 2 by
50 inserting the following- The secretary of the Executive Office of Energy and Environmental
51 Affairs shall create a “Reserves Scientific Advisory Council,” hereinafter referred to as the
52 Council, to oversee the designation and management of the Reserves, and to suggest additional
53 acquisitions to enhance the ecological value of the Reserves program as a whole. The Council
54 shall consist of eight members, including: one member from the Harvard Forest or designee, who
55 is an expert in forestry and forest management; one member from the Highstead Foundation or
56 designee, who is an expert in forest ecology; one member from the Center for Biological
57 Diversity or designee, who is an expert in nongame wildlife and endangered species; one
3 of 6

58 member from the Native Plant Trust or designee, who is an expert in the field of plant ecology;
59 one member from the Woodwell Climate Research Center or designee, who is an expert in
60 climate; one member from the University of Massachusetts who has technical training and
61 experience in the field of soil or watershed science; one member from Friends of the Middlesex
62 Fells Reservation or designee; one member from Friends of Mohawk Trail State Forest or
63 designee.
64 The members of the Council shall be reimbursed for their necessary expenses incurred in
65 the performance of their duties. Each member shall be appointed for a term of three years, except
66 that for the initial term, three members shall be appointed for one year, three members shall be
67 appointed for two years and two members shall be appointed for three years. Any person
68 appointed to fill a vacancy shall serve for the unexpired term. Any member shall be eligible for
69 reappointment.
70 The Council's duties shall include overseeing the policies and rules and regulations
71 concerning Reserves, with consultation of additional qualified scientific advisors requested by
72 the Council, to ensure that Reserves are maintained in their natural state with only minimal
73 human intervention, and then only when proven necessary to preserve ecological integrity or
74 protect public health, safety and welfare, consulting with the department regarding the
75 nomination of potential Reserves, and advising the department on budgetary matters related to
76 such Reserves. The Council shall submit a biennial report to the governor on or before May first
77 of such year, describing the condition of each Reserve, outlining any actions taken by the council
78 since the last report, and making any recommendations related to the Reserve program which the
79 Council deems necessary.

80 The department shall hold a public hearing in accordance with the provisions of Chapter
81 30A, for any substantial management or other activities in designated Reserves.
82 SECTION 4. Chapter 92A1/2 of the General Laws is hereby amended by replacing
83 Section 16 with the following-
84 Section 16. The commissioner shall at least once every five years, adopt after a
85 comprehensive public review, hearing and comment, one or more periodic watershed
86 management plans for the watershed system, which shall have been prepared with the
87 participation the of the Reserves Scientific Advisory Council, additional qualified scientific
88 advisors requested by the Council, and the appropriate watershed advisory committee. Any
89 watershed management plan shall provide for, but need not be limited to, the protection of
90 forests, fish and wildlife, soils, and water quality, the maximization of carbon storage,
91 consideration of the impacts of climate change, water yield enhancement and recreational
92 activities. All watershed lands shall be designated as Parks or Reserves, and management
93 activities shall be subject to sections 40 and 41 to 46, inclusive of Chapter 132).
94 The commissioner may not approve or provide for the installation of new commercial
95 solar arrays, wind electricity generating systems, gas pipelines, commercial communications
96 sites, commercial transmissions lines, cellular communications towers, or other such industrial
97 infrastructure; provided that new solar installations will be allowed if they have the primary
98 purpose of supplying electricity to an existing or approved public facility and the added
99 environmental impacts would not be significant, and the installation of new communication
100 hardware or replacement of existing communications hardware on existing fire towers or
101 communications sites will be allowed if they would not have significant environmental impacts.

102 SECTION 5. Chapter 132 of the General Laws is hereby amended by replacing Section
103 40 with the following-
104 Section 40. It is hereby declared that the public welfare requires the rehabilitation,
105 maintenance, and protection of forest lands for the purpose of conserving water, preventing
106 floods and soil erosion, improving the conditions for natural wildlife habitat, recreation,
107 protecting and improving air and water quality, and optimizing carbon accumulation.
108 Therefore, it is hereby declared to be the policy of the commonwealth that all lands
109 devoted to forest growth shall be kept in such condition as shall not jeopardize the public
110 interests, and that the policy of the commonwealth shall further be one of cooperation with the
111 landowners and other agencies interested in forestry practices for the proper and profitable
112 management of all forest lands in the interest of the owner, the public and the users of forest
113 products, while ensuring the highest standards of sustainable forestry and native biodiversity
114 protection.
