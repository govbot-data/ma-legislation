An Act respecting autonomy in name choice for newly married partners.
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority
of the same, as follows:
1 Chapter 46 of the General Laws, as appearing in the 2020 Official Edition, is hereby
2 amended by striking out section 1D and inserting in place thereof the following section:-
3 Section 1D . Each party to a marriage may adopt any surname, for example the present
4 or birth-given surname of either party, a previously-used birth-given surname, or any hyphenated
5 combination. Each party to a marriage may also adopt any first name, middle name or any other
6 name.
