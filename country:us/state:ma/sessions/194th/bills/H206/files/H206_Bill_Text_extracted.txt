An Act fast-tracking adoptions for children in DCF custody. 
 
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority 
of the same, as follows: 
 
1  Section 25 of Chapter 119 of the General Laws, as appearing in the 2018 Official Edition, 
2 is hereby amended by inserting at the end thereof the following words:- Children in the custody 
3 of some suitable person or licensed agency providing foster care for children or the department 
4 for not less than eighteen months shall have the right to be emancipated from their biological 
5 parents if said parents have not fulfilled court ordered obligations.
