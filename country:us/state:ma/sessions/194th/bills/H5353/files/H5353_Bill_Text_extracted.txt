HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES, April 13, 2026.
The committee on Municipalities and Regional Government, to whom
was referred the petition (accompanied by bill, House, No. 4685) of Kelly
W. Pease and Lindsay N. Sabadosa relative to battery storage facility
permitting and penalties for noncompliance, reports (under the provisions
of House Rule 27) recommending that the accompanying order (House,
No. 5353) ought to be adopted.
For the committee,
JACK PATRICK LEWIS.
