By Representative McMurtry of Dedham and Senator Rush, a joint petition (subject to Joint Rule
12) of Paul McMurtry and Michael F. Rush for legislation to establish a sick leave bank for
Edward Gillen, an employee of the Massachusetts Department of Transportation. Public
Service.
The Commonwealth of Massachusetts
_______________
In the One Hundred and Ninety-Fourth General Court
(2025-2026)
_______________
An Act establishing a sick leave bank for Edward Gillen.
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority
of the same, as follows:
1 Notwithstanding any general or special law to the contrary, the Massachusetts
2 Department of Transportation - Highway Division shall establish a sick leave bank for Edward
3 Gillen an employee of the Highway Division of the Department of Transportation. Any
4 employee of the Department may voluntarily contribute 1 or more sick, personal or vacation
5 days to the sick leave bank for use by Edward Gillen. If Edward Gillen terminates employment
6 with the Department or requests to dissolve the sick leave bank, any remaining time in the sick
7 leave bank shall be transferred to the extended illness leave bank. Sick leave bank days shall not
8 be used for absences unrelated to the illness or disability that necessitated the establishment of
9 the sick leave bank as determined by the Department.
