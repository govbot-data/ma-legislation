An Act mandating access to cancer screenings for firefighters through health care benefits plans 
or programs provided by the public employer. 
 
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority 
of the same, as follows: 
 
1  SECTION 1. Chapter 32A of the General Laws is hereby amended by adding the 
2 following section:- 
3  Section 35. The commission shall provide cancer screening pursuant to section 91 of 
4 chapter 48 for eligible enrollees. Coverage provided pursuant to this section shall not be subject 
5 to co-payment, deductible, coinsurance or any out-of-pocket expense; provided, however, that 
6 cost sharing shall be required if the applicable plan is governed by the federal Internal Revenue 
7 Code and would lose its tax-exempt status as a result of the prohibition on cost sharing for these 
8 services. 
9  SECTION 2. Chapter 48 of the General Laws is hereby amended by adding the following 
10 section:- 
11  Section 91. (a) For the purposes of this section, the Massachusetts Port Authority, the 
12 Massachusetts Military Reservation, the Barnes Air National Guard Base and the Devens 

2 of 4 13 Regional Enterprise Zone established pursuant to chapter 498 of the acts of 1993 shall be fire 
14 districts.  
15  (b) A cancer screening examination conducted by a physician shall be provided to: (i) a 
16 firefighter employed as a full-time paid employee of a fire department of the commonwealth or a 
17 city, town, fire or water district; (ii) members of the Massachusetts military reservation fire 
18 department; (iii) members of the 104th fighter wing fire department; and (iv) members of the 
19 Devens fire department established pursuant to chapter 498 of the acts of 1993. The screening 
20 shall be conducted not less than 3 years after the start of the firefighter’s employment as a 
21 firefighter and every 3 years thereafter during the course of the firefighter’s employment.  
22  (c) The examination pursuant to subsection (b) shall include screening for, at a minimum 
23 and when applicable, colon cancer, lung cancer, bladder cancer, oral cancer, thyroid cancer, skin 
24 cancer, blood cancer, breast cancer, cervical cancer, testicular cancer and prostate cancer.  
25  (d) All costs associated with screenings pursuant to this section shall be borne by the 
26 firefighter’s health care benefits plan or program provided by the firefighter’s employer.  
27  SECTION 3. Chapter 175 of the General Laws is hereby amended by adding the 
28 following section:- 
29  Section 233. Any blanket or general policy of insurance shall provide cancer screening 
30 pursuant to section 91 of chapter 48 for eligible enrollees. Coverage provided pursuant to this 
31 section shall not be subject to co-payment, deductible, coinsurance or any out-of-pocket expense; 
32 provided, however, that cost sharing shall be required if the applicable plan is governed by the 
33 federal Internal Revenue Code and would lose its tax-exempt status as a result of the prohibition 
34 on cost sharing for these services. 

3 of 4 35  SECTION 4. Chapter 176A of the General Laws is hereby amended by adding the 
36 following section:- 
37  Section 39. A contract between a subscriber and the corporation under an individual or 
38 group hospital service plan shall provide cancer screening pursuant to section 91 of chapter 48 
39 for eligible enrollees. Coverage provided pursuant to this section shall not be subject to co-
40 payment, deductible, coinsurance or any out-of-pocket expense; provided, however, that cost 
41 sharing shall be required if the applicable plan is governed by the federal Internal Revenue Code 
42 and would lose its tax-exempt status as a result of the prohibition on cost sharing for these 
43 services. 
44  SECTION 5. Chapter 176B of the General Laws is hereby amended by adding the 
45 following section:- 
46  Section 26. Any subscription certificate under an individual or group medical service 
47 agreement, except certificates that provide supplemental coverage to Medicare or other 
48 governmental programs, that is delivered, issued or renewed within the commonwealth shall 
49 provide cancer screening pursuant to section 91 of chapter 48 for eligible enrollees. Coverage 
50 provided pursuant to this section shall not be subject to co-payment, deductible, coinsurance or 
51 any out-of-pocket expense; provided, however, that cost sharing shall be required if the 
52 applicable plan is governed by the federal Internal Revenue Code and would lose its tax-exempt 
53 status as a result of the prohibition on cost sharing for these services.
