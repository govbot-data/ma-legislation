An Act to study the decriminalization of sex work. 
 
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority 
of the same, as follows: 
 
1  Chapter 272 of the General Laws is hereby amended by adding after section 53B the 
2 following section, which shall be titled “Interagency commission to study the decriminalization 
3 of sex work in the Commonwealth”:-  
4  Section 53C. Interagency Commission to Study the Decriminalization of Sex Work in the 
5 Commonwealth. 
6  (a) There shall be an interagency commission to study the decriminalization of sex work 
7 in the Commonwealth. The commission shall consist of 23 members: the secretary of health and 
8 human services or a designee, who shall serve as chair; the commissioner of public health or a 
9 designee; the secretary of labor and workforce development or a designee; the secretary of 
10 housing and  livable communities or a designee; the  commissioner of children and families or a 
11 designee; the attorney general or a designee; the president of the Massachusetts District 
12 Attorneys Association or a designee; 15 members appointed by the secretary of health and 
13 human services or a designee, 1 of whom shall be a representative from an organization focused 

2 of 3 14 on advocacy for sex workers, 1 of whom shall be a representative from an organization focused 
15 on advocating for transgender people and racial justice, 5 of whom shall be survivors or direct 
16 service providers from organizations representing survivors of trafficking for sexual servitude 
17 across regions of the commonwealth1 of whom shall be a representative from the Boston Area 
18 Rape Crisis Center, 1 of whom shall be a representative from the American Civil Liberties Union 
19 of Massachusetts, 1 of whom shall be a representative from an organization focused on harm 
20 reduction related to substance use, 1 of whom shall be a representative from an organization 
21 focused on advocating for legal defense and bonds for undocumented people, 1 of whom shall be 
22 a representative from the Committee for Public Counsel Services, and 3 of whom shall be current 
23 or former sex workers. Adequate compensation for services shall be determined by a schedule of 
24 fees adopted by the commission.   
25  (b) The commission shall be responsible for studying and making recommendations 
26 towards the following: (i) what criminal penalties and collateral consequences exist related to the 
27 criminalization of sex work, including criminal penalties for buyers of commercial sex; (ii) what 
28 labor protections need to be in place under decriminalization; (iii) what services need to be made 
29 available to people engaged in commercial sex to improve health and safety outcomes; (iv) the 
30 methods of human trafficking and exploitation in order to develop strategies to reduce these 
31 activities in collaboration with sex workers, with a focus on prevention; (v) implementation 
32 models for alternatives to policing to promote safety in the commercial sex sector, rooted in 
33 public health; and (vi) the development of a fund to prevent, reduce, prevent, and eliminate 
34 human trafficking that shall include ways to support the health, safety, and autonomy of sex 
35 workers. 

3 of 3 36  (c) The first meeting of the commission shall take place within 90 days of the effective 
37 date of this act. The commission shall file a report containing its findings and recommendations, 
38 including legislative recommendations, if any, with the clerks of the house of representatives and 
39 the senate, the house and senate committees on ways and means and the joint committee on 
40 public health no later than December 31, 2026.
