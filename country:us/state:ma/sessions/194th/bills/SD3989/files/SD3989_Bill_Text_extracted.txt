FY25 Legislative Report : Psychiatric Mental Health Nurse Practitioner Fellowship  Program   
 
In calendar year 2023, the Executive Office of Health and Human Services (EOHHS) , Tan 
Chingfen Graduate School of Nursing, a program of the University of Massachusetts Chan 
Medical School (UMass Chan GSN) and the Massachusetts League of Community Health 
Centers (M LCHC ) launched the $11.6 million  Psychiatric Mental Health Nurse Practitioner 
(PMHNP) Fellowship Program  for recent PMHNP Graduates and current PMHNP Students 
funded through item 1599 -2026 of the acts of 2021 (see appendix I for authorizing statutory 
language ). The PMHNP Fellowship Program aims to increase the community health center 
(CHC) PMHNP workforce pipeline and increase the diversity of PMHNPs working in CHCs.  
See appendix II for PMHNP Fellowship Program eligibility and prio ritization information . 
This report includes information on (I) the number of psychiatric mental health nurse practitioner 
applicants and participants; (II) participant retention; (III) efforts to diversify the psychiatric 
mental health nurse practitioners; and (IV) all program expenditur es.

III. Efforts to diversify the psychiatric mental health nurse practitioners : 
 
UMass Chan GSN and MLCHC have worked  to increase diversity in the PMHNP workforce , 
effectively matching bilingual candidates and those with cultural competencies matching CHC 
patient populations. The program maintains comprehensive demographic tracking, analyzing 
alignment between Fellow candidates and CHC service communities through detailed data on 
languag e capabilities, race, and ethnicity for both placed Fellows and the applicant pool. This 
data is compared against CHC patient population demographics to ensure sustained 
representation. Early results demonstrate successful demographic matching between plac ed 
Fellows and the communities they serve, particularly in language capabilities and cultural 
representation.

 Appendix I I: Psychiatric Mental  Health Nurse Practitioner  (PMHNP)  Fellowship Program  
Eligibility  
The Psychiatric Mental Health Nurse Practitioner (PMHNP) Fellowship Program  aims to recruit, educate, 
and retain PMHNPs at community health centers (CHCs) . All Massachusetts ’ CHCs that meet the criteria 
to qualify as a Federal Qualified Health Center (FQHC), a hospital -licensed health center, or an FQHC 
look-alike are eligible to apply for funding . Current PMHNP Student s and recent Graduate s are eligible  to 
apply  for placement . Student  Fellows will complete  a semester -long placement while Graduate Fellows 
will be under a 12 -month service commitment.   
CHC Prioritization  
CHCs that serve diverse communities, rural communities, or are in a federally designated Health 
Professional Shortage Area (HPSA)  or a Mental Health Professional Shortage Area are strongly 
encouraged to apply. At least one rural CHC will be funded during each year of the program.  
CHC Selection  
CHCs will be selected and evaluated for funding based on their demonstrated capacity to provide the 
following:  
1) A commitment to hire Graduate  PMHNP fellows who have passed the national certification exam 
by the American Nurses Credentialing Center or the American Association of Nurse 
Practitioners, have a Commonwealth of Massachusetts Advance Practice Nursing License in good 
standing with the B oard of Registration in Nursing, and are eligible to apply for Mass Controlled 
Substance License/DEA by the start of the Program.  
2) A commitment to building culturally diverse psychiatric capacity both by prioritizing hiring 
qualified PMHNP candidates who reflect the patient population's cultural and linguistic 
backgrounds and providing services to communities that are underserved by l imited access to 
behavioral healthcare.  
3) Capacity and capability to precept all Fellows.  
4) Capacity to provide adequate prescribing Preceptor support for PMHNP Graduate  Fellows.  
5) Ability to ensure adequate patient panels for Fellows in terms of size, diversity, and clinical 
complexity.  
6) Ability to ensure adequate care team support (e.g., medical assistants, registered nurses, 
community health workers, behavioral health clinicians, psychiatrists, or experienced PMHNPs 
for consult support, and support staff) for Fellows.  
7) Ability to provide adequate physical space (e.g., exam/treatment rooms) for Fellows.  
8) Ability to provide coverage and systems to ensure that the clinical needs of patients are met in the 
Fellows’ absence (e.g., sick day, vacation time, etc.).  
9) Ability to ensure that Fellows work as part of a multi -disciplinary care team.
