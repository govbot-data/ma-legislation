An Act establishing a real estate transfer fee upon the transfer of property in the town of
Arlington.
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority
of the same, as follows:
1 SECTION 1. There shall be a real estate transfer fee, hereinafter know as the fee, upon
2 the transfer of any real property interest in the town of Arlington or the transfer of a controlling
3 interest in a trust, limited liability company or other entity that directly or indirectly holds an
4 interest in any real property situated in the town of Arlington according to the price thresholds,
5 fee rates and other terms to be established pursuant to sections 2 and 3 by town meeting
6 following a recommendation by the select board of the town of Arlington.
7 SECTION 2. The fee established pursuant to section 1 of this act shall apply to any
8 transfer exceeding a threshold price between 50 per cent to 150 per cent of the state median sale
9 price of a single-family home. The threshold price shall be determined by vote of the town
10 meeting following a recommendation by the select board of the town of Arlington.
1 of 5

11 SECTION 3. The fee established pursuant to section 1 of this act shall be between 0.05
12 per cent and 2 per cent of the transfer price, as determined by a vote of town meeting following a
13 recommendation by the select board of the town of Arlington. The fee shall be assessed upon the
14 buyer or transferee, the seller or transferor or both parties, as determined by a vote of town
15 meeting following a recommendation by the select board of the town of Arlington. The town of
16 Arlington may define, by by-law, what constitutes a controlling interest and the calculation of the
17 fee.
18 SECTION 4. The following transfers of real property interests shall be exempt from the
19 fee established in section l:
20 (a) transfers for less than the threshold price determined by town meeting pursuant to
21 section 2;
22 (b) transfers made as gifts with consideration of less than $100 if the seller was, at the
23 time of the transfer, the spouse, domestic partner, lineal descendant or the lineal ancestor of the
24 purchaser, by blood, marriage or adoption;
25 (c) transfers to the federal government, the commonwealth and any of their
26 instrumentalities, agencies or subdivisions, including the town of Arlington and the Arlington
27 housing authority;
28 (d) transfers of residential property that include 1 or more units subject to affordable
29 housing restrictions, as defined in section 31 of chapter 184 of the General Laws; provided,
30 however, that the fee imposed shall be proportionately reduced based on the percentage of
31 residential units subject to affordable housing restrictions, as compared to the total number of
32 residential units;
2 of 5

33 (e) transfers made without additional consideration to confirm, correct, modify or
34 supplement a transfer previously made;
35 (f) transfers by operation of law without actual consideration, including, but not limited
36 to, transfers occurring by virtue of death or bankruptcy of the owner of real property interest;
37 (g) transfers made in partition of land and improvements thereto, pursuant to chapter 241
38 of the General Laws;
39 (h) transfers to a charitable organization, as defined in clause Third of section 5 of
40 chapter 59 of the General Laws, or a religious organization; provided, however, that the real
41 property interests so transferred shall be held solely for affordable housing related uses that shall
42 be consistent with the uses allowed by the Municipal Affordable Housing Trust Fund established
43 by the town pursuant to section 55C of chapter 44 of the General Laws;
44 (i) transfers for a mortgagee in foreclosure of the mortgage held by the mortgagee and
45 transfers of the property subject to a mortgage to the mortgagee in consideration of the
46 forbearance of the mortgagee from closing such mortgage; and
47 (j) transfers consisting of the division of marital assets pursuant to section 34 of chapter
48 208 of the General Laws or other provisions of law.
49 SECTION 5. The town of Arlington shall not, by by-law or otherwise, eliminate or
50 reduce any exemption set forth in section 4.
51 SECTION 6. The fee shall be paid to the town of Arlington and deposited into the
52 Municipal Affordable Housing Trust Fund established by the town pursuant to section 55C of
53 chapter 44 of the General Laws and appropriations therefrom shall be limited to financing
3 of 5

54 affordable housing and reasonable amounts for personnel and other costs. The town of Arlington
55 shall have such remedies to collect the fee as provided by law with respect to the collection of
56 real property taxes, including collection and imposing liens for any outstanding transfer fee.
57 SECTION 7. The town of Arlington may, by by-law, adopt additional requirements,
58 exemptions, waivers and regulations to implement or enforce the fee, consistent with this act.
59 SECTION 8. The town of Arlington shall require a copy of the deed or other instrument
60 evidencing such transfer and shall be accompanied by:
61 (a) an affidavit signed under oath or under the pains and penalties of perjury by the
62 purchaser and seller attesting to the purchase price;
63 (b) the applicable fee owed or, if applicable, an affidavit of intent to seek 1 of the
64 permissible exemptions, as described in section 4 of this act, for that property by the purchaser;
65 and
66 (c) the basis, if any, upon which the transfer shall be claimed to be exempt in whole or in
67 part from the fee.
68 SECTION 9. Upon receipt of the transfer fee or satisfactory evidence of exemption the
69 town of Arlington or its designee shall promptly thereafter issue a certificate indicating that the
70 fee has been paid or that the transfer shall be exempt from the fee. The Middlesex South registry
71 of deeds shall not record or register a deed unless the deed is accompanied by such certificate.
72 SECTION 10. The town of Arlington shall prepare and issue an annual report that:
73 (a) identifies fee receipts;
4 of 5

74 (b) quantifies affordable housing programs funded, including type and purpose; and
75 (c) evaluates the impact of the affordable housing programs, including, but not limited to,
76 to the extent reasonably possible and permitted by applicable law, the number and demographics
77 of individuals and families served as well as measures of housing stability and wealth generation
78 in the community.
79 SECTION 11. Acceptance of this act by the town of Arlington shall be first by vote of
80 approval at an annual town meeting, to be followed by an affirmative vote of a majority of the
81 voters at any regular or special election at which the question of acceptance shall be placed on
82 the ballot.
83 SECTION 12. Sections 1 to 10 of this act, inclusive, shall take effect 30 days after
84 acceptance by the town of Arlington pursuant to section 11 of this act, but not otherwise.
85 SECTION 13. Sections 11 and 12 shall take effect upon passage of this act.
