An Act relative to acute hospital governance.
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority
of the same, as follows:
1 Section 51 of Chapter 111 is hereby amended by adding at the end thereof the following
2 new paragraph:-
3 All acute-care hospitals as defined in section twenty-five B, shall have a registered nurse
4 holding a current unencumbered license from the board of registration in nursing as a sitting and
5 voting member of the governing entity responsible for developing the organization’s strategic
6 plan, structure, systems, policies and programs.
