An Act to prohibit the sale of "energy drinks" to persons under the age of 18. 
 
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority 
of the same, as follows: 
 
1  Chapter 270 of the General Laws, as appearing in the 2014 Official Edition, is hereby 
2 amended by adding Section 30:    
3  Section 30: Whoever distributes, delivers, gives away or sells, any packaged “Energy 
4 Drink” or other ingredient, substance or beverage that exceeds a caffeine content of seventy-one 
5 (71) milligrams per twelve (12) ounce serving and contains taurine and glucuronolactone or a 
6 packaged soft drink that is classified as a dietary supplement not regulated by the Food and Drug 
7 Administration and that contains eighty (80) or more milligrams of caffeine per eight (8) fluid 
8 ounces and generally includes a combination of methylxanthines, B vitamins and herbal 
9 ingredients which are advertised as being specifically designed to provide or increase energy to 
10 any persons under the age of eighteen (18) shall be punished by a fine not less than fifty (50) 
11 dollars for the first offense, one hundred (100) for the second offense and two hundred fifty 
12 (250) for the third offense.
