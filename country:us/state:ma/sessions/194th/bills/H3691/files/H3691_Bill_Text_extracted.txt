An Act relative to roadways and road markings. 
 
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority 
of the same, as follows: 
 
1  Chapter 84 of the General Laws, as appearing in the 2022 Official Edition, is hereby 
2 amended by inserting the following new section:- 
3  Section 28. Public utilities and private contractors shall be required to patch and seal 
4 roadways within 30 days after breaking ground, weather permitting, and shall be required to 
5 redraw any road markings disturbed including but not limited to crosswalks, bike lanes, and 
6 passing lanes, within 30 days of said lines being disturbed, weather permitting. If these 
7 requirements are not met, the local town or city public works commissioner shall order notice 
8 thereof to be given to the delinquent utility or private entity, who shall be assessed a penalty of 
9 $100 for the first month of noncompliance after the initial 30 days has elapsed, and a penalty of 
10 no less than $100 for each week thereafter until the work is completed.
