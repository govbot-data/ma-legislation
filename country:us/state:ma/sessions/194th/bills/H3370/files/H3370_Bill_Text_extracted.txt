By Representative Kilcoyne of Clinton, a petition (accompanied by bill, House, No. 3370) of
Meghan K. Kilcoyne and Jacob R. Oliveira relative to additional work in contracts of the
Division of Highways. State Administration and Regulatory Oversight.
The Commonwealth of Massachusetts
_______________
In the One Hundred and Ninety-Fourth General Court
(2025-2026)
_______________
An Act relative to fairness in public contracting.
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority
of the same, as follows:
1 Notwithstanding any general or special law to the contrary, the division of highways, as
2 established by Section 37 of Chapter 6C of the General Laws, is hereby directed to amend its
3 standard specifications, supplemental specifications, special provisions, and contract documents
4 to provide for a minimum of 15 percent for overhead for work deemed to be additional work or
5 extra work.
