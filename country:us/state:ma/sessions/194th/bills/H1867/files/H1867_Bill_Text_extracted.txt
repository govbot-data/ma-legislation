An Act relative to emerging adult life sentences for first-degree murder. 
 
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority 
of the same, as follows: 
 
1  Section 24 of chapter 279 of the General Laws, as appearing in the 2022 Official Edition, 
2 is hereby amended by inserting this third paragraph, after the second paragraph, the following 
3 paragraph: -  
4  In the case of a sentence of life imprisonment for murder in the first degree committed by 
5 a person on or after the person's eighteenth birthday and before the person's twenty-first birthday, 
6 the court shall fix a minimum term of not less than 30 years nor more than 60 years; provided, 
7 however, that in the case of a sentence of life imprisonment for murder in the first degree with 
8 extreme atrocity or cruelty committed by a person on or after the person's eighteenth birthday 
9 and before the person's twenty-first birthday, the court shall fix a minimum term of 40 years nor 
10 more than 60 years; and provided further, that in the case of a sentence of life imprisonment for 

2 of 2 11 murder in the first degree with deliberately premeditated malice aforethought committed by a 
12 person on or after the person's eighteenth birthday and before the person's twenty-first birthday, 
13 the court shall fix a minimum term of not less than 35 years nor more than 60 years.
