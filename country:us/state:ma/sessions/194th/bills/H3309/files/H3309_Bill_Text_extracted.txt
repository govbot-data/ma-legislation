The Commonwealth of Massachusetts
_______________
In the One Hundred and Ninety-Fourth General Court
(2025-2026)
_______________
An Act facilitating access to justice in the city of Cambridge.
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority
of the same, as follows:
1 Notwithstanding any other general or special law to the contrary, in any decision to lease,
2 sell or otherwise dispose of the former probate court space in the Middlesex South registry of
3 deeds building, the commissioner of capital asset management and maintenance shall consider
4 and prioritize the needs of the executive office of the trial court for building space in the city of
5 Cambridge. If the executive office of the trial court demonstrates to the commissioner a need for
6 the use of the building space or any portion thereof, the commissioner shall sell or lease such
7 space to the executive office.
