BUSINESS BRIEFS
TPE Solutions Inc.
MassDevelopment sold a vacant 6.73-acre parcel at 33 Lake George
Street in Devens to TPE Solutions Inc. The Shirley-based company, which
specializes in thermoplastic elastomer (TPE) solutions, plans to construct a
31,000-square-foot facility that will house office, distribution, and research
and development space.
Construction is expected to begin in late 2026, with the new facility
supporting the company’s continued expansion and ability to meet growing
client demand.
This expansion underscores Devens’ appeal as a destination for forward-
thinking manufacturers thanks to its business-friendly environment, robust
infrastructure, and strategic location.
Veranova
Pharmaceutical ingredient developer Veranova is investing $50 million to expand its Devens facility, which now serves as the company’s
headquarters. The 9,000-square-foot expansion will enhance the site's capacity to develop and manufacture complex molecules for
next-generation therapeutics. The project is expected to create up to 70 new jobs, support local economic growth, and strengthen the
U.S. supply of active pharmaceutical ingredients, reinforcing Massachusetts' leadership in health care innovation.

Marty’s Corner Café & Deli
Since opening its doors in June 2021, Marty’s Corner Café & Deli has been more than just a place to grab a bite – it’s become a local institution
that supports and reflects the people of Devens. Whether it’s sourcing ingredients locally, catering for community events, or donating food to the
nearby Loaves & Fishes Food Pantry, Marty’s Corner is deeply invested in giving back.
Owner Marty Twomey designed the café’s menu with Devens workers and residents in mind – fast and flavorful meals that are easy to grab on the
go. In a thoughtful nod to the community, some menu items are even named after local businesses, such as the popular “Dragonfly (Wellness
Center) Poke Bowl.”
Adding to its local presence, the café also operates a food truck affectionately named “Foxy” that makes appearances at local sports tournaments
and events, bringing Marty’s signature dishes to customers throughout the region.

COMMUNITY UPDATES
Devens Housing Working Group
The Devens Housing Working Group, established in 2024 through
the Mass Leads Act, was tasked with developing a strategy to
increase housing production in Devens. The group was led by the
Executive Office of Economic Development and the Executive Office
of Housing and Livable Communities, and included representatives
from the Towns of Ayer, Harvard, and Shirley; the Devens Enterprise
Commission (the local regulatory and permitting authority); the
Devens Committee (an advisory group of Devens residents); and
MassDevelopment.
In May 2025, the Working Group released its final report, which
supports a zoning amendment to allow a variety of housing types
within the Innovation and Technology Center (ITC) zoning district.
This 90-acre district is currently limited to commercial uses and
includes the historic Vicksburg Square, a former Army barracks
complex.
With the elimination of the 282-unit housing cap, this
recommendation represents a significant step toward enabling the
development of much-needed housing to serve both Devens and the
surrounding region.

Devens Public Safety Building
The new Devens Public Safety Building is set to elevate emergency response services by
bringing together all local first responders under one roof including the Devens Fire Department,
Nashoba Valley Regional Dispatch, and the Massachusetts State Police.
This 30,745-square-foot, full-service facility has been thoughtfully designed to support the
vital work of our emergency personnel while enhancing safety for residents, employers, the local
workforce, and visitors. Key features include:
• A new apparatus bay to house fire and ambulance vehicles
• Modern living quarters for firefighters
• An exterior training tower to support ongoing skill development and readiness
And let’s not forget the newest member of the team – Shane, the beloved Bernedoodle firehouse
mascot, who’s excited for his very own dedicated space in the new building!

NOTABLE EVENTS
Unity Days Powwow
Other important community events included the Annual Unity Days
Powwow at the United Native American Cultural Center (UNACC) in
Devens, where Massachusetts State Senator Jamie Eldridge presented a
citation from Governor Maura Healey honoring UNACC Co-Founder Onkwe
Tase on what would have been his 100th birthday. Tase, a member of a
Mohawk tribe in Canada, also served as Massachusetts’ first Commissioner
of Indian Affairs in 1974, advocating for the rights of Indigenous people
in the state. The Governor’s Citation was presented to his daughter, Anne
Fraser, followed by a traditional powwow dance performed by the family
and other Indigenous community members.

Devens Enterprise Commission
30th Anniversary
The Devens Enterprise Commission (DEC) marked its 30th anniversary this year
as the regulatory and permitting authority for the Devens Regional Enterprise
Zone. The DEC remains a key partner in the area's ongoing redevelopment
efforts. Notably, Devens’ streamlined “75-day or less” permitting process
continues to be recognized as one of the fastest in Massachusetts.
