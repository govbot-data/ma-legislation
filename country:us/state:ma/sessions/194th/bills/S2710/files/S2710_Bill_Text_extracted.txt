An Act addressing challenges facing public libraries and digital resource collections. 
 
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority 
of the same, as follows: 
 
1  (a) As used in this act, the following words shall have the following meanings unless the 
2 context clearly requires otherwise: 
3  “Access”, any proper means by which a person may read or use digital resources, print 
4 materials, records or other information held by a public library.  
5  “Digital resources”, any books, textbooks, workbooks, videos, databases, newspapers, 
6 magazines, periodicals, charts, graphs, movies, games, maps, interactive applications, software 
7 and other educational or entertainment materials in any electronic format, . 
8  “Print materials”, primarily paper-based materials found in physical or hard-copy format. 
9  “Public library”, (i) public library; (ii) public school library; or (iii) public research 
10 library. 
11  (b) There shall be a special legislative commission to assess and address challenges faced 
12 by public libraries and digital resource collections. 

2 of 3 13  (c) The commission shall: (i) study the cost trends and expenditures of public libraries 
14 within the commonwealth relative to digital resources and collections; (ii) study patron use of, 
15 and satisfaction with, digital resources; (iii) research the opinions of legal experts and scholars 
16 relative to the differences between existing licensing for digital resources and the use of print 
17 materials and how these differences may impact patron experience and access to diverse 
18 collections; (iv) research and describe how patron privacy records and metadata are kept, used, 
19 accessed and sold as it relates to patron use and access of digital resources through a public 
20 library; (v) make recommendations addressing the security and privacy of data related to digital 
21 resources; (vi) identify and describe concerns, if any, regarding patron protection and access 
22 issues relative to digital resources; (vii) investigate and summarize the actions of other states in 
23 addressing digital resource creation; (viii) examine the impact of digital resources on library 
24 users; and (ix) recommend legislative and executive action to: (A) support the sustainability of 
25 diverse digital resources in public libraries; (B) better support access to diverse digital resources; 
26 (C) enable the continuation of public lending to all residents; and (D) protect patron data in the 
27 course of digital resource use. 
28  (d) The commission shall consist of: 2 persons appointed by the speaker of the house of 
29 representatives, 1 of whom shall be designated as co-chair by the speaker of the house of 
30 representatives; 2 persons appointed by the president of the senate, 1 of whom shall be 
31 designated as co-chair  by the senate president; 1 person appointed by the minority leader of the 
32 senate; 1 person appointed by the minority leader of the house of representatives; the director of 
33 the board of library commissioners or a designee; the attorney general or a designee; the director 
34 of the Massachusetts office on disability or a designee; the president of the Massachusetts 
35 Library Association; the president of the Massachusetts School Library Association; the 

3 of 3 36 president of the Boston public library or a designee; the executive director of the Massachusetts 
37 Center for the Book, Inc.; and 5 members who shall be appointed by the governor,  1 of whom 
38 shall be a research librarian from a public  research institution of higher education, 1 of whom 
39 shall be a representative of the Association of American Publishers, Inc., 1 of whom shall be a 
40 representative of a digital distributor of electronic books, 1 of whom shall be an expert in the 
41 field of intellectual property or copyright law and 1 of whom shall be an expert in consumer 
42 protection laws. 
43  (e) The co-chairs shall direct the schedule and work of the commission. A simple 
44 majority vote of the commission members present may be requested for any actions of the 
45 commission and all members of the commission shall have a vote. The co-chairs may create 
46 subgroups to focus on specific responsibilities of the commission. The co-chairs may invite 
47 guests to participate in meetings as speakers.  
48  (f) The commission may seek assistance from outside experts at the discretion of the co-
49 chairs. The commission may use consulting and research firms in the course of its study, subject 
50 to appropriation. 
51  (g) Not later than October 15, 2026, the commission shall submit a report with its 
52 findings and any recommendations to the clerks of the house of representatives and senate, the 
53 governor and the attorney general.
