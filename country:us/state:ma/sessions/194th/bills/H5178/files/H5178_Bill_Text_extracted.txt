HOUSE . . . . . . . . No. 5178
The Commonwealth of Massachusetts
________________________________________
HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES, March 5, 2026.
The committee on Economic Development and Emerging
Technologies, to whom were referred the petition (accompanied by bill,
House, No. 479) of James Arciero and others relative to limited liability
company fees; the petition (accompanied by bill, House, No. 482) of Ann-
Margaret Ferrante for legislation to establish a designated port area
community fund; the joint petition (accompanied by bill, House, No. 484)
of William C. Galvin and Michael D. Brady relative to the membership of
the Brockton Economic Target Area; the petition (accompanied by bill,
House, No. 486) of Steven S. Howitt relative to reporting fees for limited
liability companies; the petition (accompanied by bill, House, No. 487) of
Bradley H. Jones, Jr., and others relative to establishing a commission
(including members of the General Court) to study the financial relationship
between Massachusetts entities and companies owned by the Chinese
government; the petition (accompanied by bill, House, No. 493) of Orlando
Ramos for legislation to establish a Puerto Rico trade commission; the
petition (accompanied by bill, House, No. 494) of Orlando Ramos relative
to filing costs for microbusinesses; the petition (accompanied by bill,
House, No. 495) of David M. Rogers relative to reducing emissions from
artificial intelligence; the petition (accompanied by bill, House, No. 500) of
Justin Thurber and John R. Gaskey that certain corporations be required to
issue disclaimers stating that they are a non-governmental organizations;
the petition (accompanied by bill, House, No. 501) of Erika Uyterhoeven
relative to limited liability companies; the petition (accompanied by bill,
House, No. 502) of Marcus S. Vaughn relative to business relocation after
receiving economic development incentive program grant funding; and the
petition (accompanied by bill, House, No. 503) of Christopher J. Worrell
for legislation to promote entrepreneurship through employee ownership,
reports recommending that the accompanying order (House, No. 5178)
ought to be adopted.
For the committee,
CAROLE A. FIOLA.
