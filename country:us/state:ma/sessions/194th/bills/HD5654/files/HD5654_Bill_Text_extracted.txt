Communications Access Trust Fund Report
Section 2E, line 1595-6153 of the FY25 GAA requires the Executive Office for
Administration and Finance to submit a report including:
(a) the distribution of the funds appropriated in this item to sheriffs’ offices
and the department of corrections;
(b) the barriers to providing calls free of charge to incarcerated individuals;
and
(c) the current volume of calls at sheriffs’ offices and the department of
corrections.

Regarding the reporting required in subsection “(b),” DOC notes that the barriers
to providing calls free of charge to incarcerated individuals include:
• Costs associated with providing free phone calls, which also includes free
video visits and email correspondences
o The Department is currently observing around a 165% increase in
volume/usage compared to November 2023 (the last month before
free phone calls were implemented and commissions ceased).
• Funding to the Communications Access Trust Fund (CATF):
2

o The CATF was appropriated $10M in FY25. DOC’s spending was
over $10M (phone calls/video visits, fees, and emails). DOC’s Fiscal
Services Division could not seek reimbursement for June 2025 costs
due to the low CATF balance, and hence that monthly cost
(approximately $850K) had to be absorbed in DOC’s operating
budget.
