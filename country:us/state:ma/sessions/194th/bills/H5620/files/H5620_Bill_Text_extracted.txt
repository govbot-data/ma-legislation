An Act promoting rule of law, oversight, trust and equal constitutional treatment. 
 
 Whereas, The deferred operation of this act would tend to defeat its purpose, which is to 
promote rule of law, oversight, trust and equal constitutional treatment, and to ensure the right of 
access to and protection in courts of justice, therefore it is hereby declared to be an emergency 
law, necessary for the immediate preservation of the public safety. 
 
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority 
of the same, as follows:
