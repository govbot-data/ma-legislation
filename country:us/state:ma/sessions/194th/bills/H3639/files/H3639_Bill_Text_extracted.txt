An Act promoting safety in the use of off-highway vehicles.
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority
of the same, as follows:
1 SECTION 1. Section 1 of chapter 61B of the General Laws, as appearing in the 2020
2 Official Edition, is hereby amended by striking out, in line 21, the words “and equine boarding”
3 and inserting in place thereof the following words:- , equine boarding and the use of recreation
4 vehicles as defined in section 20 of chapter 90B.
5 SECTION 2. Section 24N of chapter 90 of the General Laws, as so appearing, is hereby
6 amended by striking out, in line 5, the words “or section eight B” and inserting in place thereof
7 the following words:- , section 8B or section 26A.
8 SECTION 3. Section 20 of chapter 90B of the General Laws, as so appearing, is hereby
9 amended by inserting after the definition of “Directly supervised” the following definition:-
10 “Emergency off-road vehicle”, any all-terrain vehicle, recreation utility vehicle, utility
11 vehicle, recreation vehicle, off-highway vehicle or snow vehicle used by public safety personnel
12 to respond to emergencies.
