An Act amending Chapter 67 of the Acts of 2008 authorizing the city of Salem to convert 1
annual license for the sale of wine, malt and cordial beverages to be drunk on the premises to an
annual license for the sale of all alcoholic beverages to be drunk on the premises.
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority
of the same, as follows:
1 SECTION 1. Chapter 67 of the Acts of 2008, authorizing the city of Salem to grant 1
2 annual license for the sale of wine, malt and cordial beverages to be drunk on the premises, is
3 hereby amended by converting the beer, wine and cordial license to an annual license for the sale
4 of all alcoholic beverages to be drunk on the premises under section 12 of said chapter 138
5 issued to Gulu-Gulu Cafe located at 247 Essex Street in the city of Salem. The license converted
6 under this section shall be subject to all of chapter 138 of the General Laws except sections 17
7 and 17A.
8 The licensing authority shall not approve the transfer of the license converted pursuant to
9 this section to any other location but it may grant the license to a new applicant at the same
10 location if the applicant files with the licensing authority a letter from the department of revenue
11 and a letter from the department of unemployment assistance indicating that the license is in
1 of 2

12 good standing with those departments and that all applicable taxes, fees and contributions have
13 been paid.
14 If the license converted pursuant to this section is cancelled, revoked or no longer in use
15 at the location of original issuance, it shall be returned physically, with all of the legal rights,
16 privileges and restrictions pertaining thereto, to the licensing authority and the licensing
17 authority may then grant the license to a new applicant at the same location under the same
18 conditions as specified in this act.
19 SECTION 2. (a) The city of Salem shall charge a fee for the conversion of the licenses
20 authorized in section 1 which shall be paid by the licensee at the time of issuance unless the
21 licensing authority agrees to enter into a contract with the licensees to allow the division of the
22 fee into multiple payments over time from the licensee. If the city elects to accept multiple
23 payments over time from the licensees, the option shall be made available, upon request, to all
24 qualified applicants for a license.
25 (b) Any fee collected by the city of Salem for the licenses converted pursuant to section 1
26 which is greater than the amount of the fee charged for an annual renewal of a similar license
27 issued by the city shall be deposited into the city's economic development account and expended
28 by it in a manner consistent with the purposes of such account.
29 SECTION 3. This act shall take effect upon its passage.
