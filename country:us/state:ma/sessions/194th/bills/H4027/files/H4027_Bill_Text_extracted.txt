In the One Hundred and Ninety-Fourth General Court
(2025-2026)
_______________
An Act relative to motor vehicle excise tax exemptions for a person with a disability.
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority
of the same, as follows:
1 Section 1 of chapter 60A of the General Laws, as appearing in the 2022 Official Edition,
2 is hereby amended by striking out, in lines 115 and 116, the words “or permanent loss of use of,
3 both legs or both arms” and inserting in place thereof the following words:- or permanent loss of
4 use of, 1 or both feet, or permanent loss of use of, 1 or both hands, or has been determined by the
5 medical advisory board established under section 8C of chapter 90 to be permanently disabled.
