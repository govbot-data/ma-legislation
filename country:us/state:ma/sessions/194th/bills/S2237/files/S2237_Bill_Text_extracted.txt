of the same, as follows:
1 SECTION 1. Section 20 of chapter 239 of the acts of 2024 is hereby repealed.
2 SECTION 2. Section 21 of said chapter 239, as so appearing, is hereby repealed.
3 SECTION 3. The definition of “Small clean energy storage facility” in subsection (a) of

6 small clean energy storage facility shall not include pumped storage hydropower facilities.

8 chapter 239, as so appearing, is hereby amended by inserting after the words “operation of the
9 large clean energy storage facility” the following words:- provided, that a large clean energy
10 storage facility shall not include pumped storage hydropower facilities.
