An Act authorizing the town of Warren to continue the employment of Steven Perkins. 
 
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority 
of the same, as follows: 
 
1  SECTION 1. Notwithstanding any general or special law to the contrary, Steven Perkins, 
2 a member of the fire department of the town of Warren, may continue to serve in such position 
3 until the age of 70, or until the date of his retirement, non-reappointment, or the date he is 
4 relieved of his duties by the town, whichever occurs first; provided, however, that he is mentally 
5 and physically capable of performing the duties of his office. Provided further, that the town 
6 may, at its own expense, require that Steven Perkins be examined by an impartial physician 
7 designated by the town to determine such capability; and provided further, that no deductions 
8 shall be made from the regular compensation of Steven Perkins pursuant to chapter 32 of the 
9 General Laws for any service subsequent to his reaching the age of 65 in connection with his 
10 service to the town for retirement or pension purposes, and, upon retirement, Steven Perkins 
11 shall receive a superannuation retirement allowance equal to that which he would have been 
12 entitled had he retired at age 65. 

2 of 2 13  SECTION 2. This act shall take effect upon its passage.
