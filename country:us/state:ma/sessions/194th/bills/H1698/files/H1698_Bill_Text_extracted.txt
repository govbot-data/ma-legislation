An Act permitting the adoption of embryos. 
 
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority 
of the same, as follows: 
 
1  Section 1 of chapter 210 of the General Laws, as appearing in the 2012 Official Edition, 
2 is hereby amended by inserting after the first paragraph the following paragraph :— 
3  A person of full age may petition the probate court for the adoption of an embryo. Donors 
4 in creation of the embryo cannot be held financially liable for things including but not limited to 
5 child support and medical bills.
