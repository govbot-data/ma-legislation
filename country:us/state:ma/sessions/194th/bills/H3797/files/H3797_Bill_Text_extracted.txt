An Act providing for a MassPort airline regulation study. 
 
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority 
of the same, as follows: 
 
1  SECTION 1. The Massachusetts Port Authority shall commission a study of the 
2 regulation of airlines doing business in the commonwealth. The study shall examine the possible 
3 legal implications of imposing certain regulations on airlines, including, but not limited to, 
4 requiring airlines to provide lodging, new flights or other compensation to customers in the event 
5 of a flight’s cancellation. 
6  SECTION 2. The Massachusetts Port Authority shall file a report, including its findings 
7 on the possible legal implications of imposing regulations on airlines doing business in the 
8 commonwealth, with the clerks of the house of representatives and the senate and the joint 
9 committee on transportation no later than January 1, 2027.
