1  by adding the following sections:- 
2  "SECTION X. Chapter 32A of the General Laws, as appearing in the 2022 Official 
3 Edition, is hereby amended by adding the following section:- 
4  Section 34. (a) As used in this section, “multi-user breast pump” shall mean a breast 
5 pump that is: (i) capable of initiating and supporting milk supply; (ii) able to be used by multiple 
6 users safely and hygienically; (iii) durable enough to be used by a mother who is expressing 8 
7 times per day or more; (iv) is made for heavy usage with a long motor life; (v) operating using a 
8 closed system; (vi) is capable of suction power from 0 to 300 mmHg; (vii) has a multiphase 
9 system or the ability to customize vacuum and cycling for each user; and (viii) is run on 
10 electricity, not batteries alone. 
11  (b) The commission shall provide to an active or retired employee of the commonwealth 
12 who is insured under the group insurance commission coverage for the cost of the rental and 
13 usage of a multi-user breast pump by the mother of a newborn infant who is receiving care at an 
14 intensive care or special care nursery, or has cardiac, neurological, or oral anomalies that prevent 
15 direct feeding at the breast, until such time as the infant is discharged, or until the mother is no 

2 of 9 16 longer pumping breastmilk. The multi-user breast pump shall be made available to the user 
17 within 24 hours of maternal hospital discharge. 
18  (c) The commission shall provide to an active or retired employee of the commonwealth 
19 who is insured under the group insurance commission, and eligible for a covered multi-user 
20 breast pump under this section, coverage for: (i) periodic replacement parts for the multi-user 
21 breast pump including, but not limited to, connectors, valves, tubing, backflow protectors, and 
22 flanges; (ii) breast milk storage bags; and (iii) microwave bags for sterilizing the multi-user 
23 breast pump parts. 
24  (d) The commission shall provide to an active or retired employee of the commonwealth 
25 who is insured under the group insurance commission, and eligible for a covered multi-user 
26 breast pump under this section, coverage for: (i) an initial assessment, instruction and fitting by 
27 an International Board Certified Lactation Consultant (IBCLC); and (ii) ongoing assessment, 
28 support and refitting as needed by an IBCLC.
