An Act extending the timeframe for filing citizen referendum petitions in the town of 
Bridgewater. 
 
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority 
of the same, as follows: 
 
1  SECTION 1. Article VIII of the charter of the city known as the town of Bridgewater, 
2 which is on file with the state archivist, as provided by section 12 of chapter 43B of the General 
3 Laws, is hereby amended by striking out section 8-2 and inserting in place thereof the following 
4 section:- 
5           Section 8-2. Citizen Referendum Procedures; Referendum Petition; Effect on Final 
6 Passage 
7          If within 30 days after the final passage of a measure, a petition signed by voters 
8 equal in number to at least 5 per cent of the total number of voters and addressed to the town 
9 council, protesting against the measure or any part thereof taking effect, is filed with the elected 
10 town clerk, the same shall thereupon and thereby be suspended from taking effect and the town 
11 council, as the case may be, shall immediately reconsider the measure or part thereof and if such 
12 measure or part thereof is not entirely rescinded, the town council shall submit the same, to a 
13 vote of the voters either at the next regular town election or at a special election which may, in its 

2 of 2 14 discretion, be called for the purpose and the measure or part thereof shall forthwith become null 
15 and void unless a majority of the voters voting on the same at the election vote in favor thereof. 
16  SECTION 2. This act shall take effect upon approval by a majority of the voters of the 
17 city known as the town of Bridgewater, but not otherwise.
