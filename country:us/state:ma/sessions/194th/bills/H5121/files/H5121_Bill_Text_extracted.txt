HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES, February 19, 2026.
The committee on Municipalities and Regional Government, to whom
was referred the joint petition (accompanied by bill, House, No. 4739) of
Joan Meschino (by vote of the town) relative to the operation of the
municipal light plant in the town of Hull, reports recommending that the
accompanying order (House, No. 5121) ought to be adopted.
For the committee,
JACK PATRICK LEWIS.

HOUSE . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . No. 5121
The Commonwealth of Massachusetts
House of Representatives,
1 Ordered, That the committee on Municipalities and Regional Government be authorized
2 to sit during a recess of the General Court to make an investigation and study of House document
3 numbered 4739, relative to the operation of the municipal light plant in the town of Hull.
4 Said committee shall report to the General Court the results of its investigation and study
5 and its recommendations, if any, together with drafts of legislation necessary to carry such
6 recommendations into effect, by filing the same with the Clerk of the House of Representatives
7 on or before December 31, 2026.
