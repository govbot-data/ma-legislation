An Act authorizing the Town of Milford Select Board to appoint a highway surveyor.
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority
of the same, as follows:
1 Section 1. the Select Board of the Town of Milford may appoint a Highway Surveyor for
2 a term of up to five (5) years. Notwithstanding sections 108A and 108C of said chapter 41, and
3 any By-Law adopted under those sections, the Select Board may establish an employment
4 contract with a Highway Surveyor upon the same terms and conditions, and subject to the same
5 limitations as contained within section 108N of said Chapter 41, applicable to other municipal
6 employees as designated in that section.
7 Section 2. This Act shall take effect upon its passage.
