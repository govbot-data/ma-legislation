An Act relative to independent contractors.
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority
of the same, as follows:
1 Section 148B of chapter 149 of the General Laws, as appearing in the 2022 Official
2 Edition, is hereby amended by striking out paragraph (a) and inserting in place thereof the
3 following new paragraph:-
4 (a) For the purpose of this chapter and chapter 151, an individual performing any service,
5 except as authorized under this chapter, shall be considered to be an employee under those
6 chapters unless:- (1) the individual is free from control and direction in connection with the
7 performance of the service, both under his contract for the performance of service and in fact;
8 and (2) the service is performed outside the usual course of the business of the employer; or, (3)
9 the individual is customarily engaged in an independently established trade, occupation,
10 profession or business of the same nature as that involved in the service performed.
