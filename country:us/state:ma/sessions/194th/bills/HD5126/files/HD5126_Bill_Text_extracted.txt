RE: Annual Report on the Cranberry Bog Renovation Tax Credit -
Acts of 2020, Chapter 358, Section 57
September 1, 2025
Dear Committee Chair:
Please accept this letter and the attachment as our annual report for the Massachusetts Cranberry Bog
Renovation Tax Credit program, as required under the Massachusetts Cranberry Tax Credit law
referenced above. The program became fully operational on June 10, 2022, when the regulations took
effect.

The application process for cranberry growers continues to be available for renovations conducted in
calendar years 2024, 2025 and 2026. The application process for the years 2020 through 2022 has
been closed. Therefore, the data in the attached report is only complete for 2020 and 2021, whereas
for 2022 – 2025 we continue with the final processing of project documentation.
We are available to answer any of your questions and look forward to providing a more complete
report for the entire five years of this program, next year.
Thank you for the opportunity to provide this update.
Sincerely,
Rebecca L. Tepper, Secretary

DAR Commissioner, Ashley Randle

Massachusetts Cranberry Bog Renovation Tax Credit Program
September 1, 2025, Annual Report
Rebecca L. Tepper, Secretary
Executive Office of Energy & Environmental Affairs (EEA)
This document serves as the annual report for the Massachusetts Cranberry Bog Renovation Tax Credit
program, as required by Section 57 of Chapter 38 of the Acts of 2020.
Collaboration: We continue to work closely with the following partners to implement this program:
• Commissioner of Revenue and staff
• Commissioner of Agricultural Resources and staff of the Cranberry Bog Renovation grant program
• Cape Cod Cranberry Growers Association
• Conservation Land Tax Credit program staff

While we process these applications, we focus on verifying the documentation of Qualified Renovation
Expenses presented to us by the growers. Once that is complete, we are able to provide the appropriate tax
credit amounts for each.
Please note that taking 25% of the above “Total Requested Qualified Renovation Expenses from
Applications” will not yield an accurate number of the tax credits to be awarded for those years. That is
because we regularly see the expense total reduced by any state or federal grant monies directly received by
the grower for the renovations. Therefore, the final tax credit totals have only been fully determined for
calendar years 2020 and 2021.
As required, EEA submitted in May of this year its annual Transparency Report to the Department of
Revenue. The tax credit transparency provisions require administering agency heads of the refundable
and/or transferable tax credit programs to submit an annual report, by May 15th of each year, to the

Department of Revenue for credits awarded or issued during the previous calendar year(s). The report must
contain the identity of each taxpayer and project (if applicable) that has either been awarded or issued a tax
credit and the date and amount of each such tax credit awarded or issued.
Finally, even though the formal application date has passed for all years, we have indicated to the growers’
association that we are still accepting applications for calendar years 2023 – 2025. The calendar years 2020,
2021 and 2022 are closed. With the application process still open for 2023, 2024 and 2025, we may still add
to the total program count. The calendar 2026 application will be posted on the EEA website next month.
(end of report)
