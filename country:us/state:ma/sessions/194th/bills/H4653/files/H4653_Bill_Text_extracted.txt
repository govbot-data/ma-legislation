_______________
An Act protecting public higher education student information.
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority
of the same, as follows:
1 Chapter 66 of the General Laws is amended by adding at the end a new section:
2 Section 22: Public colleges and universities as defined in Section 5 of Chapter 15A shall
3 not be required to produce records defined as education records in the Family Education Rights
4 and Privacy Act (20 USC 1232g) at 34 CFR § 99.3, including those education records designated
5 by the public colleges and universities as directory information as defined in FERPA at 34 CFR
6 § 99.3. This section shall not apply to requests from federal, state, or municipal agencies.
