By Mr. Keenan, a petition (accompanied by bill, Senate, No. 381) of John F. Keenan, Mark C.
Montigny and Donald R. Berthiaume, Jr. for legislation to limit cell phones in classrooms.
Education.
The Commonwealth of Massachusetts
_______________
In the One Hundred and Ninety-Fourth General Court
(2025-2026)
_______________
An Act limiting cell phones in classrooms.
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority
of the same, as follows:
1 Section 37H of chapter 71 of the General Laws, as appearing in the 2022 Official Edition,
2 is hereby amended by inserting, after the word “department”, in line 10, the following words:- “.
3 Said policies shall prohibit the use of cell phones by students during instructional time, except
4 when expressly directed by a teacher”.
