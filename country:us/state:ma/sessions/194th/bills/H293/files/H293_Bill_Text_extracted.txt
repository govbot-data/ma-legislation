An Act to establish an office of youth development.
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority
of the same, as follows:
1 SECTION 1. Whereas, analysis of current social and health status indicators indicate
2 that an unacceptable number of urban youth are becoming involved in the criminal justice
3 system, experiencing high rates of homicide and suicide, engaged in violence and anti-social
4 behaviors, becoming abusers of drugs, alcohol and tobacco, experiencing a high rate of
5 pregnancies and sexual transmitted disease, and leaving school without marketable skills. There
6 is clear and present danger that the current socio-economic status of this generation of urban
7 youth will have a negative impact on the general well-being of the entire society of the
8 Commonwealth; and
1 of 4

9 Whereas, public schools have historically been in place as the above stated phenomenon
10 developed, and in spite of the many changes implemented and proposed, do not present the
11 model nor infrastructure needed to provide an appropriate model to ameliorate the situation with
12 urban youth; and Whereas, the current negative social, economic and health status indicators for
13 urban youth may be defined as predictors for negative future social and economic outcomes; and
14 Whereas, violent behaviors, both as victim and perpetrators, substance abuse, teen
15 pregnancy and infant mortality, sexual transmitted disease, delinquent behaviors, and suicide are
16 clearly identified as public health concerns,
17 Therefore, Notwithstanding any act to the contrary, this act provides that there shall be
18 established within the Executive Office of Health and Human Services, a state-wide office of
19 youth development within the Department of Public Health which shall organize and implement
20 pilot programs in Boston, Springfield, New Bedford, Fall Rivers, Lawrence, Lowell, Holyoke,
21 and Worcester to demonstrate a coordinated-comprehensive youth development services delivery
22 system to improve and promote good citizenship among youth, between the ages of seven and
23 eighteen. All programs and activities shall be coordinated with the Executive Office of Health
24 and Human Services organizations in each city, so as to optimize the allocation of resources.
25 SECTION 2. Programs and activities shall be provided outside of school hours, year-
26 round, including weekends for individuals who are enrolled in school, with programs and
27 activities for individuals not enrolled in school available on a daily basis.
28 SECTION 3. Programs and activities shall be designed based on youth development
29 research findings, with emphasis directed toward the development and enhancement of social
30 decorum, anti-violent and anti-gang behaviors, positive social behaviors, local and statewide
2 of 4

31 labor pools, job opportunities and career and employment options, citizenship and positive future
32 outlook, community-building, the performing and creative arts, avoiding teen-age pregnancies.
33 With some components modeled after the pre-World War II Work Project Administration
34 (WPA) and Civilian Conservation Corps (CCC) the system shall provide practical skill building
35 beyond the theoretical approach used in public schools.
36 SECTION 4. Programs and activities shall be implemented through contracts with
37 federally qualified, Community Health Centers. Policy-makers and staff of contracting
38 organizations shall reflect the participant population of the program.
39 SECTION 5. The programs and activities shall include but not be limited to the
40 following: (1) a research component to address issues pertaining to replication of successful
41 strategies identified; (2) a community coordination function to assure that service delivery are
42 coordinated in order to limit any unwanted duplication and/or overlapping; (3) to document and
43 analyze resources and services targeted for the participant population; (4) identification of the
44 services needed to advance the goals of the program; (5) develop and/or join-in with a
45 community youth worker certification program, in order to utilize “best practices” in the field of
46 youth development, this component will further enhance skill development and standards for
47 youth development as a discipline; (6) an enhanced management information system support
48 procedure to develop a common intake and referral system to increase the use of preventive
49 services, share resource information, avoid duplication and to allow participants to engage in,
50 hands-on applications and learn state-of-the-art computer use; (7) court diversion; (8) coordinate
51 mini-grants to various community agencies; (9) coordinate with already established
52 organizations and agencies; and (10) develop needed services as required for the success of the
53 program.
3 of 4

54 SECTION 6. There is hereby established and set up on the books of the
55 commonwealth a separate fund to be known as the Office of Youth Development Fund. There
56 shall be credited to say fund an amount sufficient to sustain such an initiative in the immediate
57 fiscal year commonwealth’s budget, and thereafter for a period of five (5) years.The City of
58 Springfield shall be among the first communities within the Commonwealth which the office
59 shall be established.
