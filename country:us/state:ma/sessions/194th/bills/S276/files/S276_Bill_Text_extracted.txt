An Act authorizing the town of Cohasset to issue five additional licenses for the sale of alcohol
to be drunk on the premises.
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority
of the same, as follows:
1 SECTION 1. Notwithstanding section 17 of chapter 138 of the General Laws or any other
2 general or special, rule or regulation to the contrary, the licensing authority of the Town of
3 Cohasset may grant five (5) additional licenses for the sale of alcohol to be drunk on the
4 premises pursuant to section 12 of said chapter 138. A license granted under this section shall be
5 subject to all of said chapter 138 except said section 17.
6 SECTION 2. If the license granted under this section is cancelled, revoked or no longer
7 in use, it shall be returned physically with all legal rights, privileges, and restrictions pertaining
8 thereto, to the licensing authority, which may then grant the license to a new applicant at the
9 same or a different location; or act in relation thereto.
10 SECTION 3. This Act shall take effect upon its passage.
