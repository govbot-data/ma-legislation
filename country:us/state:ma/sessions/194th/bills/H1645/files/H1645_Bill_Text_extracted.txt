An Act to update expungement. 
 
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority 
of the same, as follows: 
 
1  SECTION 1. Chapter 276 of the General Laws, as appearing in the 2022 Official Edition, 
2 is hereby amended by striking out section 100Q and inserting in place thereof the following 
3 section:-  
4  100Q. Unless otherwise provided by law, no person shall make records sealed pursuant to 
5 section 100A, section 100B or section 100C available for inspection in any form by any person. 
6 No person shall make records expunged pursuant to section 100F, section 100G, section 100H or 
7 section 100K available for inspection in any form by any person. 
8  SECTION 2. Section 100T of Chapter 276 of the General Laws, as so appearing, is 
9 hereby amended by striking out, in lines 1 to 2, the words “100A or section 100B” and inserting 
10 in place thereof the following words:- 100A, section 100B or section 100C.
