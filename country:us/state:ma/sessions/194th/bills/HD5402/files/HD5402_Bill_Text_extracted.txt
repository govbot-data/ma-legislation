An Act relative to a student safety education course in schools.
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority
of the same, as follows:
1 SECTION 1. The fifteenth paragraph of section 1I of chapter 69 of the General Laws, as
2 appearing in the 2024 Official Edition, is hereby amended by striking out clauses (l) and (m) and
3 inserting in place thereof the following 3 clauses:-
4 (l) nutrition and wellness programs;
5 (m) genocide education programs; and
6 (n) student safety education course.
7 SECTION 2. Said section 1I of said chapter 69, as so appearing, is hereby further
8 amended by inserting after the fifteenth paragraph the following paragraph:-
9 Annually, not later than December 31, the commissioner, in consultation with school
10 districts, shall provide a report on the progress of the student safety education course in private,
11 public, vocational and charter schools in the commonwealth, kindergarten to grade 12, inclusive,
1 of 3

12 to the chairs of the joint committee on education and the clerks of the house of representatives
13 and the senate. The report shall provide a description of the manner in which the student safety
14 education course is being offered including, but not limited to: (i) the number of hours of
15 instruction offered; (ii) the grade levels at which such instruction is offered; and (iii) the title and
16 description of the course in which such instruction is integrated.
17 SECTION 3. Said chapter 69 is hereby further amended by adding the following section:-
18 Section 40. (a) Every private school and school district shall, for the purpose of educating
19 kindergarten to grade 12, inclusive, provide instruction on student safety education consistent
20 with the content standards framework adopted by the board of elementary and secondary
21 education; provided, however, that a private school and school district may partner with trained
22 local city and town public safety professionals including, but not limited to, a: (i) police officer;
23 (ii) firefighter; (iii) emergency medical technician; (iv) paramedic or (v) other first responder to
24 implement a student safety education course for the purpose of educating kindergarten to grade
25 12, inclusive, students on preparedness for common dangers and emergencies.
26 (b) The student safety education course shall be taught in the month of September of each
27 school year.
28 (c) The student safety education course shall include at least a 1-hour safety
29 demonstration video, presented by, but not limited to, a local police officer, firefighter, and an
30 emergency medical technician or paramedic. The video shall cover, but not be limited to, the
31 subjects of: (i) pedestrian safety; (ii) cycling safety; (iii) how to report emergencies; (iv)
32 preventing child abductions by strangers; (v) saying no to drugs; (vi) how to handle bullying;
33 (vii) what to do if a student comes across a firearm; (viii) fire prevention and simple
2 of 3

34 extinguishing methods; (ix) how to handle a family medical emergency in a student’s presence;
35 (x) how to help another student or themselves when choking; and (xi) any emergency first aid
36 procedures.
37 (c) The department shall assist schools in providing: (i) instruction on student safety by
38 facilitating access to high-quality course materials; and (ii) professional development training,
39 including the provision of trainings, seminars, conferences and materials, for educators and
40 public safety professionals to use in the teaching of student safety.
41 (d) The department may provide trainings, seminars, conferences and materials for
42 educators and public safety professionals to use in the teaching public safety to students in
43 person or through the use of synchronous or asynchronous audio, video, electronic media or
44 other telecommunications technology.
45 (e) The department shall promulgate rules and regulations to implement the student safety
46 education course established in this section.
