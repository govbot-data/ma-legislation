HOUSE . . . . . . . . No. 5337
The Commonwealth of Massachusetts
________________________________________
HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES, April 23, 2026.
The committee on Housing, to whom were referred the petition
(accompanied by bill, House, No. 3922) of Mike Connolly and others
relative to broker fees in the city of Somerville; and the joint petition
(accompanied by bill, House, No. 4099) of Lindsay N. Sabadosa and
Joanne M. Comerford (with the approval of the mayor and city council)
that the city of Northampton be authorized to prohibit landlords and
brokers from requiring broker's commissions to be paid by tenants or
prospective tenants, reports (under the provisions of House Rule 27)
recommending that the accompanying order (House, No. 5337) ought to
be adopted.
For the committee,
RICHARD M. HAGGERTY.
