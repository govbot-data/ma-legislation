14 SECTION 5. Chapter 25A of the General Laws, as so is hereby amended by inserting:
15 Section 22: Residential Solar Consumer Protection
16 As used in this section, the following words shall have the following meanings unless the
17 context clearly requires otherwise:
18 “Solar energy product” an agreement involving the sale of equipment or power to an end
19 use customer that directly involves the installation of photovoltaic equipment or the purchase of
20 power from a photovoltaic system. This is inclusive of, but not limited to, lease agreements on
21 photovoltaic equipment, power purchase agreements involving the installation of photovoltaic
22 equipment, agreements and the direct purchase of photovoltaic equipment. This definition is not
23 inclusive of products or services which provide bill credits as part of a subscription, provided
24 those products or services are part of or participating in a solar energy incentive program
25 managed by the department.
26 The department shall regulate the sale of solar energy products to residential customers in
27 the Commonwealth with the aims of: (i) promoting the deployment and adoption of residential
28 solar energy products (ii) protecting residential customers from exploitative or unreasonable
29 contracts for solar energy products.
30 To further these aims the department shall: (i) create standard disclosure requirements,
31 and any related materials, necessary to assist consumers in understanding solar energy products,
2 of 3

32 (ii) require all residential contracts for solar energy products to be signed on paper, with the
33 customer receiving a paper copy immediately upon signing the contract at the latest; (iii) collect
34 that information which is necessary to reasonably ensure that companies selling solar energy
35 products to residential customers are doing so in a way that is in compliance with state and local
36 laws, including, but not limited to, local permitting requirements and the use of licensed workers
37 in those roles with licensure requirements, and (vi) create a licensing requirement and system for
38 all companies involved in the sale or installation of solar energy products.
39 The department may: (i) level fines against companies marketing or selling solar energy
40 products which fail to comply with the regulations put out by the department, (ii) directly, or
41 through a community organization, provide resources or support for residential customers who
42 have signed a contract for solar energy products which has caused them financial hardship, (iii)
43 regulate aspects of the relationship between third party salespeople marketing solar energy
44 products to residential customers and the companies which higher them to market solar energy
45 products, (iv) set a maximum per kWh price and escalation rate for power purchase agreements
46 for residential customers, (v) ensure that power purchase agreement contracts with residential
47 customers do not supply more power or credits than the residential customer needs, and (vi)
48 undertake other similar actions which are necessary to meet the goals outlined in the first
49 paragraph of this section.
50 Nothing in this section shall be read as interfering with the ability of the Office of the
51 Attorney General to exercise the power granted to it under Chapter 93A.
