HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES, November 17, 2025. 
 The committee on Agriculture and Fisheries, to whom was referred the 
petition (accompanied by resolve, House, No. 113) of Nicholas A. Boldyga 
for an investigation by a special commission relative to the safety of certain 
foods and food additives, reports recommending that the accompanying 
order (House, No. 4731) ought to be adopted [Representative Boldyga of 
Southwick dissents]. 
 
For the committee, 
 
NATALIE M. BLAIS.
