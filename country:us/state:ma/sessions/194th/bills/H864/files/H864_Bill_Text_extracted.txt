An Act relative to mail-in ballots. 
 
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority 
of the same, as follows: 
 
1  Section 45 of chapter 54 of the General Laws, as appearing in the 2022 Official Edition, 
2 is hereby amended by adding the following paragraph:- 
3  An official voting ballot and the accompanying papers shall be mailed postage prepaid 
4 and with return postage guaranteed to each registered voter or, if the voter so requests, delivered 
5 when called for at the office of the clerk, except as provided in subsections (b) and (c) of section 
6 91B.
