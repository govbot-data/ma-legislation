An Act eliminating no cost calls for violent incarcerated individuals. 
 
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority 
of the same, as follows: 
 
1  Chapter 64 of the Acts of 2023 is hereby amended by inserting at the end of section 2 the 
2 following subsection:- 
3  "(d) Notwithstanding any general or special law to the contrary, section 87A of chapter 
4 127 shall not apply to an individual committed to a state correctional facility, state prison, or 
5 county correctional facility who has been found guilty of any of the following crimes: rape, rape 
6 of a child, first degree murder, second degree murder, attempted murder, sexual assault, assault 
7 and battery on second offense, assault and battery with dangerous weapon, arson, stalking, and 
8 armed robbery.”
