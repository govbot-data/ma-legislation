An Act authorizing the Division of Capital Asset Management and Maintenance to convey 
certain parcels of land in the city of Brockton. 
 
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority 
of the same, as follows: 
 
1  SECTION 1.  (a) As used in this section, the following words shall, unless the context 
2 clearly requires otherwise, have the following meanings: 
3  “Affordable housing”, the development of multi-family housing 100 per cent of which 
4 shall be affordable to households with incomes at or below 80 per cent of the area median 
5 income, adjusted for household size. 
6  “Community multi-services center”, a location for the provision of services to the public, 
7 which may include, but shall not be limited to, the distribution of food, meals and clothing and 
8 the provision of childcare and vocational training. 
9  (b)  Notwithstanding sections 32 to 37, inclusive, of chapter 7C of the General Laws or 
10 any other general or special law to the contrary, the commissioner of capital asset management 
11 and maintenance, in consultation with the president of Massasoit Community College, may 
12 convey a certain parcel of land in the city of Brockton described in subsection (c) to Catholic 

2 of 6 13 Charitable Bureau of the Archdiocese of Boston, Inc. for the purposes of affordable housing and 
14 a community multi-services center. The parcel shall be conveyed by deed without warranties or 
15 representations by the commonwealth.  
16  (c) The parcel that may be conveyed by the division of capital asset management and 
17 maintenance pursuant to subsection (b) is more particularly shown as the land bounded by 
18 Beaumont avenue, Quincy street, Cresent street and Burrill avenue, also known as Christo’s way, 
19 on a on a plan of land, on file with the division of capital asset management and maintenance, 
20 entitled “Plan of Land 782 Crescent Street Brockton, Mass.” prepared for the commonwealth of 
21 Massachusetts division of capital asset management and maintenance on behalf of Massasoit 
22 Community College and dated October 4, 2013. The exact location and boundaries of the parcel 
23 shall be determined by the commissioner. 
24  (d) The deed or other instrument conveying the parcel pursuant to subsection (b) shall 
25 provide that the parcel shall be used solely for the purposes of affordable housing and a 
26 community multi-service center. Any such deed or instrument shall include a reversionary clause 
27 that stipulates that if the parcel ceases at any time to be used for those allowed purposes, title to 
28 the parcel shall, at the election of the commonwealth, revert to the commonwealth and the 
29 reversionary clause shall be enforceable notwithstanding the time limit set forth in section 7 of 
30 chapter 184A of the General Laws. The reversionary clause shall require a reasonable notice and 
31 cure period to cure any default. 
32  (e) The consideration for the conveyance authorized in this section shall be $1. 
33  (f)(1) If Catholic Charitable Bureau of the Archdiocese of Boston, Inc. does not complete 
34 a purchase of the parcel pursuant to subsection (b) on or before December 31, 2030, or such time 

3 of 6 35 as mutually agreed to by Catholic Charitable Bureau of the Archdiocese of Boston, Inc. and the 
36 commissioner of capital asset management and maintenance, then, notwithstanding sections 32 
37 to 37, inclusive, of chapter 7C of the General Laws or any other general or special law to the 
38 contrary, the commissioner of capital asset management and maintenance may sell or lease for a 
39 term up to 99 years, including all renewals and extensions, or otherwise grant, convey or transfer 
40 to purchasers or lessees an interest in the property or portions thereof, for such purposes and on 
41 such terms and conditions as the commissioner of capital asset management and maintenance 
42 considers appropriate. The parcel shall be conveyed by deed without warranties or 
43 representations by the commonwealth.  
44  (2) In making any disposition authorized pursuant to paragraph (1), the commissioner of 
45 capital asset management and maintenance shall use appropriate competitive bidding processes 
46 and procedures. Not less than 30 days before the date on which bids, proposals or other offers to 
47 purchase or lease the property or any portion thereof are due, the commissioner of capital asset 
48 management and maintenance shall place a notice in the central register published by the state 
49 secretary pursuant to section 20A of chapter 9 of the General Laws stating the availability of the 
50 property, the nature of the competitive bidding process, including the time, place and manner for 
51 the submission of bids and proposals and the opening of the bids or proposals and such other 
52 information as the commissioner considers relevant.   
53  (3) Before completing any disposition pursuant to this subsection, the commissioner of 
54 capital asset management and maintenance shall commission an independent appraisal prepared 
55 in accordance with the usual and customary professional appraisal practice by a qualified 
56 appraiser. The commissioner of capital asset management and maintenance shall submit any 
57 appraisals to the inspector general for review and comment. The inspector general shall review 

4 of 6 58 and approve any appraisals and the review shall include an examination of the methodology 
59 utilized for the appraisals. The inspector general shall prepare a report of such review and file the 
60 report with the commissioner of capital asset management and maintenance for submission by 
61 the commissioner of capital asset management and maintenance to the house and senate 
62 committees on ways and means and the joint committee on state administration and regulatory 
63 oversight. The commissioner shall submit copies of the appraisals and the inspector general’s 
64 report to the house and senate committees on ways and means and the joint committee on state 
65 administration and regulatory oversight not less than 15 days before the execution of the 
66 instrument effecting any disposition made pursuant to this subsection.  If the consideration for 
67 the parcel to be conveyed is less than the fair market value, the commissioner shall place notice 
68 in the central register of the conveyance, the amount of the transaction and the difference 
69 between the calculated value and the price received.  
70  SECTION 2. (a) Notwithstanding sections 32 to 37, inclusive, of chapter 7C of the 
71 General Laws or any other general or special law to the contrary, the commissioner of capital 
72 asset management and maintenance, in consultation with the president of Massasoit Community 
73 College, may convey certain parcels of land located within the city of Brockton described in 
74 subsection (b) to the city of Brockton for redevelopment purposes. The parcels shall be conveyed 
75 by deed without warranties or representations by the commonwealth.  
76  (b) The parcels that may conveyed by the division of capital asset management and 
77 maintenance pursuant to this section are more particularly shown as “Area 3.818 Acres” on a 
78 plan of land, on file with the division of capital asset management and maintenance, entitled 
79 “Plan of Land in Brockton, Mass. drawn for Christo’s Incorporated” and dated March 23, 1977 

5 of 6 80 and revised February 1, 1996. The exact location and boundaries of any parcel shall be 
81 determined by the commissioner.  
82  (c) Consideration for the conveyance authorized in this section shall be $1. 
83  (d) The city of Brockton may sell or lease the parcels described in this section for 
84 redevelopment. If the city of Brockton sells or leases any portion of the parcels, the net proceeds 
85 from such sale or lease, as determined by the city of Brockton and agreed to by the commissioner 
86 of capital asset management and maintenance, shall be allocated between the city of Brockton 
87 and the commonwealth in equal shares; provided, however, that the commissioner may agree to 
88 reduce the share of the commonwealth’s proceeds to not less than 40 per cent of the net proceeds 
89 to provide certain incentives to the city of Brockton to sell or lease the parcels expeditiously. If 
90 the net proceeds, as so determined, are a negative amount, the commonwealth shall not be 
91 required to make any payments to the city of Brockton.  
92  SECTION 3. Notwithstanding sections 32 to 37, inclusive, of chapter 7C of the General 
93 Laws or any general or special law to the contrary, the commissioner of capital asset 
94 management and maintenance, in consultation with the mayor of the city of Brockton and the 
95 Old Colony Planning Council, established in chapter 332 of the acts of 1967, may, for no 
96 consideration, convey to the city of Brockton any portion of parcels shown on the city of 
97 Brockton assessor’s map as parcels 143-080, 143-079 and 143-081 adjacent to Quincy street in 
98 the city of Brockton that may be necessary for vehicle safety, addition of a turning lane and 
99 improving vehicle access to the entrance to Massasoit Community College. The exact location 
100 and boundaries of the parcels to be conveyed shall be determined by the commissioner of capital 
101 asset management and maintenance.  

6 of 6 102  SECTION 4. The grantee or lessee of any real property disposed of pursuant to this act 
103 shall be responsible for all costs relating to the conveyance, including, but not limited to, 
104 appraisals, surveys, plans, recordings and any other expenses, as shall be deemed necessary by 
105 the commissioner. The grantee or lessee shall acquire the property thereon in its present 
106 condition. Upon the recording of the deed conveying the parcel to a grantee, said grantee shall be 
107 responsible for all costs, liabilities and expenses for its ownership.  
108  SECTION 5. The commonwealth’s share of the net proceeds of the sale or lease of any 
109 parcel, or any portion thereof, pursuant to this act shall be deposited into an expendable trust 
110 established on behalf of Massasoit Community College pursuant to section 6 of chapter 6A of 
111 the General Laws and 801 CMR 50.00. The funds deposited into the trust may be expended for 
112 capital repairs and improvements to facilities on the campus of Massasoit Community College, 
113 including, but not limited to, deferred maintenance, decarbonization or other capital investments, 
114 as determined by the commissioner and the president of Massasoit Community College and 
115 approved by the secretary of administration and finance.
