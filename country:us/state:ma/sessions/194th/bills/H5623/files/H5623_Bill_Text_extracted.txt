An Act relative to educational requirements for class 2 motor vehicle licenses. 
 
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority 
of the same, as follows: 
 
1  SECTION 1. Subsection (c) of section 58 of chapter 140 of the General Laws, as 
2 appearing in the 2024 Official Edition, is hereby amended by adding the following paragraph:- 
3  (9) As used in this paragraph “MIADA” shall mean the Massachusetts Independent 
4 Automobile Dealers Association, Inc. The person shall attend 8 hours of educational training 
5 related to the rules, regulations and laws pertaining to the used car industry to be provided by an 
6 organization certified by MIADA to provide educational training. MIADA shall: (i) establish the 
7 minimum core curriculum to be presented by certified training organizations; (ii) establish a 
8 standardized test, which shall be administered by all certified educational training organizations 
9 to persons attending the educational training; and (iii) consult with the National Independent 
10 Automobile Association in establishing the said core curriculum and standardized test pursuant 
11 to clauses (i) and (ii). Upon an applicant’s successful completion of the training and obtaining a 
12 passing grade, the organization providing the training shall issue a Class 2 education certificate 
13 to the person. The Class 2 education certificate shall be developed by the MIADA and include on 
14 the face a certificate number. All certified training organizations shall record the name and 

2 of 2 15 certificate number of all certificates issued to applicants successfully completing the educational 
16 training. A municipal licensing authority shall not issue or renew a Class 2 license unless a Class 
17 2 education certificate is presented to the licensing authority by the Class 2 licensee applicant. 
18 All Class 2 licensees who have successfully obtained a Class 2 education certificate shall 
19 thereafter attend 4 hours of continuing educational training every 2 years. Class 2 licensees shall 
20 be issued a continuing education certificate which shall also be a condition precedent to 
21 renewing a Class 2 license. 
22  SECTION 2. Beginning July 1, 2027, a municipal licensing authority shall not issue a 
23 new Class 2 license or renew a Class 2 license, pursuant to section 58 of chapter 140 of the 
24 General Laws, that was issued prior to July 1, 2027, unless a Class 2 education certificate is 
25 presented to the licensing authority by the Class 2 licensee applicant.
