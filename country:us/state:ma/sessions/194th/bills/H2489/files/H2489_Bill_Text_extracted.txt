HOUSE . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . No. 2489
By Representatives Luddy of Orleans and Diggs of Barnstable, a petition (accompanied by bill,
House, No. 2489) of Hadley Luddy, Kip A. Diggs and Patrick M. O'Connor relative to including
inflammatory breast cancer awareness in nursing education. Public Health.
The Commonwealth of Massachusetts
_______________
In the One Hundred and Ninety-Fourth General Court
(2025-2026)
_______________
An Act relative to including inflammatory breast cancer awareness in nursing education,
Marnie's Law.
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority
of the same, as follows:
1 SECTION 1. Section 81C of chapter 112 of the General Laws, as appearing in the 2022
2 Official Edition, is hereby amended by inserting after the first sentence the following 2
3 sentences:- An approved school’s curriculum shall include education on inflammatory breast
4 cancer, which shall be developed in consultation with experts on the study, diagnosis and
5 treatment of inflammatory breast cancer. Approved schools may receive funds from private
6 sources for costs related to the inflammatory breast cancer education component.
7 SECTION 2. This act shall be known as “Marnie’s Law.”
