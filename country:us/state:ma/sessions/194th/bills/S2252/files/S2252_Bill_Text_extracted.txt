An Act relative to municipal light plant emergency mutual aid.
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority
of the same, as follows:
1 Section 133 of Chapter 164 of the General Laws as appearing in the 2022 Official Edition
2 is hereby amended by adding in the third paragraph, in line 12, after the word “plant”, the
3 following sentence:-
4 “Any municipal lighting plant providing emergency mutual aid may sell, rent, or lease
5 equipment, fixtures, and goods of any description related to the provision of emergency mutual
6 aid”
7 And said section is further amended by adding after the fifth paragraph the following
8 sentence:-
1 of 2

9 “Any employee of a municipal lighting plant providing emergency mutual aid, shall be
10 covered by the provisions of chapter thirty-two, sections one to twenty-eight, inclusive, as may
11 be amended from time to time, and shall have the same rights and privileges thereunder, as if
12 performing such duties within the scope of his employment including voluntary assignments
13 authorized by the employer.”
14 And said section is further amended by adding in the sixth paragraph, in line 27, after the
15 word “utility”, the following words:- “or its employees”.
