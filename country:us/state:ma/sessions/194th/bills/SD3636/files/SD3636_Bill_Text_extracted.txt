_______________
An Act to get ICE out of families.
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority
of the same, as follows:
1 Chapter 268 of the General Laws, as appearing in the 2024 Official Edition, is hereby
2 amended by adding the following section:-
3 Section 32C. Notwithstanding any general or special law to the contrary, a sworn officer
4 of the United States immigration and customs enforcement agency, or a law enforcement agency
5 or law enforcement officer working in collaboration or under contract with United States
6 immigration and customs enforcement, shall not detain, delay, hold, or otherwise restrict the
7 freedom to travel of any parent or caregiver of minor child without first: (a) submitting a written
8 continuity of care plan for the minor child or children in the parent’s or caregiver’s custody to
9 the department of children and families, in a form and manner to be determined by the
10 department; and (b) receiving a written acknowledgement of receipt and approval of the written
11 continuity of care plan from the department.
