SENATE, June 4, 2026.
The committee on Senate Ways and Means to whom was referred the House Bill relative
to benefits for teachers (House, No. 4361) (also based on Senate, No. 1884); reports,
recommending that the same ought to pass with an amendment striking out all after the enacting
clause and inserting in place thereof the text of Senate document numbered 3109.
For the committee,
Michael J. Rodrigues
