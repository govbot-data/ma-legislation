An Act relative to drug recognition experts.
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority
of the same, as follows:
1 Notwithstanding any general or special law to the contrary the secretary of the executive
2 office of public safety and security shall develop a statewide standardization and accreditation
3 certification for municipal and state police officers to become drug recognition experts for the
4 purpose of providing testimony in court proceedings. The secretary shall submit by December
5 31, 2025 any legislative recommendations to the clerks of the house and senate, and the joint
6 committee on public safety and homeland security.
