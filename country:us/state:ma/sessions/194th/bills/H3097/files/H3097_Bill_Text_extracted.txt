An Act relative to the bundled cell phone taxation.
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority
of the same, as follows:
1 SECTION 1. Section 1 of chapter 64H of the General Laws, as appearing in the 2022
2 Official Edition, is hereby amended by inserting after the word “services.” in line 283 the
3 following new sentence: --
4 In the case of the sale by a vendor of a mobile telecommunications device with mobile
5 telecommunications services, the tax shall be imposed upon the sales price of the mobile
6 telecommunications device.
