An Act relative to court officers injured in service of the state.
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority
of the same, as follows:
1 SECTION 1. The fourth paragraph of section 58 of chapter 30 of the General Laws, as
2 appearing in the 2020 Official Edition, is hereby amended by adding the following sentence:- All
3 amounts payable under this section to a court officer shall be paid at the same times and in the
4 same manner as, and for all purposes shall be deemed to be, the regular compensation of such
5 court officer.
6 SECTION 2. Said section 58 of said chapter 30, as so appearing, is hereby further
7 amended by adding the following paragraph:-
8 Whoever intentionally or negligently injures a court officer for which the court officer is
9 granted a leave without a loss of pay and is paid compensation in accordance with this section
1 of 2

10 shall be liable in tort to the office paying such compensation for all costs incurred by that office
11 in replacing the injured court officer which are in excess of the amount of compensation so paid.
