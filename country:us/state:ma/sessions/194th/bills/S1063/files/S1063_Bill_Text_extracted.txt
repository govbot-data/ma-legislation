80  SECTION 13. There shall be a bail notification special commission established pursuant 
81 to section 2A of chapter 4 of the General Laws, referred to in this section as the commission. The 
82 commission shall evaluate policies and procedures related to the current bail system and 
83 recommend improvements or changes related to notification services, pursuant to section 3(t) of 
84 chapter 258B, for victims and family members of crime, as defined by chapter 258B.  
85  (a) The commission shall consist of no more than 11 members: 1 of whom shall be the 
86 executive director of the Massachusetts office for victim assistance or designee, whom shall 
87 serve as chair; 1 of whom shall be a member of the house of representatives appointed by the 
88 speaker of the house of representatives; 1 of whom shall be a member of the senate appointed by 
89 the president of the senate; 1 of whom shall be the chief justice of the trial court or designee; 1 of 
90 whom shall be the secretary of the executive office of public safety and security or designee; 1 of 
91 whom shall be the commissioner of probation service or designee; 1 of whom shall be the 
92 president of the Massachusetts chiefs of police association or designee; 1 of whom shall be the 
93 president of the Massachusetts sheriffs association or designee; 1 of whom shall be the president 
94 of the Massachusetts district attorneys association or designee; and no more than 2 of whom 
95 shall be crime victims appointed by the victim and witness assistance board.  

6 of 6 96  (b) The commission shall submit its final report to the governor, the house and senate 
97 chairs of the joint committee on the judiciary and the house and senate chairs of the joint 
98 committee on public safety and homeland security not later than December 31, 2026 which shall 
99 include: (i) an evaluation of the current practices relative to bail notification to victims and 
100 family members when an offender is released from custody; (ii) an evaluation of what shall 
101 constitute appropriate notification to victims and family members and the best practice in which 
102 to provide notification (iii) a policy recommendation to ensure appropriate notification services 
103 are provided to victims and family members dependent on the custodial authority in which an 
104 offender is held; (iv) any proposed technology or multi-disciplinary system recommendations to 
105 provide notification services, including contracting with state or non-state entities to develop 
106 implementation plans and estimated costs to the commonwealth; and (v) any additional proposed 
107 policy, regulatory, or statutory changes concerning notification services for victims and family 
108 members within the pretrial system.
