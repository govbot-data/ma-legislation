Re: Facility Inspection – MCI Shirley
Dear Superintendent Lynds:
The Massachusetts Department of Public Health (Department) Division of Environmental Health Regulations and
Standards (EHRS) conducted an inspection of the MCI Shirley on April 23, 24, and 25, 2025, accompanied by Jessica
Caffin, Environmental Health and Safety Officer, in accordance with Department regulations 105 CMR 451.000:
Minimum Health and Sanitation Standards and Inspection Procedures for Correctional Facilities.
The inspection identified 1022 total deficiencies: 182 deficiencies under the Required Standards (.100 and .200 series),
285 repeat deficiencies under the Required Standards, 236 deficiencies under the Recommended Standards (.300
series), 317 repeat deficiencies under the Recommended Standards, and 2 repeat deficiencies under 105 CMR
451.402(B) (other conditions that may constitute a threat to health or safety).
Overview
Section 1 provides details of all deficiencies, including repeat deficiencies, found during the inspection. These
are categorized by Required Standards, Recommended Standards, or additional applicable regulatory standards.
Section 2 provides information on areas that EHRS found to be compliant.
Section 3 documents the areas that EHRS did not inspect.
Section 4 provides information on submitting a Plan of Correction for the identified deficiencies.
Section 5 outlines observations and recommendations related to the inspection.

requests to be kept apprised if inmates return to the Minimum facility.

Re: Plan of Correction – MCI Shirley
Dear Superintendent Lynds:
The Massachusetts Department of Public Health’s, Bureau of Climate and Environmental Health (BCEH),
Division of Environmental Health Regulations and Standards (EHRS) received an incomplete Plan of
Correction from your facility dated August 1, 2025, in response to my inspection on April 23, 24, and 25, 2025.
On September 4, 2025, a response was sent which outlined the deficiency in your POC and detailed the
necessary requirement for acceptance. To date we have not received an updated POC which addresses the
outstanding deficiency. Please submit a revised POC to my attention. As a reminder the POC should include:
1. Specific corrective steps to be taken;
2. A timetable for the corrective action;
3. The date by which correction will be achieved; and
4. Any interim measures being implemented to ensure the health and safety of incarcerated individuals
and facility staff.
Thank you for your prompt attention to this matter, should you have any questions please contact me at the
address listed above.
Sincerely,
Thomas Murphy
Environmental Analyst, EHRS, BCEH
cc: Scott Nugent, Deputy Superintendent of Operations (electronic copy)
