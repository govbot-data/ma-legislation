4  Section 108. There shall be a manufactured housing commission consisting of 5 
5 members: 3 members shall be appointed by the governor, 1 of whom shall be a resident of a 
6 manufactured housing community, 1 of whom shall be an owner of a manufactured housing 
7 community and 1 of whom shall be an attorney authorized to practice law in the commonwealth 
8 with experience in legal issues regarding manufactured housing, landlord-tenant law or real 
9 estate law and shall not be a resident or owner of a manufactured housing community; 1 member 
10 shall be appointed by the attorney general and shall be an attorney authorized to practice law in 

2 of 9 11 the commonwealth with experience in legal issues regarding manufactured housing, landlord 
12 tenant law or real estate law and shall not be a resident or owner of a manufactured housing 
13 community; and 1 member shall be appointed by the director of housing and community 
14 development and shall be an attorney authorized to practice law in the commonwealth with 
15 experience in legal issues regarding manufactured housing, landlord-tenant law or real estate law 
16 and shall not be a resident or owner of a manufactured housing community. The governor shall 
17 not appoint more than 2 members from the same political party. In the event that the governor, 
18 attorney general or director of housing and community development cannot appoint an attorney 
19 with the required experience, the governor, the attorney general or the director of housing and 
20 community development may appoint an attorney with experience in mediation or alternative 
21 dispute resolution programs. The governor shall appoint 2 alternate members, 1 of whom shall be 
22 a resident of a manufactured housing community and 1 of whom shall be an owner of a 
23 manufactured housing community. The alternate member who is a resident of a manufactured 
24 housing community shall not reside in the same community as the member of the commission 
25 who is a resident of a manufactured housing community and the alternate member who is an 
26 owner of a manufactured housing community shall not have an ownership interest in the same 
27 manufactured housing community as the member of the commission who is an owner of a 
28 manufactured housing community. Each member shall serve a term of 2 years and may be 
29 reappointed by the appointing authority upon expiration of the member’s term. A member or 
30 alternate member of the commission may be removed by their appointing authority for neglect of 
31 duty, misconduct, malfeasance or misfeasance after being given written notice and a sufficient 
32 opportunity to be heard. The commission shall annually file with the department of housing and 
33 community development, the attorney general and the clerks of the house and senate a written 

3 of 9 34 report for the preceding fiscal year. The report shall include an accounting of activities 
35 conducted and funds received and expended from the Manufactured Housing Trust Fund 
36 pursuant to section 2ZZZZ of chapter 29. The department of housing and community 
37 development shall make the annual report available online.

7 of 9 122  Section 32T. (a) There shall be a manufactured housing dispute resolution program to 
123 assist manufactured housing communities with resolving disputes. A resident, owner or operator 
124 of a manufactured housing community may file a complaint with the attorney general alleging a 
125 violation of sections 32A to 32S, inclusive, regulations promulgated by the attorney general 
126 pursuant to said sections 32A to 32S, inclusive, or manufactured housing community rules 
127 approved pursuant to paragraph (5) of section 32L. Complaints filed pursuant to this section shall 
128 be in writing and shall include an acknowledgement signed by the party making the complaint 
129 that the party is aware of the dispute resolution program created by this section. 
130  (b) The attorney general shall review all complaints filed pursuant to this section. The 
131 attorney general may take action upon such complaints or refer the complaints to the 
132 manufactured housing commission for resolution. 
133  (c) Upon receipt of a complaint from the attorney general, the commission shall assign 1 
134 of its members to conduct a mediation between the parties involved in the dispute; provided, 
135 however, that commissioners or alternate commissioners appointed pursuant to section 108 of 
136 chapter 6 as residents or owners of a manufactured housing community shall not serve as 
137 mediators. The mediator may gather information that the mediator deems necessary to determine 
138 whether a violation has occurred. After reviewing information from the parties involved in the 
139 dispute, the mediator shall provide recommendations for the resolution of the dispute. If the 
140 parties accept the mediator’s proposal, or subsequently negotiate a settlement, the mediator shall 
141 notify both parties in writing of the terms agreed to by the parties involved in the dispute. If the 
142 parties fail to agree to a resolution of the dispute during the mediation process, 1 or both parties 
143 may request that the commission hear and issue a decision on the dispute pursuant to subsection 
144 (d). The request shall be made in writing not later than 30 days after the close of the mediation. 

8 of 9 145 All requests for a hearing shall include a fee of $25 paid by the party requesting a hearing, 
146 subject to adjustment pursuant to section 3B of chapter 7, which the commission shall deposit 
147 into the Manufactured Housing Trust Fund established by section 2ZZZZ of chapter 29. 
148  (d) A hearing held pursuant to subsection (c) shall be conducted by 3 members of the 
149 manufactured housing commission. One of the 3 members shall be the member who is the 
150 resident of a manufactured housing community and 1 shall be the member who is the owner of a 
151 manufactured housing community. Commission members appointed by the governor pursuant to 
152 section 108 of chapter 6 as either owners or residents, and that own or reside in the manufactured 
153 housing community that is the subject of the complaint, shall be prohibited from participating in 
154 hearings involving that manufactured housing community. When a commission member is so 
155 excluded from hearing a complaint, the corresponding alternate member of the commission shall 
156 replace the affected member and hear the complaint. The third member who hears the complaint 
157 shall not be the member who conducted the mediation of the complaint. Not later than 45 days 
158 after the hearing, the commission shall issue a written decision as to whether a violation has 
159 occurred and shall order appropriate action, if any, to be taken by the owner, resident, operator or 
160 any of the parties. 
161  (e) Costs incurred under this section including, but not limited to, costs to file or defend a 
162 complaint or any money that a party is ordered to pay, shall not be passed on, directly or 
163 indirectly, through a rent increase or otherwise, to a tenant, resident or occupant of the 
164 community. 
165  (f) All manufactured housing commission hearings and judicial review of commission 
166 decisions under this section shall be conducted pursuant to chapter 30A. 

9 of 9 167  (g) The attorney general, upon request of the manufactured housing commission or a 
168  party to the commission hearing or upon its own initiative, may bring an action in a court 
169 of competent jurisdiction to enforce a decision issued by the commission pursuant to subsection 
170 (d). 
171  (h) The manufactured housing commission shall issue written rules and procedures to 
172 carry out its duties under this section. 
173  (i) Nothing in this section shall supersede, preempt or allow the circumvention of any 
174 powers granted to a local rent board established by law to regulate rents, the minimum standards 
175 for use or occupancy of manufactured housing communities or mobile home park 
176 accommodations and the eviction of tenants from those communities or accommodations.

180 individual who installed the home or the company which employs that individual
