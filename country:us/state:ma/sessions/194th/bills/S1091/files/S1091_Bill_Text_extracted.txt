An Act relative to direct food donations.
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority
of the same, as follows:
1 Section 328 of chapter 94 of the General Laws, as appearing in the 2022 Official Edition,
2 is hereby amended by inserting after the first paragraph the following:-
3 No food service entity or retail store who donates food, including open-dated food whose
4 date has passed, directly to an individual without charge or at a charge sufficient only to cover
5 the cost of handling such food, shall be liable for civil damages for any injury arising out of the
6 condition of such food; provided, however, that at the time of donation such food is not
7 misbranded and is not adulterated and has not been manufactured, process, prepared, handled or
8 stored in violation of applicable regulations of the department of public health; and provided,
9 further, that such injury is not the result of negligence, recklessness or intentional misconduct of
10 the donor or any person employed by or under the control of the donor.
