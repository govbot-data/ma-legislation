By Representative Consalvo of Boston, a petition (accompanied by bill, House, No. 3468) of 
Rob Consalvo and Steven George Xiarhos relative to the listing on utility bill of discounts 
available to seniors, veterans or persons with disabilities.  Telecommunications, Utilities and

The Commonwealth of Massachusetts 
 
_______________ 
In the One Hundred and Ninety-Fourth General Court 
(2025-2026) 
_______________ 
 
An Act relative to utility bills. 
 
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority 
of the same, as follows: 
 
1  SECTION 1. Chapter 165 of the General Laws is hereby amended by inserting after 
2 section 11E the following section:-  
3  Section 11F. Any city, town, district or company engaged in supplying water to 
4 customers shall include a list of all billing discounts available to seniors, veterans or persons with 
5 disabilities on every water bill to a customer.  
6  SECTION 2. Section 1D of chapter 164 of the General Laws, as appearing in the 2020 
7 Official Edition, is hereby amended by adding the following paragraph:-  
8  All electric and gas bills sent to a retail customer shall include a list of all billing 
9 discounts available to seniors, veterans or persons with disabilities.  
10  SECTION 3. Chapter 25C of the General Laws is hereby amended by adding the 
11 following section:-  

2 of 2 12  Section 10. All bills for services provided to consumers by companies subject to the 
13 supervision of the department pursuant to section 1 shall include a list of all billing discounts 
14 available to seniors, veterans or persons with disabilities.
