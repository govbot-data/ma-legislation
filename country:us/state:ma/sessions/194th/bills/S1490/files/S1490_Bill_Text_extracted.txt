An Act relative to modernizing the regulation of clinical laboratories.
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority
of the same, as follows:
1 SECTION 1. Section 1. Chapter 111D of the General Laws is hereby amended by
2 striking out section 1 and inserting in place thereof the following section:-
3 Section 1. As used in this chapter, the following words shall, unless the context requires
4 otherwise, have the following meaning:-
5 "CLIA-waived test", a test that the federal Centers for Medicare and Medicaid Services
6 has determined qualifies for a Certificate of Waiver under the federal Clinical Laboratory
7 Improvement Amendments of 1988 (CLIA).
8 ''Clinical Laboratory'', a facility or place, however named, the purpose of which is to
9 make biological, serological, chemical, immuno-hematological, cytological, pathological, or
10 other examinations of materials derived from a human body.
1 of 4

11 ''Commissioner'', the commissioner of public health.
12 ''Company'', a corporation, partnership, limited liability company, limited liability
13 partnership, an association, a trust or and organized group of persons, whether incorporated or
14 not.
15 ''Department'', the department of public health in the executive office of human services.
16 ''Exempt test'', a test designated by the department as a simple laboratory examination or
17 a procedure that has an insignificant risk of error, including but not limited to, CLIA-waived
18 tests. Exempt test also may include tests designated by the department that the federal Centers
19 for Medicare and Medicaid Services has determined qualify for a Certificate of Provider
20 Performed Microscopy under the federal Clinical Laboratory Improvement Amendments of 1988
21 (CLIA).
22 ''Ownership interest'', interests including, but not limited to, any membership, proprietary
23 interest, shares of stock in a corporation, units or other interest in a partnership, bonds,
24 debentures, notes or other equity interest or debt instrument or co-ownership in any form.
25 ''Person'', corporations, societies, associations, partnerships, limited liability companies,
26 limited liability partnerships, trusts, organized group of persons, whether incorporated or not, an
27 individual or the individual's estate upon death, any other entity including, but not limited to,
28 medical practice, medical office, clinic, counseling center, substance use disorder treatment
29 program or sober house or a political subdivision of the commonwealth.

32 the following clause:-
33 “(9) to classify, with the advice of the advisory committee on clinical laboratories,

35 SECTION 4. Section 8 of said chapter 111D of the General Laws, as appearing in the
36 2022 Official Edition, is hereby amended by striking out clause (7) and inserting in place thereof
37 the following clause:-
38 “(7) examine any specimen derived from a human body except upon the written request
39 of a licensed physician or other licensed health care practitioner authorized under chapter 112 to
40 make such a written request or, for the sole purpose of requesting urine drug screening,
41 department-licensed substance abuse programs, state agencies or those vendors that contract with
42 state agencies and are designated by the contracting agency to request such screenings, or other
43 person authorized to use the report of such examination by provision of chapter 112, unless such
44 examination is for the sole purpose of testing the accuracy or sufficiency of the procedures or
45 equipment of a clinical laboratory and is by instruction of the director of such laboratory, or
46 unless such examination is for the purpose of providing a health promotion screening program
47 and is not used for diagnosis or treatment of patients;”
48 SECTION 5. Said section 8 of said chapter 111D, as so appearing, is hereby further
49 amended by striking out clause (11) and inserting in place thereof the following clause:-
