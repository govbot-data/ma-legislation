An Act providing a stipend for first justices and regional administrative justices.
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority
of the same, as follows:
1 Section 4 of chapter 211B of the General Laws, as so appearing, is hereby amended by
2 inserting after the fifth paragraph the following paragraph: -
3
4 "Justices serving as a first justice and justices serving as a regional administrative justice,
5 which titles shall not be deemed a judicial office as comprehended under the provisions of
6 Article 1 of Chapter 111 of Part the Second of the Constitution, shall annually receive from the
7 Commonwealth, in addition to the salary hereinbefore provided, an amount of $2,500."
