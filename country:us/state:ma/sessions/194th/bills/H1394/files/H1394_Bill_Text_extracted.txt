An Act relative to family members serving as caregivers. 
 
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority 
of the same, as follows: 
 
1  SECTION 1. Section 12 of chapter 118E of the General Laws, as appearing in the 2022 
2 Official Edition, is hereby amended by inserting, after paragraph 4, the following paragraph:- 
3  Notwithstanding the provisions of any general or special law to the contrary, any program 
4 of home and community based services funded pursuant to the provisions of this chapter, in 
5 which family members are permitted to serve as caregivers paid by MassHealth, shall include 
6 legally liable family members, including but not limited to biological, adoptive, foster or step 
7 parents, legal guardians, powers of attorney, healthcare powers of attorney, and adult children of 
8 care recipient, within the definition of family member; provided further, the executive office of 
9 health and human services shall file a state plan amendment or waiver application, as may be 
10 required, to allow legally liable family members to serve as caregivers paid by MassHealth.
