The Commonwealth of Massachusetts 
 
_______________ 
In the One Hundred and Ninety-Fourth General Court 
(2025-2026) 
_______________ 
 
An Act providing equal opportunity for residents to vote on establishment of the Great River 
Regional School District. 
 
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority 
of the same, as follows: 
 
1  SECTION 1. Notwithstanding section 15 of chapter 71 of the General Laws or any other 
2 general or special law to the contrary, the towns of Bernardston, Gill, Leyden, Northfield, 
3 Warwick and Montague shall have placed on the ballot at the first annual or special town 
4 election following the effective date of this act a question to the voters of accepting the creation 
5 of a new regional school district.   
6  If a majority of the voters present and voting on said question in the towns of 
7 Bernardston, Gill, Leyden, Northfield, Warwick and Montague shall vote in the affirmative the 
8 proposed regional school district to include all said towns shall be deemed to be established 
9 forthwith; provided, however, that if a majority of the voters of the town of Warwick present and 
10 voting does not vote in the affirmative to approve the proposed regional school district, said 
11 district shall be established to include and consist of the towns of Bernardston, Gill, Leyden, 
12 Northfield and Montague but shall not include or consist of the town of Warwick. 

2 of 2 13  SECTION 2. This act shall take effect upon its passage.
