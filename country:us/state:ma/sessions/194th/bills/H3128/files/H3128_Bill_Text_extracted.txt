 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority 
of the same, as follows: 
 
1  SECTION 1. Subsection (a) of section 6I of chapter 62 of the General Laws, as appearing 
2 in the 2018 Official Edition, is hereby amended by inserting before the definition of 
3 “Commissioner” the following definition:- 
4  “Allocatee” a taxpayer which is (i) the owner of a qualified Massachusetts project or (ii) 
5 has committed to provide funding to a qualified Massachusetts project in an amount 
6 commensurate with the allocation of low-income housing tax credit by the department to such 
7 taxpayer.
