An Act prohibiting child sex dolls.
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority
of the same, as follows:
1 Chapter 272 of the General Laws, as appearing in the 2022 Official Edition, is hereby
2 amended by adding the following section:-
3 Section 108. (a) For the purposes of this section, the term “child sex doll” shall mean an
4 anatomically correct doll, mannequin or robot, with the features of, or with features that
5 resemble those of, a minor, intended for use in sexual acts.
6 (b) Whoever knowingly uses, possesses, buys, sells, transports, delivers or distributes a
7 child sex doll in the commonwealth shall be fined not more than $10,000 or imprisoned not more
8 than 5 years, or both, for the first such offense and shall be fined not more than $10,000 or
9 imprisoned not more than 10 years, or both, for each such offense thereafter.
