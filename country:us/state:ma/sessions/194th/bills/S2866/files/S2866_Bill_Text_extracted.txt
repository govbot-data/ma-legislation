An Act to promote the inclusion of American Sign Language in the Commonwealth’s public
schools, AKA The American Sign Language Inclusion Act.
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority
of the same, as follows:
1 SECTION 1. Section 2B of Chapter 71 of the General Laws, as appearing in the 2020
2 Official Edition is hereby amended by inserting, after the word “society” in line 8 the following:-
3 “As many children with autism have been able to learn and communicate successfully via sign
4 language because it is visually based, unaided, and provides a mode of quick communication.”
5 SECTION 2. Section 2B of Chapter 71 of the General Laws, as appearing in the 2020
6 Official Edition is hereby further amended by striking out the word, in line 8, “may” and
7 inserting in place thereof the following: “shall”.
