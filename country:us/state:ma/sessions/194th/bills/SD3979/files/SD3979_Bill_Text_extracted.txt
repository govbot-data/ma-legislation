Background of Grant Program   
The Sports and Entertainment Events Fund (“the Fund”) was established by Chapter 73 of the Acts of 2025 (“the Act”) to support 
major sports or entertainment events through a competitive grant program. The Fund  is administered by the Massachusetts Office 
of Travel and Tourism (MOTT). The same Act funded the grant program with $10 million to support costs related to the 2026 FIF A 
World Cup.  This is the first round of grants for this new program.  
 
By law, no more than 50% of the balance of th e fund may go to one recipient  in a calendar year . Priorities of the Fund include direct 
and economic impacts on the Commonwealth’s tourism industry, promotion of the Commonwealth in national and international 
media, and geographic equity.  
 
This report is submitted pursuant to the reporting requirement in the Act  to report on expenditures from  line item 1595 -0605 . 
 
Status of Expenditures Through May 31, 2026  
With the FIFA World Cup 2026 ™ kicking off next month, grantees are hard at work planning transportation, public safety, 
celebrations, and more. Almost all grantees are under contract, and expenditures are starting to come in  for reimbursement . 
 
Please note: Round 2  awards  of this grant program were  announced on May 27, using a different funding source  (transfer from 
Executive Office of Administration and Finance reserve fund transfer) : six municipal public safety awards related to the World Cup 
were awarded totaling $1.757  million . A third round of  this  program,  dedicated to public safety support for Round 1 watch parties,  
using $40,000 from a declined Round 1 grant and $243,000 from the A&F reserve transfer, is currently under evaluation  after 
applications were due on May 19 .
