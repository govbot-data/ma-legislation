An Act designating Domestic Workers' Rights Day. 
 
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority 
of the same, as follows: 
 
1  SECTION 1. Chapter 6 of the General Laws is hereby amended by inserting after section 
2 15TTTTT, the following section:— 
3  Section 15UUUUU. The governor shall annually issue a proclamation setting apart the 
4 sixteenth day of June as Domestic Workers’ Rights Day, recommending its observance by the 
5 public to honor all domestic workers who work in private homes and in recognition of 
6 Massachusetts’ historic enactment of basic labor standards in the Domestic Workers’ Bill of 
7 Rights and recommending that said day be observed in an appropriate manner by the people.
