An Act preventing rampant online technological exploitation and criminal trafficking. 
 
 Whereas, The deferred operation of this act would tend to defeat its purpose, which is to 
provide forthwith certain civil and criminal liability for online technological sexual exploitation, 
therefore it is hereby declared to be an emergency law, necessary for the immediate preservation 
of the public health. 
 
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority 
of the same, as follows: 
 
1  SECTION 1. The General Laws are hereby amended by inserting after chapter 93L the 
2 following chapter: 
3  CHAPTER 93M. PROTECTING MINORS FROM HARMFUL PORNOGRAPHIC 
4 MATERIALS 
5  Section 1. As used in this chapter, the following words shall, unless the context clearly 
6 requires otherwise, have the following meanings:- 

2 of 4 7  “Interactive computer service”, any information service, system, or access software 
8 provider that provides or enables computer access by multiple users to a computer server, 
9 including specifically a service or system that provides access to the Internet and such systems 
10 operated or services offered by libraries or educational institutions. 
11  “Material harmful to minors”, any description or representation of nudity, sexual conduct, 
12 sexual excitement, or sadomasochistic abuse when it: (i) appeals to the prurient, shameful or 
13 morbid interest of minors; (ii) is patently offensive to prevailing standards in the adult 
14 community as a whole with respect to what is suitable material for minors; and (iii) is, when 
15 taken as a whole, lacking in serious literary, artistic, political, or scientific value for minors. 
16  “Minor”, a person is who is under 18 years of age. 
17  “Nudity”, a state of undress so as to expose the human genitals, pubic area or buttocks 
18 with less than a full opaque covering, or the showing of the female breast with less than a fully 
19 opaque covering of any portion thereof below the top of the nipple, or the depiction of covered or 
20 uncovered male genitals in a discernibly turgid state. 
21  “Operator”, a person who operates a pornographic internet website. 
22  “Pornographic internet website”, an internet website on which the owner of the internet 
23 website, for commercial gain, knowingly uploads, or allows others to upload, sexually explicit 
24 content that, on an annual basis, exceeds one-third of the contents published on the internet 
25 website. 

3 of 4 26  “Sexual conduct”, actual or explicitly simulated acts of masturbation, sexual intercourse, 
27 or physical contact in an act of apparent sexual stimulation or gratification with a person's 
28 clothed or unclothed genitals, pubic area, buttocks, or, if such be female, breast. 
29  “Sexually explicit content”, visual imagery of an individual or individuals engaging in an 
30 act of masturbation, sexual intercourse, oral copulation, or other overtly sexual conduct that, 
31 taken as a whole, lacks serious literary, artistic, political or scientific value. 
32  Section 2. An operator shall not knowingly allow a user to upload sexually explicit 
33 content to a pornographic internet website unless the user has submitted to the operator: 
34  (i) a statement, signed under penalty of perjury, that any individual depicted in the 
35 sexually explicit content is at least 18 years of age and has consented to the sexually explicit 
36 content being uploaded to the internet; and 
37  (ii) information sufficient to enable the operator to contact the user who has or is 
38 attempting to upload the sexually explicit content. 
39  Section 3. Any operator who knowingly or intentionally publishes or distributes material 
40 harmful to minors on a pornographic internet website shall verify that any person attempting to 
41 access such material harmful to minors is at least 18 years of age, through the use of: (i) a 
42 commercially available database that is regularly used by businesses or governmental entities for 
43 the purpose of age and identity verification; or (ii) another commercially reasonable method of 
44 age and identity verification. 

4 of 4 45  Section 4. (a) An operator who violates section 2 shall be subject to civil liability for 
46 damages resulting from the minor’s depiction in the sexually explicit content, along with 
47 reasonable attorney fees and costs. 
48  (b) An operator who violates section 3 shall be subject to civil liability for damages 
49 resulting from a minor's access to such material harmful to a minor, along with reasonable 
50 attorney fees and costs. 
51  Section 5. Nothing in this chapter shall be construed to impose an obligation or liability 
52 on a provider or user of an interactive computer service on the internet. 
53  SECTION 2. The executive office of public safety and security, the department of 
54 elementary and secondary education and the department of higher education shall collaborate to 
55 create and implement a public awareness campaign to spread awareness of chapter 93M of the 
56 General Laws and the dangers of uploading sexually explicit content unless all persons depicted 
57 in such content are at least 18 years of age and have consented to the content being uploaded to 
58 the internet.
