Resolve further regulating town elections in the town of Canton.
1 Resolved, that there shall be established a special commission to investigate and study
2 the feasibility of synchronizing municipal and statewide elections in the town of Canton. The
3 investigation and study shall include, but not be limited to, examining the process by which the
4 town of Canton may hold the regular annual election of town officials on the first Tuesday
5 following the first Monday of November.
6 The commission shall consist of: the senate and house chairs of the joint committee on
7 election laws, who shall serve as co-chairs; the ranking senate and house minority members of
8 the joint committee on election laws; the town administrator of the town of Canton or a designee;
9 the secretary of the commonwealth or a designee; the executive director of the Massachusetts
10 Municipal Association, Inc.; the president of the Massachusetts Town Clerks Association, Inc.; 1
11 member of the select board of the town of Canton who shall be appointed by the select board; 1
12 resident of the town of Canton who shall be appointed by the select board; and 1 person to be
13 appointed by the governor who shall have subject matter expertise in federal, state and local
14 election policy.
1 of 2

15 The commission shall provide specific recommendations to the general court relative to
16 the manner in which the regular town election in the town of Canton may synchronize with state
17 elections, including ensuring ballot access, single or multiple ballot issues, costs to the
18 commonwealth and the municipality and consistency with any state and local laws.
19 The commission shall report its findings and recommendations, including any legislation
20 necessary to carry out those recommendations, by filing the same with the clerks of the senate
21 and house of representatives and the joint committee on election laws not later than December
22 30, 2026.
