HOUSE . . . . . . . . No. 4790
The Commonwealth of Massachusetts
________________________________________
HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES, February 9, 2026.
The committee on Community Development and Small Businesses, to
whom were referred the petition (accompanied by bill, House, No. 302) of
Bradley H. Jones, Jr., and others relative to housing production plans; the
petition (accompanied by bill, House, No. 303) of Kristin E. Kassner and
Carmine Lawrence Gentile for program to assist cities and towns for the
purpose of creating community development plans; the petition
(accompanied by bill, House, No. 308) of Todd M. Smola for legislation
to establish an industrial mill building revitalization task force (including
members of the General Court) to stimulate the re-development,
rehabilitation and revitalization of industrial mill buildings and
surrounding areas in the Commonwealth; the petition (accompanied by
bill, House, No. 314) of Bud L. Williams for legislation to establish the
Western Massachusetts balanced sustainable development commission;
and the petition (accompanied by bill, House, No. 316) of Steven George
Xiarhos and Donald R. Berthiaume, Jr., for an investigation by a special
committee on Massachusetts business competitiveness and affordable
living, which shall be charged with oversight of matters to improve the
ability of businesses, to form and thrive under law, and to lower the cost of
living for residents and families, reports recommending that the
accompanying order (House, No. 4790) ought to be adopted.
For the committee,
ANDRES X. VARGAS.
