SENATE DOCKET, NO. 2653 FILED ON: 2/10/2025
Senate, February 10, 2025 -- Order (Senator Rodrigues) relative to procedures to be followed
when considering the House Bill making appropriations for fiscal year 2025 to provide for
supplementing certain existing appropriations and for certain other activities and projects
(House, No. 58)
The Commonwealth of Massachusetts
Senate, February 10, 2025.
_______________
In the One Hundred and Ninety-Fourth General Court
(2025-2026)
_______________
1 Ordered, That notwithstanding Senate Rule 7 or any other rule to the contrary, the House
2 Bill making appropriations for fiscal year 2025 to provide for supplementing certain existing
3 appropriations and for certain other activities and projects (House, No. 58) (Mr. Rodrigues
4 having recommended that the bill be amended by striking out all after the enacting clause and
5 inserting in place thereof the text of Senate document numbered 16) shall be placed in the Orders
6 of the Day for a second reading on Wednesday, February 12, 2025.
7 All amendments shall be filed electronically in the office of the Clerk of the Senate by
8 2:00 o'clock P.M., on Tuesday, February 11, 2025. All such amendments shall be second-reading
9 amendments to the proposed new text (Rodrigues) (Senate, No. 16), but further amendments in
10 the third degree to such amendments shall be in order. The Clerk shall further specify the
11 procedure and format for filing all amendments, consistent with this order.

12 After the bill as amended is ordered to a third reading, it shall immediately be read a third
13 time and the question shall then immediately be on passing it to be engrossed, and no
14 amendments shall be in order at the third reading of the bill unless recommended by the
15 committee on Bills in the Third Reading.
