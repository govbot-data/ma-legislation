An Act authorizing the city of Newton to issue pension obligation bonds or notes. 
 
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority 
of the same, as follows: 
 
1  SECTION 1. The city of Newton may issue, from time to time, bonds or notes for the 
2 purpose of funding all or a portion of the unfunded pension liability of the retirement system of 
3 the city.  The proceeds of any such bonds or notes, other than amounts to be applied to issuance 
4 costs, establishment of a pension system stabilization fund and expenses, shall be: (i) paid by the 
5 city to the retirement board of the city allocated solely to reduce the unfunded pension liability to 
6 which the bonds or notes relate; (ii) invested in any investments permitted under chapter 32 of 
7 the General Laws; and (iii) held and expended by the retirement board of the city in accordance 
8 with law. The terms of any such bonds or notes shall not exceed 30 years from the date of 
9 issuance and the amount of any such bonds or notes shall be outside the limit of indebtedness 
10 prescribed in section 10 of chapter 44 of the General Laws. Upon the authorization of the 
11 issuance of pension obligation bonds by the city of Newton, the city shall submit the vote and a 
12 plan demonstrating how the city will finance and allocate the debt service associated with the 
13 bonds or notes to the executive office for administration and finance and no bonds or notes 

2 of 4 14 authorized to be issued by this act shall be issued until the secretary of administration and 
15 finance has approved the plan and the issuance of such bonds or notes. Except as otherwise 
16 provided in this act, such bonds or notes shall be subject to said chapter 44.  
17  SECTION 2. The aggregate principal amount of the bonds or notes that may be issued  
18 under this act shall not exceed the amount which the retirement board of the city of Newton, with 
19 the approval of the city’s chief financial officer, shall determine to be necessary, from time to 
20 time, to be issued to fund the unfunded pension liability of the retirement system of the city as of 
21 a particular date, in addition to the amount determined by the city to be necessary to provide for 
22 issuance costs, establishment of a pension system stabilization fund as described below and any 
23 other expenses necessary or incidental thereto.  Such determination of the retirement board of the 
24 city of the unfunded pension liability shall be based upon the report of a nationally recognized 
25 independent consulting firm, which may be the consulting actuary generally retained by the 
26 retirement board.  Such report shall also set forth the present value savings to the city of Newton 
27 reasonably expected to be achieved as a result of the issuance of such bonds or notes and an 
28 allocation of the unfunded pension liability of the retirement system of the city among each 
29 governmental unit, the employees of which are members of the retirement system.  
30  SECTION 3. The maturities of such bonds or notes shall be scheduled so that the annual 
31 combined payments of principal and interest for each issue shall be as nearly equal as practicable 
32 in the opinion of the city treasurer in a manner that shall provide for a more rapid amortization of 
33 principal or in accordance with any other manner consistent with the city’s approved funding 
34 schedule and approved by the secretary of administration and finance.  In granting the approval, 
35 the secretary may require the establishment of a pension system stabilization fund and the city of 
36 Newton may establish such fund upon such terms and conditions as may be required by the 

3 of 4 37 secretary or, if no such fund is required by the secretary, such fund may be established upon such 
38 terms and conditions as the city shall determine.  A pension system stabilization fund established 
39 pursuant to this act may be funded with proceeds of bonds or notes issued pursuant to this act, 
40 available funds of the city or any combination thereof; provided, however, that such fund shall 
41 be held and controlled by the city and shall be separate from any other reserve or fund of the city 
42 allowed or required by statute. In the case of a pension system stabilization fund required to be 
43 established by the secretary of administration and finance, the secretary shall establish a method 
44 to calculate both the required amount of any annual contribution to the  fund and the minimum 
45 amount to be maintained in the fund and shall prescribe conditions for expenditure from the  
46 fund, including its use if necessary to prevent or limit any future unfunded actuarial pension 
47 liability, and the conditions under which all or a portion of the amounts in the pension system 
48 stabilization fund may be available for unrestricted purposes in which case such funds or 
49 portions thereof shall be transferred to the city treasury. All amounts held in the pension system 
50 stabilization fund shall be trust funds within the meaning of section 54 of chapter 44 of the 
51 General Laws and, except as otherwise provided in this act, shall be subject to the provisions of 
52 said section 54 of said chapter 44 and chapter 75 of the acts of 2007.  
53  SECTION 4.  If the unfunded pension liability to be funded with the proceeds of bonds or 
54 notes issued under this act relates in part to employees of a governmental unit other than the city 
55 of Newton, each such governmental unit shall be responsible for reimbursing the city for such 
56 proportion of the annual debt service expense paid by the city for bonds or notes issued 
57 hereunder as is equal to the proportion of the total unfunded pension liability to be funded with 
58 the proceeds of the bonds or notes as relates to that governmental unit. Notwithstanding any 
59 general or special law to the contrary, the public employee retirement administration commission 

4 of 4 60 shall increase the annual amount to be certified under section 22 of chapter 32 of the General 
61 Laws as the amount necessary to be paid by each such governmental unit other than the city by 
62 each such governmental unit's proportionate share of the annual debt service expense as 
63 determined herein. The city of Newton shall have the same legal rights and authority as the 
64 retirement board of the city to collect any amount so assessed to any such governmental unit.  
65  SECTION 5.  Notwithstanding chapter 70 of the General Laws or any other general or 
66 special law to the contrary, the portion of the annual debt service paid by the city of Newton for 
67 bonds or notes issued under this act applicable to school department personnel who are members 
68 of the city’s retirement system shall be included in the computation of net school spending for 
69 the purposes of said chapter 70 or any other law.  
70  SECTION 6. This act shall take effect upon its passage.
