An Act to support cannabis equity businesses.
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority
of the same, as follows:
1 Section 4 of chapter 94G of the General Laws, is hereby amended by adding the
2 following subsections:-
3 (m) The cannabis control commission, in consultation with the Massachusetts cannabis
4 advisory board, shall create a searchable online database of all operational cannabis dispensaries
5 that allows users to search, filter, and identify economic empowerment applicants, social equity
6 program participants, disadvantaged business enterprises, unionized organizations, and other
7 criteria identified by the commission and advisory board available on the commission's website
8 no later than Dec. 31, 2025; provided, however, that:
1 of 2

9 (i) the database will allow users to view all medical and adult use retail and delivery
10 operators with a license to commence operations;
11 (ii) the database will allow users to see what cities and towns each delivery operator
12 delivers to;
13 (iii) the results provided by the database shall allow users to see a business’s plan to
14 positively impact community disparately harmed by the war on drugs, its diversity plan, and its
15 progress toward those plans;
16 (iv) the results provided by the database will show what percent of sales by a delivery or
17 retail licensee are from products produced by businesses owned by a majority of social equity
18 program participants or economic empowerment applicants; and
19 (v) the results provided by the database will allow users to view all of the owners of the
20 business with the ability to view a record for each owner and see all of the other entities of which
21 they have control;
22 (n) The cannabis control commission, in consultation with the Massachusetts cannabis
23 advisory board shall establish criteria for allowing marijuana establishments and medical
24 marijuana treatment centers to satisfy a portion of their positive impact plan requirement for
25 licensure in part by stocking a no less than twenty five percent of their inventory with products
26 produced by businesses owned by a majority of owners who are economic empowerment
27 applicants or social equity program participants.
