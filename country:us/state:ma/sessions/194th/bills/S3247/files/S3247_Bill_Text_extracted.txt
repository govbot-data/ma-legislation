Resolutions to call on the congressional delegation to support the treaty on the prohibition of 
nuclear weapons. 
 
1  WHEREAS, the existence of more than 12,000 nuclear weapons worldwide poses a grave 
2 and existential threat to the citizens of the commonwealth; and 
3  WHEREAS, the residents of the commonwealth have expressed their deep concern about 
4 this continuing threat over many decades through town meetings and city council resolutions and 
5 the filing of bills and resolutions with the general court; and 
6  WHEREAS, the policies and actions of states may lead the way and inspire important 
7 changes nationally and internationally; and 
8  WHEREAS, the commonwealth played a leading role in ending the nuclear arms race 
9 with the Soviet Union in the 1980s, by adopting a nuclear freeze resolution and encouraging 
10 other states to follow its example; and 
11  WHEREAS, the Treaty on the Prohibition of Nuclear Weapons, which entered into force 
12 as international law on January 22, 2021, has been signed and ratified by 70 countries; and 

2 of 2 13  WHEREAS, the legislatures of California, Oregon and Rhode Island, the Maine state 
14 senate and the New Jersey assembly, and 21 cities and towns in the commonwealth have taken 
15 action to denounce the threat of nuclear war by endorsing the national Back from the Brink 
16 platform, which calls on our federal leaders to embrace the Treaty on the Prohibition of Nuclear 
17 Weapons and to take immediate steps to prevent nuclear war by actively pursuing a verifiable 
18 agreement among nuclear-armed states to eliminate their nuclear arsenals, renouncing the option 
19 of using nuclear weapons first, ending the President of the United States’ sole, unchecked 
20 authority to launch a nuclear attack, taking United States nuclear weapons off hair-trigger alert 
21 and canceling the plan to replace its arsenal with enhanced weapons, mindful of prioritizing 
22 national security; now therefore be it 
23  RESOLVED, that the general court urges all members of the Massachusetts 
24 congressional delegation to co-sponsor House Resolution 317 and Senate Resolution 323, which 
25 call for the United States to adopt the policy provisions of the Back from the Brink platform; and 
26 be it further 
27  RESOLVED, that copies of these resolutions be transmitted forthwith by the clerk of the 
28 Senate to the President and Vice President of the United States, the Speaker and Minority Leader 
29 of the United States House of Representatives, the Majority and Minority Leaders of the United 
30 States Senate, and to each Senator and Representative from the commonwealth in the Congress 
31 of the United States, and to the governor of the commonwealth.
