An Act to promote inclusive curriculum in schools. 
 
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority 
of the same, as follows: 
 
1  SECTION 1. Chapter 29 of the General Laws is hereby amended by inserting after 
2 section 2FFFFFF the following new section:-  
3  Section 2GGGGGG. (a) There shall be established and set up on the books of the 
4 commonwealth a separate fund to be known as the Comprehensive and Inclusive Curriculum 
5 Trust Fund. The fund shall be administered by the commissioner of elementary and secondary 
6 education. The fund shall be credited with: (i) revenue from appropriations or other money 
7 authorized by the general court and specifically designated to be credited to the fund; (ii) interest 
8 earned on such revenues; and (iii) funds from public and private sources such as gifts, grants and 
9 donations to further develop the inclusive curriculum pursuant to section 1U of Chapter 69 and 
10 provide professional development. Amounts credited to the fund shall not be subject to further 
11 appropriation and any money remaining in the fund at the end of a fiscal year shall not revert to 
12 the General Fund.  

2 of 4 13  (b) Amounts credited to the fund may be expended, without further appropriation, by the 
14 commissioner, for purposes related to the instruction of middle and high school students on 
15 inclusive curriculum, including, but not limited to: (i) for the development of curricular materials 
16 related to inclusive curriculum; (ii) for professional development training; (iii) for collaboration 
17 and sharing of best practices among school districts; and (iv) for collaboration with institutions 
18 of higher education and other stakeholder organizations. The commissioner may expend funds 
19 for grant programs and may facilitate the granting of funds and other resources to public schools 
20 and school districts, institutions of higher education, and community groups. 
21  (c) In expending amounts credited to the fund, the commissioner may prioritize: (i) 
22 underserved communities across the commonwealth, including those public schools or school 
23 districts with high concentrations of low-income students; (ii) communities that have 
24 experienced an incident motivated by racial, ethnic, gender identity, or religious bias; or (iii) 
25 public schools or school districts implementing inclusive curriculum for the first time.  
26  (d)  Amounts received from private sources shall be approved by the commissioner of 
27 elementary and secondary education and subject to review before being deposited in the fund to 
28 ensure that pledged funds are not accompanied by conditions, explicit or implicit, that would 
29 improperly shape or otherwise hinder the development and implementation of inclusive 
30 curriculum, or that would be detrimental to the neutral and rigorous teaching of inclusive 
31 curriculum. The review shall be made publicly available on the department’s website.    
32  (e) Annually, not later than December 1, the commissioner shall report to the clerks of 
33 the house of representatives and senate, the joint committee on education and the house and 
34 senate committees on ways and means on the fund's activity. The report shall include, but not be 

3 of 4 35 limited to: (i) the source and amount of funds received; (ii) the amounts distributed and the 
36 purpose of expenditures from the fund; and (iii) anticipated revenue and expenditure projections 
37 for the following year.  
38  SECTION 2. Section 1E of Chapter 69 of the General Laws is hereby amended by adding 
39 the following paragraph:- 
40  Frameworks shall accurately represent and be inclusive of all people so that students 
41 learn about the histories, experiences, perspectives, heritages, and cultures of all Americans to 
42 promote mutual understanding and prepare them to become well-informed citizens. 
43  SECTION 3. Chapter 69 of the General Laws is hereby amended by inserting after 
44 section 1T the following new section:-  
45  Section 1U. (a) Inclusive curriculum shall promote an accurate representation of all 
46 Americans and be inclusive of all people so that students learn about the histories, experiences, 
47 perspectives, heritages, and cultures of all Americans to promote mutual understanding and 
48 prepare them to become well-informed citizens. To ensure a comprehensive education, inclusive 
49 curriculum shall include, but not be limited to: (i) studying literature, books, writings, art, music, 
50 film and other works by people from underrepresented groups that will offer insight into 
51 different perspectives and experiences; (ii) celebrating people from underrepresented groups who 
52 have made notable contributions in math, science, humanities, the arts and other disciplines, and 
53 the impact these contributions have had on society; (iii) learning about the ethnic and cultural 
54 identities, heritage, and customs of underrepresented groups and celebrating their contributions 
55 to American society; (iv) accurate histories and experiences of underrepresented groups, 
56 including, but not limited to, their histories of immigration, assimilation, discrimination, and 

4 of 4 57 contributions within our society; (v) the history of slavery and segregation, and its past and 
58 present impact; (vi) the consequences of colonial settlement, land appropriation, territorial 
59 expansion, and creation of tribal reservations; (vii) the effects of discriminatory and exclusionary 
60 policies and practices; (viii) how historical events and practices relate to current issues affecting 
61 contemporary society; (ix) the identification of bias and stereotypes, and the impact of inequities 
62 and violence towards marginalized groups; and (x) issues related to how marginalization and 
63 exclusion of underrepresented groups affect opportunities for geographic and economic mobility, 
64 power and access, voting rights, and civic participation.  
65  (b) All lessons and teaching related to inclusive curriculum must be appropriate for use 
66 with students of all races, religions, nationalities, genders, gender identities, abilities, 
67 neurological profiles, sexual orientations, and ethnic and cultural backgrounds, as well as 
68 English language learners. 
69  (c) The department shall curate inclusive curriculum materials on their website for use in 
70 public schools and districts. In curating curricula, the commissioner shall solicit and incorporate 
71 input from stakeholders, including, but not limited to, educators, scholars with cultural expertise, 
72 experts in ethnic studies, and students and community members from traditionally 
73 underrepresented cultural and ethnic groups.  
74  (d) The department shall provide professional development opportunities for educators on 
75 inclusive curriculum established pursuant to subsection (a) that supports public schools and 
76 districts implementing inclusive curriculum.
