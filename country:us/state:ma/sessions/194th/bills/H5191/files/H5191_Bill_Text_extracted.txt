An Act validating a conveyance of land and authorizing the recording of a confirmatory deed in 
the city of Marlborough. 
 
 Whereas, The deferred operation of this act would tend to defeat its purpose, which is to 
authorize forthwith the recording of a confirmatory deed by the commonwealth of a certain 
parcel of land in the city of Marlborough, therefore it is hereby declared to be an emergency law, 
necessary for the immediate preservation of the public convenience. 
 
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority 
of the same, as follows:

18  SECTION 2. Said chapter 170 is hereby further amended by adding the following 3 
19 sections:-  
20  SECTION 3. Notwithstanding the absence of a recorded deed or any defect in execution 
21 or delivery, the conveyance of the parcel of land described in section 1 from the commonwealth 
22 to Pierina Monti dated May 22, 1958, is hereby validated, confirmed and ratified and shall be 
23 deemed to have been validly made and effective as of May 22, 1958. Said conveyance shall have 
24 the same force and effect as if a deed had been properly executed, delivered and recorded at the 
25 time of the original transaction, notwithstanding any defects, irregularities or omissions in the 
26 original conveyance or the failure to record the same.    
27  SECTION 4. The Middlesex southern district registry of deeds is hereby authorized and 
28 directed to accept for recording the confirmatory deed authorized in section 1, together with any 
29 supporting documentation relating to the May 22, 1958 conveyance including a copy of the deed 
30 dated May 22, 1958 intending to convey the parcel of land described in subsection (b) of section 

3 of 3 31 1, notwithstanding any requirement of law, rule or regulation that would otherwise prevent such 
32 recording. Upon recording the confirmatory deed and supporting documentation, said 
33 confirmatory deed shall be deemed to relate back to and shall be effective as of May 22, 1958, 
34 and shall serve as conclusive evidence of the commonwealth's conveyance of said parcel to 
35 Pierina Monti and her heirs, successors and assigns.    
36  SECTION 5. Upon the recording of the confirmatory deed authorized herein, the title to 
37 said parcel shall be deemed to have been vested in Pierina Monti as of May 22, 1958, and to 
38 have passed through any successive conveyances or other acts of succession to the current owner 
39 of record, free and clear of any claims by the commonwealth arising from the absence of a 
40 recorded deed for the 1958 conveyance. This act shall be conclusive evidence of the validity of 
41 said conveyance and shall cure any defects in the chain of title arising from the failure to record 
42 the original 1958 conveyance.
