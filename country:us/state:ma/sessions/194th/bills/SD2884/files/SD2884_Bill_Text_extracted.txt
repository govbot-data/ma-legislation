SENATE DOCKET, NO. 2884 FILED ON: 5/7/2025
Senate, May 8, 2025 -- (Filed by Mr. Brownsberger) Order relative to the calling of a joint
session of the two Houses for the purpose of considering proposals for amendments to the
Constitution.
The Commonwealth of Massachusetts
Senate, May 08, 2025.
_______________
In the One Hundred and Ninety-Fourth General Court
(2025-2026)
_______________
1 Ordered, That, conformably to the provisions of Article XLVIII (48), as amended by
2 Article LXXXI (81) of the Amendments to the Constitution, a joint session of the two Houses be
3 held on Wednesday, May 14, 2025, at twelve o’clock noon, for the purpose of considering
4 proposals for amendments to the Constitution.
5 Insofar as applicable, the special rules of procedure, in effect in the preceding General Court
6 shall govern said joint session, and any further joint sessions called for the purpose of
7 considering amendments to the Constitution.
