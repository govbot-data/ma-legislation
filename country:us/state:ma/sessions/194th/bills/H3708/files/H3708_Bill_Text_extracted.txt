By Representative Jones of North Reading (by request), a petition (accompanied by bill, House,
No. 3708) of John Walsh relative to traffic signal pre-emption devices. Transportation.
The Commonwealth of Massachusetts
_______________
In the One Hundred and Ninety-Fourth General Court
(2025-2026)
_______________
An Act to regulate traffic signal pre-emption devices.
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority
of the same, as follows:
1 Amend Chapter 90, section 7E to add a new paragraph 7, as follows.
2 Only persons authorized for the mounting or use of either red or blue lights may use a
3 traffic signal pre-emption device.
4 Possession of a traffic signal pre-emption device by an unauthorized person is prohibited.
