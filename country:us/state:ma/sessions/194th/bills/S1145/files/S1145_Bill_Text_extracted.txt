An Act relative to the definition of strangulation. 
 
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority 
of the same, as follows: 
 
1  Section 15D of Chapter 265, as so appearing in the 2022 Official Edition, is hereby 
2 amended by striking out the words in lines 7 through 9 and inserting in place thereof the 
3 following words:- 
4  “Strangulation”, means intentionally, knowingly, or recklessly impeding the normal 
5 breathing or circulation of the blood of a person by applying pressure to the throat or neck, 
6 regardless of whether that conduct results in any visible injury or whether there is any intent to 
7 kill or protractedly injure the victim.
