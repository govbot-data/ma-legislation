An Act empowering municipalities and local governments.
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority
of the same, as follows:
1 SECTION 1 [18]. Section 91 of chapter 32 of the General Laws, as appearing in the 2022
2 Official Edition, is hereby amended by adding the following subsection:-
3 (f) Notwithstanding the provisions of paragraphs (a) to (d), inclusive, in any period
4 during which there is a critical shortage of qualified applicants for a specific job title or class of
5 job titles, an executive agency, department, county, city, town, district or authority may, upon
6 approval from the secretary of administration and finance, or the secretary’s designee, create one
7 or more critical shortage positions in the job title or class of job titles. The agency, department,
8 county, city, town, district or authority shall submit to the secretary or their designee any
9 documentation that the secretary or their designee shall require to determine the existence of a
10 critical shortage of qualified applicants for a job title or class of job titles. The secretary or the
11 secretary’s designee shall set the maximum number of critical shortage positions that may be
12 created by the requesting entity in the job title or class of job titles.

13 A critical shortage position authorized under this subsection shall exist and be active for a
14 period not to exceed 24 months; provided, however, that the position may be extended with the
15 approval of the secretary, or the secretary’s designee if, at the end of the 24 month period, a
16 critical shortage still exists for the job title or class of job titles. In addition to and
17 notwithstanding subsections (a) to (d), inclusive, or similar provision of any special law, and
18 notwithstanding section 181 of chapter 25 of the acts of 2009, as amended by section 11 of
19 chapter 42 of the acts of 2022, any person who has been retired and who is receiving a pension or
20 retirement allowance under the provisions of this chapter or any other general or special law
21 from the commonwealth, county, city, town, district or authority may, subject to all laws, rules
22 and regulations governing the employment of persons in the commonwealth, county, city, town,
23 district or authority, be employed in a critical shortage position. Provided, however, that any
24 such retired person in a critical shortage position under this subsection shall not be deemed to
25 have resumed active membership in any relevant retirement system and said service shall not be
26 counted as creditable service toward retirement and provided, further, that in the first 6 months
27 immediately following the effective date of retirement, the earnings received by such retired
28 person when added to any pension or retirement allowance they are receiving shall not exceed
29 the salary that is being paid for the position from which they retired or in which their
30 employment was terminated.
31 SECTION 2 [19]. Subsection (k) of section 20 of chapter 32B of the General Laws, as
32 appearing in 2022 Official Edition, is hereby amended by striking out, in line 158, the words
33 “governing boards” and inserting in place thereof the followings words:- governing body.
34 SECTION 3 [83]. Notwithstanding any general or special law to the contrary, there shall
35 be a special commission to investigate and study retiree healthcare and other non-pension
3 of 5

36 benefits. The commission shall consider the range of benefits that are or should be provided as
37 well as the current and anticipated future cost of providing them. The commission shall consider
38 and may make recommendations on how best to divide the costs between the commonwealth and
39 its employees and between the commonwealth’s municipalities and their employees. Upon
40 appropriation of sufficient funds, the commission shall engage professional advisors as needed to
41 accomplish its purposes.
42 The commission shall consist of the following members: 1 of whom shall be the secretary
43 of administration and finance, or the secretary’s designee; 1 of whom shall be the treasurer, or
44 the treasurer’s designee; 1 of whom shall be the executive director of the group insurance
45 commission, or the director’s designee; 1 of whom shall be a private citizen, appointed by the
46 governor, who shall serve as chair of the commission and shall not be a member of any of the
47 105 contributory retirement systems; 2 of whom shall be members of the house of
48 representatives, 1 of whom shall be appointed by the minority leader; 2 of whom shall be
49 members of the senate, 1 of whom shall be appointed by the minority leader; 1 of whom shall be
50 selected by the governor from a list of 3 candidates submitted by the president of the
51 Massachusetts AFL-CIO; 2 of whom shall be members of the Massachusetts Municipal
52 Association, 1 of whom shall represent a rural community; and 1 of whom shall be a member of
53 the Retired State, County and Municipal Employees Association of Massachusetts.
54 The commission shall file a report of its recommendations and proposed legislation, if
55 any, with the clerks of the house and senate, the chairs of the house and senate committee on
56 ways and means and the chairs of the joint committee on public service not later than June 30,
57 2026.
