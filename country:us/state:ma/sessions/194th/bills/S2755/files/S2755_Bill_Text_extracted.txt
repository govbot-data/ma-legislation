An Act relative to debt-free public higher education. 
 
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority 
of the same, as follows: 
 
1  Notwithstanding any general or special law to the contrary, the department of higher 
2 education shall ascertain the necessary steps and cost of providing debt-free public college in the 
3 commonwealth. Debt-free public college shall be defined as (i) allowing all eligible students 
4 with unmet financial need to attend public institutions of higher education without taking on loan 
5 burdens after all sources of grant aid and expected family contribution have been applied; and 
6 (ii) meeting the basic need expenses including, but not limited to, housing, food, child care, 
7 transportation and health care, of eligible low-income students to enable them to take full 
8 advantage of tuition and fee-free college access opportunities.  
9  The department shall issue a report of its findings, including, but not limited to: (i) a 
10 definition of eligible students; (ii) a method of determining the total annual cost of debt-free 
11 college for eligible students at public institutions of higher education in the commonwealth, 
12 which may include conducting a survey of current or prospective students; (iii) a numerical 
13 calculation of the total cost of debt-free college for eligible students at public institutions of 
14 higher education in the commonwealth for not less than each of the next 3 years using current 

2 of 2 15 and estimated enrollment trends and the total annual projected cost to the commonwealth; (iv) an 
16 analysis of which student populations would most benefit from debt-free higher education; and 
17 (vi) recommendations for funding and implementing debt-free higher education, accounting for 
18 federal policy changes and state requirements, and including any statutory or budgetary changes 
19 needed to achieve said recommendations. As part of their work, the department shall consider 
20 best practices from municipalities, states and countries which provide debt-free or similar public 
21 college programs. The department may enter into a contract with a third-party for the purposes of 
22 investigating and completing said report.  
23  The department of higher education shall submit a report of its findings and 
24 recommendations to the clerks of the house and the senate, the house and senate committees on 
25 ways and means and the joint committee on higher education not later than one year following 
26 enactment of this act.
