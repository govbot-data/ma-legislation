An Act enabling cities and towns to extend voting rights in municipal elections to certain 
noncitizens of the Commonwealth. 
 
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority 
of the same, as follows: 
 
1  Chapter 51 of the General Laws is hereby amended by inserting after section 1F the 
2 following section:– 
3    Section 1G.  (a) Sections 1G, inclusive, shall take effect in any city or town upon the 
4 approval by the legislative body and acceptance by the voters of a ballot question as set forth in 
5 this section. 
6   (b)(1)  Notwithstanding the provisions of section one of chapter fifty-one of the general 
7 laws or any other general or special law to the contrary, non-citizens, 18 years of age or older, 
8 residing in a city or town that accepts section 1G, inclusive, may upon application have their 
9 names entered on a list of voters established by the municipal election officials, and may 

2 of 3 10 thereafter vote in any municipal election for school committee, school committee questions, city 
11 council and board of selectmen for so long as they remain domiciled in the municipality. 
12  (2) The election officers of a municipality that accepts the provisions of section 1G, 
13 inclusive, are authorized to formulate regulations, guidelines, and registration forms to 
14 implement the purpose of this act.  The voter registration forms shall include a declaration to be 
15 signed under pains and penalties of perjury by the non-citizen voter that: 1) s/he is residing in the 
16 municipality; 2) s/he intends in good faith to become a U.S. citizen and intends to begin that 
17 process, if eligible. 
18  (3) Nothing in this act shall be construed to confer upon non-citizens the right to vote for 
19 any state or federal office or any state or federal ballot questions. 
20  (c)(1) Upon approval by the legislative body, the action of the body shall be submitted for 
21 acceptance to the voters of a city or town at its next regular municipal or state election. The city 
22 or town clerk or the state secretary shall place it on the ballot in the form of the following 
23 question: 
24    "Shall (city or town) accept section 1G, inclusive, of chapter 51 of the General Laws, as 
25 approved by its legislative body, a summary of which appears below'' 
26    (Set forth here a fair, concise summary and purpose of the law to be acted upon, as 
27 determined by the city solicitor or town counsel.) 
28   (2) If a majority of the voters voting on said question vote in the affirmative, then its 
29 provisions shall take effect in the city or town after 90 days, but not otherwise. 

3 of 3 30  (3) The final date for notifying or filing a petition with the city or town clerk or the state 
31 secretary to place such a question on the ballot shall be 35 days before the city or town election 
32 or 60 days before the state election.
