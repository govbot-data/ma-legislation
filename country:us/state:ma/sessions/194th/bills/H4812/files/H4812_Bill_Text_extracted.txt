An Act relative to restricting telemarketer phone numbers. 
 
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority 
of the same, as follows: 
 
1  Chapter 159C of the General Laws is hereby amended by adding the following new 
2 section:- 
3  Section 15. (a) A telephone solicitor shall not make or cause to be made an unsolicited 
4 telephonic sales call to a consumer using local phone numbers or area codes that are not linked to 
5 their physical location or place of business in the commonwealth. 
6  (b) The attorney general shall enforce and may issue fines to telephone solicitors who 
7 violate this section pursuant to section 8. 
8  (c) The attorney general shall report violations of this section to the advisory group 
9 pursuant to section 14 which shall provide a mechanism for consumers to track complaints. 
10  (d) The office, in consultation with the attorney general, shall promulgate rules and 
11 regulations to implement this section.
