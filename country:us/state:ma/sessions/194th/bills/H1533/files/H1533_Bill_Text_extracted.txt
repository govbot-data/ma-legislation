An Act requiring community meetings before the opening of an emergency overflow shelter. 
 
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority 
of the same, as follows: 
 
1  SECTION 1. Section 30 of chapter 23B of the General Laws, as appearing the 2022 
2 Official Edition, is hereby amended by inserting the following subparagraph:-   
3  (H) At least one month prior to the opening of any overflow emergency shelter site, the 
4 director of the emergency shelter program, or their designee, shall hold at least one community 
5 meeting in the host community related to said emergency overflow shelter site, provided that 
6 notice for the meeting shall be posted on the website for the executive office of housing and 
7 livable communities and a copy of the notice shall be sent to the host community.  
8  SECTION 2. This act shall take effect immediately upon passage.
