An Act controlling and abusive litigation. 
 
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority 
of the same, as follows: 
 
1  SECTION 1. The General Laws are hereby amended by inserting after chapter 209D the 
2 following chapter:- 
3  Chapter 209E. 
4  CONTROLLING AND ABUSIVE LITIGATION PREVENTION 
5  Section 1. As used in this chapter, the following words shall, unless the context clearly 
6 requires otherwise, have the following meanings: 
7  “Abuse”, as defined in section 1 of chapter 209A. 
8  "Controlling and abusive litigation", litigation where the following apply: 
9  (a)(i) The opposing parties have a current or former family or household member 
10 relationship; 

2 of 5 11  (ii) The party who is filing, initiating, advancing or continuing the litigation has been 
12 found by a court to have committed abuse against the other party pursuant to an order entered 
13 under chapters 208, 209, 209A, 209C or 258E; and 
14  (iii) The litigation is being initiated, advanced or continued soley for the purpose of 
15 abusing, harassing, intimidating, threatening or maintaining contact with the other party; and 
16  (b) At least 1 of the following factors apply: 
17  (i) Claims, allegations or other legal contentions made in the litigation are not warranted 
18 by existing law; 
19  (ii) Allegations and other factual contentions made in the litigation are without the 
20 existence of evidentiary support; or 
21  (iii) An issue or issues that are the basis of the litigation have previously been filed in 1 or 
22 more other courts or jurisdictions and the actions have been litigated and disposed of unfavorably 
23 to the party filing, initiating, advancing or continuing the litigation. 
24  "Family or household members", as defined section 1 of chapter 209A 
25  “Harassment”, as defined in section 1 of chapter 258E. 
26  "Litigation", any kind of legal action or proceeding including, but not limited to: 
27  (i) filing a summons, complaint, demand or petition; 
28  (ii) serving a summons, complaint, demand or petition, regardless of whether it has been 
29 filed; 

3 of 5 30  (iii) filing a motion, notice of court date, note for motion docket or order to appear; 
31  (iv) serving a motion, notice of court date or order to appear, regardless of whether it has 
32 been filed or scheduled; 
33  (v) filing a subpoena, subpoena duces tecum, request for interrogatories, request for 
34 production, notice of deposition or other discovery request; or 
35  (vi) serving a subpoena, subpoena duces tecum, request for interrogatories, request for 
36 production, notice of deposition or other discovery request. 
37  "Perpetrator of controlling and abusive litigation", a person who files, initiates, advances 
38 or continues litigation in violation of an order restricting controlling and abusive litigation. 
39  Section 2. (a) A party may request declaratory relief from the court that a perpetrator of 
40 controlling and abusive litigation is a vexatious litigant. The request may be made in any form, 
41 including, but not limited to: 
42  (1) in any answer or response to the litigation being filed, initiated, advanced or 
43 continued; 
44  (2) by motion made at any time during any open or ongoing case; 
45  (3) in an answer or response to any motion or request for an orde 
46  (b) The chief justice of the trial court shall create forms for the motion for order 
47 restricting controlling and abusive litigation and order restricting controlling and abusive 
48 litigation. 

4 of 5 49  (c) The provisions of this section are nonexclusive and shall not affect any other remedy 
50 available. 
51  Section 3. (a) If the court finds by a preponderance of evidence that a party is engaging in 
52 controlling and abusive litigation and that any or all of the motions or actions pending before the 
53 court are controlling and abusive litigation, the litigation shall be dismissed, denied, stricken or 
54 resolved by other disposition. 
55  (b) In addition to dismissal or denial of any pending controlling and abusive litigation 
56 within the jurisdiction of the court, the court shall enter an order restricting controlling and 
57 abusive litigation. The order shall 
58  1. Declare the party to be vexatious litigant and may; 
59  2. Impose sanctions for the abuse of the judicial system and may 
60  3. Require reasonable attorney fees. 
61  (c) If the court finds by a preponderance of the evidence that the litigation does not 
62 constitute controlling and abusive litigation, the court shall enter written findings and the 
63 litigation shall proceed. Nothing in this section or chapter shall be construed as limiting the 
64 court's inherent authority to control the proceedings and litigants before it. 
65  (d) The provisions of this section are nonexclusive and shall not affect any other remedy 
66 available to the person who is protected by the order restricting controlling and abusive litigation 
67 or to the court. 
68  SECTION 4. During any active investigation or pending judicial proceeding involving 
69 allegations of abuse, neglect, or child safety, no party shall unilaterally delay, deny, terminate, or 

5 of 5 70 materially interfere with a child’s existing medical, surgical, or therapeutic care except by court 
71 order or mutual consent. 
72   (c) If the court finds by a preponderance of the evidence that the litigation does not 
73 constitute controlling and abusive litigation, the court shall enter written findings and the 
74 litigation shall proceed. Nothing in this section or chapter shall be construed as limiting the 
75 court's inherent authority to control the proceedings and litigants before it.  
76   (d) The provisions of this section are nonexclusive and shall not affect any other remedy 
77 available to the person who is protected by the order restricting controlling and abusive litigation 
78 or to the court.
