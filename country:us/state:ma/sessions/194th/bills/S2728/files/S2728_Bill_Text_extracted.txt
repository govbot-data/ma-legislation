SENATE, November 17, 2025. 
 The committee on Environment and Natural Resources, to whom was referred the 
petitions (accompanied by bill, Senate, No. 562) of John J. Cronin for legislation relative to right 
of entry to prevent environmental violations; (accompanied by bill, Senate, No. 588) of James B. 
Eldridge, Steven Owens, Mike Connolly, Patricia D. Jehlen and other members of the General 
Court for legislation to create a Climate Adaptation Superfund; (accompanied by bill, Senate, 
No. 593) of Ryan C. Fattman for legislation to reform the toxic use reduction act; (accompanied 
by bill, Senate, No. 634) of Paul W. Mark for legislation to restrict the use of traps for capturing 
furbearing mammals; (accompanied by bill, Senate, No. 645) of Michael O. Moore for 
legislation to prohibit the use of coal tar sealers; (accompanied by bill, Senate, No. 646) of 
Patrick M. O'Connor for legislation to establish the board of registration of hazardous waste site 
cleanup professionals; (accompanied by bill, Senate, No. 649) of Patrick M. O'Connor for 
legislation relative to community preservation funds for seawall repairs; and (accompanied by 
bill, Senate, No. 661) of Bruce E. Tarr for legislation to set NDMA levels, report the 
accompanying Order  (Senate, No. 2728). 
 
For the committee, 
 Rebecca L. Rausch
