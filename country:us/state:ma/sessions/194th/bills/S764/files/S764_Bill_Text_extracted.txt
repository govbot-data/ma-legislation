Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority
of the same, as follows:
1 SECTION 1. Chapter 32A of the General Laws, as appearing in the 2022 Official
2 Edition, is hereby amended by adding at the end the following new section:-
3 Section 31: Reimbursement of costs for medically appropriate evaluation and
4 management services in outpatient settings, including but not limited to office-based and
5 hospital-based clinics, in accordance with guidelines developed by the division of insurance,
6 shall be part of a basic benefits package offered by the insurer or a third party and shall not
7 require a co-payment or deductible; provided, however, that co-payments and deductibles shall
8 be required if the applicable plan is governed by the Federal Internal Revenue Code and would
9 lose its tax-exempt status as a result of the prohibition on such for these services.
