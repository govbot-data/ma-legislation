HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES, May 11, 2026. 
 The committee on Veterans and Federal Affairs, to whom was referred 
the petition (accompanied by bill, House, No. 3836) of David F. DeCoste 
and Patrick Joseph Kearney for legislation to establish a disabled veteran 
homestead exemption, reports recommending that the accompanying order 
(House, No. 5395) ought to be adopted. 
 
For the committee, 
 
JOSEPH W. MCGONAGLE, JR.
