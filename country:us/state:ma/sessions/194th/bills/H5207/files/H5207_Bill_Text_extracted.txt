HOUSE . . . . . . . . No. 5207
The Commonwealth of Massachusetts
________________________________________
HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES, March 12, 2026.
The committee on Financial Services, to whom were referred the
petition (accompanied by bill, House, No. 1149) of Rodney M. Elliott
relative to requiring dental insurance to cover certain scans; the petition
(accompanied by bill, House, No. 1159) of Dennis C. Gallagher, David T.
Vieira and Alyson M. Sullivan-Almeida relative to insurance payments for
use of ambulance services; the petition (accompanied by bill, House, No.
1174) of Homar Gómez relative to direct dental care agreements; the
petition (accompanied by bill, House, No. 1179) of Carlos González relative
to access to universal dental care; the petition (accompanied by bill, House,
No. 1204) of Bradley H. Jones, Jr., and others for legislation to establish
pregnancy as a qualifying event for eligibility for special enrollment in
certain health plans in the state health insurance exchange; the petition
(accompanied by bill, House, No. 1262) of Paul McMurtry relative to dental
insurance; the petition (accompanied by bill, House, No. 1290) of Angelo
J. Puppolo, Jr., and Bud L. Williams relative to dental insurance coverage
of periodontal treatments; the petition (accompanied by bill, House, No.
3986) of Kip A. Diggs relative to patient discharges; and the petition
(accompanied by bill, House, No. 4013) of Carmine Lawrence Gentile,
Mike Connolly and James B. Eldridge relative to for-profit health care and
insurance, reports recommending that the accompanying order (House, No.
5207) ought to be adopted.
For the committee,
JAMES M. MURPHY.
