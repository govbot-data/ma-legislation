An Act to promote healthy alternatives to sugary drinks. 
 
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority 
of the same, as follows: 
 
1  SECTION 1. Chapter 29 of the General Laws is hereby amended by inserting after 
2 section 2MMMMMM the following section:-   
3  Section 2LLLLLL. HEALTH PROMOTION FUND 
4  There shall be established and set up on the books of the commonwealth a separate fund 
5 to be known as the Commonwealth’s Health Promotion Fund. The department of public health 
6 shall administer the fund. The commissioner shall make expenditures from the fund for the 
7 purposes of benefits, services and programs for communities most impacted by health inequity 
8 and burdened by health outcomes, including, but not limited to, obesity, diabetes, and heart 
9 disease. 
10  The department of public health shall annually report on its strategy for administration 
11 and allocation of the fund. The report shall set forth the rationale for such strategy, including, but 
12 not limited to: (1) a list of the most prevalent preventable health conditions in the 
13 commonwealth, including, but not limited to, health disparities experienced by populations based 

2 of 7 14 on race, ethnicity, gender, disability status, sexual orientation or socio-economic status; (2) a list 
15 of the most costly preventable health conditions; (3) a list of evidence-based or promising 
16 community-based programs related to the conditions identified. The report shall recommend 
17 specific areas of focus for allocation of funds. 
18  SECTION 2. Section 16 of chapter 62C, as appearing in the 2022 Official Edition, is 
19 hereby amended by inserting after subsection (n) the following subsection:- 
20  (m) A distributor under chapter 64O shall, on or before the twentieth day of each calendar 
21 month or on or before the twentieth day of the month following each calendar quarter, as the 
22 commissioner shall require, file with the commissioner a return for each place of business 
23 maintained, stating the quantity of products subject to the excise tax imposed under section 2 of 
24 chapter 64O sold by the distributor in the commonwealth during the preceding calendar month or 
25 quarter, as the case may be, and such return shall contain or be accompanied by such further 
26 information as the commissioner shall require. If a licensee ceases to sell products subject to the 
27 excise tax imposed under section 2 of chapter 64O within the commonwealth, the distributor 
28 shall immediately file with the commissioner a return for the period ending with such cessation. 
29  SECTION 3. The General Laws are hereby amended by inserting after chapter 64N the 
30 following chapter:-  
31  Chapter 64O. SUGARY DRINK TAX 
32  Section 1. Definitions. As used in this chapter, the following words shall, unless the 
33 context clearly requires otherwise, have the following meanings: 

3 of 7 34  (1) "Beverage for medical use”, (A) a beverage suitable for human consumption and 
35 manufactured for use as an oral nutritional therapy for persons who cannot absorb or metabolize 
36 dietary nutrients from food or beverages, or for use as an oral rehydration electrolyte solution for 
37 infants and children formulated to prevent or treat dehydration due to illness; (B) a “medical 
38 food” as defined in section 5(b)(3) of the Orphan Drug Act (21 U.S.C. 360ee(b)(3); provided, 
39 however, that this definition shall  not include sports drinks or any substantially similar to drinks.  
40  (2) “Bottle", a closed or sealed container regardless of size or shape, including, without 
41 limitation, those made of glass, metal, paper, plastic or any other material or combination of 
42 materials. 
43  (3) "Bottled sugary drink", a sugary drink contained in a bottle that is ready for 
44 consumption without further processing, including, but not limited to, dilution or carbonation. 
45  (4) “Commissioner”, the commissioner of the department of revenue. 
46  (5) "Consumer", a person who purchases a sugary drink for consumption and not for sale 
47 to another. 
48  (6) "Department”, the department of revenue.  
49  (7) "Distributor", a person, including, but not limited to, manufacturers and wholesale 
50 dealers who receive, store, manufacture, bottle or distribute bottled sugary drinks, syrups or 
51 powders, for sale to retailers doing business in the commonwealth whether or not that person 
52 also sells such products to consumers. 
53  (8) “Medical food,” a food which is formulated to be consumed or administered enterally 
54 under the supervision of a physician and which is intended for the specific dietary management 

4 of 7 55 of a disease or condition for which distinctive nutritional requirements, based on recognized 
56 scientific principles, are established by medical evaluation. 
57  (9) “Milk”, a natural liquid milk regardless of animal or plant source or butterfat content; 
58 natural milk concentrate, whether or not reconstituted; or dehydrated natural milk, whether or not 
59 reconstituted. 
60  (10) "Natural fruit juice”, an original liquid resulting from the pressing of fruits, or the 
61 liquid resulting from the dilution with water of dehydrated natural fruit juice. 
62  (11) “Natural vegetable juice", a original liquid resulting from the pressing of vegetables, 
63 or the liquid resulting from the dilution with water of dehydrated natural vegetable juice. 
64  (12) "Nonalcoholic beverage", a beverage that contains less than one-half of one percent 
65 alcohol per volume. 
66  (13) “Non-nutritive sweetener", a non-nutritive substance with fewer than 5 calories per 
67 serving suitable for human consumption that humans perceive as sweet and including, but not 
68 limited to,  aspartame, acesulfame-K, neotame, saccharin, sucralose and stevia; provided, 
69 however, that a non-nutritive sweetener shall not include sugars.  
70  (14) "Place of business", a place where sugary drinks, syrups or powders are 
71 manufactured or received for sale in the commonwealth. 
72  (1) "Powder", a solid mixture of ingredients used in making, mixing or compounding 
73 sugary drinks by mixing the powder with one or more other ingredients, including, but not 
74 limited to, water, ice, syrup, simple syrup, fruits, vegetables, fruit juice, vegetable juice, 
75 carbonation or other gas. 

5 of 7 76  (16) "Retailer", a person who sells or otherwise dispenses in the commonwealth a sugary 
77 drink to a consumer whether or not that person is also a distributor.  
78  (17) "Sale”, a transfer of title or possession for valuable consideration regardless of the 
79 manner by which the transfer is completed. 
80  (18) “Sugars”, a monosaccharide or disaccharide nutritive sweetener such as glucose, 
81 fructose, lactose, and sucrose, including, but not limited to, cane sugar, beet sugar, high-fructose 
82 corn syrup, honey, fruit juice concentrate, and other caloric sweeteners. For purposes of this 
83 definition, "nutritive" means a substance that contains 5 or more calories per serving. 
84  (19) "Sugary drink" a nonalcoholic beverage, carbonated or noncarbonated, which is 
85 intended for human consumption and contains added sugars.  
86  (20) "Syrup", a liquid mixture of ingredients used in making, mixing, or compounding 
87 sugary drinks using one or more other ingredients including, but not limited to, water, ice, a 
88 powder, simple syrup, fruits, vegetables, fruit juice, vegetable juice, carbonation or other gas. 
89  (21) “Water”, a no-calorie liquid water, which is either non-flavored or flavored without 
90 the use of sugars, including, but not limited to, carbonated water, club soda or seltzer that is 
91 distilled or purified. 
92  Section 2.  
93  (a) A distributor who is required to file a return under section 16 of chapter 62C shall, at 
94 the time of filing such return, pay to the commissioner: (1) one cent per ounce for the sale of a 
95 sugary drink to a retailer of a sugary drink with no less than 7.5 grams of sugars per 12 fluid 
96 ounces and no more than 30 grams of sugars per 12 fluid ounces  and: (2) two cents per ounce 

6 of 7 97 for the sale of a sugary drink to a retailer with more than 30 grams of sugars per 12 fluid ounces; 
98 provided, however, that a distributor who also functions as a retailer shall set aside the collected 
99 funds on or before the time when the sugary drink is placed for sale to the public.    
100  (b) A distributor who is required to file a return under section 16 of chapter 62C shall, at 
101 the time of filing such return, pay to the commissioner: (1) one cent per ounce for the sale to a 
102 retailer of syrup or powder with no less than 7.5 grams of sugars per 12 fluid ounces and no more 
103 than 30 grams of sugars per 12 fluid ounces  and: (2) two cents per ounce for the sale of syrup or 
104 powder with more than 30 grams of sugars per 12 fluid ounces provided, however, that a 
105 distributor who also functions as a retailer shall set aside the collected funds on or before the 
106 time when the sugary drink is placed for sale to the public.    
107  Section 3: Exemptions 
108  This chapter shall not apply to the sale of:   
109  (a) Bottled sugary drinks, syrups, and powders sold to the United States Government and 
110 American Indian Tribal Governments; 
111  (b) Bottled sugary drinks, syrups, and powders sold by a distributor to another distributor 
112 that holds a permit issued pursuant to this chapter if the sales invoice clearly indicates that the 
113 sale is exempt. If the sale is to a person who is both a distributor and a retailer, the sale shall also 
114 be tax exempt and the tax shall be paid when the purchasing distributor or retailer resells the 
115 product to a retailer or a consumer. This exemption does not apply to any other sale to a retailer; 
116  (c) Beverages sweetened solely with non-nutritive sweeteners; 

7 of 7 117  (d) Beverages consisting of 100 per cent natural fruit or vegetable juice with no added 
118 sugars; 
119  (e) Beverages in which milk, or soy, rice or similar milk substitute is the primary 
120 ingredient or the first listed ingredient on the label of the beverage; 
121  (f) Coffee or tea without added sugars; 
122  (g) Infant formula; 
123  (h) Beverages for medical use; 
124  (i) Water without added sugars; 
125  (j) Unsweetened drinks to which a purchaser can add, or can request that a seller add, 
126 sugar or a sweetener at the point of sale. 
127  Section 4: Application of Tax Revenue  
128  The commissioner shall deposit revenue collected pursuant to this chapter as follows: (a) 
129 40 per cent in the Commonwealth Care Trust Fund, established in section 2OOO of chapter 29 
130 and (b) 20 per cent in the medical assistance trust fund established in section 2QQQ of chapter 
131 29; and (c) 40 per cent in the health promotion fund established in section 2LLLLLL of chapter 
132 29.
