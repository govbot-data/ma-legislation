An Act establishing safety standards for motorcross tracks. 
 
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority 
of the same, as follows: 
 
1  SECTION 1. This act shall commonly be known as "Colby's Law". 
2  SECTION 2. Chapter 143 of the General Laws, as appearing in the 2024 Official Edition, 
3 is hereby amended by inserting at the end thereof the following new sections:-- 
4  “Section 102.  
5  For the purposes of this act, the following terms shall have the following meanings:- 
6  “Motocross track” refers to a facility designed for off-road motorcycle racing. 
7  “Operator” refers to any person, organization, or entity responsible for managing or 
8 overseeing the operation of a motocross track. 
9  “Participant” refers to any individual who actively engages in riding, observing, or 
10 competing on the track. 

2 of 5 11  Section 103. (a) All motocross tracks operating in Massachusetts shall be registered with 
12 the division. 
13  (b) Annual permits shall be required for continued operation and shall be issued 
14 following inspection by the division to ensure compliance with safety standards established by 
15 this act. 
16  (c) The division shall promulgate regulations to govern the implementation of sections 
17 102 through 111, inclusive, including the development of minimum safety requirements for 
18 tracks, track employees, observers, and for riders. 
19  Section 104. (a) Tracks shall meet minimum design and maintenance standards, including 
20 but not limited to the adoption and implementation of emergency medical response plans.  
21  Section 105. (a) Riders and participants must wear appropriate protective equipment. 
22  (b) Tracks must conduct safety briefings for new participants. 
23  Section 106. All operators must maintain liability insurance to cover injuries or damages 
24 occurring on the premises. 
25  Section 107. (a) Riders under 18 years of age must have written parental or guardian 
26 consent to participate. 
27  (b) Special safety measures, such as speed limitations and restricted access to advanced 
28 courses, shall be implemented for youth riders. 

3 of 5 29  Section 108. (a) The division and the office of law enforcement within the executive 
30 office of energy and environmental affairs shall have the authority to enforce this act through 
31 inspections, fines, or suspension of operating permits for non-compliance. 
32  (b) Violations of this act may result in fines ranging from $500 to $5,000 depending on 
33 the severity of the infraction. 
34  Section 109. The division shall collaborate with the office of law enforcement within the 
35 executive office of energy and environmental affairs to develop and implement a statewide 
36 public awareness campaign about track safety, including educational materials and training 
37 programs. 
38  Section 110. Each application for a permit under Section 103 of this chapter, or for a 
39 renewal thereof, shall be accompanied by a fee. The amount of such fees as well as such fees as 
40 the division by regulation may prescribe, shall be determined annually by the commissioner of 
41 administration under the provision of section three B of chapter seven for the filing thereof.   
42  Section 111. The division shall implement and administer a grant program for the 
43 purposes of supporting capital improvements at motocross tracks to improve rider safety. 
44  SECTION 3. Section 23 of Chapter 21A of the Massachusetts General Laws, as so 
45 appearing, is hereby amended by striking the section in its entirety and inserting in place thereof 
46 the following:-- 
47  “Section 23. There is hereby established within the executive office of energy and 
48 environmental affairs a state off-highway vehicle advisory committee. The committee shall 
49 advise the secretary and the department of conservation and recreation, the department of fish 

4 of 5 50 and game, the department of environmental protection, the office of environmental law 
51 enforcement, and the division on public safety on matters involving the commonwealth's 
52 regulation of off-highway vehicles, as defined in section 20 of chapter 90B, including the 
53 development and enforcement of state regulations and policies, safety and training programs and 
54 the distribution of available state funding. The committee shall consist of 19 members to be 
55 appointed by the secretary, 3 of whom shall be representatives of off-highway vehicle users, 1 of 
56 whom shall be a representative of the New England Trail Rider Association; 1 of whom shall be 
57 a representative of the Specialty Vehicle Industry Association and the Massachusetts Powersport 
58 Dealers Association; 1 of whom shall be a representative of the department of public health; 1 of 
59 whom shall be a representative of the division of public safety; 1 of whom shall be a 
60 representative of child safety advocates associated with the safe use of off-highway vehicles; 1 of 
61 whom shall be a representative of the Snowmobile Association of Massachusetts, Inc.; 2 of 
62 whom shall be representatives of a nonprofit organization that owns and manages land open to 
63 the public; 1 of whom shall be a representative of an association or organization of large private 
64 land owners; 1 of whom shall be a member of a friends group of the Massachusetts Forest and 
65 Parks Network; 1 of whom shall be representative of a motocross track owner; 1 of whom shall 
66 be representative of a motocross track rider; and 5 of whom shall be representatives of state or 
67 local law enforcement authorities. Members shall serve without compensation. Members shall be 
68 appointed for terms of 3 years.The committee shall select a chair annually by a majority vote of 
69 the members. The committee shall meet at least twice each year and shall also meet at the request 
70 of the secretary or the committee chair.
