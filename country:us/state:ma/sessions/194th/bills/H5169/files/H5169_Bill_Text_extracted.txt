HOUSE . . . . . . . . No. 5169
The Commonwealth of Massachusetts
________________________________________
HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES, March 2, 2026.
The committee on State Administration and Regulatory Oversight, to
whom were referred the petition (accompanied by bill, House, No. 3355) of
Steven S. Howitt relative to the reasons for debarment from the consolidated
list of contractors for public contracts; the petition (accompanied by bill,
House, No. 3356) of Steven S. Howitt relative to information provided to
relevant state agencies upon the submission of applications for state grant
funding; the petition (accompanied by bill, House, No. 3362) of Bradley H.
Jones, Jr. and others for legislation to require disclosure of changes to health
care and insurance regulations by certain state agencies; the petition
(accompanied by bill, House, No. 3378) of David Henry Argosky LeBoeuf
and John R. Gaskey for legislation to simplify the language used on the state
website by executive offices and agencies; and the petition (accompanied
by bill, House, No. 3892) of Jeanne M. Kempthorne and others for
legislation to establish the offices of legislative research and fiscal analysis,
reports recommending that the accompanying order (House, No. 5169)
ought to be adopted.
For the committee,
ANTONIO F. D. CABRAL.
