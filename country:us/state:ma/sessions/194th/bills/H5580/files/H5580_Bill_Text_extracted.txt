HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES, July 16, 2026.
The committee on Financial Services, to whom was referred the
petition (accompanied by bill, House, No. 5477) of Steven Ultrino relative
to the treatment of cleft palate and cleft lip, reports recommending that the
accompanying order (House, No. 5580) ought to be adopted.
For the committee,
JAMES M. MURPHY.

The Commonwealth of Massachusetts

2 the General Court to make an investigation and study of House document numbered 5477,
3 relative to expanding the treatment of cleft palate and cleft lip.
4 Said committee shall report to the General Court the results of its investigation and study
5 and its recommendations, if any, together with drafts of legislation necessary to carry such
6 recommendations into effect, by filing the same with the Clerk of the House of Representatives
7 on or before December 31, 2026.
