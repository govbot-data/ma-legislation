An Act authorizing the city of Cambridge to allow for a personal property tax exemption of
$30,000 or less.
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority
of the same, as follows:
1 SECTION 1. Notwithstanding clause Fifty-fourth of section 5 of chapter 59 of the
2 General Laws or any other general or special law, rule or regulation to the contrary, the city of
3 Cambridge shall be permitted to allow for exemption from taxation of personal property not in
4 excess of $30,000 of value.
5 SECTION 2. This act shall take effect upon its passage.
