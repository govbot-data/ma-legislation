of the same, as follows:

82 SECTION 6. Chapter 112 of the General Laws, as so appearing, is hereby amended by
83 adding the following 12 sections:-
84 Section 290. As used in sections 291 to 301, inclusive, the following words shall
85 have the following meanings unless the context clearly requires otherwise:
86 “Board”, the board of registration of commercial interior designers established in
87 section 110 of chapter 13.
88 "Building", an enclosed structure that has human occupancy or habitation as its
89 principal purpose as defined in the state building code.
90 “Certificate of registration”, the 2-year license to practice commercial interior
91 design issued by the board.
92 “Commercial interior design”, the design and planning of interior spaces in
93 buildings subject to the 780 CMR state building code.
94 "International Building Code", the edition of the International Building Code,
95 issued by the International Code Council, as most recently adopted by the commonwealth,
96 including Massachusetts amendments.
97 “Nonstructural interior construction”, non-seismic or non-structural construction
98 elements within a building or structure which does not require structural bracing, does not affect
5 of 15

99 the structural integrity of the building, is primarily concerned with the interior aspects of design,
100 and does not include any load-bearing components.
101 “Practice of registered commercial interior design", in relation to performing or
102 agreeing to perform or holding one’s self out as able to perform professional services in
103 connection with obtaining a building permit independent of an architect licensed under sections
104 60A to 60Q, inclusive, for the design and construction of nonstructural interior construction,
105 including consultations, investigations, evaluations, preliminary studies, aesthetic design and
106 preparation of plans, specifications and contract documents, in accordance with all codes and
107 regulations, co-ordination of building systems, administration of construction contracts and any
108 other similar service or combination of services in connection with the design and construction
109 of nonstructural interior construction, regardless of whether one or all of these services are being
110 performed and regardless of whether these services are performed in person or as the directly
111 head of an office or organization performing them; provided the practice of commercial interior
112 design or registered commercial interior design shall not include the practice of architecture or
113 engineering as defined in chapter 112. The practice of registered commercial interior design does
114 not include services that alter the building’s occupancy classification and use designation,
115 structure, or primary fire, life safety, mechanical, electrical, plumbing or exits as defined by the
116 780 CMR state building code, or other related primary building systems except as explicitly
117 permitted by applicable law.
118 “Registered commercial interior designer”, any person who has been licensed to
119 engage in the practice of commercial interior design as herein defined.

120 “Registered Commercial Interior Designer Emeritus”, an honorary title issued to a
121 licensed commercial interior designer who has retired from the active practice of commercial
122 interior design in the commonwealth.
123 Section 291. Upon approval by the board, an individual who has passed the
124 interior design examination administered by the National Council for Interior Design
125 Qualification (NCIDQ) may use the title Registered Commercial Interior Designer. Such
126 individual shall, upon satisfactory completion of the aforementioned requirements and any other
127 requirements and qualifications as deemed necessary by the board, send to the board a copy of
128 documentation of the proof of: (i) passage of the NCIDQ exam; (ii) satisfactory evidence of
129 graduation from a program accredited by the Council of Interior Design Accreditation and of
130 such practical experience in commercial interior design work as the board may by regulation
131 prescribe. In lieu of evidence of graduation from a Council of Interior Design Accreditation
132 program, the applicant may submit satisfactory evidence of such other academic experience,
133 practical experience, or both, as the board may by regulation prescribe; (iii) any certifications
134 awarded to the individual by the National Council for Interior Design Qualification; (iv) and any
135 other documentation as required by the board. Such documentation shall include, but not be
136 limited to, the individual’s name, mailing address and email address; provided, however, that
137 such individual shall update the board of any changes to such information as they occur. Such
138 documentation shall be placed on file with the division of occupational licensure.
139 Section 292. (a) Each applicant seeking to become a registered commercial
140 interior designer shall pay to the board, upon filing their original application, a fee to be
141 determined annually per the provisions of section 3B of chapter 7. After verification of the
142 documentation required pursuant to section 291 and receipt of the application fee, the board shall

143 issue a certificate of registration for a period of 2 years. The commissioner of occupational
144 licensure or their designee, as the custodian of any documentation required by section 291, shall
145 enforce this section and may use said documentation, or any lack thereof, as deemed necessary,
146 notwithstanding any general or special law or rule or regulation to the contrary.
147 A registered commercial interior designer shall be required to complete
148 continuing education courses. Continuing education shall be gained through coursework
149 delivered in education credits. The quantity and content designation of education credits shall be
150 determined by the board.
151 It shall be unlawful for any individual who is not approved by the board to use the
152 title Registered Commercial Interior Designer or any title or device indicating that an individual
153 is a registered commercial interior designer unless they are registered under the provisions of
154 sections 290 through 301, inclusive.
155 Nothing in sections 290 through 301, inclusive, shall prohibit a person from
156 performing commercial interior design services or using the title commercial interior designer,
157 commercial interiors consultant, commercial interior decorator or a similar title; provided,
158 however, that the word “registered” shall not be used in conjunction with such a title.
159 A registered commercial interior designer shall not practice architecture as
160 defined in section 60A of chapter 112, engineering as defined in section 81D of chapter 112, or
161 any other profession regulated under the laws of the commonwealth, unless specifically
162 authorized to do so under sections 290 through 301, inclusive, or to prepare, sign or seal plans
163 with respect to such practice or in connection with any governmental permits unless licensed or
164 otherwise permitted to do so under such laws.

223 Section 296. Charges against a registered commercial interior designer involving
224 any matter coming within the jurisdiction of the board shall be in writing and shall be filed with
225 the board. Such charges, at the discretion of the board, shall be heard within 30 days after being
226 so filed. The accused registered commercial interior designer shall have the right at such hearing
227 to appear personally, with or without counsel, to cross-examine witnesses against them and to
228 produce evidence and witnesses in their defense. The board shall set the time and place for such
229 hearing and shall cause a copy of the charges, together with a notice of the time and place fixed

230 for the hearing, to be sent by registered mail to the accused registered commercial interior
231 designer, at their latest place of residence or business known to the board, at least 10 days before
232 such date. If after such hearing the board finds the accused registered commercial interior
233 designer guilty of the charges, or any of them, it shall issue an order revoking, suspending or
234 annulling the provisional registration or certificate of registration of the accused registered
235 commercial interior designer, or reprimanding, censuring or otherwise disciplining them. If the
236 board finds them not guilty it shall enter an order dismissing the charges. If such order be that a
237 provisional registration or certificate of registration be revoked, suspended or annulled, the board
238 shall in writing so notify the state secretary and the clerk of the city or town in the
239 commonwealth wherein such registered commercial interior designer has their principal place of
240 business.
241 The board may re-issue a provisional registration or certificate of registration to
242 any person whose provisional registration or certificate of registration has been revoked.
243 Application for the re-issuance of a provisional registration or certificate of registration shall be
244 made in such manner as the board may direct, and shall be accompanied by a fee to be
245 determined annually per the provision of section 3B of chapter 7.
246 Section 297. Every provisional registration or certificate of registration issued and
247 remaining in force, under any provision of sections 290 through 301, inclusive, shall be prima

272 established in section 292.
