2  SECTION __. Chapter 112 of the General Laws is hereby amended by adding the 
3 following section:- 
4  “Section 298. 
5  (a) As used in this section, unless the context otherwise requires: 
6  "Administrative support" means tasks performed to assist a licensed professional in the 
7 delivery of therapy or psychotherapy services that do not involve communication. 
8  "Administrative support" includes, but is not limited to, the following: 
9  (1) managing appointment scheduling and reminders; 
10  (2) processing billing and insurance claims; and 
11  (3) drafting general communications related to therapy logistics that do not include 
12 therapeutic advice. 

2 of 10 13  "Artificial intelligence" means an engineered or machine-based system that varies in its 
14 level of autonomy and that can, for explicit or implicit objectives, infer from the input it receives 
15 how to generate outputs that can influence physical or virtual environments.   
16  “Bureau” means the Bureau of Health Professions Licensure. 
17  “Consent" means a clear, explicit affirmative act by an individual that: (i) unambiguously 
18 communicates the individual's express, freely given, informed, voluntary, specific, and 
19 unambiguous written agreement, including a written agreement provided by electronic means, 
20 and (ii) is revocable by the individual. 
21   "Consent" does not include an agreement that is obtained by the following: (1) the 
22 acceptance of a general or broad terms of use agreement or a similar document that contains 
23 description of artificial intelligence along with other unrelated information; (2) an individual 
24 hovering over, muting, pausing, or closing a given piece of digital content; or (3) an agreement 
25 obtained through the use of deceptive actions. 
26  "Licensed professional" means an individual who holds a valid license issued by this 
27 State to provide therapy or psychotherapy services, including: (1) a licensed clinical 
28 psychologist; (2) a licensed clinical social worker; (3) a licensed social worker; (4) a licensed 
29 professional counselor; (5) a licensed clinical professional counselor; (6) a licensed marriage and 
30 family therapist; (7) a certified alcohol and other drug counselor authorized to provide therapy or 
31 psychotherapy services; (8) a licensed advanced practice registered nurse; (9) a licensed 
32 physician who specializes in the practice of psychiatry; and (10) any other professional 
33 authorized by this State to provide therapy or psychotherapy services. 

3 of 10 34  "Peer support" means services provided by individuals with lived experience of mental 
35 health conditions or recovery from substance use that are intended to offer encouragement, 
36 understanding, and guidance without clinical intervention. 
37  "Religious counseling" means counseling provided by clergy members, pastoral 
38 counselors, or other religious leaders acting within the scope of their religious duties if the 
39 services are explicitly faith-based and are not represented as clinical mental health services or 
40 therapy or psychotherapy services. 
41  "Supplementary support" means tasks performed to assist a licensed professional in the 
42 delivery of therapy or psychotherapy services that do not involve therapeutic communication and 
43 that are not administrative support. 
44  "Supplementary support" includes, but is not limited to, the following: (1) preparing and 
45 maintaining client records, including therapy notes; (2) analyzing anonymized data to track client 
46 progress or identify trends, subject to review by a licensed professional; and (3) identifying and 
47 organizing external resources or referrals for client use. 
48  "Therapeutic communication" means any verbal, non-verbal, or written interaction 
49 conducted in a clinical or professional setting that is intended to diagnose, treat, or address an 
50 individual's mental, emotional, or behavioral health concerns. 
51  "Therapeutic communication" includes, but is not limited to: (1) direct interactions with 
52 clients for the purpose of understanding or reflecting their thoughts, emotions, or experiences; 
53 (2) providing guidance, therapeutic strategies, or interventions designed to achieve mental health 
54 outcomes; (3) offering emotional support, reassurance, or empathy in response to psychological 
55 or emotional distress; (4) collaborating with clients to develop or modify therapeutic goals or 

4 of 10 56 treatment plans; and (5) offering behavioral feedback intended to promote psychological growth 
57 or address mental health conditions. 
58  "Therapy or psychotherapy services" means services provided to diagnose, treat, or 
59 improve an individual's mental health or behavioral health. "Therapy or psychotherapy services" 
60 does not include religious counseling or peer support. 
61  (b) As used in this section, "permitted use of artificial intelligence" means the use of 
62 artificial intelligence tools or systems by a licensed professional to assist in providing 
63 administrative support or supplementary support in therapy or psychotherapy services where the 
64 licensed professional maintains full responsibility for all interactions, outputs, and data use 
65 associated with the system and satisfies the requirements of subsection (c); and provided, that an 
66 artificial intelligence that conducts administrative support shall not communicate or interact with 
67 a patient or the patient's legally authorized representative in a manner that reasonably misleads 
68 the patient or the patient's legally authorized representative about its artificial identity; provided 
69 further that an artificial intelligence system shall not violate this clause if it includes a disclosure 
70 in its communications that is clear, conspicuous, and reasonably designed to inform the patient or 
71 patient's legally authorized representative with whom the artificial intelligence communicates or 
72 interacts that it is artificial intelligence. 
73  (c) No licensed professional shall be permitted to use artificial intelligence to assist in 
74 providing supplementary support in therapy or psychotherapy where the client's therapeutic 
75 session is recorded or transcribed unless: 
76  (1) the patient or the patient's legally authorized representative is informed in writing of 
77 the following: 

5 of 10 78  (A) that artificial intelligence will be used; and 
79  (B) the specific purpose of the artificial intelligence tool or system that will be used; and 
80  (2) the patient or the patient's legally authorized representative provides consent to the 
81 use of artificial intelligence. 
82  (d) An individual, corporation, or entity may not provide, advertise, or otherwise offer 
83 therapy or psychotherapy services, including through the use of Internet-based artificial 
84 intelligence, to the public in this State unless the therapy or psychotherapy services are 
85 conducted by an individual who is a licensed professional. 
86  (e) A licensed professional may use artificial intelligence only to the extent the use meets 
87 the requirements of subsections (b) and (c). A licensed professional may not allow artificial 
88 intelligence to do any of the following: 
89  (1) make independent therapeutic decisions; 
90  (2) directly interact with clients in any form of therapeutic communication; 
91  (3) generate therapeutic recommendations or treatment plans without review and 
92 approval by the licensed professional; or 
93  (4) detect emotions or mental states without review and approval by the licensed 
94 professional. 
95  (f) All records kept by a licensed professional and all communications between an 
96 individual seeking therapy or psychotherapy services and a licensed professional shall be 
97 confidential and shall not be disclosed except as required by law. 

6 of 10 98  (g) Any individual, corporation, or entity found in violation of this section shall pay a 
99 civil penalty to the bureau in an amount not to exceed $10,000 per violation, as determined by 
100 the bureau, with penalties assessed based on the degree of harm and the circumstances of the 
101 violation. The civil penalty shall be assessed by the bureau after a hearing. 
102  An individual, corporation, or entity found in violation of this section shall pay the civil 
103 penalty within 60 days after the date of an order by the bureau imposing the civil penalty. The 
104 order shall constitute a judgment and may be filed and executed in the same manner as any 
105 judgment from a court of record. 
106  (h) The bureau shall have authority to investigate any actual, alleged, or suspected 
107 violation of this section. 
108  (i) This section does not apply to the following: 
109  (1) religious counseling; 
110  (2) peer support; and 
111  (3) self-help materials and educational resources that are available to the public and do 
112 not purport to offer therapy or psychotherapy services.”

184  SECTION __. The health policy commission shall study and make recommendations 
185 related to government oversight or restrictions on the use of artificial intelligence in medical 
186 care. The study and recommendations shall include, but are not limited to: (i) a review of the use 
187 of artificial intelligence in the provision of medical care, including medical care provided or 
188 offered through internet-based artificial intelligence; (ii) an evaluation of existing protections for 
189 patients related to the use of artificial intelligence in the provision of medical care in the 
190 commonwealth and the sufficiency of such protections; (iii) a review of relevant literature and an 
191 analyses of the positive and negative outcomes and impacts of the use of artificial intelligence in 
192 the provision of medical care in the commonwealth and other states, including but not limited to, 
193 financial and care quality outcomes; and (iv) an evaluation of laws and regulations implemented, 
194 considered, or enacted in other states relevant to improving patient protections related to the use 
195 of artificial intelligence in the provision of medical care. The commission shall file the report of 
196 its study and recommendations with the clerks of the house of representatives and the senate, the 
197 house and senate committee on ways and means, the joint committee on health care financing, 
198 and the joint committee on advanced information technology, the internet, and cybersecurity no 
199 later than August 1, 2027.
