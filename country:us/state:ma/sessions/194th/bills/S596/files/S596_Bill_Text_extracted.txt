An Act relative to hunting near a dwelling. 
 
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority 
of the same, as follows: 
 
1  Section 58 of chapter 131 of the General Laws, as appearing in the 2022 Official Edition, 
2 is hereby amended by inserting, in line 6, after the word "thereof" the following words:- 
3  ", provided that a person may hunt with a bow and arrow from an elevated tree stand not 
4 less than 250 feet from any dwelling in use except as authorized by the owner or occupant 
5 thereof".
