SENATE DOCKET, NO. 3300 FILED ON: 10/20/2025
SENATE . . . . . . . . . . . . . . No. 2656
Senate, October 20, 2025 -- Order (Senator Spilka) relative to a precept setting forth the Special
Election to fill the vacancy of the First Middlesex Senatorial District
The Commonwealth of Massachusetts
Senate, October 20, 2025.
_______________
In the One Hundred and Ninety-Fourth General Court
(2025-2026)
_______________
1 Ordered, That by the authority of article IV of section II of chapter I of the Constitution,
2 the Senate declares that, by reason of the death of Edward J. Kennedy as senator from the First
3 Middlesex district, the office of senator from the First Middlesex district is vacant. By the
4 authority of article XXIV of the Amendments to the Constitution, the Senate directs the president
5 of the Senate to issue a precept setting forth March 3, 2026, as the day for holding an election to
6 fill the vacancy in the First Middlesex district, consisting of the city of Lowell and the towns of
7 Dracut, Dunstable, Pepperell and Tyngsborough in the county of Middlesex.
