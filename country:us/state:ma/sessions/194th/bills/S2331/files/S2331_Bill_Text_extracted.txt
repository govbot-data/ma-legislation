An Act defining agritourism. 
 
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority 
of the same, as follows: 
 
1  SECTION 135A. Chapter 128 of the General Laws is hereby amended by inserting after 
2 section 1A the following section:- 
3  Section 1B. (a) For the purposes of this section, the following terms shall, unless the 
4 context clearly requires otherwise, have the following meanings: 
5  "Agritourism", an agriculturally related educational, entertainment, historical, cultural, 
6 commercial, or recreational activity that allows or invites members of the general public to 
7 observe, participate in, experience and enjoy that activity, and that serves to encourage the 
8 purchase of agricultural products grown on the land where the activity takes place. 

2 of 2 9  “Land”, an entire parcel of land or multiple contiguous whole parcels of land owned by 
10 the same person. 
11  (b) Agritourism may be conducted on land primarily used for commercial agriculture, 
12 aquaculture, silviculture, horticulture, floriculture or viticulture. Agritourism may be conducted 
13 on land not primarily used for commercial agriculture, aquaculture, silviculture, horticulture, 
14 floriculture or viticulture if: (i) at least 75 per cent of the acreage of said land is dedicated to 
15 agriculture; (ii) at least 50 per cent of the agricultural product produced on said land, by either 
16 gross annual sales or annual volume, is utilized in agritourism activities; (iii) at least 25 per cent 
17 of the gross annual income from all activities on the land on which the agritourism activity is 
18 located results from the sale of products that have been produced on said land; and (iv) at least an 
19 additional 50 per cent of the gross annual income from all activities on said land results from the 
20 sale of products that have been produced on said land or on land in Massachusetts used for the 
21 primary purpose of commercial agriculture, aquaculture, silviculture, horticulture, floriculture or 
22 viticulture.
