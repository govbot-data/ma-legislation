(2025-2026)
_______________
An Act amending the town charter in the town of Oxford.
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority
of the same, as follows:
1 SECTION 1. Section 5-2-2 of Chapter 5 of the charter of the Town of Oxford, which is
2 on file in the office of the Archivist of the Commonwealth, is hereby amended by striking out the
3 words “The town manager need not be a resident of the town when appointed, but he shall take
4 up residency within eight months following his appointment.”
5 Section 2. This act shall take effect upon its passage.
