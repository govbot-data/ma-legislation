The Commonwealth of Massachusetts

HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES, November 10, 2025. 
 The committee on Transportation, to whom were referred the petition 
(accompanied by bill, House, No. 3659) of Michael J. Finn relative to peer-
to-peer car sharing; the petition (accompanied by bill, House, No. 3681) of 
Russell E. Holmes relative to parking with auction dealer license plates; the 
petition (accompanied by bill, House, No. 3745) of David K. Muradian, Jr., 
relative to private sale requirements for used vehicles and requiring that the 
Registry of Motor Vehicles create and maintain an approved bill of sale 
form for use by private sellers; the petition (accompanied by bill, House, 
No. 3760) of Kelly W. Pease relative to expired motor vehicle inspection 
stickers; the petition (accompanied by bill, House, No. 3770) of John H. 
Rogers for legislation to require the Department of Highways to acquire a 
certain portion of Brook Street and Willet Pond Dam in the town of 
Norwood by eminent domain; the petition (accompanied by bill, House, No. 
3771) of John H. Rogers relative to the used vehicle record book; the 
petition (accompanied by bill, House, No. 3799) of Marcus S. Vaughn 
relative to inspections of new motor vehicles; and the petition (accompanied 
by bill, House, No. 3801) of David T. Vieira relative to motor vehicle 
registration plates of federally recognized Native American tribes, reports 
recommending that the accompanying order (House, No. 4710) ought to be 
adopted. 
 
For the committee, 
 
JAMES ARCIERO.
