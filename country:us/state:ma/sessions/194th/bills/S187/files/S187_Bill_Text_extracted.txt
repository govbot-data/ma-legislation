An Act relative to protecting biometric information under the security breach law. 
 
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority 
of the same, as follows: 
 
1  SECTION 1. Section 1 of chapter 93H of the General Laws, as appearing in the 2022 
2 Official Edition, is hereby amended by inserting after the second paragraph the following 
3 paragraph:- 
4  “Biometric indicator”, any unique biological attribute or measurement that can be used to 
5 authenticate the identity of an individual, including, but not limited to, fingerprints, genetic 
6 information, iris or retina patterns, voice recognition, facial characteristics or hand geometry. 
7  SECTION 2. Said section 1 of said chapter 93H is hereby further amended by striking 
8 out, in line 45, the word “or”.  

2 of 2 9  SECTION 3. Said section 1 of said chapter 93H is hereby further amended by inserting 
10 after the word “public,” in line 52, the following words:- ; or (d) a biometric indicator.
