Dear Mr. James:
In compliance with Section 33 of Chapter 30 of the Massachusetts General Laws, as
Inspector General, I respectfully submit six legislative proposals for the 2025-2026 session of the
General Court.
1) The first proposal, An Act updating Chapter 30B, would equalize the price thresholds
in the Uniform Procurement Act. Currently, based upon a recent change in the law,
school districts are permitted to seek three written quotations for procurements up to,
but not exceeding, $100,000, rather than following the competitive sealed bidding
process. This bill would allow the same threshold and practice to occur for the
municipal side of government. The proposal also increases the sound business practice
threshold to $15,000 and allows the use of a request for proposals for procurements of
$100,000 or less in Chapter 30B.
2) The second proposal, An Act relative to snow hauling and removal, adds snow hauling
and snow removal services to Chapter 30B’s exemption for snow plowing.
3) The third proposal, An Act relative to electric vehicles and charging infrastructure,
allows a single procurement method for electric vehicles, including school buses and
their charging infrastructure under Chapter 30B. Currently, the procurement of electric
vehicles and their charging infrastructure are separate procurements.
4) The fourth proposal, An Act relative to retail electricity suppliers, strengthens the
enforcement mechanisms and bolsters surety requirements for Retail Electricity
Suppliers (RES). The OIG reviewed RES programs and found that enforcement gaps
in the programs prevented the Commonwealth from recouping millions of dollars that
suppliers owed to the state.
5) The fifth proposal, An Act relative to county land disposition, clarifies the process that
county governments must take when disposing of real property. The proposal requires
county governments to offer, in writing, surplus property to the Division of Capital
