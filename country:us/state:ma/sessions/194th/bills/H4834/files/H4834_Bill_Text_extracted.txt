An Act relative to medical health and fitness facilities. 
 
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority 
of the same, as follows: 
 
1  SECTION 1. Chapter 111 of the General Laws is hereby amended by inserting after 
2 section 248 the following section:  
3  Section 249. (a) As used in this section, the following words shall have the following 
4 meaning unless the context clearly requires otherwise – “Medical Health and Fitness Facilities” 
5 (“MHFF”) are defined as any wellness facility that has created protocols and operating 
6 procedures which all such facilities will need to achieve and maintain in order to be recognized 
7 as such a facility by the department. 
8  (b) Medical Health and Fitness Facilities as recognized by the department must adhere to 
9 and achieve the following safety standards and facility specifications and requirements: 
10  1. Medical Application Standards: MHFF facilities must maintain, adhere to and 
11 provide for all of the following medical safeguards and medical service programs:  
12  a. Written policies and procedures regarding member/guest safety; 

2 of 6 13  b. Emergency response procedures; 
14  c. Individualized health and mental health assessments; 
15  d. Disease management and prevention programs; 
16  e. Lifestyle change/health-risk and chronic pain reduction programs; 
17  f. Physical therapy, post-operative/injury; 
18  g. Physical therapy continued care program; 
19  h. Implementation and maintenance of a Medical Advisory Board; and 
20  i.  A mental health referral program.  
21  (c) Medical Advisory Board (“MAB”) is group of healthcare professionals headed by a 
22 Medical Director that meet on at least a quarterly basis for the purpose of reviewing and 
23 providing professional oversight for all aforementioned medical applications:  The members of 
24 the MAB should be recognized experts in their field. Their combined experience and valued 
25 insights should direct the facility in all efforts to enhance all existing wellness programs as well 
26 as maintain and improve the safety protocols for current participants. The MAB also plays a 
27 large part in determining what new services and or lifestyle improvement programs may be 
28 needed or valuable to neighboring communities and the client/patient community. 
29  i. Medical Director 
30             i. The Medical Director is a licensed medical physician who sits on the 
31 Medical Advisory Board and oversees, directs and reviews all communications with the MAB. 

3 of 6 32             ii. The Medical Director is responsible for the safe and appropriate 
33 application of all medical programs within the facility. 
34  2. Staffing Requirements and Qualifications 
35  a. All MHFF facilities are required to employ personnel in the following full-time 
36 positions with qualifications listed. 
37  b. Fitness Director: must have a minimum of an Exercise Physiology or Health 
38 Science Degree or the equivalent. 
39  c. Certified Pool Operator: Facilities with pools and/or hot tubs and Jacuzzi must 
40 have a certified pool operator. 
41  d. Operations Manager: Must have a BS in Facility and or Operations Management 
42 with 5+ years’ experience.  
43  e. Fitness Manager on Duty (“FMOD”):  all facilities must employ a fitness manager 
44 on duty at all times of operation.  
45  f. Medical Director: All facilities must have a licensed medical physician to oversee 
46 the MAB. The Medical Director will be responsible for overseeing the medical and behavioral 
47 change programs within each facility. The Medical Director will work with the MAB and facility 
48 management in order to coordinate various interdisciplinary teams to execute the facility’s 
49 medical and wellness policies, systems and agenda. 
50  3. Cleaning and Hygiene Procedures: MHFF facilities must maintain and enforce the 
51 following cleaning protocols in accordance with the Massachusetts Safety and Standards 
52 Checklist 

4 of 6 53  a. Provide ample and sufficient sanitation wipes easily accessed for members and 
54 staff to clean equipment before and after each individual use. 
55  b. Provide ample and sufficient hand sanitation stations easily accessed for members 
56 and staff to consistently sanitize their hands. 
57  c. Staff is required to spray down equipment with EPA approved disinfectant at 
58 scheduled times throughout the day to maintain optimal safety and cleanliness. Staff must log 
59 and track cleaning times. 
60  d. Staff will disinfect high touch, high use areas consistently throughout the day, 
61 with EPA approved N list cleaning product and log and track times. 
62  e.  Increase cleaning staff to maintain and enable consistent, thorough and 
63 continuous facility sanitization. 
64  4. Air Quality and Control 
65  a.  MHFF facilities will be required to purchase, install and continuously operate non-
66 thermal plasma self-contained electronic air and surface decontamination unit(s) 
67  b.  Said unit(s) shall have the capacity to eradicate bacteria, viruses, mold and odor at an 
68 average reduction rate of 90% in the air and 95% on surfaces in large occupied spaces by 
69 creating oxidized molecules from ambient air using non-thermal plasma technology  
70  c.  Said unit(s) will not use chemicals in any aspect of operation and will comply with 
71 USDA National Organic Standards (7 CFR Part 205) 
72  d.  Said unit(s) shall be powered by standard 120 volt outlet 

5 of 6 73  e.  Said unit(s) shall have no effect on temperature or humidity of the facility  
74  f.  Said unit(s) shall offer continuous 24 hour treatment  
75  g.  Said unit(s) can be calibrated for different operations cycles 
76  h.  Said unit(s) shall be compliant with OSHA safety and air quality regulations. 
77  5. Safety Standards and Required Protocols: MHFF facilities will be required to 
78 implement and maintain the following protocols: 
79  a. Facilities must maintain an updated member contact process e-mail or cell phone 
80  b. Signs must be posted conspicuously throughout required spaces communicating 
81 safety regulations and requirements for said spaces. 
82  c. All new members are required to fill out a detailed health check form 
83  d. Automatic External Defibrillators (AEDs) are required; one unit for every 75,000 
84 sq. ft. to ensure prompt and timely application. 
85  e. All staff must be trained in CPR and AED use 
86  f. All staff must have basic lifesaving skill certification 
87  g. Facility must have and train all employees in an emergency action plan for the 
88 following: 
89             i. Fire 
90             ii. Health emergency 

6 of 6 91             iii. Active shooter 
92             iv. Positive COVID test 
93              v. Aquatic emergency 
94  h. A facility must track and document training times and trained personnel. 
95  SECTION 2. The department of public health shall produce an application and any 
96 necessary regulations. The department, within 150 days of receipt of the Medical Health and 
97 Fitness Facility application pursuant to this act, render a final decision denying or granting a 
98 certificate.  The department shall grant a facility a certificate to operate as a Medical Health and 
99 Fitness Facility pursuant to this act if, upon receipt of the application, if the department finds 
100 that: (i) the granting of such certificate would be consistent with the best interests of the public 
101 health, welfare or safety; and (ii) the facility is in compliance with the requirements of this act 
102 and applicable regulations.  A certification shall last for a period of 24 months from the date of 
103 receipt.
