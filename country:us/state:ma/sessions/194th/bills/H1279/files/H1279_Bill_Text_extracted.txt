By Representative Owens of Watertown, a petition (accompanied by resolve, House, No. 1279)
of Steven Owens and Vanna Howard for an investigation and study by a special commission
(including members of the General Court) to develop statutory and regulatory framework to
address the setting of rates by insurance companies for properties with affordability restrictions.

Resolve creating a special commission on affordable housing insurance.
1 Resolved, that there shall be a special commission established to develop a statutory and
2 regulatory framework to address the setting of rates by insurance companies for properties with
3 affordability restrictions and properties where tenants use vouchers to pay part or all of their rent.
4 The commission shall report its findings, including any proposed legislation, to the house and
5 senate committees on ways and means, the joint committee on financial services and the joint
6 committee on housing no later than December 31, 2026.
7 The commission shall consist of 10 members including: 1 member of the house of
8 representatives appointed by the speaker of the house; 1 member of the senate appointed by the
9 senate president; the commissioner of insurance, or a designee, who will act as chair; the
10 secretary of the executive office of housing and livable communities, or a designee; the attorney
11 general, or a designee; 1 of whom shall be a representative from the Citizens' Housing And
12 Planning Association; 1 of whom shall be a representative from the Massachusetts Association
13 of Community Development Corporations; 1 of whom shall be a representative from the

14 Builders of Color Coalition; 1 of whom shall be a representative from the National Association
15 of Industrial and Office Properties Massachusetts; and 1 of whom shall be represent an insurance
