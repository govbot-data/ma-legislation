The Commonwealth of Massachusetts

HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES, December 1, 2025.
The committee on Transportation, to whom was referred the petition
(accompanied by bill, House, No. 3710) of Hannah Kane and others for
legislation to include a module on the science related to the impairment
effects of marijuana in driver education courses, reports the accompanying
order (House, No. 4780) ought to be adopted [Representative DeCoste of
Norwell dissents].
For the committee,
JAMES ARCIERO.
