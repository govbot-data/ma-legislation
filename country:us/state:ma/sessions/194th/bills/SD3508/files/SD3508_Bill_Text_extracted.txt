Dear Clerks Hurley and Carroll, and Chairs Rodrigues and Michlewitz,
Pursuant to Chapter 197 of the Acts of 2024, the Executive Office of Health and Human Services
(EOHHS) is required to submit a report on the Long-Term Care Workforce and Capital Fund.
In FY25, there were no expenditures from or revenues credited to the Fund. EOHHS will not
submit further reports on this fund unless it is capitalized in the future.
If you have any questions, please contact MassHealth’s Deputy Legislative Director Sarah
Nordberg at Sarah.Nordberg@mass.gov.
