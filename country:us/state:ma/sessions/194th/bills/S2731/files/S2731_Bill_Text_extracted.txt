An Act to protect coastal resources.. 
 
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority 
of the same, as follows: 
 
1  SECTION 1. There is hereby established the Massachusetts Coastal Erosion Innovation 
2 Center, whose purpose shall be to develop, identify, research, advance and deploy innovative 
3 means, methods, technologies and approaches for protecting and strengthening the resilience of 
4 the state's coastal shoreline, including but not limited to beaches, marshes, dunes and public and 
5 private property and infrastructure. The center shall be administered by the Executive Office of 
6 Energy and Environmental Affairs, and shall consist of representatives of the Office of Coastal 
7 Zone Management, the Department of Environmental Protection, the Department of 
8 Conservation and Recreation, the Executive Office of Housing and Economic Development, and 
9 the Executive Office of Administration and Finance, whom shall meet at intervals of not less 
10 than 6 months for the purposes set forth in Section 3, provided that such meetings shall be open 
11 to public observation, provided further that the center may elect to receive written or oral 
12 testimony with proper notice given.  
13  SECTION 2. The center shall be advised by an advisory group appointed by the 
14 governor, comprised of 3 members representing municipal officials, selected from a list 

2 of 2 15 submitted by the Massachusetts Municipal Association, 3 members representing environmental 
16 advocacy organizations in the commonwealth, 3 members representing owners of coastal 
17 property in the commonwealth, 3 members whom shall have expertise in engineering, 
18 construction, or architecture, 1 member with expertise in coastal geology, and 2 members 
19 representing the taxpayers of the commonwealth. Said advisory group shall meet not less than 
20 every 3 months, and at the request of the Innovation Center.  
21  SECTION 3. The center shall identify, develop, and research, in consultation with one or 
22 more institutions of higher learning or research in the commonwealth, innovative methods of 
23 protecting coastal resources, with prioritization for natural approaches such as thin layer marsh 
24 deposition, living shorelines, vegetation, eradication of invasive plant and shellfish species such 
25 as green crab, and any other relevant methodology. The center shall produce an annual report of 
26 its activities, which shall be provided to the house and senate chairs of the joint committee on 
27 environment and natural resources, filed with the clerks of the house and senate and posted 
28 electronically for public inspection.  
29  SECTION 4. The provisions of this act shall expire on December 31, 2035, unless 
30 otherwise repealed, modified or extended.
