An Act authorizing the release or exclusion of certain land from conservation restrictions in the 
town of Deerfield. 
 
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority 
of the same, as follows: 
 
1  SECTION 1. (a) Notwithstanding sections 32 to 37, inclusive, of chapter 7C of the 
2 General Laws or any other general or special law to the contrary, the commissioner of capital 
3 asset management and maintenance, in consultation with the commissioner of conservation and 
4 recreation, may execute a certificate of release or amendment of conservation restriction to 
5 release or exclude from a conservation restriction granted to the department of environmental 
6 management, predecessor to the department of conservation and recreation, by USGen New 
7 England, Inc., predecessor in title to Great River Hydro, LLC, that portion of land as lies within 
8 the limits of lands to be acquired by the town of Deerfield for the Massachusetts Department of 
9 Transportation’s rehabilitation of Upper road over the Deerfield river in the town of Deerfield. 
10 The portion of land is more particularly described in subsection (b). The conservation restriction 
11 is described in book 3812, page 90, dated July 18, 2001 and recorded in the Franklin county 
12 registry of deeds. The land for the conservation restriction is shown on maps on file with the 
13 federal energy regulatory commission, license No. 2323.  

 
2 of 5 14  (b) The portion of land to be released or excluded pursuant to subsection (a) is 
15 approximately 15,977 square feet and located along the northwesterly side of Stillwater road and 
16 Upper road, shown as permanent easement parcels E-1, E-2, E-3, D-S-1, D-1, S-1 and PUE-6 on 
17 plan sheets No. 9 through 11, temporary construction areas parcel TCA-1 on plan sheet No. 9 
18 and temporary occupancy areas parcels A, B, C, D and E and further TCA-1 on said plan sheets 
19 in a preliminary set of right of way plans entitled “Upper Road Over Deerfield River (Bridge No. 
20 D-06-001 (OPP)), in the Town of Deerfield, Franklin County, Preliminary Right of Way Plans” 
21 except as otherwise noted, to be filed with the chief engineer of the highway division of the 
22 Massachusetts Department of Transportation, and recorded with the Franklin county registry of 
23 deeds. The lands are partially identified on the Deerfield town assessors’ maps as parcel ID No. 
24 89-11 and 89-13, and are fully and presently owned by Great River Hydro, LLC, successor in 
25 title to USGen New England, Inc., pursuant to a deed recorded in the Franklin county registry of 
26 deeds in book 4823, page 84, and as clarified by the affidavit recorded in book 5192, page 154, 
27 of the same. 
28  (c) All parcels to be used for this construction project shall be restored to their condition 
29 prior to the construction project to the furthest extent possible considering the permanent 
30 structures to be installed thereon, in order to protect the conservation values of the surrounding 
31 lands. 
32  SECTION 2. (a) As mitigation for the release or exclusion described in section 1, the 
33 town of Deerfield shall convey to the commonwealth, to be held under the care and control of the 
34 department of conservation and recreation for the purposes of conservation and recreation 
35 pursuant to Article XCVII, a conservation restriction as defined in section 31 of chapter 184 of 
36 the General Laws over certain land in the town of Deerfield consisting of approximately 5.5 

 
3 of 5 37 acres along the Deerfield river, located on Mill Village road, formerly known as Martins Falls 
38 road, identified on the Deerfield town assessors’ maps as parcel ID No. 87-8, which is currently 
39 a vacant, undevelopable parcel of land and presently under the care, custody, management and 
40 control of the town of Deerfield select board pursuant to a deed recorded in the Franklin county 
41 registry of deeds in book 973, page 319. The land is shown on a plan titled “Plan of Land for 
42 Proposed Conservation Restriction Martins Falls Road Located in Deerfield, Massachusetts 
43 Prepared for the the Town of Deerfield”, dated April 14, 2025, on file with the town of 
44 Deerfield. 
45  (b) As additional mitigation for the release or exclusion, the town of Deerfield shall 
46 ensure access by grant of a license to the commonwealth, over other lands owned by Great River 
47 Hydro, LLC, which the department of conservation and recreation has a conservation restriction 
48 over, to enable the department of conservation and recreation and its agents to access the land by 
49 foot described in subsection (a) from a public way, and shall make improvements to such access 
50 area necessary for its use by the department. The precise location of the access area shall be 
51 determined by agreement between the town of Deerfield and the commissioner of conservation 
52 and recreation. 
53  (c) If the appraisals conducted pursuant to section 3 determine that the fair market value 
54 or value in use, whichever is greater, of the land and interests in land to be conveyed to the 
55 commonwealth pursuant to this section is less than the corresponding value of the land 
56 authorized for release or exclusion pursuant to section 1, the town of Deerfield shall compensate 
57 the commonwealth in an amount equal to 110 per cent of the difference. The town of Deerfield 
58 shall pay such sum to the department of conservation and recreation for deposit into the 

 
4 of 5 59 Conservation Trust established in section 1 of chapter 132A of the General Laws, to be used to 
60 acquire land or interests in land subject to Article XCVII, including due diligence costs. 
61  SECTION 3. The value of the property described in this act shall be determined by an 
62 independent professional appraisal of its fair market value and value in use prepared in 
63 accordance with the usual and customary professional appraisal practice by a qualified appraiser 
64 commissioned by the commissioner of capital asset management and maintenance, in 
65 consultation with the commissioner of conservation and recreation. The commissioner of capital 
66 asset management and maintenance shall submit the appraisal and a report thereon to the 
67 inspector general for review and comment. The inspector general shall review and approve the 
68 appraisal, and the review shall include an examination of the methodology utilized for the 
69 appraisal. The inspector general shall prepare a report of the review and file the report with the 
70 commissioner of capital asset management and maintenance, and the commissioner of capital 
71 asset management and maintenance shall submit copies of the appraisal, the report thereon and 
72 the inspector general’s review and approval and comments, if any, to the house and senate 
73 committees on ways and means and the chairs of the joint committee on state administration and 
74 regulatory oversight at least 15 days prior to the execution of the conveyance authorized in 
75 section 1. 
76  SECTION 4. The Massachusetts Department of Transportation shall assume all costs 
77 associated with engineering, surveys, appraisal, deed preparation and other expenses necessary to 
78 execute the conveyances authorized in this act. 
79  SECTION 5. Except as partially released or amended in accordance with this act, the 
80 conservation restriction identified in section 1 shall remain in full force and effect. 

 
5 of 5 81  SECTION 6. This act shall take effect upon its passage.
