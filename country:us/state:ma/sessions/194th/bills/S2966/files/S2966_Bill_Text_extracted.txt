An Act authorizing the reorientation and further lease of a parcel of land occupied by Riverside 
Boat Club of Cambridge. 
 
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority 
of the same, as follows: 
 
1  (a) Pursuant to chapter 65 of the acts of 2010 and notwithstanding section 5A of chapter 3 
2 of the General Laws or any other general or special law to the contrary, the commissioner of 
3 capital asset management and maintenance, in consultation with the commissioner of 
4 conservation and recreation, may lease or enter into other agreements with The Riverside Boat 
5 Club of Cambridge in the city of Cambridge for a parcel of land, reconfigured from that shown 
6 in existing records of the department of conservation and recreation, for the continued operation 
7 of its historical rowing program and boathouse. The reconfigured parcel of land is more 
8 particularly described on a plan of land on file with the department, entitled “Plan Showing 
9 Reoriented Boundary for Riverside Boat Club of Cambridge”, dated December 12, 2022, 
10 including the adjacent water sheet currently being used and appurtenant rights of access from 
11 adjoining public roadways. 
12  (b) Any such lease or other agreement shall require that, in addition to consideration for 
13 the lease or other agreement pursuant to section 2 of chapter 65 of the acts of 2010, The 

2 of 2 14 Riverside Boat Club of Cambridge in the city of Cambridge shall maintain the area from 
15 Memorial drive to the boathouse, as depicted on a plan on file with the department of 
16 conservation and recreation, entitled “Plan Showing Maintenance Parcel for Riverside Boat Club 
17 of Cambridge” dated July 22, 2025.  
18  (c) The commissioner of capital asset management and maintenance may: (i) make minor 
19 revisions to the plan pursuant to subsection (a) and shall file the final plan with the Middlesex 
20 southern district registry of deeds; and (ii) execute any leases or other agreements consistent with 
21 chapter 65 of the acts of 2010.
