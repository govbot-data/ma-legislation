An Act relative to the reckless evasion of a police officer.
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority
of the same, as follows:
1 Chapter 90 of the General Laws, as appearing in the 2016 Official Edition, is hereby
2 amended by inserting after section 25 the following section:-
3 Section 25A. Any person who flees or attempts to elude a police officer while operating a
4 motor vehicle willfully or with a wanton disregard for the safety of persons or property, shall be
5 punished by a fine of not less than one hundred dollars and not more than one thousand dollars or
6 by imprisonment for not less than six months and not more than two years, or both. A conviction
7 under this provision shall result in a driver's license suspension by the registry of motor vehicles
8 for 60 days. For the purposes of this section, willful or wanton disregard for the safety of persons
9 or property includes, but is not limited to, driving while fleeing or attempting to elude a pursuing
1 of 2

10 police officer during which time the driver causes property damage or makes three or more
11 moving violations.
