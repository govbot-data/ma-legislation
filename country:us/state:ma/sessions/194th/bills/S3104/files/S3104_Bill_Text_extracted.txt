RESOLUTIONS RESPONDING TO THE SUPREME JUDICIAL COURT’S ORDER OF
MAY 7, 2026, AND ENHANCING GOVERNMENT TRANSPARENCY.
1 WHEREAS, AS ELECTED MEMBERS OF THE MASSACHUSETTS GENERAL
2 COURT, SENATORS BEAR A RESPONSIBILITY TO UPHOLD THE CONSTITUTION
3 AND PRESERVE THE INDEPENDENCE OF THE GENERAL COURT AS A COEQUAL
4 BRANCH OF GOVERNMENT; AND
