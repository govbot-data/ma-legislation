An Act relative to coverage for chronic illness.
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority
of the same, as follows:
1 SECTION 1. Chapter 176J of the General Laws, as appearing in the 2020 Official
2 Edition, is hereby amended by inserting after section 14 the following new section:-
3 Section 14A. No carrier shall charge a co-payment or deductible for any prescriptions or
4 devices that are prescribed by a physician and are necessary for the treatment or maintenance of
5 a chronic disease, illness or condition. The department of public health shall adopt regulations
6 further defining a chronic disease, illness or condition for purposes of this section.
