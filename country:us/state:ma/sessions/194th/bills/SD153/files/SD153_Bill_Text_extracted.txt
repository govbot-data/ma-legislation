Senate, January 9, 2025 -- Order (Senator Spilka) establishing temporary committees on Ways 
and Means and Bills in the Third Reading 
 
The Commonwealth of Massachusetts 
 
 Senate, January 09, 2025. 
_______________ 
In the One Hundred and Ninety-Fourth General Court 
(2025-2026) 
_______________ 
 
 
1  Ordered, That notwithstanding Senate Rule 12 or any other rule to the contrary, until 
2 such time as permanent committees are appointed, there shall be a temporary committee on 
3 Ways and Means and a temporary committee on Bills in the Third Reading.  The membership of 
4 each temporary committee created by this order shall consist of the same members on the 
5 committees in the 193rd General Court.
