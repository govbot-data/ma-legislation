An Act authorizing William Pilleri to take the civil service examination for the position of 
firefighter in the town of Arlington notwithstanding the maximum age requirement. 
 
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority 
of the same, as follows: 
 
1  SECTION 1. Notwithstanding chapter 31 of the General Laws or any other general or 
2 special law to the contrary, William Pilleri, a resident of the town of Arlington, may take the 
3 civil service examination for appointment to the position of firefighter in the town of Arlington, 
4 notwithstanding his having reached the age of 32.   
5    
6  SECTION 2. This act shall take effect upon its passage and shall expire on June 1, 2029.
