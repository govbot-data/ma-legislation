An Act relative to professional licensure. 
 
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority 
of the same, as follows: 
 
1  Chapter 147 of the general laws is hereby amended in section 1 by striking the section in 
2 its entirety and inserting in place thereof the following new section: 
3  Section 1. (a) The commissioner of occupational licensure shall supervise the work 
4 of the several boards of registration and examination included in the division of occupational 
5 licensure.  The commissioner shall recommend changes in the methods of conducting 
6 examinations and transacting business and shall make such reports to the governor and council as 
7 they may require or as the commission may deem expedient.  
8            (b) The commissioner may consider requests to eliminate, reduce, or 
9 otherwise diminish standards or requirements for professional licensure of individuals, 

2 of 2 10 businesses, or trades; provided however that the commissioner shall not have the authority to 
11 grant said requests without approval by a majority vote of the board of registration that oversees 
12 the trade or profession said request affects.
