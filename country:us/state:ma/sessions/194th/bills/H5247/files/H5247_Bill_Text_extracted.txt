In the One Hundred and Ninety-Fourth General Court
(2025-2026)
_______________
An Act authorizing the town of Maynard to prohibit or restrict the application of second-
generation anticoagulant rodenticides.
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority
of the same, as follows:
1 SECTION 1. Notwithstanding any general or special law to the contrary, the town of
2 Maynard may enact and enforce local regulations, by-laws or ordinances prohibiting or
3 restricting the use of second-generation anticoagulant rodenticides within the boundaries of the
4 town , including but not limited to their use on public and private properties.
5 SECTION 2. This act shall take effect upon its passage.
