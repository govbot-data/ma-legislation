In the One Hundred and Ninety-Fourth General Court
(2025-2026)
_______________
An Act creating a maximum allowable check- cashing rate.
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority
of the same, as follows:
1 SECTION 1. Section 13 of chapter 169A of the General Laws, as appearing in the 2020
2 Official Edition, is hereby amended by striking out, in line 3, the words “five hundred dollars”
3 and inserting in place thereof the following words:- $1,000.
4 SECTION 2. Said chapter 169A is hereby further amended by adding the following
5 section:-
6 Section 14. (a) No licensee shall directly or indirectly charge or collect fees or other
7 considerations for cashing checks payable to natural persons in excess of the following:
8 (1) 2.5 per cent of the face value of a check plus a service charge of $1.00 for any
9 government check less than $1,500 issued by the United States, or the commonwealth.
10 (2) 2.25 per cent of the face value of a payroll check plus a service charge of $1.00.
1 of 2

11 (3) 5 per cent of the face value of a check or $5.00, whichever is greater, plus a
12 service charge of $1.00, for all personal checks.
13 (4) 3 per cent of the face value of a payment instrument less than $1,500 plus a service
14 charge of $1.00 for all other payment instruments, including checks, drafts, or money orders.
15 (b) No licensee shall charge more than $5.00 to set up an initial customer account.
16 SECTION 3. This act shall take effect twelve months after the date of enactment.
