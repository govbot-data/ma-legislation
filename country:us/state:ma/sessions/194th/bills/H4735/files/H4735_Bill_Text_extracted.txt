HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES, November 17, 2025. 
 The committee on Transportation, to whom was referred the petition 
(accompanied by bill, House, No. 3630) of Nicholas A. Boldyga relative to 
vehicle registrations for certain recreation vehicles at sanctioned races, 
rallies or events, reports recommending that the accompanying order 
(House, No. 4735) ought to be adopted [Representative DeCoste of Norwell 
dissents]. 
 
For the committee, 
 
JAMES ARCIERO.
