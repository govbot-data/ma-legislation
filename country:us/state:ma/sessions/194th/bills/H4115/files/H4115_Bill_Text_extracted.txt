An Act relative to preventing immigration reporting and ensuring the right to legal counsel. 
 
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority 
of the same, as follows: 
 
1  SECTION 1. Chapter 29 of the General Laws is hereby amended by inserting after 
2 section 2JJJJJJ the following section:- 
3  Section 2KKKKKK. (a) There shall be established and set up on the books of the 
4 commonwealth a separate fund known as the Immigrant Legal Assistance Fund, hereinafter 
5 referred to as the fund. The fund shall award funding to organizations who represent and provide 
6 legal counsel to undocumented immigrants in a court of law. The fund shall be administered by 
7 the Massachusetts Legal Assistance Corporation. 
8  (b) The fund shall be credited with: (i) revenue from appropriations or other money 
9 authorized by the general court and specifically designated to be credited to the fund; (ii) interest 
10 earned on money in the fund; (iii) contributions from private sector entities, including local 
11 immigration agencies or non-profits, foundations and other organizations; and (iv) federal grants 
12 and other gifts, grants and donations for the support of legal representation for undocumented 

2 of 2 13 immigrants. Amounts credited to the fund shall not be subject to further appropriation and any 
14 money remaining in the fund at the end of a fiscal year shall not revert to the General Fund. 
15  (c) The Massachusetts Legal Assistance Corporation shall annually, not later than 
16 December 31, report on the activity of the fund to the: (i) chairs of the joint committee on the 
17 judiciary; (ii) chairs of the house and senate committees on ways and means; and (iii) clerks of 
18 the house of representatives and senate. 
19  SECTION 2. Chapter 276 of the General Laws is hereby amended by adding the 
20 following section:- 
21  Section 104. All law enforcement officers of local and state law enforcement agencies 
22 shall not contact or share information with federal authorities, including federal immigration 
23 authorities, regarding any person's immigration status. Law enforcement officers or law 
24 enforcement agencies may share such information with federal authorities only when verified 
25 through the commonwealth’s criminal offender record information database established in 
26 section 172 of chapter 6 that the individual has a documented criminal conviction. All law 
27 enforcement officers or law enforcement agencies communications with federal authorities shall 
28 follow departmental protocols and be authorized by a supervising officer who shall document the 
29 basis for sharing the individual’s information.
