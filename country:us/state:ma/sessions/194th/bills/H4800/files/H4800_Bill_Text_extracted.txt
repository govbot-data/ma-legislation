HOUSE . . . . . . . . No. 4800
The Commonwealth of Massachusetts
________________________________________
HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES, December 1, 2025.
The committee on State Administration and Regulatory Oversight, to
whom were referred the petition (accompanied by bill, House, No. 3326) of
Rodney M. Elliott, Estela A. Reyes and Natalie M. Higgins for legislation
to designate Diwali as a legal holiday in the Commonwealth and the petition
(accompanied by bill, House, No. 3430) of Marcus S. Vaughn, Hannah
Kane and others for the issuance of an annual proclamation by the Governor
to designate the fifteenth day of the Hindu lunisolar month of Kartik as
Diwali day, reports recommending that the accompanying order (House,
No. 4800) ought to be adopted [Representative Kane of Shrewsbury
dissents].
For the committee,
ANTONIO F. D. CABRAL.

HOUSE . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . No. 4800
The Commonwealth of Massachusetts
House of Representatives, December 1, 2025.
1 Ordered, That the committee on State Administration and Regulatory Oversight be
2 authorized to sit during a recess of the General Court to make an investigation and study of
3 House documents numbered 3326 and 3430, relative to Diwali Day.
4 Said committee shall report to the General Court the results of its investigation and study
5 and its recommendations, if any, together with drafts of legislation necessary to carry such
6 recommendations into effect, by filing the same with the Clerk of the House of Representatives
7 on or before December 31, 2026.
