An Act providing for a wind-farm study. 
 
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority 
of the same, as follows: 
 
1  Chapter 21A of the General Laws is hereby amended by adding the following section:- 
2  Section 29. (a) Notwithstanding any general or special law to the contrary, prior to the 
3 installation of any offshore wind turbine, the secretary shall commission a study of the potential 
4 environmental impact of the installation and operation of such offshore wind turbine. The study 
5 shall investigate the potential impacts of the installation and operation of offshore wind turbines 
6 on the area surrounding the proposed site of installation and operation, including, but not limited 
7 to, the impact on the water, the ocean floor and the marine life in the surrounding area. 
8  (b) The secretary shall file a report, including any findings on the environmental impact 
9 of proposed offshore wind turbines, to the clerks of the house of representatives and senate and 

2 of 2 10 the joint committee on environment, natural resources and agriculture no less than 180 days 
11 before the installation of proposed offshore wind turbines is set to begin.
