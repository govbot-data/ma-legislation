HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES, December 1, 2025.
The committee on Higher Education, to whom was referred the petition
(accompanied by bill, House, No. 1424) of Antonio F. D. Cabral relative to
higher education capital investments in gateway municipalities or abutting
municipalities, reports the accompanying order (House, No. 4779) ought to
be adopted.
For the committee,
DAVID M. ROGERS.
