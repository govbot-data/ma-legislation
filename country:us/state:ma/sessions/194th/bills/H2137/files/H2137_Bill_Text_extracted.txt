An Act providing for payment by the Commonwealth of wages for members of the United States
Coast Guard during the shut-down, so-called, of the United States Government.
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority
of the same, as follows:
1 Notwithstanding any general or special law to the contrary the salaries of members of the
2 United States Coast Guard who are residents of the Commonwealth shall be paid by the
3 Commonwealth during the so-called federal government shut-down. The funds for such
4 payments shall be paid from an account to be recommended by the Governor and determined by
5 the committees on Ways and Means.
6 Upon the conclusion of the so-called federal government shut-down, the Commonwealth
7 shall seek reimbursement from the federal government for the cost of all such payments made to
8 such Coast Guard members.
