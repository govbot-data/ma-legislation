An Act relative to rodeos. 
 
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority 
of the same, as follows: 
 
1  SECTION 1. Chapter 128 of the General laws is hereby amended by inserting after 
2 section 2B the following section:- 
3  Section 2B1/2. Rodeos shall comply with all Official Rodeo Rules as appearing in the 
4 most recently published edition of the Professional Rodeo Cowboy Association Rule Book. For 
5 the purposes of this section, “rodeo” shall be defined as “a competition or exhibition in which 
6 individuals compete by or showcase skills associated with traditional cowboy activities, 
7 including but not limited to bull riding, bronc riding, bareback bronc riding, steer wrestling, calf 
8 roping, and team roping, and include the use of animals, including but not limited to bulls, steers, 
9 calves, broncs, and horses”.
